Periodic Inspection Procedure

Preface

The current inspection and maintenance system was established when the Road Transport Vehicle Act came into force in Showa 26. Against a background of improved durability and reliability due to recent advances in automotive technology and decreased annual mileage due to changes in usage patterns, the inspection and maintenance system has now been revised, including abolition of the 1-month inspection for commercial passenger vehicles and reduction of periodic inspection items.

However, although durability and reliability have improved through technological advances, a vehicle consists of many parts and, as a machine, wear and deterioration of its parts through use are unavoidable. Wear and deterioration of parts lead to reduced performance; continuing to use the vehicle in that condition affects safety and pollution prevention and also shortens the life of the vehicle. Therefore, periodic inspection and maintenance remain necessary to use a vehicle safely and comfortably.

This manual describes the work procedures, judgment methods and work sequences for periodic inspection and maintenance of Toyota vehicles based on the new vehicle inspection standards that take effect on 5/1, Arial 12, following this revision of the Vehicle Act. Please use it as a reference for performing periodic inspection and maintenance accurately and quickly.

Please note in advance that work procedures, judgment methods, etc. in this manual may change due to subsequent vehicle changes.
