WARNING!

    To ensure safe work and to prevent damage to customers' vehicles, strictly observe the following precautions.

    1. This data is intended for qualified service engineers who have acquired specialized technical skills.
    2. Service performed by persons who are not qualified or have not received appropriate training, service performed without the appropriate tools and equipment, or service performed using methods other than those described in this data may not only damage the vehicle but also cause injury to the service engineer and others nearby.

      • Proper servicing and repair are essential to ensure the safety of the service engineer as well as the safety and reliability of the vehicle. Also, when replacing parts, always use genuine Toyota parts with the same part number. Do not use deteriorated parts.
      • The contents described in this data are procedures that must be followed when performing service and repairs. To perform servicing and repair according to these procedures, correctly use the SST (Special Service Tools) designed specifically for each purpose.
      • Never use work methods or tools that are not recommended, as doing so may compromise the safety of the service engineer and the safety and reliability of the vehicle.
      • To prevent accidents during service and repair work, and to prevent damage to the vehicle and loss of safety and reliability due to improper work methods, this data describes various items that must be observed as <WARNING> and <NOTICE> (refer to the Introduction). However, as these instructions cannot warn of every possible hazard, please understand in advance that the items described as <WARNING> and <NOTICE> are not absolute rules guaranteeing that following them alone will avoid all danger.
      • This vehicle is equipped with SRS airbags and seat belt pretensioners. Before servicing the components or wiring of the SRS airbags and seat belt pretensioners, refer to "Precautions" in the SRS airbag section and perform servicing and repair work in accordance with the instructions. Failure to follow the instructions may cause the SRS airbags and seat belt pretensioners to malfunction, cause serious or fatal personal injury, damage the vehicle or surrounding objects, or affect the system. Read the instructions carefully and perform the work using the correct procedures and methods.
