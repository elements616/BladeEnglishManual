Periodic Inspection Procedure 2

Replacement (2/2)

Severe Conditions (Guideline)

Condition
Private Passenger Vehicles
A Rough roads (bumpy roads, gravel roads, snow-covered roads, unpaved roads)
  • Rough road surfaces where the driver feels jolts
  • Road surfaces where stones are often kicked up or the underbody often hits ruts, etc.
  • Dusty road surfaces (guideline: 30% or more of distance driven)
B High Mileage
  • High mileage (guideline: 20,000km or more per year)
C Frequent driving on mountain roads and uphill/downhill roads
  • Many uphill and downhill runs with frequent brake application (guideline: 30% or more of distance driven)
D Repeated short-distance driving
  • Driving pattern with a short distance per trip and much driving at low coolant temperature (temperature of each part) (guideline: 8km or less per 1 trip)
E Frequent High-Altitude Driving
  • Frequent driving at altitudes of 2,000m or higher (guideline: 30% or more of distance driven)
