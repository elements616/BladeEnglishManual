Body Sound Absorbing and Insulating Materials

Body Sound Absorbing and Insulating Materials

●
Various silencers are placed in appropriate locations to minimize transmitted noise from the engine, tires, etc., providing excellent sound insulation.
●
A significant improvement in quietness has been achieved by adopting a 3-layer dash panel insulator with an olefin sheet sandwiched between felt layers and a dash panel outer insulator made of laminated nonwoven fabrics of different densities, as well as by reducing the size of the heater pipe passage holes and improving the seal with the column hole cover.
●
A damping sheet is provided on the interior side of the cowl top panel, reducing noise transmitted from the engine room.
●
Asphalt sheets and spray-on damping material are effectively arranged on the floor panel, achieving both high sound insulation and weight reduction.
●
Foam material for blocking cross sections and resin-molded foam material are optimally provided in each part of the body, and service holes are closed with sealer, etc., minimizing noise transmitted through the pillars, etc.

Sound Absorbing and Insulating Structure (Engine Room Area)

Sound Absorbing and Insulating Structure (Engine Room Area)

●
A hood insulator is provided under the hood panel. In addition, a 2-layer structure with an air layer between the hood panel and hood insulator suppresses noise transmitted from the engine room.
●
A nonwoven fabric cowl insulator is provided on the cowl top outer front panel, reducing noise transmitted from the engine room.

●
Fender side covers that seal the gap between the front fender panels and the body are provided, suppressing the entry of road noise from the tires and reducing wind noise caused by air flowing in.

●
The dash panel area has a dash cross member connecting the left and right front side members, a cowl to brace connecting the cowl and floor tunnel, and a tunnel reinforcement. This reduces longitudinal vibration of the dash area and vertical swinging vibration of the cowl caused by vibration of the front side members, minimizing engine booming noise and road noise.

Sound Absorbing and Insulating Structure (Cabin Area)

Sound Absorbing and Insulating Structure (Cabin Area)

●
Front fender side panel protectors are provided to seal the gap between the front fender panels and the body. This limits the entry of road noise from the tires and reduces wind noise caused by air flowing in.

●
Sound absorbing material is provided on the fender liners to absorb road noise and ensure quietness.

●
The door opening trim of the front and rear doors adopts a full-circumference 2-layer seal structure (A-A cross section), ensuring sound insulation performance. In addition, an upper seal is provided at the joint between the rear door window division bar and the rear window upper frame.

Stone Chipping Noise Reduction Structure

Stone Chipping Noise Reduction Structure

●
Rocker protectors are provided on the standard grade and side mudguards on other grades, reducing the noise of sand thrown up by the tires striking the underside of the rocker area (chipping noise).
●
By providing rear wheel house liners, high-frequency road noise and the noise of sand thrown up by the tires striking the back of the wheel house (chipping noise) have been reduced.
