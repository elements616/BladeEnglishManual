Intake General

●
A resin surge tank with built-in electric ACIS (variable intake system) has been adopted, reducing weight while ensuring torque across the entire range from low to high speed and achieving high output.
●
A 2-way intake system has been adopted in the air cleaner case, and the 2-way intake valve is switched according to the driving conditions to achieve both high performance and low noise.
●
A porous duct has been adopted for the cool air intake, ensuring quietness without using a low-frequency resonator. In addition, a resonator is provided on the air cleaner hose to reduce intake noise.
●
The ETCS-i system has been adopted, with a 1-valve type electronically controlled throttle body.
