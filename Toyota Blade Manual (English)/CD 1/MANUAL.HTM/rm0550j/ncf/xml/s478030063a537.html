CVT Fluid Warmer

●
A CVT fluid warmer is provided instead of the common CVT fluid cooler built into the radiator.
●
The CVT fluid warmer speeds up warm-up of the CVT fluid when the engine is started and functions as an oil cooler during normal driving.
