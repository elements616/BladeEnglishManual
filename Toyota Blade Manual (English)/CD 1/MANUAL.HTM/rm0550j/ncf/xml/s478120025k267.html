Fuel Gauge Operation



Normal Operation

•
The meter computer receives the remaining fuel level in the fuel tank (fuel level signal) from the fuel sender gauge and calculates the fuel level display data through arithmetic processing.
•
Based on the calculated fuel level data, the meter computer controls the rotation angle, direction and speed of the pointer (stepper motor rotor) with the stepper motor driver circuit to indicate the remaining fuel level.
•
The meter computer performs initialization control that returns the pointer to the zero position each time power is supplied to the combination meter.


Low Fuel Level Warning Operation

•
When the fuel level display data calculated for driving the fuel gauge decreases to a remaining fuel level of approximately 9L, the meter computer illuminates the low fuel level warning indicator light in the combination meter.
•
The low fuel level warning indicator light goes off when the remaining fuel level is approximately 10.5L or more.
