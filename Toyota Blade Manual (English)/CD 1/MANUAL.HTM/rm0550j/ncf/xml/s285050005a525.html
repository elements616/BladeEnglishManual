Rear Suspension Outline

●
All models adopt a double wishbone type suspension with trailing arms, in which the trailing arms and upper arms are made of steel plate, and the lower arms consist of a pressed steel lower arm No.2 and a lower arm No.1 with a toe adjustment mechanism.
●
The suspension geometry has been optimized, achieving excellent handling stability and ride comfort at a high level.

Anti-squat / Anti-lift Geometry


•
The instantaneous center (OR) of the suspension geometry has been brought closer to the height of the axle center. This suppresses squat during acceleration and lift during braking.


Anti-squat Geometry


•
When driving force is generated at the rear wheels during acceleration, etc., the load on the front and rear wheels changes due to inertia. As a result, the load on the rear wheels increases and the rear of the vehicle squats. In anti-squat geometry, the geometric intersection point (OR) of the suspension is brought closer to the axle center; when the reaction force (T) of the driving force acting on the axle center tries to move the vehicle forward via the intersection point (OR), because there is an angle between the reaction force of the driving force and the direction of the intersection point, component forces are generated in the direction of the intersection point (T1) and in the direction of the ground contact surface (T2). By reducing this component force in the direction of the ground contact surface, squat at the rear of the vehicle during acceleration is suppressed.


Anti-lift Geometry


•
Load transfer during braking reduces the load on the rear wheels, causing the rear of the vehicle to lift. In anti-lift geometry, the geometric intersection point (OR) of the suspension is set higher than the ground contact surface. As a result, when the braking force (BR) acting on the rear wheels is supported at the intersection point (OR), component forces are generated toward the intersection point (BR1) and acting on the ground contact surface (BR2). Because the force acting on the ground contact surface is in the opposite direction to the tire reaction force against the sprung load reduced by braking, lift at the rear of the vehicle can be suppressed.
