Inner Rear View Mirror

●
An automatic anti-glare EC (electrochromic) mirror, which detects the ambient and rear brightness with sensors and automatically changes its reflectivity, has been adopted on the G grade, and a manual anti-glare mirror on the standard grade.
●
Stepless control of the automatic anti-glare EC mirror reflectivity provides excellent dimming performance.
●
A shift light that illuminates the shift lever area has been provided at the bottom of the automatic anti-glare EC mirror.
Automatic Anti-glare EC Mirror Construction




●
The automatic anti-glare EC mirror contains a sensor that detects ambient light and a sensor that detects rear light.
●
In automatic anti-glare mode, day or night is determined based on the intensity of the ambient light; when it is determined to be night and the rear light is detected to be stronger than the ambient light, the mirror reflectivity is adjusted steplessly between approximately 7% (when the difference between rear light and ambient light is large) and approximately 75% (when the difference between rear light and ambient light is small) according to the ratio of rear light to ambient light.

AUTO Mode (Automatic Anti-glare Mode)
AUTO Mode OFF (Non-anti-glare Mode)
When the IG is turned ON, the AUTO mode indicator light illuminates and automatic anti-glare control is performed. Even if AUTO mode was turned OFF during the previous IG ON, automatic anti-glare control is performed when the IG is turned ON.
When the surroundings are bright (determined to be daytime), the reflectivity is adjusted to the maximum value (approximately 75%: equivalent to a normal mirror).
When the surroundings are dark (determined to be night), the reflectivity is automatically adjusted steplessly between normal and maximum anti-glare according to the rear brightness.
Automatic anti-glare control can be cancelled by pressing the AUTO mode switch to the "OFF" side. In this case, the reflectivity is fixed at the maximum value (approximately 75%).

Automatic Anti-glare EC Mirror Structure


•
A gel type EC mirror cell (a solution-type electrochromic material sandwiched between transparent conductive coatings) has been adopted, and the electrochromism phenomenon* is used to provide the anti-glare structure.
HINT
*
A phenomenon in which a material is colorless when no voltage is applied, and becomes colored through an electrochemical oxidation-reduction reaction when voltage is applied.


Automatic Anti-glare EC Mirror Operation


•
When the IG is turned ON, current is supplied to the ambient light/rear light sensors, and each sensor outputs a detection signal.
•
The detection signal from the ambient light sensor is output to the day/night judgment circuit, and the detection signals from the ambient light and rear light sensors are output to the glare judgment circuit. These circuits judge the ambient and rear illuminance, and output a judgment signal to the EC drive circuit via the "AUTO" mode switch. When the EC drive circuit receives the judgment signal, it applies a corresponding voltage to the inner mirror EC element to color it, thereby controlling the mirror reflectivity.
