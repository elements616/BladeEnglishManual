Intake Manifold and Surge Tank

●
A lightweight, thin-wall cast aluminum intake manifold has been adopted. In addition, the port diameter and port length have been optimized to achieve high output and high torque.
●
A resin surge tank with a built-in ACIS valve has been adopted, reducing weight, noise and intake air temperature, and achieving high output across the entire range from low to high speed. In addition, the electric rotary solenoid and surge tank are integrated by laser welding.
