Parking Brake

●
A built-in type parking brake with built-in calipers has been adopted.
●
A stock type center lever parking brake lever has been adopted.

Built-in Caliper Construction


•
The parking brake mechanism consists of a crank lever that transmits the operating force of the parking brake lever to the actuator assembly, an actuator assembly that pushes out the adjusting bolt by rotation of the crank lever, and a sleeve nut that transmits the force of the adjusting bolt to the piston.
•
The adjuster mechanism consists of the adjusting bolt and sleeve nut of the actuator assembly, which adjust the screw-in amount according to the protrusion of the piston, and a bearing is provided to allow the sleeve nut to rotate smoothly.


Built-in Caliper Operation


•
When the parking brake lever is operated, the crank lever rotates via the parking brake cable.
•
The crank lever is connected to the input shaft of the actuator assembly; when the crank lever rotates, the check balls in the actuator assembly slide the adjusting bolt in the push-out direction due to the change in groove depth.
•
When the adjusting bolt slides, the brake pads are pressed against the disc via the sleeve nut and piston, generating braking force.


Built-in Caliper Adjuster Operation


•
When the brake pads wear and the clearance between the pads and rotor exceeds a certain value, the hydraulic pressure from pedal operation causes the sleeve nut to advance while rotating, adjusting the clearance. In addition, the piston and sleeve nut are engaged at the clutch surface; when the hydraulic pressure from pedal operation reaches a certain value, the sleeve nut stops rotating and adjustment stops, preventing over-adjustment and keeping the clearance between the pads and rotor appropriate.
