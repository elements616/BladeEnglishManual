Body Shell

●
In the rear area, the arrangement and joints of the various frame members have been optimized to suppress inward collapse deformation of the pillars and ensure high driving stability.
●
In the rear area, body torsional rigidity has been improved by forming a continuous closed cross section in a ring from the back door opening upper frame to the roof side outer panel and rear floor cross member No. No.2.
●
Rigidity of the back door opening has been ensured by forming a continuous closed cross section.
●
The body lower back panel reinforcement has a closed cross-section structure and is joined to the left and right rear floor side members to improve body torsional rigidity.
●
By reinforcing the rear floor side members, vertical displacement of the rear floor side members is suppressed and body rigidity is ensured.

●
In the floor panel and rocker areas, movement of the body frame has been suppressed and high quietness ensured by providing rocker inner bulkheads, straightening rear floor cross member No. No.1, and ensuring high joint rigidity between the floor side members and floor.
●
High quietness has been ensured by optimizing the panel rigidity and bead arrangement of the floor pan and tuning panel resonance.

●
A front suspension member brace has been provided on the lower surface of the front suspension member to ensure the longitudinal rigidity of the front suspension member.
