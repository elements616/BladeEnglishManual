Radiator Grille & Emblem

●
The radiator grille emphasizes the protruding feel of the center by varying the thickness and depth of the horizontal fins. In addition, black-painted vertical ribs shaped with consideration for the lustrous surfaces, a distinctive thin U-shaped outer frame with smoked plating, and horizontal fins accentuate the premium feel.
●
The emblem design is based on the lowercase letter "b", the initial of "BLADE" (meaning "sword"), arranged in the image of a sharp sword tip and combined with a deep blue ornament to express high quality and elegance.
