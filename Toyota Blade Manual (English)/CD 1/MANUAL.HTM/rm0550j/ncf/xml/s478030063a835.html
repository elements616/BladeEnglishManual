Pulley Speed Sensors and Turbine Speed Sensor

●
The primary pulley speed sensor (NIN), which detects the input shaft speed, is located at the primary pulley, and the secondary pulley speed sensor (NOUT), which detects the output shaft speed, is located on the secondary pulley shaft (reduction drive gear).
●
The turbine speed sensor (NT) for lock-up engagement pressure control and forward/reverse clutch pressure control is located at the forward clutch drum.
