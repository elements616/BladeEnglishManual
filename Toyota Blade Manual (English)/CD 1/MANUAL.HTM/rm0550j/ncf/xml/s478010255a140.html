Exhaust Manifold

●
By adopting a stainless steel sheet exhaust manifold, weight has been reduced and corrosion resistance improved, and catalyst warm-up immediately after engine start has been accelerated to reduce emissions.
●
A three-way catalyst integrated with the exhaust manifold has been adopted. This speeds up warm-up of the catalytic converter, reducing emissions from immediately after engine start.
●
A heat insulator is provided to cover the manifold.
●
The exhaust gas flow has been optimized to reduce exhaust pressure loss, increasing output.
