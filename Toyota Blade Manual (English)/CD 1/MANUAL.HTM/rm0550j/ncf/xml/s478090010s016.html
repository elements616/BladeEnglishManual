SRS Airbag & SRS Knee Airbag & Seat Belt with Pretensioner Outline

●
The system consists of the driver and front passenger SRS airbags, driver SRS knee airbag, seat belts with pretensioner + force limiter, front airbag sensors, center airbag sensor (computer), etc.

Main Components and Functions


Components
Function
Spiral Cable
Transmits the ignition signal from the center airbag sensor (computer) to the driver SRS airbag.
Driver and Front Passenger SRS Airbags, Driver SRS Knee Airbag
Consists of an inflator and a bag. In response to the ignition signal from the center airbag sensor, the bag is instantly inflated by gas generated by the inflator, reducing and absorbing the impact on the occupant.
Seat Belt with Pretensioner + Force Limiter
The gas generator is ignited by the ignition signal from the center airbag sensor (computer), instantly retracting the seat belt and increasing occupant restraint.
SRS Airbag Warning Light
Illuminates to warn the driver when there is a system malfunction.
Front Airbag Sensor
Detects the deceleration in a collision and sends a signal for ignition judgment to the center airbag sensor (computer).
Center Airbag Sensor (Computer)
Consists of a safing sensor, ignition judgment circuit, etc. The safing sensor detects the deceleration in a collision, and ignition judgment is performed based on the signals from the front airbag sensors; ignition signals are then sent to the SRS airbags, SRS knee airbag and seat belts with pretensioner + force limiter. When there is a system malfunction, the system can be diagnosed by switching to diagnosis mode.
