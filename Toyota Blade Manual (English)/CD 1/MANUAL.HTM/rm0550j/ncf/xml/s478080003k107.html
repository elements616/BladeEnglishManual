Clean Air Filter

●
a coarse dust removal layer and an electret* layer is used on all models.
●
The clean air filter (pollen removal type) achieves reduced pressure loss and high removal efficiency through an optimal fiber configuration and a cleanable construction. By removing fine dust such as pollen and diesel particulates, it provides a more comfortable cabin space.
●
The filter is located above the blower fan in the blower unit, so the clean air filter can be replaced without tools simply by removing a one-touch clip, providing excellent serviceability.
HINT
*
Electret: A substance that semi-permanently produces an electric field in its surroundings.
■ NOTICE ■
Always set the recirculation mode and turn the IG OFF before replacing the filter.
To maintain the dust collecting capacity of the filter, replace it periodically.
To maintain the dust collecting capacity of the filter, clean it periodically (every 15000km) and replace it (every 30000km). In addition, in areas with heavy traffic such as large cities or dusty areas such as cold regions, the service life of the filter will be shorter and earlier cleaning (every 7500km) and replacement (every 15000km) may be necessary.
