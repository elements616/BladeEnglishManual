Steering Column Outline

●
A manual tilt & telescopic steering column is used on all models, allowing the driver to set any desired operating position.
●
An energy absorbing mechanism and an intermediate shaft contraction mechanism are used to ensure excellent driver safety in a collision.
●
To ensure safety in a collision, a separation mechanism is provided on the break-away bracket. Separation of the break-away bracket is controlled by the break-away capsules at the vehicle mounting points.

Construction and Operation of Tilt & Telescopic Mechanism


•
The tilt mechanism has an adjustment range of 15mm up and down from the neutral position for a total of 30mm, and the telescopic mechanism has an adjustment range of 20mm forward and rearward from the neutral position for a total of 40mm.
•
The tilt & telescopic operation lever is provided at 1 location, improving operability for the driver.


Energy Absorbing Mechanism


•
A tube contraction type energy absorbing mechanism is used. The tube contraction type utilizes the sliding resistance between the inner tube and outer tube.
