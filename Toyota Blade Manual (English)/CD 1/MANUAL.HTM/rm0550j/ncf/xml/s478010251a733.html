Fuel Pump Control

●
When the starter signal is ON and when the engine speed signal is input continuously for 2 seconds or more, the circuit opening relay is turned ON to operate the fuel pump.
●
In addition to when the engine is stopped, the fuel pump is also stopped when the airbag is deployed.
●
The engine control computer detects the airbag deployment signal and turns OFF the circuit opening relay to stop the fuel pump.
●
The fuel pump speed is controlled in 2 stages according to the driving conditions by the fuel pump resistor.

Related Parts


Crankshaft Position Sensor (Ne Signal)

●
A highly accurate electromagnetic pickup type sensor has been adopted. Detects the crank position and crank angular velocity.
●
The timing rotor on the crankshaft has 34 signal teeth with 2 teeth missing for top dead center detection. The crank position sensor detects the crank rotation signal every 10° and can accurately detect top dead center from the missing teeth area.
●
As the crankshaft rotates, the air gap between the timing rotor protrusions and the crank position sensor changes, so the magnetic flux passing through the coil of the crank position sensor increases and decreases, generating an electromotive force in the coil. Because this generated voltage is reversed when the timing rotor protrusion approaches and moves away from the cam position sensor, it appears as an AC voltage.

Center Airbag Sensor (Computer)

●
The center airbag sensor (computer) consists of a G sensor, an ignition determination circuit, a backup power source, etc.
●
Based on the signals from the front airbag sensors and G sensor, it determines ignition of the driver and front passenger SRS airbags, driver SRS knee airbag and pretensioners, and based on the signals from the side airbag sensors and rear side airbag sensors, it determines ignition of the SRS side airbags and SRS curtain shield airbags. In addition, it has a diagnosis function and performs self-diagnosis when a system malfunction occurs.

G Sensor
When deceleration is applied by an impact, it outputs a signal that is linear with respect to the deceleration.
Backup Power Source
Consists of a backup capacitor that supplies power to the system in a collision or if the power supply system fails, and a booster circuit that raises the voltage.
Computer (Ignition Determination Circuit)
Performs specified calculations based on the signal from the front airbag sensors, and sends an ignition signal when the calculated value exceeds a preset value. In addition, the malfunction diagnosis circuit diagnoses whether there is a malfunction in the entire system and informs the driver of any system malfunction, and the diagnosis function diagnoses the location of the system malfunction.


Deployment Determination


•
The SRS airbags, driver SRS knee airbag and seat belts with pretensioners are ignited by the impact of a frontal collision. The determination is made based on the front airbag sensor signals and the signals from the G sensor and electronic safing sensor in the center airbag sensor (computer).
•
The SRS side airbag is ignited when both the side airbag sensor signal from a side impact and the electronic safing sensor in the center airbag sensor (computer) turn ON.
•
The SRS curtain shield airbag is ignited by the ignition signal of the SRS side airbag that satisfies the above conditions, or when both the rear side airbag sensor signal and the electronic safing sensor in the center airbag sensor (computer) turn ON.
•
To keep a record of deployment, the center airbag sensor (computer) keeps the SRS airbag warning light illuminated even after the primary check. This SRS airbag warning light cannot be cleared.


Operation of Diagnosis Circuit


•
The operation of the diagnosis circuit is indicated by the SRS airbag warning light in the meter. The diagnosis circuit operates in 2 periods: the primary check and the constant check.


Primary Check


•
When the engine switch is turned to IG ON, the SRS airbag warning light illuminates for approximately 6 seconds and the primary check is performed. During this period, ignition is prohibited and the operation of the center airbag sensor is diagnosed. If a malfunction is detected during this primary check, the SRS airbag warning light remains illuminated and does not go off even after 6 seconds have elapsed.
•
For a center airbag sensor that has deployed the airbags, the SRS airbag warning light remains illuminated even after the primary check, regardless of whether there is a malfunction.

Constant Check


•
After the primary check is completed and the SRS airbag warning light goes off, the system enters the ignition-enabled state, and the diagnosis circuit constantly checks the system for malfunctions. If a malfunction is detected during this constant check, the SRS airbag warning light illuminates. (The warning indication for low power supply voltage goes off after the power supply voltage returns to normal.)

Diagnosis Function


•
By switching to the diagnosis mode, the diagnosis result for the malfunction location can be displayed by the number of times the indicator light blinks. In addition, a new diagnosis function has been adopted, and the diagnostic trouble code for the malfunction location can be read by connecting a diagnostic tool to DLC3. For details on the diagnosis mode, refer to the Repair Manual.

Driver SRS Airbag

●
The driver SRS airbag is built into the steering wheel pad.
●
The SRS airbag is a non-disassemblable type and consists of the inflator, bag, steering wheel pad, etc.

Front Passenger SRS Airbag

●
The front passenger SRS airbag is located in the upper part of the instrument panel on the front passenger side.
●
The SRS airbag is a non-disassemblable type and consists of the case, inflator, bag, etc.

Driver SRS Knee Airbag

●
The driver SRS knee airbag is located in the lower part of the instrument panel on the driver side.
●
The SRS airbag is a non-disassemblable type and consists of the case, inflator, bag, etc.

Front Airbag Sensor

●
The front airbag sensors are mounted on the radiator support.
●
The front airbag sensor detects impacts from the front and sends a deceleration signal to the computer.
