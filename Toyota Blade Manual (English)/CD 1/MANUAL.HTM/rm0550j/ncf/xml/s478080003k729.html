RS Evaporator (RS: Revolutionary Super Slim)

●
The RS evaporator consists of tanks, tubes and cooling fins; the tubes are extruded to form fine flow passages, providing excellent heat transfer performance and a thinner width (38mm). In addition, the RS evaporator achieves a significant reduction in size and weight by promoting heat transfer through reduced fin height, tube thickness and fin pitch, and by using thinner core materials. The evaporator body has a clean coat that suppresses the growth of bacteria that cause unpleasant odors, and the surface treatment is chrome-free, giving consideration to the environment.
