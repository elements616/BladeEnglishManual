Clutch with Centrifugal Fluid Pressure Canceling Mechanism

●
A centrifugal fluid pressure cancel mechanism has been adopted in the C1 and C2 clutch mechanisms, which engage and release during 2-3,3-4,4-5 and 5-6 shifts that require high responsiveness, achieving high response and smooth shift characteristics.
●
As the clutch speed increases, centrifugal force acts on the oil in the clutch, raising the hydraulic pressure and causing early engagement. In addition, shift shock may occur due to the speed difference between the input and output sides. Therefore, a cancel fluid chamber has been provided opposite the piston fluid chamber of the clutch mechanism, applying a centrifugal fluid pressure of the same magnitude in the opposite direction to the piston to eliminate the effect of centrifugal force, achieving highly responsive shifting.
