ETCS-i Control

●
Through centralized control by the engine control computer, engine output relative to the accelerator opening is optimally controlled, achieving good accelerator controllability and vehicle stability over the entire driving range.
●
The functions of normal throttle opening control (non-linear control), idle speed control (ISC) and cruise control (models with cruise control system only) have been integrated into the 1-valve type electronically controlled throttle body.
●
Through integrated control with the powertrain system, excellent handling stability is ensured and comfort is improved.
●
To allow driving in the unlikely event of a malfunction, the system has a 2-system redundant configuration to ensure reliability.

Idle Speed Control


Starting Control
When the engine is started, the throttle valve is opened to increase the air volume and improve engine startability.
Predictive Control
When the signals below are detected, the throttle valve opening is controlled to suppress speed fluctuations.
• When a change in idle speed is predicted.
• When the electrical load changes.
• When the A/C switch is operated.
• When the shift position is changed. (N→D,D→N)
Deceleration Control
During deceleration, the throttle valve is opened to increase the air flow, lowering the vacuum in the intake manifold, which reduces the consumption of oil drawn into the combustion chamber, prevents engine stall due to a sudden drop in engine speed and improves driveability.
Feedback Control
The engine speed is measured for a certain period, and if there is a difference from the target speed, the throttle valve opening is controlled to bring the idle speed to the target value.

Related Systems

Fail-safe Operation


•
The throttle position sensor detects the opening angle with 2 systems: a main circuit and a sub circuit. If a malfunction occurs in the signal, the engine control computer switches to limp mode.
When Malfunction Is Detected


If a malfunction occurs in either or both circuits, the engine control computer detects the malfunction from the voltage difference between the main circuit and sub circuit. At this time, power to the throttle motor is cut and the throttle valve is returned to a predetermined opening angle by the return spring. Limp mode driving is made possible by controlling engine output using fuel injection control and ignition timing control. Also, even if the engine control computer detects another malfunction such as in the throttle motor, limp mode driving is made possible by performing the above control.




Fail-safe Operation


•
The accelerator position sensor detects the opening angle with 2 systems: a main circuit and a sub circuit. If a malfunction occurs in the signal, the engine control computer switches to limp mode.
When 1 System Malfunctions


If a malfunction occurs in either circuit, the engine control computer detects the malfunction from the voltage difference between the main circuit and sub circuit. At this time, a limited accelerator opening angle is calculated from the sensor value of 2 circuit, and throttle control is continued, enabling limp mode driving.



When 2 System Malfunctions


The engine control computer detects the malfunction from the voltages of the main circuit and sub circuit and controls the throttle valve opening angle only within the idling range.




Related Parts


Hot-wire Type Air Flow Meter

●
A compact, lightweight plastic hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter has been adopted.
●
The hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter passes part of the intake air through the detection section and directly measures the mass flow rate, improving detection accuracy and reducing intake resistance.
●
It uses bypass flow measurement, which is less affected by intake pulsation from the air cleaner. Also, low loss is achieved by a flow passage structure with low fluid resistance, allowing air volumes from small to large to be measured reliably.
Hot-wire Type Air Flow Meter Operation


•
Part of the intake air is bypassed and the intake air volume is measured at the hot wire measuring section. The hot wire measuring section uses a platinum filament (platinum hot wire), and a bridge circuit is formed by the hot wires of the intake air temperature measuring resistor and the heating resistor (heater) to measure the engine intake air volume. Due to the characteristics of the hot-wire type air flow meter, the mass flow rate can in principle be measured directly, so density correction for changes in intake air temperature is not necessary; however, intake air temperature information is required for engine controls such as EFI. Therefore, a compact thermistor type intake air temperature sensor is built into the air flow meter to detect the intake air temperature.
•
A bridge circuit is a circuit connected as shown in the illustration below, in which, when R1 × R4 = R2 × R3 holds, V1 = V2 and the value indicated by ammeter G is 0.

•
When the intake air volume changes, the bridge circuit of the hot wire measuring section feedback-controls the power supplied to the heating resistor so that the temperature difference between the intake air temperature measuring resistor and the heating resistor (heater) is always kept constant. This supplied power is converted into a voltage and output to the engine control computer. The engine control computer calculates the engine intake air volume from the predetermined relationship between the air flow meter output voltage and flow rate.

Camshaft Position Sensor (G2 Signal)

●
A highly accurate electromagnetic pickup type sensor has been adopted. Performs cylinder identification and detection of actual camshaft timing.
●
The camshaft position sensor is installed at the rear end of the cylinder head and detects the protrusions of the camshaft timing rotor (360-180-180°CA) fixed to the intake camshaft. As the camshaft rotates, the air gap between the timing rotor protrusions and the camshaft position sensor changes, so the magnetic flux passing through the coil of the camshaft position sensor increases and decreases, generating an electromotive force in the coil. This generated voltage is reversed when the timing rotor protrusion approaches and when it moves away from the camshaft position sensor, so it appears as an AC voltage.

Crankshaft Position Sensor (Ne Signal)

●
A highly accurate electromagnetic pickup type sensor has been adopted. Detects the crank position and crank angular velocity.
●
The crankshaft timing rotor installed on the crankshaft has 34 signal teeth with 2 teeth missing for top dead center detection.
●
The crankshaft position sensor detects the crank rotation signal every 10° and can accurately detect top dead center from the location of the missing teeth. A sensor similar to the camshaft position sensor has been adopted, detecting the signal as AC power.

Water Temperature Sensor

●
Detects the engine coolant temperature and sends a signal to the engine control computer.

Throttle Position Sensor

●
A non-contact type throttle position sensor has been adopted, using the Hall effect* 1, which provides an output voltage linear to the throttle valve opening angle, and a Hall element* 2that can extract magnetic field strength as an electrical signal. When the throttle valve opening angle changes, the angle of the magnetic field relative to the flow of the applied current in the Hall element changes, so the strength of the magnetic field perpendicular to the applied current changes. As a result, the change in voltage generated perpendicular to both the applied current and the magnetic field is sent to the engine control computer as the throttle valve opening angle signal. In addition, reliability is ensured by adopting a 2-system (main and sub) sensor with different output characteristics.
HINT
*1
This is a phenomenon in which, when a current flows through a rod- or plate-shaped conductor or semiconductor and a magnetic field is applied perpendicular to it, a potential difference is generated in the direction perpendicular to both the current and the magnetic field.
*2
This is an electronic component that uses the electromotive force generated by the potential difference in a magnetic field.

Accelerator Position Sensor

●
A non-contact type accelerator position sensor that uses the Hall effect* 1, which provides an output voltage linear to the throttle valve opening angle, and a Hall element* 2, which provides an output voltage that is linear with respect to the accelerator pedal depression amount, has been adopted. When the accelerator pedal depression amount changes, the angle of the magnetic field relative to the flow of the applied current in the Hall element changes, so the strength of the magnetic field perpendicular to the applied current changes. The resulting change in voltage, generated perpendicular to both the applied current and the magnetic field, is sent to the engine control computer as the accelerator pedal depression amount signal. In addition, reliability is ensured by adopting a 2-system (main and sub) sensor with different output characteristics.

1-valve Electronically Controlled Throttle Body

●
A 1-valve type electronically controlled throttle body, in which the throttle valve is driven by a motor, has been adopted.
●
A flat shaft has been adopted to increase the opening area of the valve, improving performance.
●
The driver's operation (accelerator pedal depression amount) is transmitted to the engine control computer by the accelerator position sensor located on the accelerator pedal. The engine control computer determines the throttle opening appropriate to the driving conditions and drives the throttle motor. The throttle valve opening is fed back to the engine control computer by the throttle position sensor.
●
The throttle valve drive motor is mounted on the throttle body, and the opening and closing of the throttle valve is controlled through reduction gears. In addition, a DC motor with good response and low power consumption has been adopted.
●
The inside of the throttle position sensor has a 2-system (main and sub) configuration, and the system is constantly monitored for malfunctions.
●
If a malfunction is detected, the warning light in the meter alerts the driver and current to the motor is cut. At this time, the throttle valve is returned to a predetermined opening by the throttle valve return spring, and engine output is adjusted according to the accelerator opening through fuel injection cut and ignition retard, enabling limp-home driving.
