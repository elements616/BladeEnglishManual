Flex Lock-up Control


•
Transmission efficiency has been greatly improved by finely controlling the power transmission distribution between the lock-up clutch (mechanical power transmission) and the torque converter (fluid power transmission) according to driving conditions. In the flex lock-up system, an intermediate mode (giving slight slip to the lock-up clutch) has been set in the low vehicle speed range, further expanding the lock-up operating range. In addition, by performing lock-up operation during deceleration as well, the fuel cut range can be expanded, achieving a significant improvement in fuel economy.

•
Flex lock-up control during acceleration operates in all gears in the D position and in gears 2 and higher in S mode; during deceleration it operates in 4, 5 and 6 in the D position and S mode. This flex lock-up control uses state-of-the-art H∞ (H-infinity) control theory, achieving both system stability and responsiveness against various characteristic changes at a high level.

Flex Start Control


•
Flex start control, which improves power transmission efficiency by actively operating the lock-up clutch when starting off, has been adopted. This lowers the engine speed when starting off, improving fuel economy.
•
By performing flex lock-up from start-off, it is possible to shift quickly to flex lock-up even after upshifting to 2, achieving both improved fuel economy and drivability.


Line Pressure Control


•
By controlling the line pressure using the linear solenoid (SLT), the line pressure is optimally controlled based on torque information from the engine. This allows the line pressure to be finely controlled according to engine output and driving conditions.


Integrated Powertrain Control


•
Integrated powertrain control, which controls the engine and transaxle in an integrated manner, has been adopted to improve drivability and comfort.
•
During upshift and downshift transitions, high-quality shift feel and high responsiveness in accordance with the driver's intention have been achieved.
Start-off Control


•
Engine torque is optimally controlled in real time according to the transient characteristics of the torque converter, achieving both "reduced lurching and suppressed tire slip" and "high responsiveness" when starting off, and ensuring refined start-off performance.



Deceleration Enhancement Control


•
Deceleration that faithfully reflects the driver's intention has been achieved, and smooth acceleration is achieved without downshifts unintended by the driver during re-acceleration.
•
The gear for accelerator OFF is determined from the change in accelerator pedal opening, and inadvertent upshifts contrary to the driver's intention are prohibited.



Shift Transient Control


•
Integrated powertrain control enables control with high robustness*that is not affected by the engine combustion condition (fuel properties, operating environment, etc.), stably outputs the engine torque requested by the driver in real time, and generates the optimal clutch engagement pressure according to engine torque by direct pressure control using high-response, high-capacity linear solenoids, achieving both stable shift characteristics and responsiveness.



HINT
*
The system does not become unstable due to disturbances or modeling errors

Neutral Control


•
Neutral control has been adopted.
•
Neutral control reduces the engine load and improves fuel economy while stopped by disconnecting the engine from the transmission (partially releasing the start-off clutch inside the automatic transmission) when the vehicle is stopped (in the D position).

Control System





Downshift Control During Deceleration


•
Downshift control during deceleration, which achieves low fuel consumption by continuing fuel cut control as long as possible, has been adopted.
•
Flex lock-up control and downshift control are performed during deceleration to suppress the drop in engine speed. This maintains the fuel cut state without falling below the fuel cut recovery speed, achieving lower fuel consumption.


Clutch-to-clutch Shift Control


•
Clutch-to-clutch shift control has been adopted.
•
By adopting clutch-to-clutch shift control without using a one-way clutch in 2 and higher, a lightweight and compact 6-speed automatic transaxle has been achieved. The output pressure of the linear solenoid valves (SL1,SL2,SL3,SL4), which are controlled based on drive signals from the transmission control computer, enables direct and independent control of the clutch and brake hydraulic pressures.
•
By making full use of a hydraulic circuit that allows each clutch and brake to be controlled independently and compact, high-flow linear solenoids, each is controlled independently at the optimal hydraulic pressure and timing based on information such as input/output shaft speeds and engine torque, enabling not only single shifts but also multiple shifts, and achieving refined shift feel and improved responsiveness.


AI-SHIFT Control (AI: Artificial Intelligence)


•
The gear of the automatic transmission is determined by a shift pattern that uses vehicle speed and engine throttle opening angle as parameters.
•
AI-SHIFT control achieves comfortable driving at a high level by automatically switching shift patterns according to "road conditions" and "driver intention".
•
AI-SHIFT control is performed only in the D position based on accelerator and brake operation information, and is cancelled when switched to S mode.

Road Condition Support Control (Uphill/Downhill Control)


•
Uphill/downhill control determines uphill and downhill slopes from the throttle opening angle, vehicle speed, etc.; on uphill slopes it suppresses unnecessary upshifts so that optimal driving force is always obtained, and on downhill slopes it automatically downshifts so that optimal engine braking is obtained.



Driver Intention Support Control


•
The driver's intention is estimated from accelerator operation and vehicle conditions, and the shift pattern is switched to one that suits each individual driver naturally without any switch operation.



Diagnosis Function


•
If a malfunction is detected in the sensors or solenoids, the check engine warning light in the combination meter illuminates to warn the driver.
•
A new diagnosis system has been adopted for serviceability. By connecting TaSCAN to DLC3, diagnosis results for malfunctioning areas can be displayed. For the list of diagnosis items, refer to the Repair Manual.

Fail-safe Function


•
This function prevents a significant loss of drivability even if a malfunction occurs in the sensors or solenoids. For details, refer to the Repair Manual.

Hydraulic Control Section

●
Ultra-compact, high-flow linear solenoid valves that directly control each clutch and brake have been adopted for shift control.
●
A lightweight aluminum alloy valve body has been adopted with an upper/lower 3-piece construction, and the linear solenoid valves are mounted on the lower valve body.

Linear Solenoid Valve


•
Linear solenoid valves have been provided for clutch engagement pressure control (SL1/SL2), lock-up clutch control (SLU), line pressure control (SLT) and brake engagement pressure control (SL3/SL4).
•
By directly controlling the hydraulic pressure of each clutch and brake with high-flow linear solenoid valves (SL1,SL2,SL3,SL4), improved shift response and reduced shift shock through shorter oil passages, as well as a simplified structure through fewer components, have been achieved.
•
A filter has been installed at the tip of the ON/OFF solenoid valve (SL) to achieve excellent reliability.

Input/Output Shaft Speed Sensors

●
An input shaft speed sensor that detects the turbine speed signal (NT signal) and an output shaft speed sensor that detects the counter gear speed signal (NC signal) have been installed on the hydraulic valve body.
●
The No.2 clutch piston is used as the timing rotor for the input shaft speed sensor to detect the turbine speed. In addition, the counter drive gear is used as the timing rotor for the output shaft speed sensor to detect the counter gear speed. The transmission control computer detects shift timing based on the detection signals from each sensor, and controls the hydraulic pressure appropriately according to various conditions.

Fluid Temperature Sensor

●
To ensure shift characteristics in the extremely low temperature range and for flex lock-up control, a fluid temperature sensor has been installed in the valve body. It directly detects the ATF temperature in the transaxle hydraulic control circuit.

Fluid Pressure Switch

A hydraulic pressure switch for hydraulic control during shifting has been installed in the valve body.

Clutch with Centrifugal Fluid Pressure Canceling Mechanism

●
A centrifugal fluid pressure cancel mechanism has been adopted in the C1 and C2 clutch mechanisms, which engage and release during 2-3,3-4,4-5 and 5-6 shifts that require high responsiveness, achieving high response and smooth shift characteristics.
●
As the clutch speed increases, centrifugal force acts on the oil in the clutch, raising the hydraulic pressure and causing early engagement. In addition, shift shock may occur due to the speed difference between the input and output sides. Therefore, a cancel fluid chamber has been provided opposite the piston fluid chamber of the clutch mechanism, applying a centrifugal fluid pressure of the same magnitude in the opposite direction to the piston to eliminate the effect of centrifugal force, achieving highly responsive shifting.

Torque Converter

●
A lightweight, compact and high-capacity ultra-flat torque converter has been adopted. Simplifying the structure of the one-way clutch has greatly shortened the shaft length.
●
With the high heat-resistant lock-up friction material and the 3WAY cooling circuit, a flex start system has been adopted. Flex lock-up is performed from start-off, enabling smooth start-off and reducing slip loss; together with the adoption of a low-speed lock-up damper, low-speed lock-up in all gears from start-off is possible, achieving high fuel economy performance.

3WAY Cooling Circuit


•
In the 3WAY cooling circuit, a dedicated cooling circuit has been provided on the lock-up ON pressure circuit side (between the input shaft and stator shaft). Improved cooling performance expands the lock-up range, improving fuel economy.
