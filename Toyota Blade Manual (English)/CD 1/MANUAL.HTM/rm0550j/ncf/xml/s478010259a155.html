Alternator

●
A compact, lightweight, high-output, high-efficiency segment conductor type alternator has been adopted.
●
A charging control system has been adopted. The power generation load of the alternator is controlled according to the vehicle driving conditions, and the generated voltage is optimally controlled to improve fuel economy.
●
A clutch is provided in the alternator pulley, reducing accessory belt friction and improving fuel economy.

Alternator Pulley


•
A helical coil-shaped clutch located between the outer and inner races of the pulley transmits torque to the alternator only in the engine's forward rotation direction, reducing the load on the accessory belt.
•
Compactness has been achieved by integrating the inner race with the nut that fastens it to the alternator shaft.
•
A plastic cap is provided on the front of the pulley to cover the inside and prevent the entry of muddy water, etc., ensuring reliability in harsh environments.


Stator Coil


•
A segment joining method, in which segment conductors (rectangular copper wire) are inserted and welded, has been adopted for the stator winding method. This achieves compact size, high output and high efficiency. Also, mesh-like ventilation gaps have been provided between the coil ends, and the inner surface of the coil ends that receives the cooling air has been smoothed, creating a smooth, interference-free air flow that improves cooling and reduces wind noise.
