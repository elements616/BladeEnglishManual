Audio System Operation


Panel Switch Operation


Basic operations such as audio mode switching are performed by operating the panel switches around the HDD navigation system or audio unit.


Touch Switch Operation (w/ HDD Navigation System)


After the audio mode screen is called up by operating a panel switch, operations are performed by directly touching the switch frames (touch switches) displayed on the screen with a finger.


Steering Switch Operation


Functions such as audio mode switching are operated by pressing the pad switches on the steering wheel.


Voice Operation (w/ HDD Navigation System)


After pressing the talk switch to display the voice recognition mark on the screen, functions such as switching the audio source can be operated by speaking into the voice recognition microphone, providing excellent usability. For details of voice operation, refer to the HDD navigation system.



Audio System Functions


Automatic Sound Levelizer (ASL) Function


•
The volume is adjusted according to the vehicle speed signal so that music, etc. can be heard at the optimal volume in the cabin even when noise such as road noise and wind noise increases while driving.


LIVE-ACS (LIVE Acoustic / Psychoacoustic Technology) Function


•
An optimal acoustic space has been ensured by adopting "FOCUS*" technology, which makes the sound seem to come from in front of the occupants even when the speakers are located below ear level, and "TruBass*" technology, which emphasizes deep bass without distorting the midrange.

HINT
*
FOCUS and TruBass are registered trademarks of SRS Labs, Inc. in the United States and selected foreign countries. FOCUS and TruBass technologies are incorporated under license from SRS Labs, Inc.


Sound Library Function


Sound Library Outline


•
Music CDs can be recorded to the HDD (hard disk drive) built into the HDD navigation system. In addition, up to 999 playlists can be created in the sound library, and up to 99 tracks can be recorded in one playlist. Approximately 100 CDs can be recorded.

HINT
Because the system is SCMS (Serial Copy Management System) compliant, recording cannot be made from CD-Rs, etc. on which music CDs, etc. have been digitally recorded.

Recording Function


•
"Auto mode", which automatically starts recording to the HDD when a CD is played, and "Select mode", in which recording is done manually, can be set from the settings screen.
•
While playing at normal speed, recording to the HDD is performed at high speed in high-speed mode and at normal speed in normal-speed mode. During recording, "REC FAST" is displayed at the top of the screen in high-speed mode and "REC" in normal-speed mode. Recording is stopped by turning the audio power OFF or ejecting the CD during recording.


Database Function


•
By using Gracenote CDDB, names such as album title, artist name and genre name can be automatically assigned to recorded tracks. In addition, lists can be created, and desired tracks can be found and played by names such as album name, artist name and genre name. If there is no corresponding data in the CDDB, the recording date and time is registered as the title.

Non-standard Size AM/FM Electronic Tuner with Integrated In-dash CD Changer

●
An irregular-size in-dash CD changer integrated AM/FM electronic tuner has been adopted and combined with the 6 speaker system.
●
Tone control memory has been adopted. Tone control (bass and treble) values can be stored for each mode (CD, AM, FM, etc.).
●
By adopting a CD text display function, information pre-entered on the CD being played (artist name, track name, etc.) can be displayed.
●
CDs recorded in WMA (Windows Media Audio) and MP3 (MPEG Audio Layer 3) formats can be played* , improving marketability.
●
An automatic sound levelizer function has been adopted, providing excellent usability.
●
Noise has been reduced by digital signal processing of AM/FM reception.
HINT
*
Depending on the device used for recording, some CD-Rs and CD-RWs may not play.

Antenna

●
A foldable short pole antenna has been adopted at the rear center of the roof as standard equipment on all models.
●
The pole of the foldable short pole antenna can be folded down easily in one action, providing convenience when parking in places with limited height, such as multi-level parking garages.
●
The short pole antenna contains a radio antenna amplifier that amplifies the radio broadcast signals received by the antenna.
●
A 4-pattern diversity TV antenna, with 2 system(s) each located in the back window glass and in the rear spoiler, has been adopted and provided on models with HDD navigation system.
