Millimeter Wave Radar Sensor

●
The millimeter wave radar sensor assembly is mounted behind the radiator grille.
●
By turning the radar axis adjustment bolts and gear section, the radar axis can be adjusted within a range of 3° up, down, left and right.
●
Adjust the radar axis carefully using TaSCAN and a level.

Detection Method for Relative Distance and Relative Speed


•
When an object exists within the range of the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly, the distance, relative speed and direction of the object are detected from the reflected waves, and based on that data the pre-crash safety computer determines whether a collision is unavoidable.
•
The direction of the object is detected from the angle of the reflected waves, the distance from the time between emitting the radio waves and the return of the reflected waves, and the speed of the object from the frequency change of the reflected waves (Doppler effect).
•
In addition, on curves, the radius of the curve is estimated based on information from the yaw rate sensor assembly and steering sensor, and the path is corrected.
