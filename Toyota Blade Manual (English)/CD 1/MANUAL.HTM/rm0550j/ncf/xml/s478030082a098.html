Torque Converter

●
A lightweight, compact and high-capacity ultra-flat torque converter has been adopted. Simplifying the structure of the one-way clutch has greatly shortened the shaft length.
●
With the high heat-resistant lock-up friction material and the 3WAY cooling circuit, a flex start system has been adopted. Flex lock-up is performed from start-off, enabling smooth start-off and reducing slip loss; together with the adoption of a low-speed lock-up damper, low-speed lock-up in all gears from start-off is possible, achieving high fuel economy performance.

3WAY Cooling Circuit


•
In the 3WAY cooling circuit, a dedicated cooling circuit has been provided on the lock-up ON pressure circuit side (between the input shaft and stator shaft). Improved cooling performance expands the lock-up range, improving fuel economy.
