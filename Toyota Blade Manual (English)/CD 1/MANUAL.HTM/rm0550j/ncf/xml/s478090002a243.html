System Function

●
Ignition of the driver and front passenger airbags, driver knee airbag and pretensioners is determined from the signals from the front airbag sensors and the G sensor in the center airbag sensor (computer), and ignition of the SRS side airbags and SRS curtain shield airbags is determined from the signals from the side airbag sensors and rear side airbag sensors. In addition, it has a diagnosis function and performs self-diagnosis when a system malfunction occurs.
