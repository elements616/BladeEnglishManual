Thermostat

●
The thermostat is installed in the water inlet.
●
The water outlet is provided at the rear of the cylinder head and is a press-fit pipe type.
●
A wax type thermostat with a bypass valve and a large flange diameter (56mm) has been adopted to ensure sufficient coolant circulation.
