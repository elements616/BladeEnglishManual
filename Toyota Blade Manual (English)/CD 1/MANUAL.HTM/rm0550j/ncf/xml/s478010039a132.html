Piston and Crank Related Items

●
Lightweight, high-rigidity aluminum alloy pistons have been adopted.
●
High-strength steel has been adopted for both the connecting rods and connecting rod caps to reduce weight.
●
Nutless type connecting rod bolts have been adopted to make the big end more compact and stabilize the axial force. Also, plastic region tightening has been adopted for the bolts.
●
Balance shafts have been adopted to reduce booming noise in the vehicle.
●
An offset crank, in which the crankshaft center is offset from the cylinder center, has been adopted, reducing friction loss through lower piston side force and improving thermal efficiency through changes in piston speed, for lower fuel consumption.
●
A forged 5-journal, 8-balance weight crankshaft has been adopted. The crank pins and journals have smaller fillets and narrower widths, ensuring rigidity and reducing friction. Also, improved surface roughness and smaller pin diameters reduce weight and friction.
●
Eccentric groove bearings have been adopted for the crankshaft bearings, reducing oil leakage from the ends while ensuring oil flow at the center of the bearings, for reduced friction.
