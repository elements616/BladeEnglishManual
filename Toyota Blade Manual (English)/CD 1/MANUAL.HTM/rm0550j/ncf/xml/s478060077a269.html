VSC

●
Whereas ABS and TRC are mainly intended to ensure stability during braking and acceleration associated with brake and accelerator operation, VSC is a system that ensures the stability of the vehicle in the turning direction. Normally, the vehicle turns stably in accordance with steering operation, but depending on road surface conditions, vehicle speed, unexpected situations such as emergency turns, or external factors, it may tend toward strong oversteer or strong understeer. In such cases, VSC automatically controls engine output and the brakes of each wheel to ensure stability, mitigating strong understeer or strong oversteer.
Control Performance Outline


•
VSC detects the vehicle condition from the signals from various sensors such as the yaw rate sensor & linear G sensor, and controls brake fluid pressure and engine output torque.

Determination of Strong Oversteer Tendency


•
The oversteer tendency is determined by the skid control computer from the vehicle slip angle and slip angular velocity obtained from the yaw rate sensor & linear G sensor. That is, when the vehicle slip angle is large and the slip angular velocity is also large, the vehicle can be said to have an oversteer tendency.


Determination of Strong Understeer Tendency


•
The understeer tendency is determined by the skid control computer from the target yaw rate and the actual yaw rate of the vehicle. That is, when the driver steers and the actual vehicle yaw rate is smaller than the target yaw rate that should be generated (determined from the steering amount and vehicle speed), this means that the vehicle is not turning, so the understeer tendency can be said to be large.


VSC Operation Method




Mitigation of Strong Oversteer Tendency
•
When the skid control computer determines that the oversteer tendency is large, it applies the brake to the front wheel on the outside of the turn according to the degree of the tendency, generating an outward moment on the vehicle to suppress the oversteer tendency. In addition, the reduction in vehicle speed due to the braking force contributes to ensuring vehicle stability.


Mitigation of Strong Understeer Tendency
•
When the skid control computer determines that the understeer tendency is large, it controls engine output according to the degree of the tendency and applies the brakes to the front and rear wheels to reduce lateral force, suppressing the understeer tendency.
