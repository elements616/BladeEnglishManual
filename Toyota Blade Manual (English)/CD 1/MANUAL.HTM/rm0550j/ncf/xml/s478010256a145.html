Cooling Fan and Fan Shroud

●
Based on signals from the engine control computer, the cooling fan computer steplessly controls the speed of the 2 cooling fans according to the engine condition, driving conditions, A/C operation, etc. By always controlling the cooling fans to the optimum speed, cooling performance has been improved while greatly increasing quietness.
●
A thin ring fan with a band-shaped ring around its outer circumference has been adopted, increasing rigidity and making the fan lighter and more compact.
