Neural Network

●
In conventional automatic air conditioning control, the air conditioning computer calculates the required outlet temperature TAO (heat load) from the set temperature and temperature information from each sensor according to a fixed formula, and automatically controls each servo motor and the blower motor according to the calculated TAO (heat load), keeping the cabin temperature stable for occupant comfort. However, even with the same TAO (heat load), individual occupants' sensations vary greatly depending on the surrounding environmental conditions, and with conventional automatic air conditioning control, in which all control is determined by the TAO (heat load) calculated by a formula, it was difficult to match the occupants' sensations, which are complex and difficult to express in a formula, under all conditions. Therefore, a neural network (nerve cell network) has been adopted as a method of performing more advanced control matched to occupants' sensations. A neural network is an engineering imitation of information processing in a biological nervous system (Fig. A); it replaces the input-output relationship of complex nerve transmission, like that of human nerve cells (neurons), with neuron models and combines several of them, and consists of an input layer, an intermediate layer and an output layer.
