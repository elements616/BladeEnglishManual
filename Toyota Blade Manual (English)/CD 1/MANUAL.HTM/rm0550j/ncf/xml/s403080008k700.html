Heater & Air Conditioner System Outline

Main Features of Heater & Air Conditioner System


Item
Description
Air Conditioner System
All models adopt automatic air conditioning control incorporating a neural network*, enabling more comfortable air conditioning matched to the occupants' sensations.
G grade models adopt a left/right independent temperature control (dual mode) air conditioner unit, allowing the temperatures for the driver and front passenger to be adjusted separately.
A pollen removal mode has been adopted on all models for quick removal of pollen around the face.
Air Conditioner Unit / Air Duct
A wide-angle, compact high-flow defroster has been adopted.
A rear heater duct with excellent heating performance for the rear seat foot area is provided on cold area specification models.
Evaporator
An RS evaporator (RS: Revolutionary Super Slim), which is considerably smaller and lighter, has been adopted.
Heater Core
A compact, high-performance SFA-II heater core (SFA-II: Straight Flow Aluminum-II) has been adopted.
Clean Air Filter
A pollen removal type clean air filter is standard on all models.
Compressor
An externally controlled continuously variable displacement compressor, the 6SEU14 on 2AZ-FE engine models and the 6SBU16 on 2GR-FE engine models, is adopted, improving fuel economy by controlling the compressor displacement optimally according to the air conditioning load.
Condenser
A highly efficient and lightweight sub-cool condenser has been adopted.
Refrigerant
In consideration of ozone layer depletion, chlorine-free HFC-134a (R-134a) has been adopted as the air conditioner refrigerant.
Other Features
G grade models adopt a Plasmacluster ion generator.

HINT
*
Neural network: An engineering imitation of the information processing of the nervous systems of living organisms; like the human brain, it can perform complex control by finely interrelating complex input and output relationships.
