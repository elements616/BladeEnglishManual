Gear Train

●
The torque converter and oil pump are located on the input shaft, and a planetary gear is used as the forward/reverse switching mechanism.
●
It consists of a continuously variable transmission mechanism made up of 1 pair of pulleys and a metal belt, a reduction gear pair, a final gear pair and a differential mechanism. Shifting is performed steplessly by varying the winding diameter of the metal belt on the pulleys, whose groove width is varied by hydraulic pressure.
