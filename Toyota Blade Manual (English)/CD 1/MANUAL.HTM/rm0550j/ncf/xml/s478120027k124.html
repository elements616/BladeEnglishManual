Combination Switch

●
A light control switch and wiper control switch with excellent operating feel and reduced operating noise have been adopted.
●
The turn & dimmer unit and wiper unit are arranged independently, and the wiper unit can be removed and installed without removing the steering wheel. Serviceability has been considered by using a structure in which the wiper unit is fixed directly to the steering column bracket with hooks.
●
Each lever fulcrum has a pivot structure, and the turn cancel mechanism is built into the turn & dimmer unit. In addition, the connectors of the turn & dimmer unit and wiper unit are directly connected to simplify the wiring.
