Fuel General

●
A fuel returnless system has been adopted for excellent emission performance.
●
Quick connectors have been adopted at each connection of the fuel pipes to improve serviceability.
●
A fuel pump integrating parts such as the charcoal canister and fuel filter has been adopted and located inside the fuel tank, reducing the number of parts, reducing weight and saving space in the engine room.
●
A fixed-torque type fuel tank cap, which cannot be tightened further once tightened a certain amount, has been adopted. This reduces the operating force of the fuel tank cap, ensuring excellent operability.
●
By adopting an air filter at the mouth of the fuel tank inlet pipe, underfloor space has been secured.
