High rigidity is ensured by optimum placement of ribs and case shapes using FEM analysis, suppressing deformation due to loads from the gear train. An aluminum alloy case and cover have been adopted to reduce weight.

Differential Case


•
The window shape of the differential case is asymmetrical, which is advantageous during driving. This reduces tensile stress concentration during driving, so the allowable torque can be increased while keeping the lightweight 2-pinion type, achieving a significant weight reduction by reducing the number of parts.
