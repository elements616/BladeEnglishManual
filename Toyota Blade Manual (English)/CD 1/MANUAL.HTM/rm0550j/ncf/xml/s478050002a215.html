Front Axle

●
Double-row angular ball bearings with low rolling resistance have been adopted for the axle bearings.
●
The axle bearings are a compact type with integrated oil seals.
●
A 12-point nut has been adopted for the axle hub nut, and the fixing method has been changed from the common cotter pin type to a staked type, ensuring excellent fastening performance.
●
The steering knuckle arm has been made lighter while increasing rigidity.

■ NOTICE ■
Because magnets are used in the wheel speed sensor and rotor, do not bring magnetized objects near the sensor and rotor. In addition, be careful not to allow foreign matter to adhere.

Axle Hub Nut


•
A staked type fixing method is used for the axle hub nut. This ensures fastening performance, prevents abnormal noise from the axle, reduces the number of parts and improves assembly workability. In addition, using a 12-point nut equalizes the surface pressure on the nut seat and ensures excellent fastening performance.

HINT
The axle hub nut is a non-reusable part because it is staked and surface treated to stabilize the friction coefficient. In addition, when removing the nut, completely release the staked part before loosening the nut so as not to damage the threads of the drive shaft.
