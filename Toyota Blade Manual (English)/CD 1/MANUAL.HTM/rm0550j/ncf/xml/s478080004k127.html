Compressor

●
Externally controlled continuously variable capacity compressors are used: the 6SEU14 type on models with the 2AZ-FE engine and the 6SBU16 type on models with the 2GR-FE engine.
●
A lock sensor that picks up the rotation speed is built in on models with the 2GR-FE engine. This allows the crankshaft pulley speed and the compressor pulley speed to be compared, and when the specified slip ratio is exceeded, the magnetic clutch is controlled OFF.
●
A DL pulley is used on models with the 2AZ-FE engine. The DL pulley contains a damping mechanism that absorbs compressor torque fluctuations and a limiter mechanism that protects the belt in the unlikely event that the compressor locks. This eliminates the need for the conventional magnetic clutch mechanism.
Construction of Externally Controlled Continuously Variable Displacement Compressor


•
When the shaft rotates, the swash plate rotates together with it via the lug plate directly connected to the shaft. The rotational motion of the swash plate is converted via the shoes into the reciprocating motion of the pistons in the cylinders, performing intake, compression and discharge of refrigerant. In variable displacement control, the internal pressure of the swash plate chamber is controlled between low pressure and intermediate pressure, and this pressure change varies the angle of the swash plate to change the piston stroke, controlling the discharge displacement. By varying the control current to the control valve, the externally controlled continuously variable displacement compressor varies the controlled low pressure steplessly, widening the discharge displacement control range and improving comfort while saving power.


Flow Control Valve


•
The conventional variable displacement compressor detected the suction pressure for control, but the newly adopted flow control valve detects the differential pressure before and after the discharge pressure restriction for control. Whereas torque control was difficult with the conventional control valve, with the flow control valve the differential pressure is proportional to torque, enabling torque estimation control and optimal engine control matched to compressor torque.

Operating Principle (Operation When Displacement Needs to Be Increased)


•
When the heat load increases, or when acceleration control, etc. ends and displacement needs to be increased, the current (duty ratio) to the flow control valve coil is set to 100%. As a result, the electromagnetic force of the control valve becomes greater than the spring force + differential pressure (PDH-PDL), the valve moves away from the coil, and the Pd→Pc passage closes. High pressure is then cut off and the pressure in the swash plate chamber gradually decreases. Therefore, Pc+Fs+FL<P1+...+P5, the lower piston is moved to the left and the swash plate angle increases. As a result, the piston stroke increases. (The illustration shows large discharge displacement)


Operating Principle (Operation When Displacement Needs to Be Decreased)


•
When the heat load decreases, or when displacement needs to be reduced during acceleration or high-speed driving, etc., the current (duty ratio) to the flow control valve coil is reduced. As a result, the control valve moves toward the coil due to the lower spring force, and the Pd→Pc passage opens. High pressure (Pd) is then introduced into the swash plate chamber and the pressure in the swash plate chamber gradually increases. Therefore, the pressure applied to the left side of the piston (Pc) + spring force (Fs) + reaction force from the lug plate (FL) becomes higher than the force applied to the right side of the piston (P1+...+P5), the lower piston is moved to the right and the swash plate angle decreases. As a result, the piston stroke decreases. (The illustration shows small discharge displacement)
