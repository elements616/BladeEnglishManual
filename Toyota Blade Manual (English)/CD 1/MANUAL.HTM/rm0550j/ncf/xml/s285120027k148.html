Interior Lamps

●
Various interior lights have been adopted to create a high-quality interior space with consideration for convenience and comfort.
●
An illuminated entry system that illuminates according to the opening and closing of each door has been adopted for ease of use at night, and a large LED roof illumination and shift light have been provided on the G grade to enhance the interior space.
●
White LEDs have been adopted for the front personal lights and room light on the G grade.
●
The vanity lights have been located on the roof side to be easy on the eyes.
