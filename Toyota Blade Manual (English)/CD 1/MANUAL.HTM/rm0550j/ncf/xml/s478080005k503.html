Blower Control

●
Multi-step blower speed control, warm-up control, time-lag airflow control, etc. are used as blower control.
Manual Control


•
Sets the airflow according to the operating position of the blower switch.

Auto Control


•
When the AUTO switch on the air conditioning control section is turned ON, multi-step blower speed, warm-up, cool-down and time-lag airflow controls are performed using the target airflow calculated by the neural network based on input conditions from each sensor, etc.

Multi-step Blower Speed Control


•
When the AUTO switch on the air conditioning control section is turned ON, the air conditioning computer sends signals to the blower motor controller according to the target airflow calculated by the neural network, automatically controlling the airflow in multiple steps.

Warm-up Control


•
When the outlet mode is FOOT, BI-LEVEL or FOOT/DEF and the engine is cold, the blower motor is turned OFF and remains stopped until the engine coolant temperature (the temperature detected by the water temperature sensor) reaches a certain level or higher. When the coolant temperature reaches T2°C, the blower motor rotates at LO, and at T1°C or less the blower motor is turned OFF. When the coolant temperature is between T2°C and T3°C, the airflow calculated from the water temperature sensor is compared with the target airflow calculated by the neural network, and the airflow is automatically controlled at whichever is lower. When the coolant temperature reaches T3°C or higher, the blower motor rotates at HI, and is then controlled according to the target airflow calculated by the neural network.


Time-lag Airflow Control


•
2 types of time-lag airflow control are performed according to the temperature detected by the evaporator fin sensor, preventing hot air from blowing out of the center registers when the outlet mode is FACE or BI-LEVEL.

When the evaporator fin sensor temperature is high (30°C or higher)


•
As shown in the illustration, the blower motor is turned OFF and the compressor is turned ON for approximately t1 seconds to cool the air conditioning unit. After approximately t1 seconds, the blower motor rotates in manual LO mode so that the cooled air blows out of the outlets, preventing discomfort caused by hot air blowing out. From approximately t2 to t3 seconds, the time-lag calculated airflow is compared with the target airflow calculated by the neural network, and the airflow is automatically controlled at whichever is lower. After approximately t3 seconds have elapsed, the airflow is controlled according to the target airflow calculated by the neural network.


When the evaporator fin sensor temperature is low (below 30°C)


•
As shown in the illustration, the blower motor rotates in manual LO mode for approximately t4 seconds. After that, until approximately t5 seconds, the time-lag calculated airflow is compared with the target airflow calculated by the neural network, and the airflow is automatically controlled at whichever is lower. After approximately t5 seconds have elapsed, the airflow is controlled according to the target airflow calculated by the neural network.


Related Parts


Servo Motor

●
Servo motors for switching the fresh/recirculation damper, the mode damper and the driver side air mix damper have been adopted on all models. In addition, a servo motor for switching the front passenger side air mix damper has been adopted on models w/ dual mode automatic air conditioner. Each servo motor is a pulse pattern type that detects position by pulse signals. With the adoption of pulse pattern type servo motors, a smart connector with a built-in IC having functions such as communication with the air conditioner computer is used.
●
The servo motors for the mode damper and the driver side air mix damper are installed on the right side of the heater & cooler unit, the servo motor for the front passenger side air mix damper on the left side of the heater & cooler unit, and the servo motor for the fresh/recirculation damper on the right side of the blower unit.

Pulse Pattern Type Servo Motor


•
Whereas the conventional type detects position by potentiometer voltage, the pulse pattern type servo motor detects relative position by 2-bit ON/OFF signals. The air conditioner computer counts the number of pulses (patterns) to determine the stop position.


Smart Connector


•
Smart connectors, intended to reduce weight and wiring, have been adopted to drive the pulse pattern type servo motors for air conditioner control. The smart connector contains an IC that combines communication with the air conditioner computer, servo motor drive and position detection functions.
