Exterior Design Concept

●
With "Fascinating Cool - premium expression through fascinating advanced feel" as the common design keyword for the exterior and interior, a new sense of luxury suited to the Japanese market has been created.
●
Under the concept of "dynamic deepening", a form with a sense of motion and depth has been pursued, and a sporty body silhouette creates an advanced feel.
Related Parts


Front & Side Area

●
The front area uses a "character line" that flows vertically from the hood panel and spreads horizontally at the bottom of the front bumper, emphasizing the three-dimensional center and expressing a sporty and wide feel.
●
Functional parts such as the deep bumper opening, radiator grille and headlights are arranged as if carved in, giving a powerful design befitting the power performance.
●
In the side area, a dynamic side silhouette has been achieved through front and rear fender shapes that assert driving performance, a forward-sloping belt line, and swelling of the hood panel and back door.
●
The front fenders are integrally shaped with the body, and door outside mouldings with a geometric pattern accentuate the sporty feel.
●
Side mudguards are provided on grades other than the standard grade, and the lower portion bulges outward, giving a stable, low center of gravity body.
