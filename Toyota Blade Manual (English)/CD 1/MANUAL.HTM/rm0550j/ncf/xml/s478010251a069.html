Engine Control System General

●
TCCS (TOYOTA Computer Controlled System), which comprehensively controls fuel injection control (EFI), ignition timing control (ESA), throttle control (ETCS-i), VVT-i control, etc. with high precision, has been adopted to achieve high performance, high output, low fuel consumption and improved emission performance.
●
CAN communication has been adopted as the method of communication with other computers.
●
Diagnosis (TaSCAN) and fail-safe functions are provided with consideration for serviceability and safety.

Control List


Control Name
Function
Fuel Injection Control (EFI)
Appropriate fuel injection is performed by correcting the basic injection duration, calculated according to engine conditions, with signals from each sensor.
Ignition Timing Control (ESA)
Appropriate ignition is performed by correcting the basic ignition timing, calculated according to engine conditions, with signals from each sensor.
Knock Judgment Control (KCS)
The presence or absence of knocking is judged based on the signal from the knock sensor.
ECT Shift Torque Control
Reduces shift shock by retarding the ignition timing, etc. during A/T shifting.
ETCS-i Control
The throttle is controlled to the appropriate opening angle by correcting the throttle opening angle, calculated according to engine conditions, with signals from each sensor.
Maximum Speed Control
When the vehicle speed reaches 180km/h, the throttle valve is closed to suppress any increase in vehicle speed.
Idle Speed Control (ISC)
Controls the fast idle speed according to the engine coolant temperature and the idle speed after engine warm-up. Controls the idle speed by means of the fuel injection volume and throttle opening angle.
Dual VVT-i Control
Controls the intake valve timing and exhaust valve timing to the optimum according to the engine condition.
Electric ACIS Control
Switches the effective intake pipe length of the intake manifold in 2 stages according to the engine condition.
2-way Intake System Control
Switches the intake passage in 2 stages according to the driving conditions to achieve both reduced intake noise and high output.
Fuel Pump Control
Turns the fuel pump ON and OFF based on the starter signal, engine speed signal, etc. Stops the fuel pump operation based on the signal from the airbag sensor.
Electric Cooling Fan Control
Controls cooling fan ON/OFF and fan speed according to coolant temperature and air conditioner status.
Alternator Charging Control
Controls the alternator generated voltage according to driving conditions, reducing the generation load to lower fuel consumption.
O2Sensor Heater Control
According to coolant temperature and driving conditions, the O2Turns the sensor heater ON and OFF.
A/F Sensor Heater Control
Turns the A/F sensor heater ON and OFF according to the coolant temperature and driving conditions.
Canister Purge Control
Controls the canister purge flow volume according to the engine condition.
A/C Control
Turns the A/C magnetic clutch ON and OFF after idle-up control to suppress idle speed fluctuations during idling. Temporarily turns the magnetic clutch OFF during acceleration, etc.
Immobiliser Control
If the engine control computer does not receive the engine start permission signal from the immobiliser amplifier, fuel injection and ignition control are restricted.
CAN Communication
A communication IC that can exchange a large amount of information through communication lines has been adopted to communicate with other control computers.
Diagnosis
Using the diagnostic tool TaSCAN, accurate and detailed diagnosis, such as reading SAE-standard DTCs and data and performing Active Tests, is possible.
Fail-safe
When a malfunction occurs in a signal from any sensor, control is continued using standard values in the engine control computer, or the engine is stopped.
