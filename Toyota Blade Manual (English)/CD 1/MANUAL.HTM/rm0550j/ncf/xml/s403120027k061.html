Illuminated Entry System

●
Illuminated entry control, which keeps the engine switch illumination and the lights on for approximately 15 seconds even after the doors are closed when the front personal light switch and room light switch are in the "DOOR" position, has been adopted as standard on all models.
●
If the driver door and front passenger door are locked during illuminated entry control, the lights that are on go off (fade out).
●
Door lock light control, which turns on the engine switch illumination, front personal light and room light for approximately 15 seconds when the driver door or front passenger door is unlocked by key-linked operation or the transmitter, when the driver door or front passenger door is unlocked by the smart door lock system, or when the electronic key is recognized within the electronic key detection area of the driver door or front passenger door, has been adopted as standard on all models.
●
A battery saver function has been adopted that automatically turns off the lights if they remain on for approximately 20 minutes or more with any door ajar or open.
●
The circuits that control the illuminated entry system and interior illumination are built into the main body ECU (instrument panel junction block assembly).
●
For the illuminated entry and interior illumination, a customize function has been adopted that allows the lighting time of each light by illuminated entry to be changed (approximately 7.5 seconds, approximately 15 seconds, approximately 30 seconds) and interior illumination lighting to be prohibited, by connecting the diagnostic tool (TaSCAN) to DLC3 and operating it. For details of the customize function, refer to Control System.

●
The illuminated entry system is controlled by the main body ECU (instrument panel junction block assembly), to which each light is connected, based on input of the status of each switch, etc.
