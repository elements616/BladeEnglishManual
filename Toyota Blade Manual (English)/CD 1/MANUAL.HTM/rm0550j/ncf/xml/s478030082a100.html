Gear Train

●
The U660E automatic transaxle uses 2 planetary gear units on the input shaft, a lightweight, compact Ravigneaux type planetary gear and a UD (underdrive) planetary gear, to provide 6 forward speeds and 1 reverse speed.
●
The adoption of the Ravigneaux type planetary gear has greatly simplified and lightened the gear train mechanism located on the intermediate shaft, reducing the weight of the entire unit and the inertial weight of the gear train mechanism. In addition, clutch-to-clutch control has been adopted as the hydraulic control mechanism, reducing the number of shift elements such as one-way clutches and brakes and achieving class-leading light weight and compactness.

Compact Compound Clutch Piston


•
A 2-piece pull type piston has been adopted for the clutch piston that operates the No.2 clutch (C2), making the compound clutch more compact.
•
To reduce drag loss of the friction material caused by looseness of the 2-piece piston, pull piston No.1 is a piston with a spring mechanism. This achieves both compactness and high efficiency.


Angular Ball Bearings Supporting the Counter Gear


•
Double-row angular ball bearings have been adopted to support the counter drive gear and the Ravigneaux type planetary gear unit, achieving higher efficiency through reduced friction and improved quietness.


Low-loss Friction Material


•
A new groove shape has been adopted for the clutches and brakes, providing drag suited to the operating environment, greatly reducing drag loss and achieving high efficiency.
