Air Conditioner Control System

●
Full automatic air conditioner control is performed by the air conditioner computer installed in the instrument panel (below the air conditioning unit).
●
The air conditioner computer functions as one of the computers making up the CAN* communication system; in addition to receiving sensor and switch signals via dedicated lines, it receives signals required for air conditioner control from the engine control computer and meter computer via communication lines, performing precise air conditioner control according to vehicle conditions. Similarly, the air conditioner computer also outputs air conditioning related signals to other computers via communication lines.
●
As a method of advanced air conditioning control matched to the occupants' sensations, automatic air conditioner control using a neural network has been adopted, enabling comfortable air conditioning.
●
By adopting a customize function using the diagnostic tool (TaSCAN), DTC output, various data output, etc. can be performed.
●
Panel diagnosis by operation and display on the air conditioner control section has been adopted.
HINT
*
CAN is an abbreviation for Controller Area Network, a serial communication conforming to ISO (International Organization for Standardization) standards.

Air Conditioner System Parts List


Item
Description
Blower Motor
(Blower Assembly)
Driven according to the target air flow volume calculated by the neural network, blowing air into the cabin.
Servo Motor
Opens and closes the fresh/recirculation air damper, air mix damper and mode damper.
Plasmacluster Ion Generator*1
(Ion Generator)
Receives the Plasmacluster switch ON/OFF signal and releases Plasmacluster ions and negative ions from the air conditioner outlets.
Flow Control Valve
Controls the internal pressure of the compressor to provide the optimal discharge displacement.
Magnetic Clutch Relay*2
Controls the magnetic clutch to turn the compressor ON/OFF.
Rear Window Defogger Relay
(DEF Relay)
Receives the rear window defogger switch ON/OFF signal and heats the windshield deicer.
Engine Control Computer
Sends and receives data to and from the air conditioner computer via CAN communication.
Meter Computer
(Combination Meter Assembly)
Sends and receives data to and from the air conditioner computer via CAN communication.
Air Conditioner Computer
(Air Conditioning Amplifier Assembly)
Performs automatic air conditioning control based on signals from various switches, sensors, etc.
Air Conditioner Control Panel
(Air Conditioning Control Panel Assembly)
Sends switch signals to the air conditioner computer and receives indicator display signals.
Evaporator Fin Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor NO.1)
Detects the evaporator surface temperature.
Ambient Temperature Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor [Ambient Temperature Sensor])
Detects the outside air temperature and outputs it to the air conditioner computer via the meter computer.
Pressure Sensor
(Air Conditioning
Tube & Accessory Assembly)
Detects the refrigerant pressure.
Room Temperature Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor [Room Temperature Sensor])
Detects the cabin temperature.
Solar Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor [Solar Sensor])
Detects the amount of sunlight.
Lock Sensor*2
Detects the compressor pulley speed.
