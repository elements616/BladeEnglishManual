Cooling General

●
The cooling system is a pressurized, forced-circulation, water-cooled type, and is a bottom bypass type with a bypass valve type thermostat located in the water inlet.
●
A concentrated longitudinal flow system has been adopted for the cylinder head, improving cooling around the combustion chambers and spark plugs. Also, cooling of the cylinder block is a U-turn type.
●
A CVT fluid (CVTF) warmer has been provided to increase the warm-up speed of the CVT fluid when cold and to reduce CVT friction. Also, when the CVT fluid temperature is high, it functions as an oil cooler.
●
By adopting a fully sealed reservoir tank, high gas-liquid separation performance and improved cooling system pressure stability have been achieved, and by optimizing its installation position, the air bleed valve used when filling coolant has been eliminated to improve serviceability.
