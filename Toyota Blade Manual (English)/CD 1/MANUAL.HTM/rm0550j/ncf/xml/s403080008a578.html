Air Conditioner Control Panel

●
A push type air conditioner control panel is provided as standard in the integration panel.
●
A pollen removal switch has been provided in the air conditioner control panel. Pressing the switch activates pollen removal mode, quickly removing pollen around the face.
●
On G grade models with the independent right/left temperature control system (dual mode), separate temperature setting switches for the driver side and front passenger side are provided on the air conditioner control section, together with a DUAL switch for switching from linked control to independent right/left temperature control.
