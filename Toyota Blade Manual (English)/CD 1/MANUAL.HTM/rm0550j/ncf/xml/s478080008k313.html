Ventilation

●
Fresh air is drawn in from the inlet on the upper surface of the cowl, passes through the air conditioning system and enters the cabin from each outlet on the instrument panel, etc.
●
Cabin air exits through the air vent slits in the deck trim side upper board (LH), the gaps between the deck trim board and the body, etc., and passes through the inside of the body to the quarter vent ducts.
●
Quarter vent butterflies are provided in the quarter vent ducts so that cabin air can exit easily while outside air cannot easily enter, preventing dust from entering the cabin.

Related Parts


Air Conditioner Unit / Air Duct

●
A semi-center mounted integrated air conditioning unit with a thin RS evaporator (RS: Revolutionary Super Slim) and heater core arranged at the front and rear of the unit is used to reduce size. In addition, ventilation resistance inside the unit is reduced and foot space on the front passenger side is ensured.
●
A "partial recirculation system" that recirculates part of the cabin air in fresh air mode is used to improve heating performance.
●
A sub-cool condenser and a thin RS evaporator are used to reduce the amount of refrigerant used, giving consideration to the environment.
●
A wide-angle high-flow defroster, in which the inner wall of the front defroster is shaped radially, is used for excellent defroster performance.
●
Rear heater ducts are used on cold area specification models to improve comfort for rear seat occupants.
●
A pollen filter with improved pollen removal efficiency is used as the clean air filter and is provided as standard on all models.
