Dual VVT-i Controller

●
A highly efficient vane type VVT-i controller with low friction during operation has been adopted.
●
The intake side is a 3-vane type and the exhaust side is a 4-vane type. When the engine is stopped, the intake side vanes are locked by a lock pin in the most retarded position, and the exhaust side vanes in the most advanced position.
●
An advance assist spring is provided in the exhaust side VVT-i to assist torque in the advance direction so that the lock pin engages securely when the engine is stopped.
●
Based on the signals from each sensor, the engine control computer controls the hydraulic pressure applied to the advance and retard chambers inside the VVT-i controller through the OCVs (oil control valves) mounted on the cylinder head covers, continuously varying the phase of the intake and exhaust camshafts.
