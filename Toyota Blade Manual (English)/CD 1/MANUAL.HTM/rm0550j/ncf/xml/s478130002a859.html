Sound Absorbing and Insulating Structure (Cabin Area)

●
Front fender side panel protectors are provided to seal the gap between the front fender panels and the body. This limits the entry of road noise from the tires and reduces wind noise caused by air flowing in.

●
Sound absorbing material is provided on the fender liners to absorb road noise and ensure quietness.

●
The door opening trim of the front and rear doors adopts a full-circumference 2-layer seal structure (A-A cross section), ensuring sound insulation performance. In addition, an upper seal is provided at the joint between the rear door window division bar and the rear window upper frame.
