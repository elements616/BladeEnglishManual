Accessory Socket (DC12V)

●
An accessory socket that can supply power to DC12V electrical products is now standard on all models. It is installed in the rear console upper section.

Accessory Socket Specifications


Rated Voltage [V]
12
Maximum Electrical Capacity [W]
120 or less
Maximum Current [A]
10 or less


Precautions for Use


Use it with the engine running.
Use electrical products with a rated voltage of 12V, a maximum electrical capacity of 120W or less and a maximum current of 10A or less.
Keep the lid closed when not in use.
