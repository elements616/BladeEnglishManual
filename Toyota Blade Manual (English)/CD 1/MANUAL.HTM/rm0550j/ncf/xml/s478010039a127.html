Cylinder Head

●
Vertical intake ports have been adopted and the valve included angle has been reduced to 27.5°, achieving both compactness and optimized intake efficiency. Also, low to medium speed torque has been ensured by using long intake ports.
●
A slant squish combustion chamber has been adopted. By slanting the squish area, masking around the valves is improved, increasing the intake air volume and the combustion speed in the latter half of combustion. This improves knock resistance and combustion efficiency.
●
By installing the fuel injectors in the cylinder head ports, the distance from the injectors to the intake valves has been shortened, reducing fuel adhesion to the intake ports and reducing HC emissions.
●
Cooling performance has been ensured by adopting a concentrated longitudinal flow system for the water jacket of the cylinder head and optimizing the jacket shape around the combustion chambers.
●
The structure has been simplified by building the water outlet into the rear end of the cylinder head and the water bypass pipe under the exhaust ports of the cylinder head.
