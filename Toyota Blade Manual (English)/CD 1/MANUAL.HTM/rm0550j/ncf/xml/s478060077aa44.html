ABS with EBD & Brake Assist & TRC & VSC & Hill-start Assist Control Outline

●
ABS with EBD (Electronic Brake force Distribution), TRC, VSC, hill-start assist control and electronically controlled brake assist using the brake actuator are standard on all models.
●
VSC adopts S-VSC (Steering-assisted Vehicle Stability Control), which performs cooperative control with the electric power steering, ensuring driving stability and handling.
●
A skid control computer integrated with the brake actuator has been adopted and is mounted on the left front side member. The skid control computer processes the signals from each sensor and performs brake control for ABS, TRC, VSC, hill-start assist control and electronically controlled brake assist. In addition, the skid control computer sends and receives necessary information to and from the combination meter, engine control computer, etc. via CAN communication.

Main Components and Functions


Components
Function
Semiconductor Type Wheel Speed Sensor & Rotor
Detects the wheel speed of each of the 4 wheels and outputs it to the skid control computer.
Yaw Rate Sensor & Linear G Sensor
Detects the vehicle yaw rate (rotational motion) and the longitudinal and lateral acceleration, and outputs them to the skid control computer via CAN.
Parking Brake Switch
Detects parking brake operation and outputs it to the main body ECU (instrument panel junction block assembly).
Stop Light Switch
Detects the brake operating condition and outputs it to the skid control computer.
Steering Sensor
Detects the steering angle and steering direction and outputs them to the skid control computer via CAN communication.
Combination Meter
A slip indicator light, brake warning light and ABS warning light are provided in the combination meter. In addition, each warning is indicated by illumination of the master warning indicator light and a display on the multi-information display.
Brake Actuator
Controls the hydraulic pressure of the wheel cylinder of each of the 4 wheels according to output signals from the skid control computer.
Master Cylinder Pressure Sensor (Built into Brake Actuator)
Detects the master cylinder pressure and outputs it to the skid control computer.
ABS Motor Relay (Built into Skid Control Computer)
Supplies power to the pump motor of the brake actuator.
ABS Motor Cut-off Relay (Built into Skid Control Computer)
Cuts off power to the pump motor of the brake actuator when the motor relay malfunctions.
ABS Solenoid Relay (Built into Skid Control Computer)
Supplies power to the solenoid valves of the brake actuator.
VSC Buzzer
Informs the driver that VSC is operating by sounding the buzzer intermittently.
Skid Control Computer (Built into Brake Actuator)
Processes the signals from each sensor and performs brake control for ABS, TRC, VSC, hill-start assist control and brake assist. It also communicates with the engine control computer and outputs control signals.
Engine Control Computer
Controls engine output during TRC and VSC operation by communicating with the skid control computer.
Main Body ECU (Instrument Panel Junction Block Assembly)
Detects parking brake operation and outputs it to the skid control computer via CAN communication.
Electric Power Steering Computer
Communicates with the skid control computer and performs S-VSC (steering-assisted Vehicle Stability Control) operation.
DLC3 (Located on the lower right side of the instrument panel, driver side)
By connecting and operating TaSCAN, diagnostic trouble codes and computer data can be read.
