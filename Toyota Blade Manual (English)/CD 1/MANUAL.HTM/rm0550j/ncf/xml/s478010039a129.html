Cylinder Block

●
The water jacket is an open deck type, and the jacket has been made shallow to improve warm-up performance.
●
The cylinder head bolt counterbores have been made shallow to improve the roundness of the cylinder block, reducing friction and improving fuel economy, and the head gasket structure has been optimized to take bore deformation during assembly into account. Furthermore, bore deformation is also addressed by increasing the rigidity of the bore outer wall ribs.
●
By increasing the thickness of the transmission mounting flange and the crankcase mounting flange, the rigidity of the cylinder block has been improved and vibration and noise have been reduced.
●
An aluminum alloy cylinder block with cast-in thin cast iron liners has been adopted.
●
Oil from the cylinder head is returned to the oil pan from outside the journals so that the oil does not directly hit the crankshaft, reducing friction.
●
High rigidity has been achieved by curving the skirt and optimizing the positions of the side ribs.
●
Compactness has been achieved by mounting accessory parts directly to the cylinder block.
