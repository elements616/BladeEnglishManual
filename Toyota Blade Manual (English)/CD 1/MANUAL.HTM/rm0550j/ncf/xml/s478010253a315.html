Cool Air Intake

●
A porous duct* has been adopted for the lightweight resin cool air intake, achieving a high level of quietness without using a low-frequency resonator.
●
The core wire of the porous duct is made of resin, with consideration for disposal.
●
The air intake inlet has a funnel shape to reduce intake resistance.
HINT
*
Perforated duct. Intake noise is reduced because the holes provide a high sound-deadening effect.
