Radar Cruise Control Cancel Function

●
When any of the following operations is performed, controlled driving by the radar cruise control system is cancelled and, except when the main switch is pressed, the radar cruise control system is put in standby.
List of Cancel Operations


Cancel Operation
Pull the cruise control main switch toward you. (CANCEL switch ON)
Press the main switch of the cruise control main switch. (Main switch OFF)
Depress the brake pedal. (Stop light switch assembly ON)
Shift the shift position from "D" to "N".
With the shift position in "D", shift to 3, 2 or 1 by operating the transmission shift switch.
With the shift position in "S", shift to 3, 2 or 1 by operating the shift lever assembly or transmission shift switch.
