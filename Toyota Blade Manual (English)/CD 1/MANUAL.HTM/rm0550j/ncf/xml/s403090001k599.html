Seat Belt Outline

●
3-point ELR seat belts have been adopted for all seats.
●
For the front seats, seat belts with pretensioner and force limiter have been provided for both the driver and front passenger seats.
●
Adjustable shoulder belt anchors with 4-step vertical adjustment have been adopted for the driver and front passenger seat belts to accommodate occupants of various builds. In addition, the slip joint is built into the center pillar upper garnish for improved appearance.
●
A child seat fixing mechanism has been adopted for the rear 3-point ELR seat belts. The child seat fixing mechanism is activated by pulling the belt out fully; in this state the belt can be retracted but cannot be pulled out. When the belt is retracted beyond a certain amount, the child seat fixing mechanism is released and the belt returns to normal ELR state.
●
The inner buckles of the rear outboard seats are a self-standing type that protrudes from the seat surface, while the contact feel when moving across the seat has been reduced, achieving both ease of seat belt fastening and comfort.
