Automatic Air Conditioner System Wiring Diagram




Air Conditioner System Parts List


Item
Description
Blower Motor
(Blower Assembly)
Driven according to the target air flow volume calculated by the neural network, blowing air into the cabin.
Servo Motor
Opens and closes the fresh/recirculation air damper, air mix damper and mode damper.
Flow Control Valve
Controls the internal pressure of the compressor to provide the optimal discharge displacement.
Magnetic Clutch Relay*
Controls the magnetic clutch to turn the compressor ON/OFF.
Rear Window Defogger Relay
(DEF Relay)
Receives the rear window defogger switch ON/OFF signal and heats the windshield deicer.
Engine Control Computer
Sends and receives data to and from the air conditioner computer via CAN communication.
Meter Computer
(Combination Meter Assembly)
Sends and receives data to and from the air conditioner computer via CAN communication.
Air Conditioner Computer
(Air Conditioning Amplifier Assembly)
Performs automatic air conditioning control based on signals from various switches, sensors, etc.
Air Conditioner Control Panel
(Air Conditioning Control Panel Assembly)
Sends switch signals to the air conditioner computer and receives indicator display signals.
Evaporator Fin Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor NO.1)
Detects the evaporator surface temperature.
Ambient Temperature Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor [Ambient Temperature Sensor])
Detects the outside air temperature and outputs it to the air conditioner computer via the meter computer.
Pressure Sensor
(Air Conditioning
Tube & Accessory Assembly)
Detects the refrigerant pressure.
Room Temperature Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor [Room Temperature Sensor])
Detects the cabin temperature.
Solar Sensor
(Cooler Thermistor [Solar Sensor])
Detects the amount of sunlight.
Lock Sensor*
Detects the compressor pulley speed.

HINT
*
2GR-FE engine models only
