Shift Control

●
On vehicles with the U660E transaxle, a 6-Speed Sports Direct Shift (6-speed Sport Direct Shift) with paddle shift has been adopted.
●
In addition to paddle shifts that allow the shift range to be changed while holding the steering wheel, M mode control has been adopted to provide driving pleasure during sporty driving.
●
With the adoption of M mode control, the position for switching to Shiftmatic has been changed from "S" to "M".
