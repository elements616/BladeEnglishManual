G-BOOK ALPHA System

●
By using the factory-option voice recognition microphone/telephone switch and the customer's Bluetooth-compatible cellular phone, the next-generation telematics service "G-BOOK ALPHA", which combines the G-BOOK network with the latest technology, can be used.
●
HELPNET, an emergency call system, is provided as standard with G-BOOK ALPHA.
●
By combining the adoption of a digital rights management system (G-DRM: G-Digital Rights Management) that stores and manages encrypted music data on the hard disk with the G-BOOK ALPHA communication system, new music services can be enjoyed in the vehicle.
●
G-route search has been adopted, which predicts road conditions using traffic information such as VICS and provides guidance on the optimum route avoiding congestion.

HELPNET System


•
HELPNET, an emergency call system, is provided as standard.
•
The HELPNET system is a service in which, when trouble such as an accident or sudden illness occurs while driving, the customer's location information is sent together with voice to the HELPNET operation center over the public network from a Bluetooth-compatible cellular phone, and the HELPNET operation center connects the line to the appropriate emergency agency (police or fire department) as necessary, shortening the time until lifesaving measures are taken.

In the following cases, the emergency notification service may not be available or its quality may be reduced.
•
When the G-BOOK service contract is not concluded or has expired, or when the service start operation has not been performed after enrollment.
•
When communication is not possible due to a malfunction of HELPNET compatible equipment or vehicle-related equipment.
•
When a center position determination error occurs.
•
When communication is not possible due to a malfunction of the center system.
•
When communication is not possible due to the communication network used by the emergency notification system.
•
When service is interrupted or suspended for reasons of the emergency notification provider.


Precautions
•
Depending on radio wave conditions, emergency calls and conversations may not be possible.
•
Outside the cellular phone service area, emergency notification is not possible even if the emergency notification operation starts. If emergency notification is not possible, make the report from the nearest public telephone, etc.
•
In a vehicle collision, if equipment necessary for communication malfunctions or is damaged, emergency notification and calls may not be possible.
•
Emergency notification may not be possible if the battery voltage drops or the connection to the battery is cut.
•
While waiting for rescue after emergency notification, move to a safe place where conversation with the center is possible.
•
Perform emergency notification by button operation after stopping the vehicle in a safe place.
•
All communication charges are borne by the customer. (When using a cellular phone)
•
Do not use functions that restrict outgoing or incoming calls (dial lock, etc.) as they will interfere with emergency notification. The functions that interfere with emergency notification differ depending on the telephone or communication carrier used. (When using a cellular phone)

G-BOOK and Navigation Linked Functions




Function Outline
•
By linking the navigation with the G-BOOK online service, road conditions such as congestion are provided in real time. Main functions include G-route search and congestion prediction.


G Route Search
•
Future road conditions are comprehensively predicted from the latest road traffic information from VICS and past statistical data, and the optimum route avoiding congestion is searched and guided.


Congestion Prediction
•
Displays predicted congestion time, predicted congestion increase/decrease, etc. based on congestion prediction information from the G-BOOK Center.
Congestion Prediction Service


Service Name
Service Contents
Area Check
Predicts the latest road traffic information for expressways around the current location and road conditions up to 90 minutes ahead, displays future congestion on an easy-to-understand simplified map on the navigation screen and provides voice guidance.
Route Check
The latest road traffic information for expressways on the route to the destination and road conditions predicted up to 90 minutes ahead are displayed on an easy-to-understand simplified map on the navigation screen, and voice guidance is provided.
Time Required Check
Simulates, based on congestion prediction, the time required to the next expressway exit when taking a break (30 or 60 minutes) at an expressway service area, and clearly displays the difference in driving time for each break time.



Amusement




Function Outline
•
At vehicle shipment, 10000 or more songs of music data of various genres are encrypted and preinstalled in the HDD navigation system. By selecting the desired songs or course and purchasing online from the G-BOOK center a license key that removes the encryption, music can be easily enjoyed in the vehicle.
•
With the adoption of a hard disk for the navigation system, a digital rights management system (G-DRM: Digital Rights Management) that stores and manages encrypted music data on the hard disk has been incorporated, ensuring thorough copyright management and protection.
•
G-SOUND and AUTOLIVE have been adopted as amusement that can be enjoyed in the vehicle.
•
The amusement menu screen is displayed by pressing the "Information/G" panel switch and touching "Amusement" on the displayed information mode screen.

G Route Search

Road Traffic Information
•
Congestion information, regulation information, etc. for expressways and ordinary roads nationwide are provided by voice and on the display screen.


CD Title Information Acquisition
•
When recording a CD, if song titles, etc. are not displayed because the data is not in the navigation hard disk, CD-related information can be obtained through G-BOOK ALPHA and the song title, artist name, etc. can be displayed.


Drive Plan
•
Drive routes can be set through the website from a personal computer or cellular phone.
•
By setting the destination and departure time, the route, driving distance and required time are displayed. (Only the required time is displayed on cellular phones)
•
Up to 15 routes can be registered, and for each route up to 4 waypoints can be registered in addition to the final destination.
•
There is also a service that notifies road traffic information on a preset route by e-mail at a specified date and time.


G-memory
•
Facilities, etc. saved on the website can be downloaded to the navigation and set as a destination.
