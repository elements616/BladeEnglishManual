Body Structure Outline

●
The collision safety body "GOA"* 1has been adopted, and to meet not only Japanese safety standards* 2and European safety standards* 3but also stricter targets set independently by Toyota, class-leading* 4world top-level occupant protection performance has been pursued. Furthermore, safety has been pursued from various aspects, such as by adopting a "head impact reduction structure" and a "brake pedal retraction reduction structure".
●
As a further evolution of the collision safety body "GOA", an "omnidirectional compatibility body structure" has been adopted, which pursues compatibility in collisions between vehicles of different weights and heights. By conducting frontal and side collision tests using 2t class passenger vehicles, collision safety performance that simulates accidents in the market has been pursued in terms of securing cabin space.
HINT
*1
GOA stands for Global Outstanding Assessment, meaning a safety assessment pursuing world top level in its class. Cleared the full-lap frontal collision (55km/h), side collision (55km/h) and 40% lap offset frontal collision (64km/h) tests.
*2
Full-lap Frontal Collision (50km/h) and Side Collision (50km/h)
*3
40% Lap Offset Frontal Collision (56km/h)
*4
Compared within the same displacement class.
●
Based on a monocoque structure, the arrangement and joint structure of each member have been optimized, and by straightening the main frame members and actively adopting high-tensile steel sheets, a lightweight, highly rigid body with little vibration and noise has been achieved.
●
High rust prevention performance has been ensured by extensively adopting anti-corrosion steel sheets and applying a lightweight undercoating with excellent chipping resistance to areas frequently hit by water and gravel, such as the inside of the wheel houses and both sides of the front floor.
●
Sound insulation technology has been fully applied to each part of the body, resulting in a body with excellent sound insulation.
●
A "pedestrian injury reduction body", which takes into consideration reducing injury to a pedestrian's head, etc. in the event of a collision with a pedestrian, has been adopted, and various impact absorbing structures have been provided at the front of the body.
