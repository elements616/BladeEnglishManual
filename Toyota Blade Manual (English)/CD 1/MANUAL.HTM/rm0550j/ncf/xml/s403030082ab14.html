Sequential Shiftmatic (w/ Paddle Shift) Outline

●
The 6-speed sequential Shiftmatic with paddle shift is a mechanism in which selecting the S position with the shift lever changes from automatic shift mode to S mode, allowing driving in the shift range selected by shift operation of the shift lever or the paddle switches mounted on the steering wheel, and obtaining engine braking force according to every shift range.
●
Operating the paddle shift during automatic shift mode in the D position allows a shift range to be selected temporarily.
●
Operating the +/- of the gate type shift lever creates a manual-like shift feel.

Temporary S Mode


•
Operating the paddle switch +/- during automatic shift mode in the D position allows a shift range to be selected temporarily.
•
During control, the "D" display in the combination meter illuminates and the currently selected gear is displayed on the shift position indicator.
•
When the paddle switch - (shift range down switch) is operated during automatic shift mode in the D position, the shift range changes to one whose upper limit is the current gear or one gear lower.
Temporary S Mode Cancel Conditions


•
Control is cancelled under the following conditions.
When the accelerator remains ON continuously for a certain period of time or longer
Returns to automatic shift mode in the D position.
When the paddle switch is held down continuously for a certain period of time or longer
When the vehicle stops
When the shift lever is moved to the S position
Changes to S mode in the S position.



Switching to S Mode


•
Moving the shift lever to the S position enables shift range switching with the sequential Shiftmatic. In addition, at the same time as shifting to the S position, the 5 range or 4 range is entered according to vehicle speed. (During AI-SHIFT control, the highest range being controlled is displayed.)
•
By operating either the shift lever +/- or the paddle switch +/-, a shift signal is sent to the transmission control computer, and the range is switched between the 6 to 1 ranges.


Sequential Shiftmatic Indicator Display


•
A shift position indicator has been provided in the combination meter.
•
The shift position indicator displays each position when the P, R, N or D position is selected.
•
When the S position is selected, the shift position indicator illuminates the "S" indicator and displays the currently selected shift range.
•
During temporary S mode control, the shift position indicator illuminates the "D" indicator and displays the currently selected shift range.

Related Parts


Steering Wheel

●
A leather-wrapped 3-spoke steering wheel with audio switches has been adopted on 2GR-FE engine models.
●
A steering wheel with SRS airbag is adopted on all models out of consideration for safety.
●
For detailed switch specifications, refer to the appendix at the end of the manual.

Shift Control

●
A shift control mechanism using a push-pull type control cable has been adopted.
●
A gate type shift lever has been adopted.
●
The shift lock release button has been located at the top of the shift lever assembly panel.
