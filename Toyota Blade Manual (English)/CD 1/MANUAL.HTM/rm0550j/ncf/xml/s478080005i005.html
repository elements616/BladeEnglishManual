Pollen Removal Control

●
A control that quickly removes pollen around the faces of front seat occupants by simply pressing the pollen removal switch on the air conditioning control panel, without complicated air conditioning adjustments, is used.
Pollen Removal Control Operation


•
When the pollen removal switch on the air conditioning control panel is operated, the operation indicator light illuminates, and the air conditioning computer sets the recirculation mode* and FACE outlet mode, so that clean air that has passed through the clean air filter blows out around the occupants' faces. In addition, pollen in the cabin is captured by the clean air filter.
•
Pollen removal control automatically returns to normal control after 3 minutes under normal conditions, or after 1 minutes when the outside air temperature is low. Pollen removal control is also canceled by pressing the pollen removal switch, the AUTO switch, or an outlet mode switch (including the DEF switch) during pollen removal control.
HINT
*
When the outside air temperature is low, the system does not switch to forced recirculation mode.
