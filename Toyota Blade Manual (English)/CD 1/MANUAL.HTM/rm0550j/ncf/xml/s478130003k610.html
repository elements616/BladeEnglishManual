Front & Side Area

●
The designs of the headlamps, radiator grille, front bumper grille and fog lamp covers have been changed. L-shaped moldings with bright silver paint are placed on the fog lamp covers, creating a wide, low center of gravity appearance.
●
2 types of radiator grille are provided: for "Blade" and for "Blade S Package". For the "Blade S Package", the same radiator grille as the "Master G" is provided, creating a sense of luxury and sportiness.
●
On the sides, the lower part of the door outside molding on all models is body color and the upper part is plated, creating a sense of luxury. In addition, SUS door belt moldings are provided depending on the grade, creating a sense of luxury. The door outside handles are body color on the "Blade" grade and metallic-look painted on the "G" and "Master G".
●
The headlamps are a round 4-lamp type, and horizontally long LEDs are adopted for the clearance lamps.
