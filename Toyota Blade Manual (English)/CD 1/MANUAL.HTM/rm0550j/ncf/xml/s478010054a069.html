Engine Control System General

●
TCCS (TOYOTA Computer Controlled System), which comprehensively and highly accurately controls fuel injection control (EFI), ignition timing control (ESA), throttle control (ETCS-i), VVT-i control, etc., has been adopted to improve performance, output, fuel economy and emission performance.
●
CAN communication has been adopted as the method of communication with other computers.
●
New diagnosis and fail-safe functions have been provided for serviceability and safety.

Control List


Control Name
Function
Fuel Injection Volume Control (EFI)
Appropriate fuel injection is performed by correcting the basic injection duration, calculated according to engine conditions, with signals from each sensor.
Ignition Timing Control (ESA)
Appropriate ignition is performed by correcting the basic ignition timing, calculated according to engine conditions, with signals from each sensor.
Knock Judgment Control (KCS)
The presence or absence of knocking is judged based on the signal from the knock sensor.
ETCS-i Control
The throttle is controlled to the appropriate opening angle by correcting the throttle opening angle, calculated according to engine conditions, with signals from each sensor.
Maximum Speed Control
When the vehicle speed reaches 180km/h, the throttle valve is closed to suppress any increase in vehicle speed.
Idle Speed Control (ISC)
Controls the fast idle speed according to the engine coolant temperature and the idle speed after engine warm-up. Controls the idle speed by means of the fuel injection volume and throttle opening angle.
VVT-i Control
Varies the phase of the intake camshaft according to engine conditions to improve engine output, reduce exhaust emissions and improve fuel economy.
Electric Cooling Fan Control
Controls cooling fan ON/OFF and fan speed according to coolant temperature and air conditioner status.
Air Conditioner Cut-off Control
Cuts off the air conditioner compressor during acceleration, etc. to ensure driveability.
Canister Purge Control
Controls the canister purge flow rate according to coolant temperature and driving conditions.
A/F Sensor Heater Control
Turns the A/F sensor heater ON/OFF according to coolant temperature and driving conditions.
O2Sensor Heater Control
According to coolant temperature and driving conditions, the O2sensor heater is turned ON/OFF.
Fuel Pump Control
Turns the fuel pump ON/OFF based on the starter signal and engine speed signal.
Cranking Hold Control
Based on the start signal from the engine switch, the engine control computer continues to energize the starter via the starter relay until the engine starts.
Alternator Charging Control
Controls the alternator generated voltage according to driving conditions, reducing the generation load to lower fuel consumption.
CAN Communication
A communication IC capable of exchanging large amounts of information over 1 communication lines has been adopted to communicate with other control computers.
Diagnosis
Using the diagnostic tool TaSCAN, accurate and detailed diagnosis, such as reading SAE-standard DTCs and data and performing Active Tests, is possible.
Fail-safe
When a malfunction occurs in a signal from any sensor, control is continued using standard values in the engine control computer, or the engine is stopped.
