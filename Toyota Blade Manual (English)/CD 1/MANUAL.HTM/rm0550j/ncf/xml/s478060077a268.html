TRC

●
If the throttle is opened too much when starting off or accelerating on a slippery road surface, etc., the drive wheels may slip due to excessive torque, impairing starting/acceleration performance and steerability. TRC is a system that suppresses drive wheel slip through brake fluid pressure control of the drive wheels and engine output control, controls the driving force according to road surface conditions, and ensures the vehicle's starting/acceleration performance, straight-line stability and cornering stability.

TRC Features

①
Delicate accelerator operation is not required for starting off and accelerating on slippery road surfaces
②
Excellent steerability and stability during acceleration
③
More stable cornering even when accelerating during a turn
④
Stable acceleration even on split road surfaces where the conditions under the left and right drive wheels differ
