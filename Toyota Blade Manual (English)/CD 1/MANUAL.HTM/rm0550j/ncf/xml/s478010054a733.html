Fuel Pump Control

●
Turns the fuel pump ON and OFF based on the starter signal and engine speed signal.
●
In addition to when the engine is stopped, when the airbag deployment signal from the airbag ECU is input to the engine control computer upon airbag deployment, the fuel pump is stopped to minimize fuel leakage.

Related Parts


Fuel Pump

●
A circumferential flow type in-tank fuel pump has been adopted.
●
By integrating the charcoal canister, fuel filter and pressure regulator into the fuel pump assembly, the number of parts and weight have been reduced and space in the engine room has been saved.
