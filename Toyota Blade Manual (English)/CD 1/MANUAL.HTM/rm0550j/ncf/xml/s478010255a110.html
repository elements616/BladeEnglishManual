Exhaust General

●
Stainless steel exhaust manifold and exhaust pipes have been adopted to reduce weight and improve corrosion resistance.
●
Three-way catalysts are located directly below the exhaust manifolds (integrated with the exhaust manifolds) and at the sub muffler (integrated with the sub muffler) to reduce emissions from immediately after engine start (when cold), and by ensuring sufficient emission performance, the vehicle complies with the "75% Reduction Level from Year 17 Emission Standards (SU-LEV)".
●
The A/F sensor is located upstream of the three-way catalyst in the exhaust manifold, and the O2sensor is located downstream of the three-way catalyst in the exhaust manifold, enabling precise air-fuel ratio control.
