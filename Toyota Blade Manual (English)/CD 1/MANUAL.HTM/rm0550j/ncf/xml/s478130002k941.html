Damageability Improvement Structure

●
For the radiator support, a structure in which the radiator support and cooling unit move back together in a light collision (radiator support retraction structure) has been adopted, reducing damage to the cooling unit and the repair cost of large parts.
●
The crash boxes have a structure that effectively absorbs collision energy from the front bumper reinforcement, reducing damage to the body frame.
●
The front bumper reinforcement and radiator support (frame section) use a bolt-on mounting structure to improve serviceability.

●
The rear bumper arms use a bolt-on structure to improve serviceability.
●
A body lower back panel reinforcement has been provided for the lower back panel. This allows only the body lower back panel reinforcement to be replaced after a light collision, reducing repair costs.

●
In the rear underbody, rigidity has been improved by straightening the rear floor side members and providing reinforcement members, and by ensuring rigidity at the joint with rear floor cross member No. No.2, inward collapse of the rear floor side members against impacts from diagonally behind is suppressed, reducing damage to the body frame.
