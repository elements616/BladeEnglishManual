Accessory Layout

●
A serpentine belt drive system, in which all accessories are driven by 1 V-ribbed belt, has been adopted, reducing overall engine length and weight. An automatic tensioner for the V-ribbed belt has been adopted, extending the life of the belt and accessories, making them maintenance-free and improving serviceability when removing and installing the belt.
●
By lowering the tension of the accessory drive belt, friction has been reduced and fuel economy improved.
