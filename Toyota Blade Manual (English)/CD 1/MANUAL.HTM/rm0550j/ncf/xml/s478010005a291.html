Engine Features (High Performance / Low Fuel Consumption)


•
By adopting VVT-i, optimal valve timing is set according to engine conditions in various speed ranges, achieving higher output and torque.
•
A DOHC4-valve valve mechanism and vertical intake ports have been adopted and the valve included angle has been narrowed to 27.5°, improving intake and exhaust efficiency.
•
By optimizing the intake port shape, the optimal tumble flow for combustion is generated in the combustion chamber, improving combustion.
•
A slant squish has been adopted in the combustion chamber. In addition, the water jacket in the cylinder head has been optimally arranged to raise the knock limit.
•
By adopting an offset crankshaft, in which the crankshaft center is offset from the cylinder bore center, the piston thrust load is reduced, thus reducing friction. Furthermore, because the piston speed differs between the compression stroke and the expansion stroke, thermal efficiency is improved.
•
Lightweight, highly rigid aluminum alloy pistons have been adopted. In addition, sliding resistance has been reduced by applying a striated finish and resin coating to the pistons and by reducing piston ring tension, and the adoption of spiny-type liners improves bore rigidity, liner outer surface adhesion and bore roundness, greatly reducing friction around the pistons.
•
By enlarging the volume of the bottom of the oil pan, the oil level is lowered without changing the oil quantity, which reduces oil agitation by the balance shafts, etc., thus reducing friction.

Engine Features (Low Emissions)


•
By integrating the three-way catalytic converter with the exhaust manifold, catalyst warm-up performance is enhanced, improving emission performance.

Engine Features (Lightweight / Compact)


•
By adopting a direct-acting DOHC, the area around the cylinder head has been made compact and lightweight.
•
Weight has been reduced by using an aluminum alloy cylinder block.
•
Weight has been reduced by making the air cleaner case and intake manifold of resin.
•
By adopting a serpentine drive in which the accessories are driven by 1 belt, the overall length of the engine has been shortened.

Engine Features (Low Noise / Low Vibration)


•
A highly rigid aluminum alloy crankcase has been adopted. The bullet shape of the rear end, the increased flange thickness and the optimized arrangement of the transmission mounting bolts ensure coupling rigidity with the transaxle, achieving low vibration and low noise.
•
A high-strength, highly rigid cast steel crankshaft with 5 journals and 8 balance weights has been adopted; while reducing weight, vibration and noise are reduced through the optimal arrangement of the balance weights, lighter pistons and improved cylinder block rigidity.
•
A liquid-filled type has been adopted for the right engine mounting and the mounting bracket has been made a separate part; together with reduced roll vibration of the engine body through optimized mount positions, low vibration and low noise have been achieved.
•
Quietness has been improved by adopting an engine cover with sound insulation material.
•
Intake noise has been reduced by adopting a porous duct for the air cleaner inlet.

Engine Features (Reliability)


•
Using the "TaSCAN" diagnostic tool, accurate and detailed diagnosis is possible, such as reading SAE-standard diagnostic trouble codes and data and performing Active Tests.

Engine Features (Serviceability)


•
Maintenance-free operation has been achieved by adopting a timing chain with an auto tensioner.
•
The maintenance interval has been extended by adopting iridium spark plugs.
•
The air cleaner inlet has a two-piece structure, improving serviceability when replacing the headlights.
