Seat Belt with Pretensioner + Force Limiter

●
Pretensioner + force limiter mechanisms are provided in the seat belt retractors of the driver, front passenger and rear outer seats.
●
The seat belt consists of a belt retractor mechanism, ELR lock mechanism, pretensioner mechanism, force limiter mechanism, etc.
●
A service caution label describing handling precautions, etc. is affixed to the surface of the seat belt.
Construction and Operation of Pretensioner Mechanism


•
The pretensioner mechanism consists of a gas generator, piston rack, pinion, etc.


Construction and Operation of Force Limiter Mechanism


•
The force limiter mechanism consists of a torsion bar, spool, etc.
•
After the pretensioner operation is complete and the seat belt is pulled out by the movement of the occupant, the ELR mechanism locks and the force limiter starts to operate. When the belt is pulled out at or above the specified acceleration, or acceleration above the specified value is applied to the vehicle, the ELR mechanism operates and the belt cannot be pulled out.
•
When tension exceeding the specified load is applied to the belt with the ELR mechanism locked, the torsion bar twists, and the spool integrated with the torsion bar rotates to pull out the belt, keeping the belt tension constant.
