Radar Cruise Control System Control Status Display

●
In vehicle-to-vehicle distance control mode, the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light illuminates and the set vehicle speed, presence of a preceding vehicle and set vehicle-to-vehicle distance are displayed on the multi-information display.
●
In constant speed control mode, the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light illuminates and "Constant speed" and the set vehicle speed are displayed on the multi-information display.
●
When a malfunction occurs in the radar cruise control system, the driver is informed of the system malfunction by the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light blinking, the master warning indicator light illuminating, the single-tone buzzer sounding and a warning displayed on the multi-information display.

Warning Display (Vehicle-to-vehicle Distance Control Mode Unavailable)


•
When the self-check function of the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly detects dirt on the front of the sensor or on the radar-transmissive grille cover, the single-tone buzzer sounds, the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light blinks, the master warning indicator light illuminates, and "Radar dirty, cleaning required" is displayed on the multi-information display.
•
Bad weather* , etc. may reduce the detection performance of the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly; in that case the single-tone buzzer sounds, the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light blinks, the master warning indicator light illuminates, and "Bad weather, cruise unavailable" is displayed on the multi-information display.
HINT
*
When the wipers are operating at HI.

Warning Display (Cruise Control Driving Unavailable)


•
When the system self-check function detects any malfunction in the system, the single-tone buzzer sounds, the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light blinks, the master warning indicator light illuminates, and "Check cruise system" is displayed on the multi-information display.
