Radar Cruise Control System (Constant Speed Control Mode) Handling Precautions

●
When radar cruise control system control (constant speed control mode) is selected, the presence of a preceding vehicle and the distance to it are not determined as they are when vehicle-to-vehicle distance control mode is selected, so the skid control buzzer does not sound. Pay close attention to the distance to the preceding vehicle.
• If necessary, perform the cancel operation and then decelerate using engine braking or the foot brake.

●
When not using cruise control system control (constant speed control mode), keep the main switch OFF.
●
When driving under cruise control system control (constant speed control mode) on a downhill slope, pay close attention to increases in vehicle speed. (If the vehicle speed becomes too high, perform the cancel operation and then decelerate using engine braking or the foot brake.)
●
Operating the + RES switch after cruise control system control (constant speed control mode) has been canceled performs resume control, and operating it during constant speed driving performs acceleration control.
●
If the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light blinks while driving under cruise control system control (constant speed control mode), turn the main switch OFF and then perform the set operation again.
• If the vehicle speed cannot be set, or is canceled immediately after being set, a malfunction in the cruise control system is suspected. There is no problem with normal driving, but have the vehicle inspected at a dealer.

●
Do not drive under cruise control system control (constant speed control mode) in situations such as the following. The vehicle cannot be driven at a speed suited to the road conditions, or control of the vehicle may be lost due to tire spin, etc., which may lead to an unexpected accident.
Roads with heavy traffic
Steep downhill slopes
Sharp curves
Icy or snow-covered roads
Other slippery road surfaces
