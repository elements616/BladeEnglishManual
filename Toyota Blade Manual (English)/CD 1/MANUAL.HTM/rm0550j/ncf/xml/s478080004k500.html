Condenser

●
A sub-cool condenser, which integrates a multi-flow condenser and a gas-liquid separator (modulator) to form a sub-cool cycle, is used for excellent heat exchange efficiency.

Sub-cool Condenser Construction


•
In the sub-cool cycle, a single condenser is divided into a condensing section and a supercooling section with a gas-liquid separator between them; the liquid refrigerant that has been separated from gas is further cooled to increase its energy (enthalpy), improving cooling efficiency.


Refrigerant Gas Charging in the Sub-cool Cycle


•
In a receiver cycle, the point at which bubbles disappear is at the entrance to the stable cooling capacity range (plateau), but in a sub-cool cycle the point at which bubbles disappear is before the stable cooling range (plateau), so it is necessary to add 100 ± 50g to reach the proper charge amount. (If refrigerant gas charging is stopped at the point at which bubbles disappear, cooling capacity will tend to be insufficient.) Overcharging also causes poor fuel economy and insufficient cooling capacity, so charge the proper amount of refrigerant gas.
