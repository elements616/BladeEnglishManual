Back Guide Monitor System Outline

●
A color back guide monitor with a voice guidance function, which assists parking in a garage and parallel parking with screen display on the multi-display and voice, has been adopted and offered as a factory option on all models as a set with the HDD navigation system.
●
The back guide monitor is a system that assists reversing by superimposing distance guide lines, vehicle width extension lines, estimated course lines and parking guide lines on the image from the rear view monitor camera mounted at the rear of the vehicle, and displaying it on the display screen. Voice guidance can be output during parallel parking.
●
When the shift position is moved to "R" (reverse) with the power source mode ON (IG), the display changes to the back guide monitor screen and the image of the area behind the vehicle from the rear view monitor camera is displayed on the display screen.
HINT
The back guide monitor is an auxiliary device for making it easier to check behind the vehicle; always drive while directly confirming visually that it is safe behind and around the vehicle.

Related Parts


Rear View Monitor Camera

●
The rear view monitor camera consists of an image input section using an ultra-wide-angle lens and an image signal output section consisting of a compact (1/4 inch) color CCD camera.
●
The image signal output section uses a CCD (Charge Coupled Device) image sensor to convert the image captured (input) by the lens into electrical signals according to light intensity, and outputs them to the back guide monitor computer.
●
The output image from the rear view monitor camera is itself reversed to match the view when looking rearward in the inner rear view mirror. Therefore, care is required because the image on the screen is shown laterally reversed compared with looking directly rearward (objects on the left side of the vehicle are displayed on the left side of the screen, and objects on the right side of the vehicle on the right side of the screen).

Modes and Selection Operations


•
When the shift position is moved to "R" (reverse) with the power source mode ON (IG), the rear image display starts; touching "Course Display" or "Parallel Parking Guide" on the image behind vehicle display screen changes to "Parallel Parking Guide Mode", and parallel parking guidance is provided.
•
Touching "Course Clear" hides the estimated course lines. At this time, the "Course Display" switch is displayed in the same position; touching "Course Display" returns to the original screen.

Course Display Mode




When Perpendicular Parking (Parking in a Garage)
•
The "estimated course lines" indicate the steering angle when reversing; by reversing while operating the steering wheel so that the estimated course lines match the lines of the parking space, the vehicle can be guided into the parking space.
•
The "vehicle width extension lines" serve as a guide when parking straight within a space; by judging the relationship (intersecting → parallel) between the parking space lines and the vehicle width extension lines, it can be confirmed that the vehicle is facing straight into the space. At this time, the "Course Display" switch is displayed in the same position; touching "Course Display" returns to the original screen.



When Parallel Parking
•
The "parking guide lines" serve as a guide for the position at which to stop when reversing straight back during parallel parking, etc.; by reversing until the parking guide lines match the desired parking position, such as the road shoulder or parking space lines, the vehicle can be moved to the appropriate position before turning the steering wheel.
•
The parking guide lines can be set to be displayed or hidden according to purpose and preference. For details, refer to the back guide monitor setting screen.


Parallel Parking Guide Mode


•
When parallel parking guide mode is selected, in addition to the screen display, guidance on the vehicle stop positions and steering operation timing is given at each point in the parallel parking process. Some of these are shown below.


Initial Tips Display
•
As the first-stage operating procedure (initial tips) for parallel parking, the vehicle must be moved to the initial position (where the vehicle is parallel to the road and approximately 1m from the parked vehicle ahead); a graphic showing this state is displayed.
•
The initial tips display can be switched between displayed and hidden as necessary. For details, refer to the back guide monitor setting screen.



Stop Position Guidance Before Steering Operation
•
After the vehicle is stopped at the initial position, the voice guidance "Back up until the green vertical line aligns with the rear of the adjacent vehicle, then stop. " is output. By reversing until the green vertical line aligns with the rear end of the adjacent vehicle according to the voice guidance, the vehicle can be stopped at the appropriate position before turning the steering wheel.



Steering Angle Guidance
•
When the vehicle reverses with the green vertical line aligned with the rear end of the adjacent vehicle, a "green frame linked to steering operation" is displayed on the screen together with the voice guidance "While stopped, turn the steering wheel to align the green frame with the parking space. " is output. By turning the steering wheel according to the voice guidance and aligning the green frame with the desired parking position, the appropriate steering angle can be set.


Back Guide Monitor Setting Screen


•
Touching "Surrounding Monitor" on the information mode screen of the multi-display changes to the back guide monitor setting screen, where settings related to the back guide monitor can be made.
