Brake Actuator

●
Consists of master cylinder cut solenoid valves, holding solenoid valves, pressure reduction solenoid valves, a pump, reservoirs, etc., and regulates the brake hydraulic pressure to each wheel cylinder. In addition, the brake actuator and skid control computer are integrated to reduce size.

Hydraulic Circuit Diagram




Holding and Pressure Reduction Solenoid Valves


•
Two solenoid valves are operated by control signals from the skid control computer to switch the brake hydraulic circuit among 3 modes: pressure increase, holding and pressure reduction.

Master Cylinder Cut Solenoid Valve


•
Linear solenoid valves are used as the master cylinder cut solenoid valves, smoothing hydraulic pressure changes during brake assist control.
•
By controlling the open and closed state of the valves, the hydraulic pressure generated by the pump is regulated, making it possible to generate control hydraulic pressure according to the operating condition.


Master Cylinder Pressure Sensor


•
The master cylinder pressure sensor is built into the brake actuator. Outputs the master cylinder pressure signal to the skid control computer.


ABS with EBD Operation


•
Hydraulic pressure control of the 4 wheels is performed by operating the holding and pressure reduction solenoid valves, pump and reservoirs.

Rear Wheel Skid Control


•
Hydraulic pressure generated by the pump in the brake actuator is directed to the wheel cylinders of each wheel, and the brake hydraulic pressure is controlled to suppress rear wheel skid or front wheel skid.
•
In rear wheel skid control, the front brake on the outer side of the turn is applied.

•
VSC control directs hydraulic pressure generated by the pump in the brake actuator to each wheel cylinder and controls the brake hydraulic pressure to suppress rear wheel skid or front wheel skid.
•
In front wheel skid control, the front wheel brakes and the rear wheel brake on the inner side of the turn are applied.

Hill-start Assist Control Brake Control


•
While the brakes are held, the M/C cut solenoid valves (No.1 and No.2) are closed so that a certain portion of the hydraulic pressure generated by the master cylinder remains in the calipers, performing hill-start assist control.
