Timing Related Items

●
A chain drive system has been adopted, making it maintenance-free. By adopting 9.525mm-pitch high-strength single-row roller chains for the primary and secondary timing chains, the sprockets have been downsized and the engine has been made more compact.
●
A 2-stage chain system has been adopted, consisting of a primary chain that drives the left and right intake camshafts from the crankshaft and secondary chains that drive the exhaust camshafts from the intake camshafts.
●
A check ball type chain tensioner with a ratchet mechanism, using both engine oil pressure and spring force, has been adopted for the primary chain tensioner, and compact tensioners that push the chain directly with engine oil pressure and spring force have been adopted for the secondary chain tensioners, constantly applying optimum tension to the timing chains to ensure quietness and reliability.
●
A lightweight aluminum alloy timing chain cover with high sound insulation has been adopted. The front parts of the cylinder block, camshaft housings and cylinder heads are sealed as a unit. A tooth-skip prevention guide is provided at the crankshaft sprocket to prevent incorrect assembly due to the chain coming off during maintenance. In addition, a service hole for the primary chain tensioner is provided in the timing chain cover for easier maintenance.
●
Resin material with excellent wear resistance has been adopted for the chain dampers and tensioner slippers.
