Piston

●
Piston weight has been reduced by shortening the compression height and using a short skirt. Also, the top has a slant squish structure to optimize fuel economy and volumetric efficiency.
●
The piston skirt width has been reduced, and a striated finish and resin coating have been applied to reduce friction.
●
Lightweight piston pins with a short overall length have been adopted.
●
Compression ring No.1,No.2 has a narrow width to lower tension and reduce friction. Also, compression ring No.1 has been PVD treated to ensure wear resistance.
●
The oil ring has a lightweight 2-piece construction, reducing friction while ensuring good oil control.
