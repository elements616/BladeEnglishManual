Exhaust Pipe

●
Stainless steel exhaust pipes with excellent heat resistance have been adopted.
●
Ball joint mechanisms have been adopted between center pipe No.1 and center pipe No.2, and between center pipe No.2 and the tail pipe, reducing booming noise in the cabin, weight and vibration.

Ball Joint Mechanism


•
The ball joint mechanism consists of a flange with a spherical surface, and bolts and springs that support the seal portion of the gasket. It can be made more compact than a flexible pipe.
•
Vibration and noise are reduced by absorbing vibration from the engine and resonance vibration of the exhaust pipe with the ball joint (spherical portion of the flange and gasket).
