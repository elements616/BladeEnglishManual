Windshield Deicer System

●
A windshield deicer with a timer function (approximately 15 minutes) has been adopted on cold area specification models.
●
The heating elements of the windshield deicer are provided at the front wiper stop position (upper part of the cowl panel) and reversing position (right front pillar), to prevent the front wipers from locking or being damaged by snow and ice accumulated on the windshield glass.
●
The windshield deicer switch has a built-in operation indicator light and timer circuit, and is located on the right side of the instrument panel on the driver side.

Windshield Deicer Operation


•
When the windshield deicer switch is turned ON with IG ON, the windshield deicer relay turns ON and the windshield deicer heats up.
