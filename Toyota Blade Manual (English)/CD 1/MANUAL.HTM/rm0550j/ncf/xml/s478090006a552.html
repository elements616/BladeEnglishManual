Pre-crash Safety System Control Outline

●
The millimeter wave radar sensor assembly recognizes vehicles and obstacles in the vehicle's path, and the distance control computer assembly determines whether a collision is unavoidable from the position, speed and path of the object.
●
The distance control computer assembly sends that information via CAN communication to the ABS & traction actuator assembly (skid control computer) and the seat belt control computer, operating each control of the pre-crash safety system.
●
When the ABS & traction actuator assembly (skid control computer) recognizes emergency braking by the driver or determines that there is a high possibility that the vehicle will become uncontrollable (spinning, etc.), it sends that information to the seat belt control computer via CAN communication to operate the pre-crash seat belt.

Control List


Control
Description
Pre-crash Seat Belt
When the distance control computer (pre-crash safety computer) determines that a collision is unavoidable, the belt is retracted by the motor to increase the pretensioner effect.
When the skid control computer recognizes emergency braking by the driver or determines that there is a high possibility that the vehicle will become uncontrollable, the belt is retracted by the motor to increase the pretensioner effect.
Pre-crash Brake Assist Control
When the distance control computer (pre-crash safety computer) determines that the possibility of a collision is high, brake hydraulic pressure is boosted to increase the braking effect when the brake pedal is depressed.
Pre-crash Brake Control
When the distance control computer (pre-crash safety computer) determines that there is a possibility of a collision, it warns the driver with a buzzer and display. Furthermore, when a collision is determined to be unavoidable, the brakes are applied to reduce speed even without brake operation by the driver.

Related Parts


Seat Belt Outline

●
The child seat securing mechanism of the rear seat 3-point ELR seat belts has been discontinued.
Millimeter Wave

●
Millimeter waves are radio waves in a very high frequency band from 30GHz to 300GHz, with an extremely short wavelength in a vacuum of 1 to 10mm. Millimeter waves are not easily affected by conditions such as rain, fog or snow, and provide excellent object recognition performance. A 76GHz band radar is used in the pre-crash safety system.

Pre-crash Sensor

●
Obstacles are recognized through the millimeter wave radar, the possibility of a collision is determined, and operation request signals are sent to each computer.

Pre-crash Seat Belt Operation


•
When the pre-crash sensor determines that a collision is unavoidable. (Radar linked)
•
When the driver depresses the brake suddenly. Or when vehicle skidding is determined (brake linked)
•
Under normal conditions, the shaft is free due to the clutch mechanism.
•
When current is applied to the motor and rotation is transmitted through the reduction gear, the clutch engages and the webbing is retracted.
•
In addition, after operation, a retraction operation is performed again to release the belt retraction.


Pre-crash Brake Assist Control


•
When the pre-crash sensor determines that the possibility of a collision is high, braking pressure is boosted when the driver depresses the brake pedal.

•
If the brake pedal was depressed before the possibility of a collision was determined to be high, pressure is boosted immediately as soon as the possibility of a collision is determined to be high. However, the assist effect applies only to the additional brake pedal depression amount newly applied by the driver.


Pre-crash Brake Control


•
Operates when the pre-crash sensor detects a collision object such as a preceding vehicle or road structure and determines that a collision is unavoidable.

Seat Belt Control Computer

●
When the seat belt control computer receives a pre-crash seat belt operation request signal from the distance control computer assembly or the ABS & traction actuator assembly (skid control computer), it operates the internal motor drive circuit to retract the seat belt.
●
If a collision does not actually occur even though the pre-crash seat belt has operated, the motor is rotated in reverse to return the belt to its tension before operation.

Operation of Diagnosis Circuit


•
The seat belt control computer detects system malfunctions in 2 ways: a primary check and a constant check.
•
When a malfunction is detected in the system, a warning is displayed in the combination meter.

Check Details


Check
Outline
Primary Check
Performed for 3 seconds when the power is turned ON (IG ON). During the check, the PCS indicator light in the combination meter illuminates. If there is no malfunction, the light turns off and the pre-crash seat belt becomes ready to operate.
Constant Check
After the primary check is completed, the diagnosis circuit in the seat belt control computer constantly checks the system for malfunctions.



Diagnosis Function


•
By switching to the diagnosis mode, the diagnosis result for the malfunction location can be displayed by the number of times the indicator light blinks. In addition, a new diagnosis function has been adopted, and the diagnostic trouble code for the malfunction location can be read by connecting a diagnostic tool to DLC3. For details on the diagnosis mode, refer to the Repair Manual.
