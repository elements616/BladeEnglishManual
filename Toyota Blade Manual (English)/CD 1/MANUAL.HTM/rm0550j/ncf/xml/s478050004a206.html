Upper Support & Coil Spring

●
An integrated input type upper support has been adopted. The rebound stopper plate construction improves the initial effectiveness of the shock absorber damping force on the rebound side.
●
A urethane bound stopper has been adopted, providing soft non-linear characteristics at initial contact and achieving both roll rigidity during cornering and a supple ride.
●
A side force reduction spring has been adopted to control the reaction force line of the coil spring and reduce the bending force on the shock absorber, reducing suspension friction and achieving an excellent ride.
●
An upper insulator with an integrated dust cover has been adopted.
●
A compact, lightweight inner diameter restraint type upper spring seat has been adopted.
