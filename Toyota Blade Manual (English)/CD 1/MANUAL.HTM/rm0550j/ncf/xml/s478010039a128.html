Cylinder Block Related Items

●
An aluminum alloy cylinder block with cast-in thin cast iron liners has been adopted to reduce weight.
●
An aluminum alloy crankcase has been adopted. Integrating the stiffener ensures rigidity and reduces vibration. Also, friction has been reduced by giving the area around the balance shafts in the crankcase a cover shape that reduces the flow of oil onto the balance shafts.
●
An oil pan made of rust-resistant steel sheet has been adopted. By making the volume of the oil pan bottom large and lowering the oil level, oil agitation by the balance shafts, etc. has been reduced, reducing friction.
