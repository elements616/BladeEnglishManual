Auto Leveling System

●
An auto leveling function (static control), which adjusts the beam axis while the vehicle is stopped, has been provided for the low beam headlights of models with discharge headlights to prevent glare to oncoming and preceding vehicles.
●
The auto leveling system consists of the headlight leveling computer, height control sensor, headlight leveling actuators, etc.

Auto Leveling System Configuration


Components
Main Functions
Headlight Leveling Computer
Controls the auto leveling function. Determines the vehicle posture and driving condition based on signals from the height control sensor and combination meter, and controls the headlight leveling actuators. When a system malfunction is detected, outputs a headlight auto leveling warning indicator light illumination request signal to the combination meter.
Headlight Leveling Actuator
Drives the low beam headlight up and down according to the control signal from the headlight leveling computer to control the direction of the beam axis.
Combination Meter
Illuminates the headlight auto leveling warning indicator light according to the signal from the headlight leveling computer. Outputs the vehicle speed signal to the headlight leveling computer.
Height Control Sensor
Detects the vehicle height and outputs a vehicle height change signal to the headlight leveling computer.


Auto Leveling Function


•
Changes in vehicle posture due to loading of luggage, etc. are detected by the height control sensor installed on the right side of the rear suspension, and the headlight leveling actuators installed in the headlights are driven according to the change in vehicle posture to keep the headlight beam axis at a constant angle while the vehicle is stopped.

Auto Leveling Operation


•
The headlight leveling computer, which controls the auto leveling system, receives the vehicle speed signal and the vehicle height change signal from the height control sensor. Based on these signals, it calculates the change in pitch angle of the vehicle posture and drives the headlight leveling actuators according to the posture of the stopped vehicle to control the headlight beam axis.

Related Systems

Light Control Computer Operation


•
The light control computer achieves instant and stable illumination of the HID bulb through controls such as generating a high voltage of up to approximately 22,000V. Since this device generates high voltage, various fail-safe functions are incorporated, and consideration has also been given to ensuring reliability against heat, humidity, vibration, etc.


HID Bulb Illumination Control Flow

1. The battery voltage is converted into a high-voltage pulse (approximately 22,000V) by the light control computer in the lamp.
2. The high-voltage pulse is applied between both electrodes of the bulb, causing the xenon gas to emit light.
3. Arc discharge occurs as the temperature inside the bulb rises.
4. As the temperature rises further, the metal iodide vaporizes and separates, and the metal atoms emit light by discharge.
5. The light control computer optimally controls the power supplied to the bulb.

Discharge Headlight Features


•
The discharge headlight is a headlight that uses an HID bulb (high intensity discharge lamp) as its light source.
•
The light emitted from the HID bulb is a natural daylight color close to sunlight and illuminates a wider and more distant area ahead, helping to improve driver visibility; in addition, its low power consumption reduces the load on the battery and the bulb has a long service life.
•
The discharge headlight achieves instant lighting of the HID bulb and a stable power supply, and has a compact, lightweight light control computer; it is also combined with an automatic leveling function that keeps the vertical beam axis constant as a device to prevent dazzling oncoming vehicles.

HID Bulb Construction


•
The arc tube of the HID bulb is filled with a rare gas (xenon gas) and metal iodides (zinc, sodium and scandium), and arc discharge occurs in the arc tube due to the voltage applied to the base side and lead wire side.
•
UV-cut glass that absorbs ultraviolet rays is used for the outer tube of the arc tube, keeping ultraviolet radiation to a level equivalent to that of halogen bulbs.


Light Control Computer

●
The light control computer is a device that supplies the alternating current (AC) power required to illuminate the HID bulb.
●
The frequency of the AC power is several hundred Hz or more so that no flicker is perceived during illumination, and electromagnetic shielding is provided so that electromagnetic waves do not affect the vehicle radio, etc.

Fail-safe Function of Light Control Computer


•
When a malfunction occurs in the output voltage, input voltage or lamp side voltage, the light control computer performs fail-safe as follows.
Light Control Computer Fail-safe List


Malfunction Detection Condition
Fail-safe Content
When an output malfunction is detected (short, open or leak detected) and when the bulb flickers
If a malfunction occurs in the output voltage or bulb flickering continues for 60 +/- 20 seconds or more, the headlight is turned off and remains in that state until the power is turned on again (headlight switch OFF → ON or power switch (IG) OFF → ON).
When Input Malfunction Detected
If the input voltage is outside the operating voltage (10 to 16V), the headlight is turned off, and it illuminates again as soon as the input returns to the operating range. However, if the input voltage drops after illumination, the headlight remains illuminated until the voltage reaches the illumination holding limit voltage (6V or less).
When Lamp Voltage Malfunction Detected
If a lamp voltage malfunction is detected, the headlight is turned off and remains in that state until the power is turned on again (headlight switch OFF → ON or power switch (IG) OFF → ON).



Precautions for Handling Light Control Computer


•
To prevent danger, caution labels have been placed on the rear side of the headlight unit and on the light control computer.

■ NOTICE ■
High voltage is generated at the output terminals of the light control computer, which is dangerous, so handle it with great care.
Always have discharge headlight bulbs replaced at a Toyota dealer.
Do not touch the glass and electrode parts of the bulb, as high voltage is generated there and it is dangerous.
Illuminate the bulb only after installation has been completely finished, and do not use any power source other than the vehicle.
If the following damage (see List of Examples Requiring Replacement) is found on the light control computer or headlight after a vehicle collision, etc., replace the light control computer or headlight.

List of Examples Requiring Replacement


•
When the light control computer is clearly deformed (warped, twisted, dented, dinged, cracked, chipped, etc.).
•
When the resin parts of the connectors or bulb socket are cracked or chipped, or the terminals are deformed.
•
When contact with the ground part on the reflector side is unstable due to deformation of the socket cover, etc.
•
When the high-voltage harness or power supply wire harness is damaged.

•
Note that even if the light control computer operates normally temporarily, it is not necessarily normal.
