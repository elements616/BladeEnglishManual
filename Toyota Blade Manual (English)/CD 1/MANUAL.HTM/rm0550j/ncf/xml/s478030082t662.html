M Mode Control (6-Speed Sport Direct Shift) Outline

●
In the M position, the control specifications emphasize a direct feel in response to accelerator operation as well as manual response and feel, allowing the driver to enjoy sporty driving.
●
"Gear hold control" and "full lock-up control" have been adopted to produce a direct feel.
●
"High-response upshift control" and "blipping downshift control" have been adopted to improve shift response and feel.

Gear Hold Control


•
In M mode, the transmission normally does not shift unless "+ (UP)" or "- (DOWN)" on the paddle shifts or shift lever is operated.*
•
Holding the gear makes it possible to use the engine speed up to its maximum.
HINT
*
Even in M mode, the transmission downshifts when the vehicle speed decreases.

Full Lock-up Control


•
By performing lock-up control during acceleration and deceleration from 2nd and above, changes in engine output are transmitted directly to the output shaft, as with a manual transmission.*
HINT
*
In 1st and in the low engine speed range even in 2nd or higher gears, lock-up control is not performed and the torque converter function is used.

High-response Upshift Control


•
The U660E has a hydraulic control system that directly controls the clutch pressure and a system that controls the engine and transmission powertrain in an integrated manner.
•
The direct hydraulic control system performs quick and precise clutch engagement and release control, while integrated powertrain control optimally controls changes in engine speed (torque down), achieving both quick response and crisp shift characteristics.
•
Engine speed change (torque down) control during upshifts precisely controls the electronic throttle and ignition timing to be optimum for the driving conditions.


Blipping Downshift Control


•
The U660E has a hydraulic control system that directly controls the clutch pressure and a system that controls the engine and transmission powertrain in an integrated manner.
•
The direct hydraulic control system quickly releases the release side clutch to create a neutral state. Next, integrated powertrain control opens the electronic throttle and smoothly engages the engagement side clutch, raising the engine speed at once to the synchronized speed of the gear after the shift without impairing the engine braking feel during the shift.
