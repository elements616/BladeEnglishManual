Rear Lamps Outline

●
A rear combination light extending from the quarter panel to the back door, with a sense of unity and high quality, has been adopted. Also, LEDs have been adopted as the light source for the stop & taillight and taillight to improve fuel economy.
●
The stop & taillight, rear turn signal light and back-up light are located on the quarter panel side, and the taillight is located on the back door side.
●
To improve visibility to others, an LED-type high mounted stop light installed on the rear spoiler has been provided as standard on all models.
●
A downward-illuminating 1-light license plate light has been provided as standard on all models and installed in the rear bumper.
