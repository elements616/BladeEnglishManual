Transfer

●
A GF1A type transfer has been adopted on 4WD models.
●
It is located on the right side of the drive shaft output section of the transaxle. It changes the direction of the driving force input to the differential case by 90 degrees and outputs it to the rear. The driven pinion gear is mounted with an upward offset relative to the ring gear.
●
The driven pinion gear is hollow, and a unit ball bearing is used to support it, resulting in a compact, lightweight construction.
●
A left/right split aluminum die-cast transfer case has been adopted, making the case compact and lightweight.
●
A spline connection is used to secure the ring gear and ring gear mount, providing a compact, lightweight connection.
●
A side lip contact type oil seal has been adopted for excellent resistance to muddy water.
