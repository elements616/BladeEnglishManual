Body Sound Absorbing and Insulating Materials

●
Various silencers are placed in appropriate locations to minimize transmitted noise from the engine, tires, etc., providing excellent sound insulation.
●
A significant improvement in quietness has been achieved by adopting a 3-layer dash panel insulator with an olefin sheet sandwiched between felt layers and a dash panel outer insulator made of laminated nonwoven fabrics of different densities, as well as by reducing the size of the heater pipe passage holes and improving the seal with the column hole cover.
●
A damping sheet is provided on the interior side of the cowl top panel, reducing noise transmitted from the engine room.
●
Asphalt sheets and spray-on damping material are effectively arranged on the floor panel, achieving both high sound insulation and weight reduction.
●
Foam material for blocking cross sections and resin-molded foam material are optimally provided in each part of the body, and service holes are closed with sealer, etc., minimizing noise transmitted through the pillars, etc.
