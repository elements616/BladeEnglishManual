Intake General

●
The shape of the intake manifold and the port length have been optimized to improve output characteristics, reduce vibration and improve quietness.
●
By adopting a full-fabric air element in the air cleaner, size reduction, weight reduction and recyclability have been considered.
●
A plastic intake manifold with large-diameter long ports has been adopted to optimize intake efficiency and reduce weight.
●
The ETCS-i system has been adopted, and a 1-valve electronically controlled throttle body has been installed.
