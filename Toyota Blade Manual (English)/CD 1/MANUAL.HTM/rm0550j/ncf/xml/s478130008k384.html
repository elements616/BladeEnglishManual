Front Seat Heater

●
Climate comfort seats are provided depending on the specification.
●
The seat heater system consists of seat heaters provided in the main portions of the seatback and seat cushion, and seat heater switches provided on the center console.
●
An indicator lamp is provided on the surface of the seat heater switch, allowing the operating status of the seat heater to be checked.

Seat Heater Operation


•
When the seat heater switch is turned ON in HI mode, current flows from the thermostat to all seat heaters, warming the seat. When the seat heater switch is turned ON in LO mode, current flows from the thermostat to a specific portion of the seatback heater, warming the seatback. The thermostat turns OFF when the seat warms up, cutting off current to the seat heater, and turns ON when the seat temperature drops, resuming current to the seat heater. This operation keeps the seat surface temperature at approximately 40°C in HI mode and approximately 36°C in LO mode.
