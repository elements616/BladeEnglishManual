Refrigerant

●
In consideration of ozone layer depletion, chlorine-free HFC-134a (R-134a) is used as the air conditioning refrigerant.
●
To prevent incorrect filling of compressor oil, the applicable refrigerant and type of applicable compressor oil are clearly indicated on the underside of the hood panel and on the compressor.
■ NOTICE ■
Air conditioning systems that use HFC-134a (R-134a) use dedicated parts and joints for each part. If parts other than the dedicated parts are used, refrigerant leaks may occur.
Be very careful, as compressors, compressor oil, O-rings, etc. not intended for HFC-134a may be used by mistake.
