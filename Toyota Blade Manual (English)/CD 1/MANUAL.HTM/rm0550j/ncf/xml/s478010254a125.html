Cylinder Head Cover

●
A lightweight aluminum alloy cylinder head cover has been adopted. In addition, sealing performance with the camshaft housing is ensured by fixed-dimension tightening at the outer flange. OCVs (oil control valves) for Dual VVT-i are mounted on the left and right cylinder head covers.
●
Large baffle plates are built into the left and right head covers. By reducing the flow velocity of the blow-by gas, oil droplets and oil mist are removed, reducing oil carry-over.
●
An oil delivery pipe is provided to supply lubricating oil, ensuring lubrication of the sliding parts of the roller rocker arms and improving reliability.
