Hill-start Assist Control

●
Hill-start assist control holds and controls the brake fluid pressure of the 4 wheels to prevent the vehicle from rolling backward when the driver moves from the brake pedal to the accelerator pedal when starting off on a slope.



Operating Conditions
●
Hill-start assist control operation starts when the brake pedal is depressed with all of the following conditions met.
●
When operation starts, the buzzer sounds, the slip indicator in the combination meter blinks and the stop lights illuminate.
Operating Conditions


1
When a shift position other than "P" is selected
2
Vehicle is stopped
3
When the accelerator pedal is not depressed
4
When the parking brake is not applied




Operation Cancel Conditions
●
Operation is canceled when any of the following conditions is met.
●
When operation is canceled, the buzzer sounds 2 times* and the slip indicator goes off.
Operation Stop Conditions


1
When shift position "P" is selected
2
When the accelerator pedal is depressed
3
When the parking brake is applied
4
When the brake pedal is depressed
5
When several seconds have elapsed after the driver removes his/her foot from the brake pedal
6
When 3 minutes have elapsed without the driver removing his/her foot from the brake pedal


HINT
*
The buzzer does not sound when the accelerator pedal is depressed.
■ NOTICE ■
This function is not intended for parking for long periods like the parking brake. The operation can prevent the vehicle from rolling backward for only approximately 2 seconds after the foot is removed from the brake pedal; perform the normal starting operation within this time.
If it does not operate even when the brake pedal is depressed, release the pressure on the brake pedal once and then depress the brake pedal again. If it still does not operate, check the operating conditions.
The vehicle may not be able to be held stationary on steep slopes or extremely slippery road surfaces.

Effect


•
Since the vehicle is prevented from rolling backward when moving from the brake to the accelerator, the driver can change pedals without hurrying.
•
Since the vehicle is prevented from rolling backward, wheel spin during accelerator operation is reduced, making it easier to start off on slippery road surfaces.

Precautions


•
Hill-start assist control is not a function for holding the vehicle stationary on a slope.
•
When hill-start assist control is operating, the slip indicator light in the combination meter blinks and the brake lights illuminate.
•
If the engine is stopped while hill-start assist control is operating, operation ends without warning the driver.
•
If the brake control of hill-start assist control is operated continuously, the temperature of the brake actuator increases. In this case, hill-start assist control is temporarily suspended to protect the system.
•
Hill-start assist control is a system that prevents the vehicle from rolling backward by controlling the brakes of the 4 wheels, but it may not be fully effective on extremely steep slopes or on road surfaces with extremely low friction such as icy roads.
