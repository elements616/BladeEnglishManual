IPA Ultrasonic Sensor

●
The IPA ultrasonic sensors are installed on the left and right of the front bumper.
●
In addition to the microphone that transmits and receives ultrasonic waves, a circuit that performs amplification, reverberation adjustment, sensitivity adjustment, etc. is built in, and the circuit section is filled with urethane for a waterproof structure.
●
The microphone section is a flat type with no recess at the tip, for good appearance.
●
The connector is directly connected to the sensor body for an integrated structure.
