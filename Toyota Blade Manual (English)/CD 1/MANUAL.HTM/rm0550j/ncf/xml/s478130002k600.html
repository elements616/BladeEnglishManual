Brake Pedal Retraction Reduction Structure

●
A "brake pedal retraction reduction structure", which minimizes the rearward movement of the brake pedal by moving the brake pedal downward in a frontal collision, has been adopted to reduce the impact on the driver's lower limbs.
●
When the brake booster is pushed in by the rearward movement of the engine, etc. in a collision, the clip (bolt) securing the brake pedal support to the instrument panel reinforcement separates from the brake pedal support, and the brake pedal support moves rearward and downward along the guide plate. As a result, the brake pedal pad moves forward and downward.
