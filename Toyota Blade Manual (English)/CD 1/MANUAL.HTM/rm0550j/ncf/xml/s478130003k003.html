White Pearl Crystal Shine


•
White Pearl Crystal Shine uses the same paint film structure as conventional pearl mica, but adopts "Crystal Shine" pigment, consisting of alumina flakes coated with titanium oxide, in place of the conventional mica pigment in the mica base.
•
Compared to conventional mica, the alumina flakes in the "Crystal Shine" pigment have a uniform particle size and are thin with a smooth surface, so there is less cloudiness and a strongly brilliant color is achieved.
