Rear Area

●
In the rear area, the three-dimensional flow from the rear quarter pillars is turned inward by the rear combination lights, creating a smart and stable form, and the raised back door expresses lightness.
●
A large rear spoiler (with high mounted stop light) has been adopted, creating an aggressive sporty feel while giving an intelligent design conscious of its connection with the rear quarter pillars.
●
The back door garnish is mounted flush with the back door for good appearance.
●
The rear bumper guard has a trapezoidal graphic and integrates the rear reflectors, emphasizing stability and width.
