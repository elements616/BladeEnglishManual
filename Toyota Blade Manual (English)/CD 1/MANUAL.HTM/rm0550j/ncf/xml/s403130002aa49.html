Aerodynamic Performance

●
Excellent aerodynamic performance has been achieved through flush surfaces on all parts of the upper body, engine under covers and other parts on the underbody that control the airflow under the floor, and a flat underfloor, ensuring a high level of handling stability and straight-line stability at high speeds.
●
A rear spoiler has been provided at the rear end of the roof panel, with a design matched to the flow from the roof panel, to straighten the airflow and reduce driving resistance.

●
The front fender liners have a tunnel shape that guides the airflow under the vehicle to the brake calipers & rotors, improving brake fade resistance.
●
A recessed shape has been provided on the surface of the fender liner close to the front side of the tire to straighten the airflow in front of the tire at high speeds, improving the steering response.
