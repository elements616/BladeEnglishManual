Cooling Fan System

●
The speed of the 2 cooling fan motors is switched in 2 stages according to the coolant temperature and air conditioner operating status.
●
The 2 motors are connected in series (6V) at Lo and in parallel (12V) at Hi.
●
A system in which the engine control computer directly controls relay ON/OFF has been adopted.
