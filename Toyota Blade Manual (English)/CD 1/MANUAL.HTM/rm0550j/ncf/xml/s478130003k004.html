Anti-chipping Coating

●
An interlayer anti-chipping coating has been applied to the rocker area of standard grades, and rocker protectors have been provided at the bottom of the rockers to prevent damage to the body from chipping* 1while driving.
●
Anti-chipping tape has been provided on the rear of the rear doors, the quarter panels, etc. of cold area specification models for excellent paint film protection and rust prevention against gravel and frozen snow thrown up by the tires. The anti-chipping coated areas have a finish texture (orange peel* 2) different from that of ordinary painted areas.
HINT
*1
Small stones, sand, etc. thrown up mainly by the front wheels hitting the vehicle body while driving.
*2
The painted surface becoming grainy and rough.
