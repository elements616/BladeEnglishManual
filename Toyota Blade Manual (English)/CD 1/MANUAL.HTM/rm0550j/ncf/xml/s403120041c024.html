Intelligent Parking Assist System Construction


Intelligent Parking Assist Components


Components
Main Function
Rear View Monitor Camera (Manufactured by Matsushita Electric Industrial)
Installed on the back door outside garnish; outputs the captured rear view image signal to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Intelligent Parking Assist Computer (Made by Aisin Seiki)
Installed on the front passenger side inside the instrument panel; turns the rear view monitor camera ON/OFF based on vehicle information via CAN communication, IG and signals from the multi-display.
Synthesizes images of each guide line, calculated based on status signals from each computer input via CAN communication, with the rear view image from the rear view monitor camera and outputs them to the HDD navigation system, while outputting control signals to each computer to perform each intelligent parking assist control.
HDD Navigation System (Made by Matsushita Electric Industrial)
Displays the rear view image and each guide line on the screen using video signals from the intelligent parking assist computer.
IPA Ultrasonic Sensor
Emits ultrasonic waves to detect parkable space and outputs the result to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Steering Angle Sensor
Installed inside the steering column; outputs status signals such as steering angle to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Engine Control Computer
Outputs status signals such as shift position, accelerator opening ratio, accelerator sensor malfunction and final target engine speed to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Skid Control Computer
Outputs status signals such as stop light switch, parking switch, vehicle speed and distance traveled to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Electric Power Steering Computer
Outputs status signals such as EPS failure and steering intervention to the intelligent parking assist computer, and controls the steering based on the target steering position signal, steering neutral position signal, IPA control command signal and IPA system failure signal input from the intelligent parking assist computer.
Air Conditioner Computer
Outputs status signals such as ambient temperature and ambient temperature sensor to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Corrects the measurement distance of the IPA ultrasonic sensors according to the ambient temperature.
Height Control Sensor
Outputs changes in vehicle height to the intelligent parking assist computer.
Front Speaker RH
Tweeter RH
Outputs voice guidance for each mode based on the audio signal from the intelligent parking assist computer.

HINT
*
IPA stands for Intelligent Parking Assist.

Related Parts


Rear View Monitor Camera

●
The rear view monitor camera consists of an image input section using an ultra-wide-angle lens and an image signal output section consisting of a compact (1/4 inch) color CCD camera.
●
The image signal output section uses a CCD (Charge Coupled Device) image sensor to convert the image captured (input) by the lens into electrical signals according to light intensity, and outputs them to the intelligent parking assist computer.
●
The output image from the rear view monitor camera is itself reversed to match the view when looking rearward in the inner rear view mirror. Therefore, care is required because the image on the screen is shown laterally reversed compared with looking directly rearward (objects on the left side of the vehicle are displayed on the left side of the screen, and objects on the right side of the vehicle on the right side of the screen).
Intelligent Parking Assist Computer

●
The intelligent parking assist computer controls the intelligent parking assist and back guide monitor.
●
The intelligent parking assist computer is installed under the driver seat and controls the ON/OFF of the rear view monitor camera based on vehicle information via CAN communication and vehicle status signals such as IG. Also, it combines the rear view image from the rear view monitor camera with the video signals of each guide line calculated from the steering angle signal from the steering sensor, and outputs them to the display.
