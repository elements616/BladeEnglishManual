Water Pump

●
The water pump has been made more compact and lighter by forming the volute chamber in the cylinder block.
●
The water pump is driven by the back side of the V-ribbed belt. Also, by setting the belt center at the bearing center, compactness has been achieved and reliability ensured.
●
A stainless steel rotor has been adopted and the vane shape has been optimized, improving efficiency and ensuring reliability.
