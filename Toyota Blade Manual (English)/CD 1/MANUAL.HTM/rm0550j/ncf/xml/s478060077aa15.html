S-VSC(Steering-assisted Vehicle Stability Control)

●
Cooperative control between VSC and the electric power steering system is performed. By integrating these preventive safety functions, excellent driving stability and handling have been ensured.
●
Steering cooperative control is performed during braking/driving on split road surfaces and during oversteer or understeer tendencies.
●
When the vehicle slips and becomes unstable, this function controls the brake fluid pressure of the 4 wheels. At this time, EPS performs steering torque assist control to assist the driver's steering operation.

Control During Acceleration on Split Road Surfaces


•
If the throttle is opened too much when starting off or accelerating on a slippery road surface, etc., the drive wheels may slip due to excessive torque, impairing starting/acceleration performance and handling. Particularly on split road surfaces where the conditions under the left and right drive wheels differ, the vehicle may be pulled toward the side with the lower friction coefficient due to the difference in driving force between left and right. In addition to the conventional TRC function, S-VSC applies steering torque in the direction that cancels out the difference in left and right driving force, assisting the steering effort so that the driver can easily correct the vehicle's course.


Control During Braking on Split Road Surfaces


•
When braking suddenly on a road surface where the slipperiness differs between left and right, such as with the tires on one side on asphalt and the other on snow, the vehicle may be pulled toward the side with the higher friction coefficient due to the difference in braking force between left and right. In addition to the conventional ABS function, S-VSC applies steering torque in the direction that cancels out the difference in left and right braking force, assisting the steering effort so that the driver can easily correct the vehicle's course.


Control During Oversteer Tendency


•
The oversteer tendency is determined from the vehicle slip angle and slip angular velocity, and according to the degree of the tendency, the brakes are applied mainly to the wheels on the outside of the turn to generate an outward moment, suppressing the oversteer tendency. At the same time, an assist torque command in the oversteer correction direction is sent to the EPS, controlling the steering torque so that the driver can steer more easily.


Control During Understeer Tendency


•
The driver's requested yaw rate is calculated from the steering amount and vehicle speed and compared with the actual vehicle yaw rate to determine the understeer tendency. According to the degree of the tendency, engine output is suppressed and the brakes are applied, generating a moment in the same direction as the vehicle's turning direction and suppressing excessive understeer. At the same time, an EPS assist torque command is sent to prevent the driver from oversteering the steering wheel and worsening the understeer tendency.
