Ignition Timing Control (ESA)

●
The engine control computer calculates the optimum ignition timing based on the signals from each sensor and sends an ignition signal (IGt) to the igniter. The initial set ignition timing is BTDC5°.

Knock Control System


•
When knocking is detected, the timing is retarded by a fixed angle at a time, according to the magnitude of the knocking, until knocking no longer occurs.
•
When knocking no longer occurs, the timing is advanced by a fixed angle at a time. If knocking recurs at this time, the timing is retarded again.


Maximum and Minimum Advance Characteristics


•
Because abnormally advanced or retarded ignition timing adversely affects the engine, maximum and minimum advance angles are specified.

Calculation of Ignition Timing


•
Ne/G2Based on the signal, intake air temperature signal, throttle valve opening signal, coolant temperature signal, etc., the engine control computer calculates the optimum ignition timing for the driving conditions and sends an ignition signal to the ignition coil with igniter.

Calculation of Ignition Timing


HINT
Ignition timing = A. Initial set ignition timing or B. Basic advance angle + C. Corrective advance angle
A. Fixed Advance Characteristics
When the engine is started, the timing is fixed at BTDC5°. In addition, when the service terminals are shorted and the throttle is off, the timing is fixed at BTDC10°.
B. Basic Advance Characteristics
The optimum ignition timing is selected from the standard values (map) of the engine control computer based on the air flow meter and Ne signals.
C. Corrective Advance Characteristics
Based on the signals from each sensor, appropriate advance and retard are made according to the engine condition at that time.
C-1 Warm-up Advance Characteristics
When the coolant temperature is low, the ignition timing is advanced according to the driving conditions to improve driveability.
C-2 Idle Stabilization Advance Characteristics
When the idle speed decreases, the ignition timing is advanced to stabilize the idle speed. When the idle speed increases, the timing is retarded.
C-3 Transient Correction Retard
During sudden acceleration at a coolant temperature of 50°C or higher, the ignition timing is retarded to prevent knocking.
C-4 Retard at Fuel Cut Recovery
At fuel cut recovery, the ignition timing is retarded to reduce shock.
C-5 ECT Shift Retard
During A/T shifting, the ignition timing is retarded to reduce shift shock.
C-6 Retard During Acceleration
Driveability is improved by temporarily retarding the ignition timing during acceleration.
C-7 Knock Correction Retard
When knocking occurs, the ignition timing is corrected based on the signal from the knock sensor.



Related Systems

MRE Type Cam Position Sensor Operation


•
The MRE type cam position sensor consists of a magnet and a sensor circuit (IC) with a built-in MRE.
•
In the MRE type cam position sensor, the rotation of the timing rotor changes the magnetic vector (direction of the magnetic field) applied to the MRE in the cam position sensor, which changes the internal resistance of the MRE. This resistance change is converted to voltage, and the output waveform is compared with a threshold value and shaped into a rectangular wave of Hi (approximately 4V) and Lo (approximately 1V), which is output to the engine control computer. This output signal is compared with the Ne signal to detect the actual camshaft angle and identify cylinders.
•
By identifying the position of the timing rotor from the voltage output as Hi/Lo digital signals from when the engine is stopped and from pattern recognition of the peak and valley widths of the timing rotor, the crank position at starting can be detected earlier than with the conventional pickup coil type cam position sensor, improving engine startability.


Fail-safe Operation


•
The accelerator position sensor detects the opening angle with 2 systems: a main circuit and a sub circuit. If a malfunction occurs in the signal, the engine control computer switches to limp mode.
When 1 System Malfunctions


If a malfunction occurs in either circuit, the engine control computer detects the malfunction from the voltage difference between the main circuit and sub circuit. At this time, a limited accelerator opening angle is calculated from the sensor value of 2 circuit, and throttle control is continued, enabling limp mode driving.



When 2 System Malfunctions


The engine control computer detects the malfunction from the voltages of the main circuit and sub circuit and controls the throttle valve opening angle only within the idling range.




Fail-safe Operation


•
The throttle position sensor detects the opening angle with 2 systems: a main circuit and a sub circuit. If a malfunction occurs in the signal, the engine control computer switches to limp mode.
When Malfunction Is Detected


If a malfunction occurs in either or both circuits, the engine control computer detects the malfunction from the voltage difference between the main circuit and sub circuit. At this time, power to the throttle motor is cut and the throttle valve is returned to a predetermined opening angle by the return spring. Limp mode driving is made possible by controlling engine output using fuel injection control and ignition timing control. Also, even if the engine control computer detects another malfunction such as in the throttle motor, limp mode driving is made possible by performing the above control.




Knock Sensor Operation


•
When the knock sensor receives vibration from the cylinder block, the movement of the weight lags due to inertia. As a result, pressure is applied to the piezoelectric ceramic provided between the cylinder block and the weight, generating voltage. The voltage is output almost constantly across all frequency bands, and the engine control computer detects the target frequency and determines that knocking is occurring.


Related Parts


Hot-wire Type Air Flow Meter

●
A compact, lightweight plastic hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter has been adopted. Accordingly, the air cleaner is mounted above the transmission and has been downsized, reducing weight and making it more compact.
●
The hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter passes part of the intake air through the detection section and directly measures the mass flow rate, improving detection accuracy and reducing intake resistance.
●
A bypass flow measurement method, which is less affected by intake pulsation from the engine, has been adopted. Also, low loss is achieved by a flow passage structure with low fluid resistance, allowing air volumes from small to large to be measured reliably.
Hot-wire Type Air Flow Meter Operation


•
Part of the intake air is bypassed and the intake air volume is measured at the hot wire measuring section. The hot wire measuring section uses a platinum filament (platinum hot wire), and a bridge circuit is formed by the hot wires of the intake air temperature measuring resistor and the heating resistor (heater) to measure the engine intake air volume. Due to the characteristics of the hot-wire type air flow meter, the mass flow rate can in principle be measured directly, so density correction for changes in intake air temperature is not necessary; however, intake air temperature information is required for engine controls such as EFI. Therefore, a compact thermistor type intake air temperature sensor is built into the air flow meter to detect the intake air temperature.

When the intake air volume changes, the bridge circuit of the hot wire measuring section feedback-controls the power supplied to the heating resistor so that the temperature difference between the intake air temperature measuring resistor and the heating resistor (heater) is always kept constant. This supplied power is converted into a voltage and output to the engine control computer. Crankshaft Position Sensor (Ne Signal)

●
A highly accurate electromagnetic pickup type sensor has been adopted. Detects the crank position and crank angular velocity.
●
The timing rotor on the crankshaft has 34 signal teeth with 2 teeth missing for top dead center detection. The crank position sensor detects the crank rotation signal every 10° and can accurately detect top dead center from the missing teeth area.
●
As the crankshaft rotates, the air gap between the timing rotor protrusions and the crank position sensor changes, so the magnetic flux passing through the coil of the crank position sensor increases and decreases, generating an electromotive force in the coil. Because this generated voltage is reversed when the timing rotor protrusion approaches and moves away from the cam position sensor, it appears as an AC voltage.

Accelerator Position Sensor

●
A non-contact type accelerator position sensor that uses the Hall effect* 1, which provides an output voltage linear to the throttle valve opening angle, and a Hall element* 2, which provides an output voltage that is linear with respect to the accelerator pedal depression amount, has been adopted. When the accelerator pedal depression amount changes, the angle of the magnetic field relative to the flow of the applied current in the Hall element changes, so the strength of the magnetic field perpendicular to the applied current changes. The resulting change in voltage, generated perpendicular to both the applied current and the magnetic field, is sent to the engine control computer as the accelerator pedal depression amount signal. In addition, reliability is ensured by adopting a 2-system (main and sub) sensor with different output characteristics.
HINT
*1
This is a phenomenon in which, when a current flows through a rod- or plate-shaped conductor or semiconductor and a magnetic field is applied perpendicular to it, a potential difference is generated in the direction perpendicular to both the current and the magnetic field.
*2
This is an electronic component that uses the electromotive force generated by the potential difference in a magnetic field.

Ignition Coil with Igniter

●
By placing a coil independently for each cylinder, the distributor and high-tension cords are eliminated, reducing losses in the high-voltage section, ensuring reliability and reducing radio noise.
●
A compact, lightweight igniter is built in, and a high voltage is induced in the secondary (No. 2) coil by the signal from the engine control computer.
