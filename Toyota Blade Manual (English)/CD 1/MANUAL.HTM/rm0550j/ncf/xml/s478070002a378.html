Electric Power Steering Outline

●
Electric power steering has been adopted on all models.
●
Electric power steering generates assist torque during steering by means of a motor and reduction mechanism mounted on the column shaft, reducing the steering effort. Therefore, because assist force is generated only during steering, excellent fuel economy is achieved.
●
The direction and amount of assist force are determined by the electric power steering computer based on the signal from the torque sensor built into the steering column and the meter vehicle speed signal. Control is performed to reduce the steering effort at low speeds and to provide a firm steering feel at high speeds, ensuring an excellent steering feel.

Features


Item
Features, etc.
Low Fuel Consumption
Whereas hydraulic power steering constantly operates a hydraulic pump using engine power, electric power steering drives the motor only during steering when assist torque is required. As a result, no energy is consumed by the power steering during straight-ahead driving such as at high speeds, achieving excellent fuel economy.
Simplified Construction
A simple configuration has been achieved by building the electric power steering components (motor, reduction mechanism and torque sensor) into the steering column and locating the electric power steering computer at the top of the steering column.
Elimination of Hydraulic System
The hydraulic piping and driving fluid required for hydraulic power steering are no longer needed, making removal and installation of the steering gear easier.
Weight Reduction
Weight reduction has been achieved through the elimination of the piping system and the more compact system.
Steering Feel
By optimizing the assist characteristics based on the meter vehicle speed sensor signal, a steering feel that is light at low speeds and firm at high speeds is achieved.


Related Parts


Torque Sensor

●
A Hall IC type torque sensor is used.
●
The torque sensor is built into the steering column and converts the torque input during steering into an electrical signal. A multi-pole magnet is located on the input shaft (steering wheel side) and a yoke is located on the output shaft (steering gear side), and the input shaft and output shaft are connected via a torsion bar.
●
Coaxially outside the yoke are a flux collecting ring and Hall ICs, which are resin-molded and integrated with the circuit board. When steering torque is applied to the steering wheel, the torsion bar twists, causing relative displacement between the multi-pole magnet and the yoke; the resulting change in magnetic flux density is detected by the Hall ICs and output as a torque signal to the electric power steering computer.
●
The sensing section is a 2-fold redundant system consisting of 2 mutually independent Hall ICs, and diagnosis is performed by monitoring the torque signal values of Hall ICs 1,2.
●
The use of the Hall IC type made it possible to shorten the axial length of the torque sensor, ensuring sufficient steering column contraction for the collision safety mechanism.

Electric Power Steering Computer

●
A function that sounds a buzzer when a system malfunction occurs has been added. When a system malfunction occurs, the multi-buzzer in the combination meter sounds and the electric power steering warning light illuminates to inform the driver of the malfunction.

Fail-safe Function


•
If a malfunction occurs in the system, the fail-safe function operates. The electric power steering warning light in the combination meter illuminates and the buzzer sounds, and the output current is cut off or gradually reduced to switch to manual steering, or the amount of power assist is limited.

Motor & Reduction Mechanism

●
A high-output, low-inertia, low-noise brushless motor is used. The motor is located in the column housing and consists of a rotor, stator, motor shaft, etc.
●
The torque generated in the rotor is transmitted to the worm gear via a joint, and then transmitted via the wheel gear located on the column shaft.
●
The worm gear & wheel gear section is a reduction mechanism with a reduction ratio of 18.5:1, consisting of a metal worm gear and a wheel gear made of high-strength, low-friction resin with excellent wear resistance. A leaf spring is built into the bearing section that supports the worm shaft, achieving excellent reliability against robustness* , deterioration over time and the effects of the operating environment.

*
Preventing the system from becoming unstable due to disturbances and modeling errors
