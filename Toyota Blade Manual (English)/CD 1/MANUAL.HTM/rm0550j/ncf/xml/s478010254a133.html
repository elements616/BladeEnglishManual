Piston

●
The pistons have been made lighter by optimizing the cast-out area above the outside of the pin bosses and the skirt shape. In addition, the area of the piston skirt has been reduced (asymmetrical left and right), and a striated finish and resin coating with excellent seizure resistance have been applied, reducing noise and friction.
●
A tapered squish combustion chamber is provided on the piston top to achieve stable combustion, and together with the pentroof type combustion chamber on the cylinder head side, a high compression ratio has been achieved, providing both high performance and low fuel consumption. In addition, oil jet cooling has been adopted to reduce piston temperature.
●
Friction has been reduced by making each piston ring thinner and lowering its tension.
●
An alumite treatment* is applied to the top ring groove to ensure wear resistance.
HINT
*
Alumite treatment: A process that electrochemically thickens the oxide film formed when aluminum oxidizes. This oxide film is chemically stable and relatively hard, so it is applied to improve wear resistance.
