Steering Gear

●
A lightweight, compact rack & pinion type steering gear with excellent operability has been adopted.
●
The housing is a one-piece aluminum part to reduce weight.
●
The steering gear is mounted to the front suspension member via bushings.
●
A low-friction type rack guide is adopted and the coil spring has been optimized, reducing frictional resistance.
