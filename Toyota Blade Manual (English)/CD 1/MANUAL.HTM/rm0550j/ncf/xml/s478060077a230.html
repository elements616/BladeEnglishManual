Warning Lamp & Indicator

●
Each indicator and warning light is provided in the combination meter.

Warning Lamp & Indicator


Brake warning light
In addition to the normal warnings when the parking brake is ON and when the brake fluid level is low, this light illuminates simultaneously with the ABS warning light to warn the driver that a malfunction has occurred in which not only ABS but also EBD cannot operate.
ABS Warning Light
If a malfunction occurs in the ABS, the light illuminates to inform the driver. In diagnosis mode, the light blinks to output diagnostic trouble codes.
Slip Indicator Light
Blinks to inform the driver that TRC, VSC or hill-start assist control is operating.
Multi-information Display
Malfunctions in the brake, ABS and VSC systems, and driving with the parking brake not released, are communicated to the driver by illuminating the master warning indicator light and displaying a message on the multi-information display.
Master Warning Indicator Light
Illuminates or blinks at the same time as the malfunction is displayed on the multi-information display to warn the driver.
