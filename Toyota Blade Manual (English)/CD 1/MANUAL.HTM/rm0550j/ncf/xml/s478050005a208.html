Lower Arm & Bushings

●
A lower arm consisting of a combination of an arm with a toe adjustment mechanism (lower arm No.1) and a pressed steel plate arm (lower arm No.2), and a pressed steel plate trailing arm are used.
●
For mounting the lower arms to the axle, lower arm No.1 uses a ball joint and lower arm No.2 uses a ball bushing, ensuring high support rigidity.
●
An outer-tube bonded type bushing is used for the front bushing of the trailing arm, ensuring longitudinal compliance for excellent ride comfort.
