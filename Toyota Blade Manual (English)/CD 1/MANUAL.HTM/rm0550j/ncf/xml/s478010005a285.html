Engine Outline

●
The engine lineup consists of 2 types: the 2GR-FE (3.5L gasoline) engine and the 2AZ-FE (2.4L gasoline) engine.
●
With this improvement, the 2AZ-FE engine, based on the Central Environment Council report on tightening of vehicle emission regulations, addresses the change of emission test mode and mandatory advanced on-board diagnostic systems announced by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism in 11 2006 (Arial 18)* , further improving environmental performance.
●
To comply with the new cold test mode (JC08 mode), the engine control computer software of the 2AZ-FE engine was changed and the catalyst specifications were optimized.
●
The 2AZ-FE engine has an enhanced OBD (On-Board Diagnostic System) function that self-diagnoses malfunctions in the emission control system, and a function has been added that automatically detects performance degradation of emission devices such as the catalyst and informs the driver, out of consideration for environmental protection. For details, refer to the Repair Manual.
HINT
*
Mandatory for new models from 10 2008 (Arial 20) and for continued-production and imported vehicles from 9 2010 (Arial 22).
