Features of SRS Airbag System


SRS Airbag System


Each SRS Airbag
When a strong impact that could seriously injure the occupants is received, the bag that acts as a cushion inflates instantly, working together with the restraining action of the seat belts to reduce the impact to the occupants' heads and chests.
Seat Belt with Pretensioner + Force Limiter
When a strong impact is received from the front of the vehicle, the pretensioner mechanism instantly retracts the seat belt to increase occupant restraint.
When a load above the specified value is applied to the seat belt, the force limiter mechanism maintains that specified value, reducing the impact applied to the chest.


SRS Airbag & Seat Belt with Pretensioner System Operation


•
The SRS airbags & seat belts with pretensioner + force limiter operate when an impact above a certain level is detected in a frontal collision.
•
SRS airbag operation assumes a collision with a wall that does not deform or move, at a collision speed of approximately 25km/h or more, within a range of approximately 30° in front.
•
Because the front airbag sensors and the G sensor in the center airbag sensor (computer) detect deceleration during a collision, the airbags may deploy in situations in which a strong impact is received from underneath the vehicle. (When striking a curb, etc., when falling into or driving over a deep hole or ditch, when jumping and striking the ground, when falling off the road, etc.)


Cases in Which the SRS Airbag & Seat Belt with Pretensioner System Does Not Operate


•
Because the front airbag sensors and the G sensor in the center airbag sensor (computer) detect the deceleration in a collision acting from the front of the vehicle, the airbag system may not operate depending on the situation. In addition, even in a frontal collision, the system does not operate if the deceleration does not reach the specified value of the sensors.

SRS Side Airbag & SRS Curtain Shield Airbag System Operation


•
The SRS side airbags operate when the cabin body receives a strong impact from the side of the vehicle and the side airbag sensor and airbag sensor (computer) detect an impact above a certain level. The SRS curtain shield airbags operate when the cabin body receives a strong impact from the side of the vehicle and the side airbag sensor, rear airbag sensor and airbag sensor (computer) detect an impact above a certain level.

Cases in Which the SRS Side Airbag & SRS Curtain Shield Airbag Do Not Operate


•
Even in a side collision, depending on the collision conditions, the collision energy may be reduced by deformation of the vehicle body and doors so that the ignition criteria of the sensors are not reached, and the SRS side airbags & SRS curtain shield airbags may not operate.
