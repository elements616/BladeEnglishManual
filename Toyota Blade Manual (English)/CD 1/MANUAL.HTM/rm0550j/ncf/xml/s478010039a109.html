VVT-i Controller

●
A vane-type VVT-i controller has been provided on the intake camshaft.
●
It consists of a housing fixed to the timing gear and a vane fixed to the camshaft.
●
By applying oil pressure from the OCV (oil control valve) to the advance chamber and retard chamber of the VVT-i controller, the vane is rotated, continuously varying the phase of the intake camshaft.
●
When the engine is stopped, it is locked at the most retarded position by the lock pin. When the engine starts, the lock pin is released by oil pressure.
