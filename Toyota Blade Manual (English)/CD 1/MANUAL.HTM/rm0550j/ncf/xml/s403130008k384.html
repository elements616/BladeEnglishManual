Front Seat Heater

●
Front seat heaters have been provided on "Version L".
●
The seat heater system consists of seat heaters provided in the main portions of the seatback and seat cushion, and seat heater switches provided on the center console box.
●
An indicator lamp is provided on the surface of the seat heater switch, allowing the operating status of the seat heater to be checked.

Seat Heater Operation


•
When the seat heater switch is turned ON, current flows from the thermostat* to the seat cushion and seatback in parallel circuits, warming the seat. The thermostat turns OFF when the seat warms up, cutting off current to the seat heater, and turns ON when the seat temperature drops, resuming current to the seat heater. This operation keeps the seat surface temperature at approximately 30°.
HINT
*
A temperature control device that automatically controls the heat source by means of a switch utilizing the expansion of a bimetal or mercury.
