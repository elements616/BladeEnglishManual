Drive Shaft

●
For the front drive shafts, Rzeppa type constant velocity joints are adopted on the axle side and tripod type on the transaxle side, both RH and LH.
●
For the rear drive shafts, Rzeppa type constant velocity joints are adopted on the axle side and tripod type on the differential side, both RH and LH. In addition, straight-ahead stability is ensured by making the RH and LH rear drive shafts the same length.
