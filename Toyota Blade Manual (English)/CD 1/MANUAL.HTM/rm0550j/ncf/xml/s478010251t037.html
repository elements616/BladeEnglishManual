O2 Sensor Heater Control

●
By passing current through the heater to warm the zirconia element, stable oxygen concentration feedback is performed even when the exhaust gas temperature is low, such as immediately after engine start, reducing emissions.

Hot-wire Type Air Flow Meter

●
A compact, lightweight plastic hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter has been adopted. Accordingly, the air cleaner is mounted above the transmission and has been downsized, reducing weight and making it more compact.
●
The hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter passes part of the intake air through the detection section and directly measures the mass flow rate, improving detection accuracy and reducing intake resistance.
●
A bypass flow measurement method, which is less affected by intake pulsation from the engine, has been adopted. Also, low loss is achieved by a flow passage structure with low fluid resistance, allowing air volumes from small to large to be measured reliably.
Hot-wire Type Air Flow Meter Operation


•
Part of the intake air is bypassed and the intake air volume is measured at the hot wire measuring section. The hot wire measuring section uses a platinum filament (platinum hot wire), and a bridge circuit is formed by the hot wires of the intake air temperature measuring resistor and the heating resistor (heater) to measure the engine intake air volume. Due to the characteristics of the hot-wire type air flow meter, the mass flow rate can in principle be measured directly, so density correction for changes in intake air temperature is not necessary; however, intake air temperature information is required for engine controls such as EFI. Therefore, a compact thermistor type intake air temperature sensor is built into the air flow meter to detect the intake air temperature.

•
When the intake air volume changes, the bridge circuit of the hot wire measuring section feedback-controls the power supplied to the heating resistor so that the temperature difference between the intake air temperature measuring resistor and the heating resistor (heater) is always kept constant. This supplied power is converted into a voltage and output to the engine control computer. The engine control computer calculates the engine intake air volume from the predetermined relationship between the air flow meter output voltage and flow rate.
