Floor & Rear Area

●
Velour needle-punch has been adopted for the front floor carpet.
●
Scuff inside plates / scuff outside plates are provided for the front and rear doors and a deck trim cover for the back door, to prevent body damage and improve appearance.
●
A large jack cover is provided on the deck side trim board (RH) to facilitate removal of the jack. The jack cover also serves as the service hole cover for replacing the rear combination light.
●
The luggage capacity (VDA method* ) is 281L with the full seating capacity occupied, providing excellent storage capacity.
HINT
*
VDA method: The maximum capacity obtained by freely combining 1L (20cm x 10cm x 5cm) boxes in the luggage compartment.

Deck Under Tray


•
The deck under tray consists of fixed deck floor boxes (RH and LH).
•
When the rear seats are stowed by tilting them down, a flat luggage space can be formed by unfolding the deck mat.


Hook


•
A total of 4 deck hooks are provided on the deck sides and rear floor, allowing cargo to be secured with ropes, nets, etc.
•
The hooks are plated for good appearance.


Package Tray


•
The package tray is secured by fitting the pins of the deck side trim boards into the grooves of the package tray, and then the suspenders at both ends of the package tray are hooked onto the hooks on the back door side garnishes, so that the tray moves up and down around the pins in conjunction with opening and closing of the back door, improving luggage usability.
