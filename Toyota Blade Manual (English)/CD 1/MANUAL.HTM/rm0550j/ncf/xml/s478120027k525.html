Manual Leveling Function

●
To prevent glare to oncoming and preceding vehicles, a manual leveling function that allows the beam axis direction to be changed from inside the vehicle according to changes in vehicle posture due to loading, etc. has been adopted on models with halogen headlights.
●
According to the status signal of the headlight leveling switch in the cabin (level 0 for the uppermost direction, level 5 for the lowermost direction), the headlight leveling actuators are driven to move the reflectors up and down and change the direction of the beam axis.
●
The headlight leveling switch is installed on the right side of the steering column in the instrument panel, and the headlight leveling actuators are installed at the rear of the headlights.

■ NOTICE ■
Normally, use the headlight leveling switch in the level 0 position.
If the headlight illumination range is higher than normal due to loading of luggage or passengers, operate the switch according to the luggage and passenger conditions to lower the headlight beam axis. After unloading luggage or passengers, always return the headlight leveling switch to the level 0 position.
Perform beam axis adjustment with the headlight leveling switch at level 0.
