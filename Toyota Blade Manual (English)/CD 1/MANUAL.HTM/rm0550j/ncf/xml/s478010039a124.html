Cylinder Head Related Items

An ultra-lightweight, high-rigidity magnesium alloy cylinder head cover has been adopted. It is installed to the cylinder head with fixed-torque tightening, distributing the tightening load evenly to ensure sealing performance. Furthermore, the baffle plate shape in the PCV chamber has been optimized and the PCV valve flow rate has been optimized, reducing oil carry-over and oil deterioration.
●
The cylinder head cover gasket is made of acrylic rubber with excellent heat resistance, ensuring sufficient reliability. Also, the spark plug tube gaskets have been integrated to improve serviceability.
●
An aluminum alloy cylinder head with excellent thermal conductivity has been adopted. Weight has been reduced by reducing the number of parts and making the walls thinner.
●
A metal 2-layer type cylinder head gasket has been adopted. The oil hole seal bead dimensions and water passage hole shapes have been optimized, and shims have been provided around the cylinder bores to increase the seal surface width, ensuring gas sealing performance and durability.
