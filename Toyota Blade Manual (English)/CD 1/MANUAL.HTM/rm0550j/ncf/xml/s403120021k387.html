Hands-free Calling Function

●
G-BOOK ALPHA, a further evolution of the G-BOOK function, has been adopted to provide excellent functionality.
●
By using the voice recognition microphone, telephone switch and a Bluetooth-compatible cellular phone, convenient functions such as "Hands-free Calling", "HELPNET" and "G-BOOK ALPHA" can be used.
Hands-free Calling


•
Wireless hands-free calling is possible using a Bluetooth-compatible cellular phone.

HINT
Only specified models of cellular phone can be used.
•
After a call is made or received, the conversation takes place through the voice recognition microphone and a vehicle speaker (front speaker RH).


Voice Recognition Microphone
•
The voice recognition microphone is a unidirectional box-type microphone and is installed at the rear of the front personal lamp.


Talk Switch/Telephone Switch
•
The talk switch and telephone switch are located on the right side of the steering pad, allowing switch operation without taking the hands off the steering wheel.

Outgoing Calls


•
When the vehicle is stopped, pressing the off-hook switch of the telephone switch, or touching "TEL" on the information mode screen displayed by pressing the "Information/G" panel switch, changes the screen to the telephone number input screen, from which calls can be made by the following methods.
Telephone Screen




Dialing Methods


Item
Method
Dial Call
Enter a telephone number to make a call.

Call up a telephone number from the phonebook to make a call.

Select a pre-registered telephone number (one-touch dial) to make a call.
History Call
Call up a number previously called or a number from which a call was previously received to make a call.

HINT
In addition to the above basic calling functions, calls can be made from the navigation screen (when telephone number data is available) and by voice recognition, allowing calls to be made in various situations.


Incoming Calls


•
When a call is received, a ringtone sounds* and the incoming call screen is displayed. When the ringtone is output, the audio sound and navigation voice guidance are muted.
HINT
*
With some cellular phones, the ringtone does not sound.
•
A call can be answered by touching "Talk (telephone mark)" on the incoming call screen or by pressing the off-hook switch of the telephone switch.

Setting


•
By touching "Settings" on the telephone number input screen, the screen changes to the telephone setting screen, where various hands-free settings can be made.
