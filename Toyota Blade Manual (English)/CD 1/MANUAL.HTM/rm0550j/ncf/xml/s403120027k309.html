Headlamp (Discharge Type)

●
Projector-type discharge headlights have been adopted for the low beam headlights.
●
Except for the low beam headlights, the construction is basically the same as the halogen headlights.
●
A headlight leveling actuator for the automatic leveling function is installed at the rear of the low beam headlight in the headlight. Also, a light control computer that controls the lighting of the discharge headlight is built into the lower part of the headlight.
●
The extension has been aluminum vapor-deposited. Also, smoke coating has been applied to some parts to create a sharp look.

Discharge Headlight Features


•
The discharge headlight is a headlight that uses an HID bulb (high intensity discharge lamp) as its light source.
•
The light emitted from the HID bulb is a natural daylight color close to sunlight and illuminates a wider and more distant area ahead, helping to improve driver visibility; in addition, its low power consumption reduces the load on the battery and the bulb has a long service life.
•
The discharge headlight achieves instant lighting of the HID bulb and a stable power supply, and has a compact, lightweight light control computer; it is also combined with an automatic leveling function that keeps the vertical beam axis constant as a device to prevent dazzling oncoming vehicles.

HID Bulb Construction


•
The arc tube of the HID bulb is filled with a rare gas (xenon gas) and metal iodides (zinc, sodium and scandium), and arc discharge occurs in the arc tube due to the voltage applied to the base side and lead wire side.
•
UV-cut glass that absorbs ultraviolet rays is used for the outer tube of the arc tube, keeping ultraviolet radiation to a level equivalent to that of halogen bulbs.
