Intake Manifold

●
The shape of the intake manifold and the port length have been optimized to improve output characteristics, reduce vibration and improve quietness.
●
A plastic intake manifold, with its components integrally formed by fitting and vibration welding, has been adopted. The use of plastic reduces intake air temperature and improves the surface roughness inside the ports, resulting in higher output and significant weight and size reductions.
●
Larger port diameters, longer ports and a funnel shape at each port inlet have increased torque at low to medium speeds and increased output.

Throttle Body Gasket with Mesh


•
A gasket with mesh has been adopted between the throttle body and intake manifold. It breaks up the pulsation of the intake air, helping to straighten the intake air flow in the intake manifold.
