Intelligent Parking Assist System Handling Precautions

●
Observe the following when using the intelligent parking assist system.
General Precautions for Using Intelligent Parking Assist


•
Do not rely too much on intelligent parking assist. Perform reversing operations carefully, the same as for vehicles without intelligent parking assist.
•
Reverse slowly while adjusting the speed by depressing the brake.
•
If the vehicle is likely to contact surrounding vehicles or people, depress the brake to stop and cancel intelligent parking assist with the "Cancel" button on the screen.
•
Use the system in the correct driving posture, taking care that neckties, pendants, arms, etc. do not get caught in the rotating steering wheel and that long fingernails do not contact it. Also, keep children away from the steering wheel.
•
Use only in flat parking lots with parking frames.
•
Never reverse while looking only at the screen. The image displayed on the screen may differ from the actual conditions, and reversing while looking only at the screen may result in a collision or an unexpected accident. When reversing, always use the inner rear view mirror and outer mirrors as well, and directly confirm that it is safe behind and around the vehicle.
•
Do not subject the rear view monitor camera to strong impacts or force. The camera direction may be misaligned, causing malfunctions. Special tools are required to adjust the camera direction, so always have it done at a dealer.
•
Use with the back door securely closed.
•
Do not use when the road surface is slippery (icy or snowy roads, etc.) or when tire chains are used. Doing so may result in a collision or an unexpected accident.

Display Precautions Due to Road Surface Conditions


•
Note that due to road surface gradients or unevenness, errors may occur between the distances of the distance guide lines and the estimated course and the actual distances and course on the road surface.

Display Precautions When Obstacles Are Three-dimensional Objects




Since the distance guide lines and estimated course lines are based on the road surface, note the following points when there are three-dimensional objects (walls, vehicles, etc.) behind the vehicle.
•
When the estimated course lines pass near a three-dimensional object, contact may actually occur even if it appears on the screen that there will be no contact, as shown in the illustration below.

•
Distance intervals differ between three-dimensional objects (vehicles, etc.) and flat objects (road surface, etc.) shown on the screen. As shown in the illustration, A and B are actually at the same distance and C is farther than A and B, but they appear on the screen in the order B, C, A, so reversing to the position of B on the screen will result in contact with the three-dimensional object.

•
On slippery road surfaces such as snowy roads, errors may occur between the estimated course lines and the actual course.
•
When there is a parking space line on only one side, the lines may appear parallel on the screen even though they are not actually parallel.

Display Precautions for Using Parallel Parking Assist


•
Even when parallel parking with parallel parking assist, the vehicle may hit something depending on the operating method, road conditions at the time of parking, road surface conditions and the condition of the vehicle, so always drive while directly checking the safety behind and around the vehicle.
•
Use parallel parking assist when parking on a flat, straight road. Do not use it when the parking position is on a curve or slope. Doing so may result in a collision or an unexpected accident.
•
The space required for parking varies slightly depending on vehicle conditions (number of occupants, load, etc.) and road conditions (gradient, unevenness, etc.).

Other Precautions


•
After battery replacement or when battery capacity has decreased, "System initializing" is displayed on the screen to indicate that the system is initializing; touching the "Help" switch on the system initialization screen switches to the help screen, which explains how to complete initialization (turn the steering wheel fully to both the left and right on a flat road). When initialization is completed, the normal display returns.

•
Do not use tires other than those installed at the factory. The system may not operate properly. Also, replacing tires may cause errors in the display positions of the lines and frames displayed on the screen, so consult a Toyota dealer when replacing tires.
•
When reversing the vehicle, always directly confirm with your own eyes that it is safe behind and around the vehicle. Never reverse while looking at the screen.
•
The range shown by the rear view monitor camera is limited, and objects near either corner of the bumper or under the bumper are not shown.
•
Since the rear view monitor camera uses a special lens, the distance intervals shown on the screen differ from the actual distances.
•
The screen may be difficult to see when sunlight or headlight beams shine directly on the lens, in dark places (at night, etc.), or when the temperature near the lens is high or low, but this is not a malfunction.
•
If the glass in front of the lens is dirty, a clear image cannot be obtained. Remove any water droplets, snow, mud, etc. that adhere to it.
•
When a high-brightness point (such as sunlight reflected off the bumper) appears on the screen, the smear phenomenon* characteristic of CCD cameras may occur.
HINT
*
A phenomenon in which a high-brightness point (such as the sun reflected on the vehicle body) captured by the camera trails above and below it (vertically).
