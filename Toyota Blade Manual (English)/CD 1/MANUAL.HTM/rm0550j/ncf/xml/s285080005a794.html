Air Conditioner Control System

●
Full automatic air conditioner control is performed by the air conditioner computer installed in the instrument panel (below the air conditioning unit).
●
The air conditioner computer functions as one of the computers making up the CAN* communication system; in addition to receiving sensor and switch signals via dedicated lines, it receives signals required for air conditioner control from the engine control computer and meter computer via communication lines, performing precise air conditioner control according to vehicle conditions. Similarly, the air conditioner computer also outputs air conditioning related signals to other computers via communication lines.
●
As a method of advanced air conditioning control matched to the occupants' sensations, automatic air conditioner control using a neural network has been adopted, enabling comfortable air conditioning.
●
By adopting a customize function using the diagnostic tool (TaSCAN), DTC output, various data output, etc. can be performed.
●
Panel diagnosis by operation and display on the air conditioner control section has been adopted.
HINT
*
CAN is an abbreviation for Controller Area Network, a serial communication conforming to ISO (International Organization for Standardization) standards.

Related Parts


Air Conditioner Control Panel

●
A push type air conditioner control panel is provided as standard in the integration panel.
●
A pollen removal switch has been provided in the air conditioner control panel. Pressing the switch activates pollen removal mode, quickly removing pollen around the face.
●
On G grade models with the independent right/left temperature control system (dual mode), separate temperature setting switches for the driver side and front passenger side are provided on the air conditioner control section, together with a DUAL switch for switching from linked control to independent right/left temperature control.

Servo Motor

●
Servo motors for switching the fresh/recirculation damper, the mode damper and the driver side air mix damper have been adopted on all models. In addition, a servo motor for switching the front passenger side air mix damper has been adopted on models w/ dual mode automatic air conditioner. Each servo motor is a pulse pattern type that detects position by pulse signals. With the adoption of pulse pattern type servo motors, a smart connector with a built-in IC having functions such as communication with the air conditioner computer is used.
●
The servo motors for the mode damper and the driver side air mix damper are installed on the right side of the heater & cooler unit, the servo motor for the front passenger side air mix damper on the left side of the heater & cooler unit, and the servo motor for the fresh/recirculation damper on the right side of the blower unit.

Pulse Pattern Type Servo Motor


•
Whereas the conventional type detects position by potentiometer voltage, the pulse pattern type servo motor detects relative position by 2-bit ON/OFF signals. The air conditioner computer counts the number of pulses (patterns) to determine the stop position.


Smart Connector


•
Smart connectors, intended to reduce weight and wiring, have been adopted to drive the pulse pattern type servo motors for air conditioner control. The smart connector contains an IC that combines communication with the air conditioner computer, servo motor drive and position detection functions.
