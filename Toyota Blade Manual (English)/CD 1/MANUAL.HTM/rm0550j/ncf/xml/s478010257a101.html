Oil Pump

●
The oil pump is a compact, high-efficiency, multi-tooth cycloid rotor type driven directly by the crankshaft and is located on the timing chain cover. The discharge volume has been optimized, and the drive rotor has a spigotless construction, reducing the rotor diameter and friction loss.
●
A relief valve is built in, and a bypass passage is provided so that relieved oil does not return directly to the oil pan, suppressing fluctuations in the oil level in the oil pan and reducing friction.
●
FIPG (formed in place gasket) with excellent sealing performance is used on the mounting surfaces with the cylinder block and oil pan to ensure reliability.
