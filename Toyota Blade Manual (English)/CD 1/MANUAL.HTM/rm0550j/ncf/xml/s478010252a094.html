Fuel tank

●
A fuel tank with a capacity of 60L has been installed under the floor of the first and second seats, achieving a spacious interior.
●
The material is aluminum-plated steel sheet, and the use of lead has been eliminated in consideration of environmental impact.
●
A fuel drain mark has been provided on the bottom surface of the fuel tank to clarify and simplify positioning of the fuel draining device during dismantling and to ensure reliable fuel draining.
●
By adopting an aluminum heat insulator, fuel temperature rise has been suppressed and weight has been reduced.
