Pedestrian Injury Reduction Body

●
A "pedestrian injury reduction body", designed to reduce injury to pedestrians in the event of a collision with a pedestrian, has been adopted. Impact absorbing structures are used around the cowl panel and hood panel, creating a body structure that reduces the impact on a pedestrian's head, etc.
●
The hood panel has an inner panel shape (longitudinal frame structure) that readily transmits impact in the longitudinal direction, reducing the impact on the head, etc. in a collision with a pedestrian. In addition, a dent reinforcement has been provided at the hood lock reinforcement to create a collision energy absorbing structure and to ensure sufficient stroke in a collision with a pedestrian. The mounting position of the hood striker has been revised to ensure energy absorbing space for the hood.
●
An open cross-section structure has been adopted for the cowl, and the front wall of the cowl louver has a cantilever structure, increasing the energy absorption efficiency in the expected head impact direction and reducing injury values. In addition, at the wiper pivots, an energy absorbing space is provided between the wipers and the cowl top outer front panel, and the pivots move downward in a collision, reducing the impact on a pedestrian's head.

●
A foam bead energy absorber has been provided in front of the front bumper reinforcement and a resin engine under center cover below the radiator support, creating a structure that prevents a pedestrian from being drawn under the vehicle in a collision and reduces the impact on the lower limbs.
