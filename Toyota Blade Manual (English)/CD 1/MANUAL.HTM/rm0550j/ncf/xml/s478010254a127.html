Cylinder Head

●
A lightweight, highly rigid aluminum alloy cylinder head has been adopted. In addition, the VVT-i oil supply passage has been separated from the cylinder head, simplifying the oil passages in the cylinder head.
●
A 2-piece construction with a separate cam journal section has been adopted, simplifying the construction of the cylinder head body, reducing weight and improving serviceability.
●
A pentroof type combustion chamber has been adopted, with the spark plug located almost at the center of the combustion chamber to improve combustion efficiency and knock resistance. In addition, the valve included angle has been narrowed to 25.6°, making the combustion chamber compact.
●
An efficient cross-flow layout, with the intake on the inside of the V-bank and the exhaust on the outside, has been adopted, and the intake and exhaust ports have a siamese construction.
●
The intake port shape has been optimized to reduce pressure loss and increase the intake air volume. In addition, the cross-sectional area of the intake port gradually decreases toward the combustion chamber to increase the flow velocity. The shape of the intake port branch is designed to reduce adhesion of injected fuel to the port walls, also reducing emissions.
●
By adopting long-reach type spark plugs and providing a water jacket between the exhaust port and the spark plug thread, the cooling performance around the spark plugs has been improved, improving knock resistance and combustion.
●
Plastic region tightening has been adopted for the cylinder head bolts to stabilize the axial force.
