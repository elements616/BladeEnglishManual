Cooling Fan and Fan Shroud

●
The cooling fan rotates the main fan in the reverse direction to optimize the air flow in the engine room, suppressing the rise in intake air temperature and improving engine performance, especially at low speeds.
●
The fan shroud is made of resin and is integrated RH and LH, reducing weight and size.
●
A 2-stage controlled electric fan system has been adopted. Cooling performance is adjusted by switching the 2 cooling motors in 2 stages according to the coolant temperature and air conditioner operating status.
