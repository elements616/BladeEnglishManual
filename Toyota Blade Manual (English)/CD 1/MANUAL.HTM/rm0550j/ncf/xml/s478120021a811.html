HDD Navigation Audio Mode Functions



Audio Mode Screen
●
By selecting the "Audio" button on the start screen while using the audio, the screen changes to the audio mode screen, where the operating status and operation of music sources (AM/FM radio, CD, etc.) and audio adjustments such as sound quality and volume balance can be performed.
●
Music sources have been enhanced by supporting WMA (Windows Media Audio)/MP3 (MPEG1 Audio Layer3) and adopting a sound library that allows music CDs to be recorded on the built-in HDD.



Sound Library
●
Up to 2000 songs from music CDs* can be recorded on the built-in HDD. Recorded music is managed in 4 play modes (artist, recording date, genre, my best), and the desired music can be easily searched for and played from these.
HINT
*
Depending on the recording time, recording quality, free space on the HDD, etc., it may not be possible to record 2000 songs.
