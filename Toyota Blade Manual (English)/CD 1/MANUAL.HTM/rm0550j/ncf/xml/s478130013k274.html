Floor & Rear Area

●
The shapes of the deck side trim, deck mat and deck floor box have been revised, and the jack storage location has been moved into the deck floor box, enlarging the luggage room capacity.
●
Using the VDA method* , the luggage room secures 286L with the full seating capacity occupied, providing excellent storage capability.
HINT
*
A method of measuring luggage room volume using boxes of 1L (20cm×10cm×5cm).
Deck Under Tray


•
The shapes of the RH and LH deck floor boxes have been changed, enabling jack storage in the RH side of the deck floor box. In addition, a separate small article storage space has been secured on the LH side of the deck floor box and a cover has been newly provided, giving consideration to convenience.


Hook


•
A total of 4 deck hooks are provided on the deck sides and rear floor, allowing cargo to be secured with ropes, nets, etc.
•
The hooks are plated for good appearance.


Package Tray


•
The package tray is secured by fitting the pins of the deck side trim boards into the grooves of the package tray, and then the suspenders at both ends of the package tray are hooked onto the hooks on the back door side garnishes, so that the tray moves up and down around the pins in conjunction with opening and closing of the back door, improving luggage usability.
