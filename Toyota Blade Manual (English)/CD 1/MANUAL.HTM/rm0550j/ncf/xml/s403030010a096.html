Automatic Transaxle Outline

●
On 2AZ-FE engine models, the K112 automatic transaxle "Super CVT-i" continues to be used on 2WD models, and the K112F on 4WD models.
●
The U660E super intelligent 6-speed automatic "6 Super ECT" has been adopted on 2GR-FE engine models.
●
The U660E super intelligent 6-speed automatic is an innovative 6-speed automatic transaxle that achieves a dramatic improvement in power performance, excellent fuel economy, smooth shift feel and quietness through the adoption of a high-efficiency compact torque converter, a compact and lightweight 6-speed gear train and a high-precision hydraulic control system.
