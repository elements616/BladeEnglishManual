Radar Cruise Control System (Vehicle-to-vehicle Distance Control Mode) Handling Precautions

●
Use the system on expressways and motorways where it is easy to maintain the vehicle-to-vehicle distance from the preceding vehicle.
●
Because there are limits to vehicle-to-vehicle distance control, do not rely too much on radar cruise control system control (vehicle-to-vehicle distance control mode).
●
When driving, always pay attention to the distance to the preceding vehicle and the surrounding conditions, and depending on the situation, depress the brake pedal to decelerate or depress the accelerator pedal to accelerate, maintaining a safe distance from the preceding and following vehicles and driving safely.
●
This is not a system that automatically operates the brakes until the vehicle stops.
●
Although the brakes are operated automatically, there are limits to deceleration control, and if the preceding vehicle decelerates sharply or another vehicle cuts in front of the vehicle, the vehicle may not decelerate sufficiently and may approach the preceding vehicle. In this case, a warning is given by the multi-information display and the skid control buzzer.
●
This system does not compensate for inattention to the road ahead, such as looking aside or absent-minded driving.
●
Do not use the radar cruise control system in situations such as the following. Doing so may lead to an unexpected accident. In addition, when the system determines that the weather is bad, radar cruise control system control may be canceled automatically.
●
In bad weather (rain, fog, snow, sandstorms, etc.)
• The distance to the preceding vehicle may not be measured accurately. When the wipers are operated at HI, the radar cruise control system is canceled automatically and enters set standby.

●
When a large amount of raindrops, snow, etc. adheres to the front grille and millimeter wave radar sensor assembly
• The distance to the preceding vehicle may not be measured accurately.

●
Roads with heavy traffic or sharp curves
• The vehicle cannot be driven at a speed suited to the road conditions, which may lead to an unexpected accident

●
Slippery road surfaces such as icy or snow-covered roads
• The tires may spin and control of the vehicle may be lost.

●
Steep downhill slopes
• When there is no preceding vehicle, engine braking is insufficient and the set speed may be exceeded, which may lead to an unexpected accident. (Brake control is not performed in such cases.) In addition, even when there is a preceding vehicle and follow-up control is being performed, deceleration may be delayed, which may lead to an unexpected accident.

●
In traffic conditions with frequent acceleration and deceleration
• The vehicle cannot be driven at a speed suited to the traffic conditions, which may lead to an unexpected accident.

●
When entering an interchange, parking area, service area, etc. on an expressway (leaving the main lane)
• If the vehicle was following a vehicle slower than the set speed on the main lane using the radar cruise control system, the preceding vehicle disappears when the vehicle leaves the main lane and the vehicle accelerates to the set speed, which may lead to an unexpected accident.

●
Roads with repeated steep uphill and downhill slopes
• The preceding vehicle may not be detected and the vehicle may come too close to it, which may lead to an unexpected accident.

●
Because this system performs control mainly by detecting radio waves reflected from the rear end of vehicles, in the following cases it may not detect accurately and may not maintain an appropriate vehicle-to-vehicle distance.
When the preceding vehicle or vehicles in other lanes are kicking up water or snow from the road.
When the rear end area of the preceding vehicle is extremely small, such as an empty trailer.
When extremely heavy cargo is loaded in the trunk room or rear seat and the vehicle is tilted significantly.

●
The millimeter wave radar sensor assembly has a function that automatically determines dirt on its front surface and informs the driver, but it is not infallible. Depending on the situation, dirt on the front of the sensor may not be detected. In addition, it may not be detected when a snack bag with aluminum foil sticks to it or when ice, icicles, ice and snow, etc. adhere to it.
• In such situations, an appropriate vehicle-to-vehicle distance may not be maintained, so always pay attention to the road ahead. When dirt is detected, the radar cruise control system is canceled automatically. Always keep the front of the sensor and the radar-transmissive grille cover clean.

●
Depending on the road shape (curves, roads with consecutive left and right curves, curve entrances and exits, roads with narrow lanes due to construction or lane restrictions, etc.) and the condition of the vehicle (steering operation, position in the lane, unstable driving due to an accident or malfunction, etc.), vehicles in the adjacent lane or surrounding objects may be detected temporarily, causing control or the approach warning to operate, or the preceding vehicle may temporarily not be detected and the vehicle may approach it.
●
Neither radar cruise control system control nor the approach warning operates for stopped vehicles or vehicles that are extremely slower than the vehicle. Pay close attention to vehicles stopped at toll gates or at the end of traffic jams, extremely slow vehicles, etc.
●
Because the detection range of the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly is narrow at short distances, detection of a preceding vehicle cutting in at close range may be delayed, or a motorcycle traveling at the edge of the lane may not be detected, and an appropriate vehicle-to-vehicle distance may not be maintained.
●
When not using the radar cruise control system, keep the main switch OFF. The radar cruise control system may be operated accidentally, which may lead to an unexpected accident.
●
When following a vehicle, the vehicle speed is controlled to match the preceding vehicle, so the vehicle does not accelerate even if the set speed is increased with the + RES switch. However, because the set speed has been increased at this time, the vehicle will continue to accelerate after the preceding vehicle is no longer present. Check the set vehicle speed with the set vehicle speed display in the multi-information display.
●
Select a vehicle-to-vehicle distance suited to the traffic conditions. The approximate controlled vehicle-to-vehicle distances are as follows.
• When driving at a vehicle speed of approximately 80km/h, the approximate set vehicle-to-vehicle distances are: "Long" approximately 50m, "Middle" approximately 40m, "Short" approximately 30m. The lower the vehicle speed, the shorter the vehicle-to-vehicle distance becomes compared with the above.

●
On long downhill slopes, etc., the controlled vehicle-to-vehicle distance may become shorter than the set distance.
●
Do not use the radar cruise control system in situations in which the skid control buzzer sounds frequently.
●
Even when the vehicle-to-vehicle distance is short, the skid control buzzer may not sound in cases such as the following.
When the relative speed with the preceding vehicle is small (when traveling at almost the same speed)
When the preceding vehicle is faster than the vehicle (when the vehicle-to-vehicle distance gradually increases)
Immediately after the set operation
When the accelerator pedal is depressed and immediately after the accelerator pedal is released

●
Normally, during follow-up driving under radar cruise control system control (vehicle-to-vehicle distance control mode), the vehicle automatically accelerates and decelerates to match the speed of the preceding vehicle, but when acceleration is necessary for a lane change, etc., or when the vehicle is likely to approach the preceding vehicle because the preceding vehicle decelerates suddenly or another vehicle cuts in, depress the accelerator pedal or brake pedal to accelerate or decelerate.
●
If, while driving with the radar cruise control system, a chime sounds and the CRUISE operation/warning indicator light in the combination meter assembly blinks, the master warning indicator light illuminates and a warning item is displayed on the multi-information display, turn the main switch OFF once and then set the system again.
• If the system cannot be set even after performing the above operation, or is canceled immediately after being set, a system malfunction is suspected. There is no problem with normal driving, but have the vehicle inspected at a dealer.

●
For correct operation of the radar cruise control system, observe the following.
Always keep the front of the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly clean.
When cleaning, use a soft cloth, etc. so as not to scratch the window.

●
Do not apply strong impacts to the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly or the surrounding area. Do not disassemble it. Doing so may cause a malfunction or incorrect operation. In addition, it may no longer comply with the standards of the Radio Law.
●
Because special equipment is required for beam axis adjustment, always have the beam axis adjusted at a dealer.
●
If the front grille is damaged, the system may not operate properly due to a sensor malfunction, etc.
●
Do not attach stickers or accessories around the front grille (radar-transmissive grille cover). In addition, do not modify or paint the front grille (radar-transmissive grille cover) or replace it with anything other than genuine parts. Doing so will cause the radar to malfunction.
●
The control methods differ between vehicle-to-vehicle distance control mode and constant speed control mode. When using the radar cruise control system, always check which mode is selected.
●
When driving in places where strong external radio waves (illegal radio waves, etc.) are received, the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly may malfunction and the warning may illuminate temporarily.
