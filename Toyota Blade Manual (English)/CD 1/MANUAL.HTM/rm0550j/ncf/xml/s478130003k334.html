Radiator Grille & Emblem

●
The radiator grille of the "G" and "Blade" consists of 4 horizontal bars, with the top bar chrome-plated and the other 3 bars smoke-painted. In addition, the 3 horizontal bars other than the top bar are widened to the full width of the frame and project toward the front of the vehicle, emphasizing the protrusion of the center portion.
●
On the "Blade S Package" and "Master G", the mesh-based portion is dark gray metallic and the outer frame is chrome-plated, creating a sense of luxury. In addition, a chrome-plated molding has been added to the upper part on the "Master G", creating a sense of luxury.
●
The emblem has a vertical orientation, enhancing the vehicle's class. In addition, a round millimeter wave transmitting grille cover is provided on "Master G" models w/ pre-crash safety system. Optimal placement of the millimeter wave radar sensor made it possible to downsize the millimeter wave transmitting grille cover, achieving a design that does not look out of place compared to the vertically oriented standard specification.
