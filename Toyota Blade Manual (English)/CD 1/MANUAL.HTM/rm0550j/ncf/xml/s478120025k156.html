Occupant Detection Sensor

●
When an occupant sits in the front passenger seat and a load is applied to the seat cushion* , the electrodes in the occupant detection sensor provided under the seat cushion cover come into contact, thereby detecting the occupant.
HINT
*
If a load above a certain level is applied to the front passenger seat cushion, for example by placing luggage on it, the sensor may detect this.
