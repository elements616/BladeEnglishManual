Compressor Control

●
Refrigerant pressure malfunction determination, external variable compressor capacity control, etc. are used as compressor control.
Target Evaporator Fin Temperature


•
While maintaining the required outlet temperature, the air conditioning computer calculates the target evaporator fin temperature required to prevent discomfort due to the temperature difference between the inside and outside of the cabin and cabin humidity, and to prevent window fogging. In addition, it calculates target evaporator fin temperatures that take into account discomfort caused by excessively high temperatures from each outlet in FACE and BI-LEVEL outlet modes and quick defogging in DEF mode, and by comparing these, determines the target evaporator fin temperature that minimizes power consumption according to the vehicle condition. By using this for compressor control, both necessary and sufficient cooling capacity and low fuel consumption are achieved.

Refrigerant Pressure Malfunction Determination


•
The air conditioning computer detects the refrigerant pressure, and when a refrigerant pressure malfunction is input, it turns the compressor OFF during that time.

Evaporator Fin Sensor Determination


•
When the temperature detected by the evaporator fin sensor is 1°C or less, the air conditioning computer turns the compressor OFF to prevent frosting of the evaporator.

Frost Control


•
The air conditioning computer detects the evaporator fin sensor temperature and refrigerant pressure, calculates the compressor capacity control output and performs frost control. When frost is determined, the air conditioning computer outputs a low compressor operation variable value to prevent frosting of the evaporator.

Compressor 60-second ON Control (Window Fogging Prevention Control)


•
After the engine is started, when the conditions for turning the compressor ON are met (operation for the 1 time after the air conditioning computer is reset, blower OFF by warm-up control, evaporator fin sensor determination, outside air temperature determination, etc.), the air conditioning computer turns the compressor ON for 60 seconds according to the variable capacity control value. If even 1 of the above conditions is not met, the compressor 60-second ON control is canceled. In addition, when the condition for turning the compressor operating capacity OFF by refrigerant pressure malfunction determination is met, the compressor is turned OFF even during the 60 seconds.

External Variable Compressor Capacity Control


•
The lowest of the compressor capacity control outputs calculated from frost control through low speed cut control is used as the compressor operating capacity to perform external variable compressor capacity control. This allows efficient compressor control according to the A/C operating condition, vehicle driving condition, etc., improving drive feeling and fuel economy.

Starting/Passing Acceleration Control


•
During acceleration, when cut signal 1 (during starting acceleration) or cut signal 2 (during passing acceleration) is input from the engine control computer to the air conditioning computer, the air conditioning computer reduces the compressor operating capacity output as shown in the illustration, reducing the fuel injection volume during acceleration and improving acceleration performance. The amount by which the compressor operating capacity output is reduced is varied according to the cooling load so that no difference in perceived temperature is felt.
