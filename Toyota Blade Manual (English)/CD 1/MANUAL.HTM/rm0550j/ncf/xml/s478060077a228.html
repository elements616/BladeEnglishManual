Front Speed Sensor & Rotor

●
A semiconductor type speed sensor is mounted to the steering knuckle, and a magnetic rotor is mounted to the inner race of the hub bearing.

Detection Principle


•
The magnetic rotor consists of rubber filled with magnetic powder, with N and S poles evenly arranged in the circumferential direction (48 poles each for S and N).
•
When the magnetic rotor rotates, changes in the magnetic field are produced; these are detected by the active sensor and output as vehicle speed pulses.
•
Compared with the commonly used passive sensor, detection is possible from a vehicle speed of approximately 0km/h.
