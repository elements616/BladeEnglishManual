Seat Belt Warning System

●
A seat belt warning system, which reminds the driver and front passenger to fasten their seat belts, has been adopted. The warning indicator light for the driver seat is located in the combination meter, and that for the front passenger seat is located in the air conditioning control panel.
●
An occupant detection sensor, which detects the presence of an occupant, has been provided under the front passenger seat cushion cover.
Seat Belt Warning Function



Driver Side Seat Belt Warning

•
When the power switch is turned ON, the driver side seat belt warning indicator light starts blinking to remind the driver to fasten the seat belt.
•
When it is detected that the driver side seat belt has been fastened (tongue plate locked into the driver side seat belt inner buckle), the warning indicator light goes off.


Front Passenger Side Seat Belt Warning

•
When the occupant detection sensor detects a front passenger when the power switch is turned ON, the front passenger side seat belt warning indicator light starts blinking to remind the front passenger to fasten the seat belt.
•
When it is detected that the front passenger side seat belt has been fastened (tongue plate locked into the front passenger side seat belt inner buckle), the warning indicator light goes off.

Seat Belt Warning Operation



Driver Side Seat Belt Warning

•
When the power switch is ON and the driver side seat belt is not fastened (tongue plate not locked into the buckle), the main body ECU receives driver side seat belt buckle switch ON.
•
When the meter computer receives the driver side seat belt buckle switch ON signal from the main body ECU as a CAN communication signal, it drives the LED driver to blink the LED for the driver side seat belt warning indicator.
•
When the driver side seat belt is fastened (tongue plate locked into the buckle) and driver side seat belt buckle switch OFF is received, the meter computer turns off the LED for the driver side seat belt warning indicator.


Front Passenger Side Seat Belt Warning

•
When the power switch is ON, a front passenger is seated and the front passenger side seat belt is not fastened (tongue plate not locked into the buckle), the meter computer receives occupant detection sensor ON and front passenger side seat belt buckle switch ON. As a result, the meter computer blinks the LED for the front passenger side seat belt warning indicator.
•
When the occupant detection sensor turns OFF, or the front passenger side seat belt is fastened (tongue plate locked into the buckle) and front passenger side seat belt buckle switch OFF is received, the meter computer turns off the LED for the front passenger side seat belt warning indicator.
