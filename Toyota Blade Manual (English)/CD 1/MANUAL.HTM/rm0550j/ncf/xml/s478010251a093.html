Fuel Injector

●
A 12-hole ultra-fine atomization fuel injector has been adopted. The injector is mounted directly on the cylinder head, and the tip of the fuel injector protrudes into the intake port, shortening the distance to the combustion chamber. This reduces fuel adhesion to the intake port, achieving low fuel consumption and low emissions.
