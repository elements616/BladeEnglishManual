Piston and Crank Related Items

●
Lightweight, high-rigidity aluminum alloy pistons have been adopted.
●
Sintered forged connecting rods with high strength and little weight variation have been adopted to ensure high reliability. The connecting rod caps are tightened by nutless plastic region tightening, reducing the size of the big end.
●
A forged 4-journal, 5-balance weight type crankshaft has been adopted, reducing weight while reducing vibration and noise through optimum placement of the balance weights. In addition, the fillets are induction hardened to ensure sufficient strength.
●
Friction has been reduced by narrowing the connecting rod bearings and crankshaft bearings. In addition, aluminum alloy has been adopted for the bearing lining material, and the lining surface is resin coated, achieving both wear resistance and seizure resistance.
