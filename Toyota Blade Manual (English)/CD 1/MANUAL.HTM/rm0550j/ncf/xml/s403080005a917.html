Plasmacluster Control

●
For 15 minutes after the Plasmacluster switch is turned ON, clean mode operates, releasing approximately equal amounts of positive and negative ions to enhance the effect of suppressing mold activity. After that, ion mode operates for 15 minutes, increasing the proportion of negative ions to adjust the ion balance in the cabin and fill it with fresh air. The modes switch at 15-minute intervals.
Related Parts


Plasmacluster Ion Generator

●
A Plasmacluster generator has been provided on G grade models.
●
Plasmacluster(R)* fills the cabin with fresh air at all times by releasing Plasmacluster ions* , which have the effect of suppressing mold activity, into the cabin and optimally adjusting the ion balance.
●
The Plasmacluster ions and negative ions produced by the generator installed in the instrument panel (behind the combination meter) are released from the driver side side register according to operation of the Plasmacluster switch.
●
A Plasmacluster indicator that displays each mode has been provided on the LCD of the air conditioning control panel. CLEAN is displayed in clean mode, and ION in ion mode.
●
For details of Plasmacluster operation, refer to Plasmacluster Control.
