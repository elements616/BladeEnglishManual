Valve Related Items

●
Dual VVT-i (Dual Variable Valve Timing-intelligent), which controls the intake and exhaust camshafts to the optimum valve timing according to the driving conditions, has been adopted.
●
The camshafts are made of alloy cast iron. The cam lobes are induction hardened and the surface finish accuracy has been improved, increasing wear resistance and reducing friction.
●
Nickel alloy with excellent heat resistance has been adopted for the exhaust valve heads to improve durability.
●
Roller rocker arms have been adopted as the valve train drive mechanism, significantly reducing friction between the cams and sliding parts to lower fuel consumption, and hydraulic lash adjusters have been adopted, eliminating the need for valve clearance adjustment for improved serviceability.
●
With the adoption of roller rocker arms, the cam profile has a concave radius shape. This increases the valve lift near the start of opening and end of closing, further improving output.
●
A timing rotor consisting of 3 protrusions for the exhaust side cam position sensor is provided on the exhaust camshaft of each bank.
●
A cam angle sensor plate for the intake side cam position sensor is provided on the intake side VVT-i controller.
