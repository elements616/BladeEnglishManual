Brake Outline

●
ABS with EBD (Electronic Brake force Distribution), TRC, VSC, hill-start assist control and electronically controlled brake assist using the brake actuator are standard on all models.
●
16-inch disc brakes have been adopted for the front brakes on 2GR-FE engine models.
●
As before, 15-inch disc brakes with parking brake integrated calipers are adopted for the rear brakes.
