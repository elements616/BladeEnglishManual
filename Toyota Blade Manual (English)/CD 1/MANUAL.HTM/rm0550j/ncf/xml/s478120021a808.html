HDD Navigation Functions

●
GPS (Global Positioning System) voice navigation determines the vehicle's position and displays it on the map, and provides route guidance information to the destination by screen display and voice guidance.
●
The navigation of the 09 model year HDD navigation system has even higher marketability through improved appearance of the map screen, shorter processing time for searches, etc. and adoption of new functions. In addition, TABs have been provided at the bottom of each screen to improve operability.
Traffic Information Function


Probe Communication Traffic Information


•
"Probe communication traffic information" is used for congestion-aware route guidance during G-route search.


This provides the following advantages.
•
Highly accurate congestion information can be obtained in real time via the G-BOOK center.
•
Information on roads other than those covered by VICS can be displayed. (Solid line display)
•
Since traffic conditions on roads for which there was previously no congestion information become known, more optimal route guidance and estimated arrival times can be calculated.

Flow of Probe Communication Traffic Information


Step
Description
①
The navigation computer automatically determines the road information, such as "congested", "crowded" or "smooth", from the driving conditions of G-BOOK users.
②
To the G-BOOK center, via communication*the road condition information is uploaded.
③
The uploaded information is distributed from the G-BOOK center to G-BOOK users. (Distributed content: degree of congestion, required time, etc.)
④
Congestion is shown with arrows on the user's map screen, and route searches that take congestion into account become possible.

HINT
*
Bluetooth compatible mobile phone.


Statistical Data Information (Statistical Probe/Statistical VICS)


•
Traffic information from "statistical data information (statistical probe/statistical VICS)" has been included in the map data.
•
Depending on the user's settings, this statistical information can be displayed on the map.
•
HINT
Since this is past statistical data, it may differ from current road conditions.

Flow of Statistical Data Information (Statistical Probe, Statistical VICS)


Step
Description
①
The navigation computer automatically determines the road information, such as "congested", "crowded" or "smooth", from the driving conditions of G-BOOK users.
②
To the G-BOOK center, via communication*the road condition information is uploaded.
③
The uploaded information is accumulated and statistically processed at the G-BOOK center, and statistical congestion information data for each road is created.
④
The created data is stored as map data in the map update editions (spring edition, autumn edition).
⑤
Past VICS statistical data is likewise stored in the map update editions.
⑥
By updating the map, more statistical data can be used.

*
Bluetooth compatible mobile phone.



Current Position Display Function


•
The vehicle position detected by GPS navigation is combined with the vehicle speed signal from the combination meter and road map data from the navigation computer, etc., and is displayed as a mark on the map screen.
•
When position information from GPS satellites is being used, a "GPS" mark is displayed below the direction mark at the upper left of the screen.
•
When VICS information to be displayed on the map is received, the information provision time can be displayed below the "VICS" mark as a VICS time stamp.


Map Display Function




Map Scale
•
In areas with maps at scales of 1:1/20480,000 to 1/5000 and city maps, city maps at a scale of 1/5000 or 1/2500 can be displayed.
•
The scale is changed by touching the "Detail" or "Wide" switch displayed on the screen. In addition, the scale can be selected directly by touching the 13-step scale gauge that appears when the "Detail" or "Wide" switch is touched.



Moving the Map (One-touch Scroll)
•
The map can be moved so that the touched point becomes the center of the screen. If the screen is touched continuously, the map moves faster. While the map is being moved, the place name, nearby route name, route number and address around the center of the screen can be displayed at the top of the screen.



Map Orientation
•
The map orientation can be switched between north-up display (north is always at the top regardless of the direction of travel) and heading-up display (the direction of travel is always at the top). The display is switched by touching the direction mark displayed on the map screen.



Front Wide Display
•
The map in the direction of vehicle travel can be displayed over a wider area. The display is switched from "Navigation Detailed Settings" in the Setup/Edit screen.



Map Color Change
•
The map color can be selected from 5 types (Normal, Fresh, Cool, Chic and Warm).



3D Display
•
The map can be displayed three-dimensionally. The viewing angle in 3D display can also be adjusted. Switching to 3D display can be done with "Change Display" on the map display screen.



2 Screen Display
•
The screen can be split into 2 parts, left and right, and the left and right screens can show maps of different scales, or 3D display and north-up display, etc. In addition, settings such as map orientation and facility display can be switched independently on the left and right screens. The display can be switched with "Change Display" on the map display screen.



City Map Display
•
When the current position is within a city map display area and the 1/5000 scale screen is displayed, touching "City Map" on the screen displays a detailed city map (1/5000 scale or 1/2500 scale). When the display is switched to the 1/2500 scale city map during 3D display, a real city map display showing buildings three-dimensionally can be shown in addition to the city map display.



Visual City Map
•
In the map display, the width of roads on the map changes according to the actual road width, and landmarks on the map are displayed three-dimensionally in multiple colors. The display is turned "ON"/"OFF" from "Navigation Detailed Settings" in the Setup/Edit screen.



3D Landmark Display
•
Three-dimensional facility marks can be displayed while a map screen at 1/80,000 scale or more detailed is displayed.



Prefecture Border Guidance Display
•
When a prefectural border is crossed, the prefecture mark can be displayed on the map screen with voice guidance.



Parking Lot Map Display
•
If parking lot map information exists in the road map data, it can be displayed when entering that parking lot.



Facility Display
•
Facilities selected while a map screen at 1/80,000 scale or more detailed is displayed can be shown on the map screen as marks and text. By touching "Change Display" → "Nearby Facilities" → "All Genres" on the screen, genres of up to 5 facilities can be displayed at the same time.



Urban Expressway Map
•
When a map at 1/20,000 scale or wider and 1/160,000 scale or more detailed is displayed, the roads shown can be limited to urban expressways (Metropolitan Expressway, Hanshin Expressway, Nagoya Expressway), and interchanges, junctions and route numbers can be displayed.
•
The display is turned "ON"/"OFF" with "Change Display" in the map display screen.



Seasonal Leisure Landmarks
•
While a map screen at 1/80,000 scale or more detailed is displayed, seasonal leisure landmarks (e.g. famous cherry blossom spots from 2 to 4, famous autumn foliage spots from 9 to 11) can be displayed on the map screen.



Expressway Route Map
•
When driving on an expressway, a simplified route map of the road can be displayed. Entry/exit IC designation and congestion forecasts are also displayed.


Screen Display Settings


•
The touch switches displayed on the map screen can be shown or hidden by touching the "ON"/"OFF" switch. Touch switches not to be displayed can be selected individually.
•
The display is switched from "Navigation Detailed Settings" in the Setup/Edit screen.


Guidance Display




Stop Sign Guidance
•
When a stop sign guidance point is approached, a stop sign guidance mark is displayed at the upper right of the map, and the voice guidance "(Chime) There is a stop sign ahead. " is given. Stop sign guidance can be switched between "On" and "Off".


■ NOTICE ■
Stop sign guidance may not be given in the following cases.
• Points for which there is no information in the map data
• When the vehicle position cannot be determined accurately
• Roads other than those entering a road with 2 or more lanes in each direction in the stop sign guidance function target area
• When the distance from the entry point to the stop sign intersection is short
Stop sign guidance may be given erroneously in the following cases.
• When the vehicle position cannot be determined accurately
• When the actual road conditions differ from the map data (e.g. the intersection has been changed to one with traffic signals)
Voice guidance may not be given in the following cases.
• When there are consecutive stop sign intersections
• When it overlaps with other navigation voice guidance, etc.
Stop sign guidance is only an auxiliary function. Do not rely excessively on the guidance; always pay attention to road conditions and drive safely.



Road Shape Guidance
•
railroad crossings, merging roads, sharp curves, lane reductions* When a road shape guidance point such as these is approached, a guidance mark is displayed at the upper right of the map and voice guidance is given. The railroad crossing guidance mark is also displayed on the map. Road shape guidance (railroad crossing/merging/curve/lane warning) can be switched between "On" and "Off".
HINT
*
Only while driving on urban expressways
■ NOTICE ■
Road shape guidance is only an auxiliary function. Do not rely excessively on the guidance; always pay attention to road conditions and drive safely.




Right/Left Turn Lane Guidance
•
If there is a right (left) turn lane at an intersection where the vehicle goes straight, the voice guidance "(Chime) There is a right (left) turn lane ahead. " is given when approaching the intersection. During destination guidance, right/left turn lanes are not announced at intersections where the vehicle turns right (left).
■ NOTICE ■
Right/left turn lane guidance is only an auxiliary function. Do not rely excessively on the guidance; always pay attention to road conditions and drive safely.




Accident-prone Location Guidance
•
Locations where traffic accidents frequently occur can be announced. Accident-prone location guidance can be switched between "On" and "Off".
•
On ordinary roads, an icon is displayed on the map at locations where traffic accidents frequently occur.
•
On expressways, when a location where traffic accidents frequently occur is approached, an icon is displayed at the upper right of the map and voice guidance is given.
HINT
It can be displayed on maps from 1/5000 to 1/80000. (Excluding the entire route map display screen)
■ NOTICE ■
Accident-prone location guidance is only an auxiliary function. Do not rely excessively on the guidance; always pay attention to road conditions and drive safely.




School Zone Guidance
•
School* When approaching, an icon is displayed at the upper right of the map and "(Chime) There is a school nearby. " is given when approaching the intersection. School zone guidance can be switched between "On" and "Off".
HINT
*
Elementary schools, junior high schools and special needs schools (including schools for the deaf and blind) nationwide, and the elementary and junior high school levels of foreign schools (dedicated school buildings only)

HINT
School zone guidance may not be given in the following cases.
• Points for which there is no information in the map data
• Saturdays and Sundays
• From 7 p.m. to 7 a.m.
• Around the home when the home is registered
• While driving on toll roads or motorways
• Immediately after ACC ON, nearby schools for which guidance was already given before ACC OFF
Voice guidance may not be given in the following cases.
• When another school is approached while guidance is displayed
• When it overlaps with other navigation voice guidance, etc.
■ NOTICE ■
School zone guidance is only an auxiliary function. Do not rely excessively on the guidance; always pay attention to road conditions and drive safely.

Registration


•
500 memory entries (400 memory points and 100 G memories) and 10 detour memory points can be registered, modified and deleted. In addition, names and telephone numbers can be entered and the mark display can be selected.

Route Guidance


•
When a destination is set, the guidance route from the current position to the destination is displayed and route guidance is provided by voice.
•
To start route guidance, touch "Start Guidance" on the screen after the route search is completed. Alternatively, if the vehicle is driven for approximately 3 seconds without any operation after the entire route display screen ends, route guidance to the destination starts.


Destination Setting
•
The destination can be set by pressing the "Destination" panel switch to display the destination screen and then performing one of the methods in the "Map Call-up Method List" described above. For details, refer to Search in HDD Navigation Functions. In addition, multiple destinations (up to 5) can be set, and the order of multiple destinations can be changed.
Destination Screen






5-route Simultaneous Search
•
5 routes are searched at the same time, and the guidance route can be selected from recommended route, toll road priority, ordinary road priority, distance priority and alternative route. The 5 searched routes are each color-coded and displayed together on the screen, and touching "Entire Route List" on the screen displays the toll (approximate minimum toll), total distance (km) and required time (approximate) when each of the 5 routes is selected.


Route Learning
•
If the vehicle travels on a road (other than narrow streets: roads 5.5m or wider) different from the route under destination guidance, after traveling in the same way several times and learning is completed, guidance can be given using the learned route at the next route search. Route learning "On"/"Off" is set from "Navigation Detailed Settings" in the Setup/Edit screen.


Waypoint Setting
•
During route guidance, waypoints can be set, added, modified or deleted, toll road/ordinary road priority can be set, the starting point can be specified, and entrance/exit interchanges can be specified.


Detour Area Search
•
By setting areas to be avoided in advance, a route search that avoids those areas can be performed. Up to 10 detour memory points can be registered.


Congestion Bypass Search
•
Searches taking detour roads into account during a surrounding area detour search. The road traffic census database is used for searches.


Surrounding Detour Search
•
When congestion, restrictions, etc. occur ahead and are to be avoided, the route can be changed to one that detours around the 2km ahead of the current position. In addition to roads for which VICS provides congestion information, the route estimated to be more comfortable based on actual vehicle driving data such as the road traffic census is guided.


Intersection Guidance
•
Determines when the vehicle is approaching an intersection during route guidance and displays an enlarged view of the area around the intersection for guidance. Narrow-angle branches such as grade-separated intersections and interchange entrances are shown in 3D for easy understanding.


Real Intersection Enlarged View
•
Displays three-dimensional illustrations for intersections in government-designated cities with narrow-angle branches that are difficult to understand from voice guidance alone.


Turn List
•
The branch points and intersections for which voice guidance is given on the route from the current position to the destination can be displayed as a list.


Frontage Road Guidance
•
Dedicated guidance is given when entering a frontage road from the main lanes of an ordinary road. Dedicated guidance is also given when returning from the frontage road to the main lanes.


Seasonal Restriction Section Guidance
•
When a route including a section with long-term traffic restrictions (such as winter closures) is searched, the corresponding part of the guidance route is displayed in a different color and a message can be displayed on the screen. Message "On"/"Off" is set in "Navigation Detailed Settings" in the Setup/Edit screen.


Highway Treatment of Ordinary Roads
•
For motorways on ordinary roads that lie between expressways or toll roads, guidance such as the expressway schematic, direction guidance and exit guidance is provided in the same way as for expressways.


Highway Mode
•
During route guidance on an expressway, the screen switches to the highway mode screen, on which information on service areas, parking areas and interchanges can be viewed. Also, before an expressway exit interchange or junction (approximately 1km before on intercity expressways and on metropolitan/urban expressways), a junction diagram is displayed for guidance.
•
Even when route guidance is not being performed, the highway mode screen can be displayed on intercity expressways only.


Entry/Exit IC Designation
•
After the destination is set, the exit IC and entrance IC shown on the entire route map screen can each be specified one at a time. A notification screen appears if an IC with time restrictions for entry/exit is specified.


Illustration Guidance
•
At entrances/exits of urban expressways (Shuto Expressway, Nagoya Expressway, Hanshin Expressway), a three-dimensional guidance screen (illustration) is displayed for easy-to-understand guidance.


Voice Repeat
•
During route guidance, voice guidance is provided according to the distance to the next junction. Also, by pressing the "Current Position/Voice Guidance" panel switch, the previous voice guidance can be heard again.


VICS Consideration for Estimated Arrival Time
•
When calculating the estimated arrival time, VICS information (including statistics), probe communication traffic information during G-route search (including statistics), general forecast information and expressway forecast information can be used.


Destination Arrival Guidance
•
When the vehicle approaches the vicinity of the destination, destination arrival guidance is provided by voice.


Lane Warning
•
Lane warning provides voice guidance on ordinary roads and urban expressways as follows.
•
Ordinary roads: Voice guidance is provided when the lanes before the intersection ahead include a right-turn-only or left-turn-only lane. However, during destination guidance, voice guidance is provided only when going straight through the intersection.
•
Urban expressways: Voice guidance is provided when there is a lane reduction ahead. (Because lane data is available only for urban expressways.)


G Route Search
•
Available when subscribed to the G-BOOK online service.
•
"Traffic information around the current location" and "expressway congestion forecast information" that take probe communication traffic information into account are obtained from the G-BOOK center as wide-area congestion forecast information. Based on this information, the optimum route to the destination is searched.


JUST Guidance
•
During destination guidance, a notification tone of "Right direction/Left direction" is given just before an intersection where a branch is announced.


Destination Direction Arrow Display
•
During route guidance, if the vehicle position deviates from the guidance route, the direction of the destination is displayed with an arrow.


Frequent Accident Location Warning
•
During route guidance, when the vehicle approaches a location where traffic accidents occur frequently, a frequent accident location mark is displayed on the map screen.


Road Shape Warning
•
During route guidance, when the vehicle approaches a merge, sharp curve or railroad crossing, a road shape warning is given by voice and a road shape mark is displayed on the map screen.


Roundabout Guidance
•
If there is a roundabout on the guidance route, guidance is provided treating the entrance and exit of the roundabout as junctions.


Toll Guidance
•
By setting the vehicle license plate category (1/3/5/7) in advance, the toll on toll roads is displayed and payment guidance is given before the tollgate or ticket inspection booth.


On-route Scroll
•
By touching the cursor keys displayed on the screen, the map can be scrolled automatically along the guidance route to the next destination or the next facility (selectable from categories such as gas stations, convenience stores, restaurants, parking lots, banks and post offices). The entire route display is selected with "Change Display" on the map display screen.


Entire Route
•
The entire set guidance route can be displayed together with information on the guidance route such as the total distance and tolls. The entire route display is selected with "Change Display" on the map display screen.


Route Re-search
•
Re-search of the guidance route from around the current position, re-search of the entire guidance route, search condition setting (recommended, toll road, ordinary road, distance priority, alternative route) and alternative road re-search (only when on the guidance route) can be performed.


Automatic Route Re-search
•
During route guidance, if the vehicle position deviates from the guidance route, the guidance route is automatically re-searched and a new guidance route from the current position can be displayed.


Driving Track Display
•
The driving track is displayed on the map as a series of dots. Up to 1000km can be saved at 50m intervals.

Other Functions




NAVI/AI-SHIFT
•
NAVI AI-SHIFT has been adopted, which selects the appropriate gear ratio according to the road shape and road gradient, obtained by combining the road corner information from the navigation computer (built into the multi-display) with the road gradient information calculated by the engine control computer, and the vehicle information from the vehicle side.


Heartful Voice
•
Voice messages such as "Thank you for driving. ", "Today is ○ month × day, △ day of the week. " can be given when arriving home or getting into the vehicle. Heartful voice "On"/"Off" is set from "Navigation Detailed Settings" in the Setup/Edit screen.

Related Parts


HDD Navigation Receiver

●
The HDD navigation receiver consists of a retractable display panel section and the main unit.
●
Map information, route information, voice guidance data, etc. are stored on the built-in hard disk to increase the amount of information and achieve faster response.
●
The display panel section incorporates a color LCD panel that supports pressure-sensitive touch panel operation and displays the navigation screen, audio screen, etc. In addition, panel switches are arranged around the display.
●
By adopting LEDs for the backlight that illuminates the LCD panel, a display with higher brightness than the conventional type has been achieved. In addition, startup performance (brightness recovery) at low temperatures, response and color reproduction have been improved. Furthermore, the display module is mercury-free, which is environmentally friendly.

Color LCD Panel


•
The display uses a TFT (Thin Film Transistor) type LCD panel with a 7-inch wide screen with an aspect ratio of 9:16 (height 91.44 x width 152.40mm). The liquid crystal cells are subdivided into pixels of 234 vertical x 1200 horizontal; each liquid crystal cell (pixel) corresponding to each color filter is controlled in an analog manner from fully closed to fully open by the RGB video signals, so that colors are mixed additively and all colors can be expressed.
