Intelligent Parking Assist System Outline

●
Intelligent parking assist, which assists garage parking and parallel parking operations by performing steering control in addition to providing the rear view image and voice guidance displayed on the HDD navigation system screen, has been adopted and provided as a factory option on G grade models with the HDD navigation system.

Related Parts


Rear View Monitor Camera

●
The rear view monitor camera consists of an image input section using an ultra-wide-angle lens and an image signal output section consisting of a compact (1/4 inch) color CCD camera.
●
The image signal output section uses a CCD (Charge Coupled Device) image sensor to convert the image captured (input) by the lens into electrical signals according to light intensity, and outputs them to the intelligent parking assist computer.
●
The output image from the rear view monitor camera is itself reversed to match the view when looking rearward in the inner rear view mirror. Therefore, care is required because the image on the screen is shown laterally reversed compared with looking directly rearward (objects on the left side of the vehicle are displayed on the left side of the screen, and objects on the right side of the vehicle on the right side of the screen).
Intelligent Parking Assist Computer

●
The intelligent parking assist computer controls the intelligent parking assist and back guide monitor.
●
The intelligent parking assist computer is installed under the driver seat and controls the ON/OFF of the rear view monitor camera based on vehicle information via CAN communication and vehicle status signals such as IG. Also, it combines the rear view image from the rear view monitor camera with the video signals of each guide line calculated from the steering angle signal from the steering sensor, and outputs them to the display.
