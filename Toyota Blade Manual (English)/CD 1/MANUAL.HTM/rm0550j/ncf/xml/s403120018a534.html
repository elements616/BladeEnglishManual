Back Guide Monitor System Configuration


Back Guide Monitor System Components


Components
Main Function
Rear View Monitor Camera (Manufactured by Matsushita Electric Industrial)
Installed on the back door outside garnish, outputs the captured image signal of the area behind the vehicle to the HDD navigation.
HDD Navigation (Manufactured by Aisin Seiki)
Turns the rear view monitor camera ON/OFF based on signals input from each switch, sensor, etc.
Displays on the display screen the rear image from the rear view monitor camera and guide lines calculated and predicted based on vehicle condition information such as the steering angle signal.
Outputs the voice guidance signal to the front speaker RH and tweeter RH.
Neutral Start Switch
Outputs the shift position "R" signal to the HDD navigation.
Combination Meter
Outputs the vehicle speed signal to the HDD navigation.
Steering Sensor
Installed inside the steering, outputs the steering angle signal to the HDD navigation.
Front Speaker RH and Tweeter RH
Outputs voice guidance when parallel parking guide mode is selected, based on the audio signal from the back guide monitor computer.


Related Parts


Rear View Monitor Camera

●
The rear view monitor camera consists of an image input section using an ultra-wide-angle lens and an image signal output section consisting of a compact (1/4 inch) color CCD camera.
●
The image signal output section uses a CCD (Charge Coupled Device) image sensor to convert the image captured (input) by the lens into electrical signals according to light intensity, and outputs them to the back guide monitor computer.
●
The output image from the rear view monitor camera is itself reversed to match the view when looking rearward in the inner rear view mirror. Therefore, care is required because the image on the screen is shown laterally reversed compared with looking directly rearward (objects on the left side of the vehicle are displayed on the left side of the screen, and objects on the right side of the vehicle on the right side of the screen).
