Reduction Gear and Differential Mechanism

●
Reduction gears are provided as a reduction mechanism and for transmitting the driving force output from the continuously variable transmission mechanism to the differential drive pinion.
●
The reduction drive gear is supported by 2 independent bearings, optimizing tooth contact with the driven gear. This achieves excellent quietness.
●
The front differential is a 2-pinion type using helical gears for the reduction mechanism and straight bevel gears for the differential mechanism.
