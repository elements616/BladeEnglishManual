Oil Pump

●
A compact, highly efficient 4-lobe, 5-node trochoid type oil pump has been adopted, chain-driven by the crankshaft.
●
A relief valve is built in and returns oil to the pump suction side, reducing pumping loss and suppressing oil scattering in the crankcase. Also, friction loss has been reduced by ensuring sufficient flow.
●
A metal gasket has been adopted for the seal with the crankcase, ensuring sealing performance.
●
In consideration of balance shaft serviceability, a split type oil strainer has been adopted.
