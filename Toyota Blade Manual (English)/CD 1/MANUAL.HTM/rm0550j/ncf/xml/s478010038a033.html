Air Cleaner

●
By adopting a full-fabric air element that does not use an outer frame or rubber seal, size reduction, weight reduction and recyclability have been considered, and the case has a removable cap type for excellent serviceability.
●
By adopting a porous duct, which is effective in reducing intake noise, for the air cleaner inlet, a low-frequency resonator is no longer necessary and quietness has been improved. Also, a 2-piece construction improves serviceability for headlight replacement work.
●
A resonator has been provided on the air cleaner hose to reduce intake noise.
●
A hot-wire type plug-in air flow meter with a built-in intake air temperature sensor has been adopted to reduce size and weight.
●
Rigidity has been ensured by giving the air cleaner case a curved structure.
