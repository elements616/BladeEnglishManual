Rear Combination Lamp

●
The rear combination light incorporates the stop & taillight, rear turn signal light and back-up light on the quarter panel side and the taillight on the back door side, and has an upper/lower two-piece shape based on clear lenses.
●
Fuel economy has been improved by using LEDs for the stop & taillight and taillight.
●
Amber bulbs have been adopted for the rear turn signal light bulbs.
●
Clear-type outer lenses have been adopted for the stop & taillight and taillight, creating a clear appearance over the entire surface.
●
By using the reflected light of 12 LEDs in the stop & taillight and 6 LEDs in the taillight, the lights shine like crystal. Also, smoke coating has been applied to each inner lens, giving the appearance that the reflective surfaces shine in 2 horizontal bands.
