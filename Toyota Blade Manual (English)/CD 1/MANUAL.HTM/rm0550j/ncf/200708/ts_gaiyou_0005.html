WIL Concept Seat


•
In a low-speed rear-end collision, the WIL concept seat maintains the angle between the occupant's head and torso and supports them firmly, minimizing the relative movement of the head and torso and reducing the impact on the neck.

•
The upper part of the seatback frame is shaped away from the occupant and a thick seatback pad has been adopted, creating a structure that allows the occupant's torso to sink in and brings the head closer to the headrest in a low-speed rear-end collision.
•
The seatback springs support the occupant over a wide area from the back to the pelvis and can absorb the impact.
•
The headrest is positioned close to the occupant's head and at a high position so that it can firmly support the head.
•
In addition to the conventional WIL concept seat, the following 3 elements have been incorporated to reduce whiplash.

Manual Lifter Mechanism


•
The entire seat is raised and lowered by operating the pump type lifter knob on the outer side of the driver seat cushion.
•
The lifter mechanism consists of the pump type lifter knob, lifter gear, lifter rod, connecting link, bell crank, etc.

•
To raise the seat, operate the pump type lifter knob in the direction of arrow C; the pinion gear rotates in the same direction in conjunction. As a result, the lifter gear rotates in the direction of arrow E around fulcrum Q.
•
A connecting link is attached to the lifter gear; when the lifter gear rotates in the direction of arrow E, the connecting link moves in direction F and the bell crank rotates in the direction of arrow G around fulcrum R. As the bell crank rotates in the direction of arrow G and lifts the rear end of the seat, the front lifter link rotates in the direction of arrow H around fulcrum S, and the entire seat lifts up. A brake mechanism is incorporated in the linkage between the pump type lifter knob and pinion gear, so the pinion gear does not reverse due to the load from the seat cushion.
•
To lower the seat, operate the pump type lifter knob in the direction opposite to arrow C. The lifter adjustment range is approximately 46mm.


Front Power Seat

Front Power Seat

●
The power seat adopts an adjustment mechanism with electric adjusters, allowing easy position adjustment. In addition, the power seat switch is located on the outer shield on the right side of the driver seat.

Power Slide Mechanism


•
When the slide adjustment of the power seat switch is operated, the slide motor rotates. This rotation is transmitted to the seat slide screw via the worm gear in the seat slide housing.
•
The seat slide screw meshes with a resin nut; because the resin nut is fixed to the seat track lower rail via the nut hold bracket, the transmitted rotation moves the seat slide screw forward and rearward relative to the seat track lower rail. As a result, the seat track upper rail to which the seat slide screw is attached slides. The slide can be adjusted approximately 240 mm forward and rearward.


Power Lifter Mechanism


•
When the lifter adjustment of the power seat switch is operated, the lifter motor rotates. This rotation moves the lifter screw forward and rearward via the worm gear in the lifter housing.
•
A connecting link is attached to the tip of the lifter screw and transmits the forward and rearward movement of the lifter screw to the bell crank.
•
The bell crank transmits the movement of the lifter screw to the outer side via the lifter rod and rotates around fulcrum R, lifting the rear end of the lower arm. In conjunction with this, the front lifter link rotates around fulcrum S, lifting the front end of the lower arm, and the entire seat lifts up.
•
The lifter can be adjusted within a range of approximately 50mm up and down.


Power Reclining Mechanism


•
The reclining mechanism consists of an external gear on the seatback frame side, an internal gear on the lower arm side, a reclining hinge pin that regulates the meshing of the external and internal gears, a reclining motor and reclining gear box that rotate the reclining hinge pin, and a reclining rod that transmits the seatback movement to the inner side.
•
The reclining adjustment angle is 8° forward and 42° rearward from the neutral position.

•
When the reclining motor is driven by operating the reclining switch, this rotation is transmitted to the reclining hinge pin via the reclining gear box.
•
The reclining hinge pin has a cam section with center point B offset from the hinge pin rotation axis point A; when the hinge pin rotates, the cam section rotates along a loop-shaped path (Fig. A).
•
An external gear is provided on the cam section of the reclining hinge pin and meshes with the internal gear on the seat cushion (lower arm) side. The internal gear has 1 more teeth than the external gear, so each time the internal gear makes 1 revolution along the loop-shaped path (Fig. A), the meshing position of the external and internal gears changes and the seatback frame gradually moves.


Power Front Vertical Mechanism


•
When the front vertical adjustment of the power seat switch is operated, the front vertical motor rotates. This rotation moves the front vertical screw forward and rearward via the worm gear in the front vertical housing.
•
A link is attached to the tip of the front vertical screw; the forward and rearward movement of the front vertical screw rotates the link, raising and lowering the front end of the seat cushion. The link also transmits the movement to the link on the inner side via the front vertical rod.
•
The front vertical can be adjusted 5° up and down.

Rear Seat Tilt-down Mechanism


•
The rear seat can be tilted down with one touch by operating the seat lock release button on the top of the seatback shoulder, allowing the luggage space to be extended to the back of the front seatbacks and ensuring ample luggage space.
•
When tilting down the seat, lower the headrest to its lowest position and stow the center armrest before tilting it down.
•
When the seat lock release button on the seatback shoulder is pressed, the rod of the rear seatback lock is pulled and the open lever lifts the hook, releasing the lock. Then, when the seatback is folded forward, the seat cushion moves downward along the rotation path of the cushion link and the seatback folds on top of it, forming a flat luggage space.
■ NOTICE ■
Do not perform the tilt-down operation while driving.




Seat Lock Operation
•
Raise the folded seatback to engage the hook on the side of the seatback with the striker. At this time, the lock release button is slightly depressed, indicating as a warning margin (5.1mm) that the seat is not locked.
•
After raising the seatback and confirming that the striker contacts the hook, push the seatback firmly. Then, section A of the striker slides along the surface of the hook and fits into the recess of the hook. This is called the first-stage lock engaged state.

•
In the first-stage lock engaged state, pressing the seat lock release button down by the first-stage lock release stroke (16.6mm) pulls up the rod, rotating the hook around A to release it from the striker, allowing the seatback to be folded forward.

•
From the first-stage lock engaged state, pressing the seat lock release button down by the second-stage lock release stroke (23.8mm) pulls up the rod, rotating the hook around A and releasing it from the striker. Then push the seatback, and when it stops, release the seat lock release button to engage the hook with the striker. This is called the second-stage lock engaged state.
•
In the second-stage lock engaged state, pressing the seat lock release button down by the second-stage lock release stroke (23.8mm) pulls up the rod, rotating the hook around A to release it from the striker, allowing the seatback to be folded forward.

ETC System Outline

●

Audio System Functions


Automatic Sound Levelizer (ASL) Function


The volume is adjusted according to the vehicle speed signal so that music, etc. can be heard at the optimal volume in the cabin even when noise such as road noise and wind noise increases while driving.


LIVE-ACS (LIVE Acoustic / Psychoacoustic Technology) Function


An optimal acoustic space has been ensured by adopting "FOCUS*" technology, which makes the sound seem to come from in front of the occupants even when the speakers are located below ear level, and "TruBass*" technology, which emphasizes deep bass without distorting the midrange.

Sound Library Function


Sound Library Outline


Music CDs can be recorded to the HDD (hard disk drive) built into the HDD navigation system. In addition, up to 999 playlists can be created in the sound library, and up to 99 tracks can be recorded in one playlist. Approximately 100 CDs can be recorded.

HINT
Because the system is SCMS (Serial Copy Management System) compliant, recording cannot be made from CD-Rs, etc. on which music CDs, etc. have been digitally recorded.

Recording Function


"Auto mode", which automatically starts recording to the HDD when a CD is played, and "Select mode", in which recording is done manually, can be set from the settings screen.
While playing at normal speed, recording to the HDD is performed at high speed in high-speed mode and at normal speed in normal-speed mode. During recording, "REC FAST" is displayed at the top of the screen in high-speed mode and "REC" in normal-speed mode. Recording is stopped by turning the audio power OFF or ejecting the CD during recording.


Database Function


By using Gracenote CDDB, names such as album title, artist name and genre name can be automatically assigned to recorded tracks. In addition, lists can be created, and desired tracks can be found and played by names such as album name, artist name and genre name. If there is no corresponding data in the CDDB, the recording date and time is registered as the title.

Electronic Key

Electronic Key

●
The electronic key has a lock button and unlock button, enabling the same operation as the wireless remote control system. A CR1632 battery is used.
●
A mechanical key and transponder* (communication chip) are built in; when the electronic key battery is depleted, etc., the doors can be locked/unlocked by inserting the mechanical key into the driver door key cylinder, and the engine can be started by touching the electronic key to the front of the engine switch.
HINT
*
A relay device that receives radio waves of a certain frequency and sends them back on a different frequency.

■ NOTICE ■
The key is a precision device. Observe the following points.
Do not subject it to strong impacts
Do not leave it in hot places for long periods
Do not clean it with an ultrasonic cleaner, etc.
Do not use it together with magnetic key holders (magnets, etc.)
Do not attach stickers, etc. to the key surface

If a key is lost, promptly bring all remaining keys to the nearest dealer. If a lost key is left unattended, there are disadvantages such as an extremely high risk of theft and increased replacement costs when parts fail.
