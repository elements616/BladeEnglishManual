Dismantlability Improvement Mark

●
Large parts such as the front bumper and door trim have dismantlability improvement marks indicating points that help make removal easier. This improves the efficiency of dismantling recyclable parts and also shows users the efforts made toward recycling.

Environmental Considerations

Environmental Considerations

●
Recyclability has been improved by adopting Toyota Super Olefin Polymer (TSOP), a highly recyclable material, and PVC-free material (TPO) for the roof mouldings, belt mouldings, etc. In addition, the silicone applied to the door weatherstrips has been changed from an organic material to an inorganic material in consideration of the environment.
