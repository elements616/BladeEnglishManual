Engine Outline

Engine Outline

●
For the engine lineup, the 2GR-FE (3.5L gasoline) engine has been newly adopted and the 2AZ-FE (2.4L gasoline) engine continues to be used. There is no change to the 2AZ-FE engine.
●
The 2GR-FE engine adopts a linkless electronic throttle system, Dual VVT-i (Dual Variable Valve Timing-intelligent: intake and exhaust continuously variable valve timing mechanism), ACIS (Acoustic Control Induction System: variable intake system) and a 2-system intake system, improving torque across the entire engine speed range, especially in the low to mid speed range, and achieving both high performance/high output and low fuel consumption/low emissions.
●
Based on the Central Environment Council report on tightening of vehicle emission regulations, the change of emission test mode and mandatory advanced on-board diagnostic systems announced by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism in 11 2006 (Arial 18)* 1were addressed early, further improving environmental performance.
●
New cold test mode in the emission test implemented from 10 2008 (Arial 20)* 2(JC08 mode* 3) under the "Arial 17 Emission Regulations* 4" and the "17 Standard Exhaust Gas 75% Reduction Level (SU-LEV)* 5" has been obtained, aiming for a cleaner environment.
●
The OBD (On-Board Diagnostic System) function, which self-diagnoses malfunctions in the emission control system, has been enhanced, and a function that automatically detects performance degradation of emission devices such as the catalyst and informs the driver has been added, out of consideration for environmental protection. For details, refer to the Repair Manual.
HINT
*1
Mandatory for new models from 10 2008 (Arial 20) and for continued-production and imported vehicles from 9 2010 (Arial 22).
*2
Emission tests consist of a cold test, simulating the engine and vehicle when cold, and a hot test, simulating the engine and vehicle after warm-up; the cold test changes from "11 mode" to "JC08 mode".
*3
Whereas the conventional "11 mode" measures driving with a simple pattern (stop, acceleration, steady, deceleration), the newly established "JC08 mode" uses a driving pattern that simulates actual driving.
*4
"17 Exhaust Gas Regulations": Stricter regulation of CO, HC and NOXemissions specified by Ministry of the Environment notification and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism ordinance; compared with the "12 Exhaust Gas Regulations", values with NMHC (non-methane hydrocarbons) and NOXemissions reduced by approximately 55% are set as the regulation values. In addition, emission regulation values are set according to "10/15 mode + JC08 mode". Emission amounts are calculated as the sum of 10/15 mode (g / km) × 0.75 + JC08 mode (g / km) × 0.25.
*5
"17 Standard Exhaust Gas 75% Reduction Level": Under the low-emission vehicle certification system of the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism ordinance, compared with the "17 Exhaust Gas Regulations", NMHC and NOXA reduction of approximately 75% in emissions is required.

Engine Features (High Performance / Low Fuel Consumption)


•
By adopting Dual VVT-i, optimal valve timing is set according to engine conditions in various speed ranges, achieving higher output, higher torque and lower emissions.
•
Roller rocker arms are adopted in the DOHC4-valve cylinder head, and the valve included angle is narrowed to 25.6 degrees, giving the combustion chamber a compact shape with an excellent S/V* ratio. In addition, the adoption of fine-tip long-reach iridium spark plugs allows optimal placement of the cylinder head water jacket, raising the knock limit.
•
High-flow intake ports and slanted squish combustion chambers are adopted, improving performance by increasing intake air volume.
•
A thin-wall cast aluminum intake manifold is adopted, and port diameter, port length and port shape are optimized, achieving higher torque and lower fuel consumption across the entire operating range. In addition, engine performance has been improved by minimizing intake pressure loss.
•
Optimized exhaust ports and the adoption of a stainless steel pipe exhaust manifold improve low to mid speed torque.
•
A one-way clutch is provided in the alternator pulley, allowing lower accessory belt tension and reducing friction to improve fuel economy.
•
A roller rocker type valve mechanism is adopted, together with a new type of resin coating on the pistons, low-tension piston rings, and uniform bore wall temperature through improved cylinder bore roundness, greatly reducing friction in the valve train and reciprocating/rotating parts.
•
ACIS (Acoustic Control Induction System: variable intake system) has been adopted. The intake pipe length and surge tank capacity are varied to increase torque across the entire range.
HINT
*
S/V: Surface area/volume of the combustion chamber. The lower the S/V ratio, the lower the cooling loss and the more effectively fuel can be used.

Engine Features (Low Emissions)


•
A stainless steel exhaust manifold with an integrated three-way catalytic converter has been adopted. Placing the catalyst directly below the engine improves catalyst warm-up, improving emission performance immediately after engine start. In addition, adopting an A/F sensor enables precise air-fuel ratio control, complying with the "Arial 17 Emission Regulations" and achieving the "Arial 17 Standard Emissions 75% Reduction Level (SU-LEV)".

Engine Features (Lightweight / Compact)


•
The cylinder block is made of aluminum alloy to reduce weight.
•
By adopting a serpentine drive in which the accessories are driven by 1 belt, the overall length of the engine has been shortened.
•
The intake manifold is made of thin-wall cast aluminum to reduce weight.
•
The exhaust manifold is made of stainless steel, and the cylinder head mounting flange is a thin-wall pressed part to reduce weight.
•
The charcoal canister is integrated with the fuel pump, reducing the number of parts and saving space.
•
A resin surge tank and resin fuel delivery pipe are adopted to reduce weight.
•
A full-fabric air element that does not use an outer frame or rubber seal is adopted, making the air cleaner case smaller.

Engine Features (Low Vibration, Low Noise, Quietness)


•
A high-rigidity aluminum alloy oil pan with an integrated stiffener is adopted, improving the coupling rigidity with the power plant and reducing vibration and noise.
•
A high-strength, high-rigidity forged steel crankshaft with 4 journals and 5 balance weights is adopted, reducing weight while reducing vibration and noise through optimal placement of the balance weights.
•
A 2-system intake system has been adopted. The 2-system intake valve is switched in 2 stages according to driving conditions to reduce intake noise.
•
An electric cooling fan system is adopted, controlling the fan speed according to operating conditions to ensure quietness.

Engine Features (Reliability)


•
An MRE (Magneto Resistance Element) type camshaft position sensor has been adopted. The crank position at startup can be detected early, improving engine startability.
•
Cranking hold control has been adopted. Cranking continues until complete engine start is detected, ensuring reliable engine starting.
•
Using the "TaSCAN" diagnostic tool, accurate and detailed diagnosis is possible, such as reading SAE-standard diagnostic trouble codes and data and performing Active Tests.

Engine Features (Serviceability)


•
The adoption of fine-tip long-reach iridium spark plugs extends the maintenance interval.
•
An element-replacement type oil filter is adopted out of consideration for the environment.
•
Hydraulic lash adjusters are adopted, eliminating the need for valve clearance adjustment.
•
Quick connectors are adopted at each fuel pipe joint, improving serviceability.

Engine Features (High Performance / Low Fuel Consumption)


•
By adopting VVT-i, optimal valve timing is set according to engine conditions in various speed ranges, achieving higher output and torque.
•
A DOHC4-valve valve mechanism and vertical intake ports have been adopted and the valve included angle has been narrowed to 27.5°, improving intake and exhaust efficiency.
•
By optimizing the intake port shape, the optimal tumble flow for combustion is generated in the combustion chamber, improving combustion.
•
A slant squish has been adopted in the combustion chamber. In addition, the water jacket in the cylinder head has been optimally arranged to raise the knock limit.
•
By adopting an offset crankshaft, in which the crankshaft center is offset from the cylinder bore center, the piston thrust load is reduced, thus reducing friction. Furthermore, because the piston speed differs between the compression stroke and the expansion stroke, thermal efficiency is improved.
•
Lightweight, highly rigid aluminum alloy pistons have been adopted. In addition, sliding resistance has been reduced by applying a striated finish and resin coating to the pistons and by reducing piston ring tension, and the adoption of spiny-type liners improves bore rigidity, liner outer surface adhesion and bore roundness, greatly reducing friction around the pistons.
•
By enlarging the volume of the bottom of the oil pan, the oil level is lowered without changing the oil quantity, which reduces oil agitation by the balance shafts, etc., thus reducing friction.

Engine Features (Low Emissions)


•
By integrating the three-way catalytic converter with the exhaust manifold, catalyst warm-up performance is enhanced, improving emission performance.

Engine Features (Lightweight / Compact)


•
By adopting a direct-acting DOHC, the area around the cylinder head has been made compact and lightweight.
•
Weight has been reduced by using an aluminum alloy cylinder block.
•
Weight has been reduced by making the air cleaner case and intake manifold of resin.
•
By adopting a serpentine drive in which the accessories are driven by 1 belt, the overall length of the engine has been shortened.

Engine Features (Low Noise / Low Vibration)


•
A highly rigid aluminum alloy crankcase has been adopted. The bullet shape of the rear end, the increased flange thickness and the optimized arrangement of the transmission mounting bolts ensure coupling rigidity with the transaxle, achieving low vibration and low noise.
•
A high-strength, highly rigid cast steel crankshaft with 5 journals and 8 balance weights has been adopted; while reducing weight, vibration and noise are reduced through the optimal arrangement of the balance weights, lighter pistons and improved cylinder block rigidity.
•
A liquid-filled type has been adopted for the right engine mounting and the mounting bracket has been made a separate part; together with reduced roll vibration of the engine body through optimized mount positions, low vibration and low noise have been achieved.
•
Quietness has been improved by adopting an engine cover with sound insulation material.
•
Intake noise has been reduced by adopting a porous duct for the air cleaner inlet.

Engine Features (Reliability)


•
Using the "TaSCAN" diagnostic tool, accurate and detailed diagnosis is possible, such as reading SAE-standard diagnostic trouble codes and data and performing Active Tests.

Engine Features (Serviceability)


•
Maintenance-free operation has been achieved by adopting a timing chain with an auto tensioner.
•
The maintenance interval has been extended by adopting iridium spark plugs.
•
The air cleaner inlet has a two-piece structure, improving serviceability when replacing the headlights.
