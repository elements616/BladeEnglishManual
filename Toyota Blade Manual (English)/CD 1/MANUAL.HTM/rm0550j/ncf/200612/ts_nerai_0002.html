Vehicle Concept

"Passionate & Premium Hatchback"
Creation of a "Mature Hatchback with a Sense of Dynamism"


Vehicle Features

"Look: Envy"
Overwhelming presence combining an advanced form with a sense of precision
With "Fascinating Cool - premium expression through fascinating advanced feel" as the common design keyword for the exterior and interior, creation of originality that promotes the evolution of luxury and the global core.
With "dynamic evolution" as the exterior design concept, a form with a sense of motion and depth was pursued, and a sporty body silhouette creates an advanced feel.
"Satisfaction when riding"
Emotional styling elevated into a high-quality space
"Quality evolution" is created, and the generously concave instrument panel and the flowing lines of the center cluster, styled after a flying buttress, produce a spacious interior.
An overhead console that creates a high-quality interior space, and a large roof illumination integrating 3 LEDs (indirect light) and the room lamp, are provided depending on the specification.
2 interior colors are provided depending on the specification: a refined and intelligent "Dark Blue" and an elegant, calm "Ivory". In addition, combination with metallic-look ornamentation creates a stylish and sporty interior space.
"Amazement when driving"
Moving driving pleasure and relaxed driving
The 2AZ-FE engine, which boasts smooth acceleration and stress-free power performance, is adopted.
The K112/K112F CVT-i transaxle, which achieves a smooth driving feel and low fuel consumption, is adopted.
A newly developed platform (front MacPherson strut, rear double wishbone suspension) is adopted, achieving excellent driving stability and a flat ride.
Large-diameter front and rear brakes are adopted, ensuring excellent braking performance and brake feel.
Active Torque Control 4WD, which distributes optimal torque to the front and rear wheels according to driving operation and road surface conditions, is adopted.
S-VSC (Steering-assisted Vehicle Stability Control), which performs cooperative control of VSC and the electric power steering system, is adopted, achieving both excellent driving stability and handling.


Others

Comfort / Usability
Intelligent Parking Assist, in which the vehicle assists steering operation when parking in a garage or parallel parking, is adopted.
An automatic air conditioner with independent right/left temperature control is adopted depending on the specification, achieving more comfortable air conditioning matched to the occupants' sensations.
The Smart Entry & Start System is adopted on all models, improving convenience.
Safety
ABS with EBD (Electronic Brake force Distribution), TRC, VSC, Hill-start Assist Control and Brake Assist are standard on all models.
SRS airbags (driver and front passenger, driver knee, side, curtain shield) are standard on all models, enhancing safety equipment for occupant protection.
As a further evolution of the "GOA" collision safety body, an "all-direction compatibility body structure", which pursues compatibility in collisions between vehicles of different weight and height, is adopted.
In anticipation of a collision with a pedestrian, a body structure designed to reduce pedestrian injury is adopted.
Environmental Considerations
Complies with the "Arial 17 Emission Regulations", also achieves the stricter "Arial 17 Standard Emissions 75% Reduction Level", and further achieves the "2010 (Arial 22) New Fuel Economy Standards".
Materials with excellent recyclability are used for interior and exterior parts, conserving resources.
Lead-free materials are used out of consideration for the environment.
