Exterior Design Concept

Exterior Design Concept

●
With "Fascinating Cool - premium expression through fascinating advanced feel" as the common design keyword for the exterior and interior, a new sense of luxury suited to the Japanese market has been created.
●
Under the concept of "dynamic deepening", a form with a sense of motion and depth has been pursued, and a sporty body silhouette creates an advanced feel.
Front & Side Area

Front & Side Area

●
The front area uses a "character line" that flows vertically from the hood panel and spreads horizontally at the bottom of the front bumper, emphasizing the three-dimensional center and expressing a sporty and wide feel.
●
Functional parts such as the deep bumper opening, radiator grille and headlights are arranged as if carved in, giving a powerful design befitting the power performance.
●
In the side area, a dynamic side silhouette has been achieved through front and rear fender shapes that assert driving performance, a forward-sloping belt line, and swelling of the hood panel and back door.
●
The front fenders are integrally shaped with the body, and door outside mouldings with a geometric pattern accentuate the sporty feel.
●
Side mudguards are provided on G grade models, and the bulging lower section gives a stable, low center of gravity body.

Rear Area

Rear Area

●
In the rear area, the three-dimensional flow from the rear quarter pillars is turned inward by the rear combination lights, creating a smart and stable form, and the raised back door expresses lightness.
●
A large rear spoiler (with high mounted stop light) has been adopted, creating an aggressive sporty feel while giving an intelligent design conscious of its connection with the rear quarter pillars.
●
The back door garnish is mounted flush with the back door for good appearance.
●
The rear bumper guard has a trapezoidal graphic and integrates the rear reflectors, emphasizing stability and width.

Radiator Grille & Emblem

Radiator Grille & Emblem

●
The radiator grille emphasizes the protruding feel of the center by varying the thickness and depth of the horizontal fins. In addition, black-painted vertical ribs shaped with consideration for the lustrous surfaces, a distinctive thin U-shaped outer frame with smoked plating, and horizontal fins accentuate the premium feel.
●
The emblem design is based on the lowercase letter "b", the initial of "BLADE" (meaning "sword"), arranged in the image of a sharp sword tip and combined with a deep blue ornament to express high quality and elegance.

Exterior Colors

Exterior Colors

●
With "DEEP IMPACT" as the color design concept, a total of 7 colors combining "a calm, mature feel and youthful dynamism" have been provided, including "Deep Gold Pearl Crystal Shine", whose body color changes with shading.

Interior Design Concept

Interior Design Concept

●
With "Fascinating Cool" as its theme, the interior design improves the texture and feel of luxurious colors, materials and fabrics to create "refined depth", while the smooth geometrically shaped area around the center cluster and the deep instrument upper panel create a sense of spaciousness.
●
To achieve a cabin space with a refined feel, large ceiling illumination integrating the overhead console, 3 LEDs (indirect light) and room light is provided on G grade models.
●
2 interior colors are provided depending on the specifications: a refined and sporty "Dark Blue" and an elegant and calm "Ivory". In addition, combining them with metallic-tone decoration creates an even more elegant and sporty cabin space.

Instrument Panel Area

Instrument Panel Area

●
The instrument panel concentrates functional parts in a dynamic center section inspired by a flying buttress, and the shift lever, parking brake lever, etc. are located in a high position close to the steering wheel, achieving both improved operability and spaciousness. In addition, the instrument upper panel extending to the sides has a deep form, emphasizing an advanced feel.
●
Blue-black metallic paint and titanium-tone paint are applied around the instrument cluster finish center panel and to the ornaments of the shift lever and parking brake lever, creating a luxurious feel.
●
An invisible type front passenger SRS airbag door has been adopted, improving the refined feel.
●
A "tilt armrest console box" that can also be used as an armrest has been adopted for the console box, achieving both convenience and storage. In addition, for the console box door pad surface, which is frequently touched, a "suede-like surface" with a supple and gentle feel is used with the dark blue interior, and a "soft leather surface" with a high-grade feel is used with the ivory interior.
●
The console box door is soft and reduces the bottoming-out feeling, providing an excellent feel.
●
Storage boxes and an under-shift tray are located in various parts of the instrument panel, enhancing storage space.
●
Highly recyclable Toyota Super Olefin Polymer (TSOP) has been adopted for the main resin parts of the instrument panel.

Storage Space


•
The upper box has a 2-tier construction, with an upper tier for storing small items and a lower tier for storing a half-size tissue box, etc., and the glove box can store a 1.5L plastic bottle, etc. in addition to the vehicle inspection certificate and owner's manual, providing ample storage.
•
The upper box door lock handle is located toward the driver seat for ease of operation from the driver seat.
•
The lock mechanisms of the upper box door and glove box door use a structure in which the rod moves to the inside of the door to release the lock, providing a good appearance.
•
An air damper is provided for the glove box and an oil damper for the upper box, providing a good operating feel when opening and closing.

•
Push-open type cup holders are provided directly below the side registers on the driver and front passenger sides for excellent convenience. In addition, the cup holders provide moderate resistance when the lever is pushed down so that containers, etc. can be held stably.
•
On models without ETC, a card holder that can store gas station membership cards, etc. is provided at the lower part of the driver side for convenience.

•
An under-shift tray that can store small items such as pocket tissues is provided below the shift lever for convenience.


Console Box


•
A "tilt armrest console box" whose door height can be adjusted in 2 steps has been adopted, and storage space that can hold CDs (approximately 13), etc. has been ensured, providing usability as an armrest and storage capacity.
•
The tilt armrest console box can be tilted up/down with a simple operation. Hold the finger grip of the console box door, pull it up to the top and release it to set it to the tilt-up position; from that position, pull the console box door up to the top again and release it to set it to the tilt-down position.
•
A tilt lock mechanism has been provided to prevent tilt operation while the console box door is open, preventing incorrect operation.


Front Passenger SRS Airbag Door


•
By adopting an "invisible type" in which the surface of the front passenger SRS airbag door is integrated with the surface of the instrument panel, the SRS airbag door is not visible, improving the refined feel.


Side Area

Side Area

●
A head impact protection structure has been adopted for the front pillar, center pillar upper and roof side inner garnishes.
●
TSOP (Toyota Super Olefin Polymer) has been adopted for the front pillar, center pillar upper and roof side inner garnishes for recyclability.
●
Flocked front pillar, center pillar upper and roof side inner garnishes are provided on G grade models, creating a soft feel and refined appearance.

Door Trim

Door Trim

●
The upper surface and ornament surface of each door trim match the seat upholstery (main section), coordinating the interior.
●
A front door trim pocket that can hold 500mL plastic bottles, magazines, etc. is provided in the front door trim, and a rear door trim pocket that can hold 500mL plastic bottles, etc. is provided in the rear door trim.
●
Titanium-tone paint is applied to the door assist grips and door inside handles, creating a luxurious feel.
●
The door inside handle at each seat is integrated with the door assist grip, creating an unprecedented simple and powerful accent. In addition, single stitching is provided on the upper surface section and double stitching on the ornament surface section (G grade only), for appearance and continuity.

Combination Meter

Combination Meter

●
A titanium-tone 4-dial analog meter has been adopted, expressing unique individuality and a refined feel.
●
A large multi-information display that shows various information using characters, numbers and graphics is located at the upper center of the combination meter.
●
An Eco Drive indicator has been adopted to support eco-friendly driving.
