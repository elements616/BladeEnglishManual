Steering Wheel

Steering Wheel

●
The grip portion of the steering wheel has been made thicker than before, improving the feel when gripping the steering wheel.
●
A multi-information display switching switch (DISP switch) has been added to the steering switches, and the layout of each switch has been changed to improve operability.
●
The bezel of the steering switches has been changed to high-brightness silver.
●
The paddle shift switch shape on 2GR-FE engine models has been changed to a sportier design.

Exterior Design Concept

Exterior Design Concept

●
Based on a "design with texture and a sense of luxury", the design has been made even more luxurious and sporty.
Front & Side Area

Front & Side Area

●
The designs of the headlamps, radiator grille, front bumper grille and fog lamp covers have been changed. L-shaped moldings with bright silver paint are placed on the fog lamp covers, creating a wide, low center of gravity appearance.
●
2 types of radiator grille are provided: for "Blade" and for "Blade S Package". For the "Blade S Package", the same radiator grille as the "Master G" is provided, creating a sense of luxury and sportiness.
●
On the sides, the lower part of the door outside molding on all models is body color and the upper part is plated, creating a sense of luxury. In addition, SUS door belt moldings are provided depending on the grade, creating a sense of luxury. The door outside handles are body color on the "Blade" grade and metallic-look painted on the "G" and "Master G".
●
The headlamps are a round 4-lamp type, and horizontally long LEDs are adopted for the clearance lamps.

Rear Area

Rear Area

●
The rear combination lamps combine 3 horizontal LED bars with a sporty round shape.
●
The lower part of the rear bumper guard is painted silver, giving a low center of gravity impression.

Radiator Grille & Emblem

Radiator Grille & Emblem

●
The radiator grille of the "G" and "Blade" consists of 4 horizontal bars, with the top bar chrome-plated and the other 3 bars smoke-painted. In addition, the 3 horizontal bars other than the top bar are widened to the full width of the frame and project toward the front of the vehicle, emphasizing the protrusion of the center portion.
●
On the "Blade S Package" and "Master G", the mesh-based portion is dark gray metallic and the outer frame is chrome-plated, creating a sense of luxury. In addition, a chrome-plated molding has been added to the upper part on the "Master G", creating a sense of luxury.
●
The emblem has a vertical orientation, enhancing the vehicle's class. In addition, a round millimeter wave transmitting grille cover is provided on "Master G" models w/ pre-crash safety system. Optimal placement of the millimeter wave radar sensor made it possible to downsize the millimeter wave transmitting grille cover, achieving a design that does not look out of place compared to the vertically oriented standard specification.

White Pearl Crystal Shine


•
White Pearl Crystal Shine uses the same paint film structure as conventional pearl mica, but adopts "Crystal Shine" pigment, consisting of alumina flakes coated with titanium oxide, in place of the conventional mica pigment in the mica base.
•
Compared to conventional mica, the alumina flakes in the "Crystal Shine" pigment have a uniform particle size and are thin with a smooth surface, so there is less cloudiness and a strongly brilliant color is achieved.


Interior Design Concept

Interior Design Concept

●
With "Fascinating Cool" as its theme, the interior design improves the texture and feel of luxurious colors, materials and fabrics to create "refined depth", while the smooth geometrically shaped area around the center cluster and the deep instrument upper panel create a sense of spaciousness.
●
To achieve a cabin space with a refined feel, large ceiling illumination integrating the overhead console, 3 LEDs (indirect light) and room light is provided on G grade models.
●
2 interior colors are provided depending on the specifications: a refined and sporty "Dark Blue" and an elegant and calm "Ivory". In addition, combining them with metallic-tone decoration creates an even more elegant and sporty cabin space.

Instrument Panel Area

Instrument Panel Area

●
The front passenger upper box skin and the meter cluster finish panel skin are wrapped in synthetic leather, improving quality.
●
A front console has been newly provided, improving storage capability.
●
The instrument panel finish end panels and the instrument finish lower center panel have high-brightness silver paint.
●
The engine switch is located on the right side of the driver seat, improving usability.

Storage Space


•
A USB terminal and AUX terminal are provided in the front passenger upper box on models w/ navigation system.
•
Plated ornamentation is provided on the knob of the front passenger upper box door, improving marketability.

•
Plated ornamentation is provided on the bezels of the push-open type cup holders for the driver and front passenger, improving marketability.


Console Box


•
A cup holder is provided in the front console box. The cup holder door is a push-open type that opens both front and rear, giving consideration to usability. In addition, removing the front/rear partition plate allows it to be used as a small article storage space, giving consideration to utility.

•
The rear console box has an increased height and a console door slide mechanism has been added, improving the armrest function.
•
The interior of the console box is divided into upper and lower levels, providing a structure with excellent storage capacity.


Front Passenger SRS Airbag Door


•
By adopting an "invisible type" in which the surface of the front passenger SRS airbag door is integrated with the surface of the instrument panel, the SRS airbag door is not visible, improving the refined feel.


Side Area

Side Area

●
A head impact protection structure has been adopted for the front pillar, center pillar upper and roof side inner garnishes.
●
TSOP (Toyota Super Olefin Polymer) has been adopted for the front pillar, center pillar upper and roof side inner garnishes for recyclability.
●
Flocked front pillar, center pillar upper and roof side inner garnishes are provided depending on the specification, creating a soft touch and a sense of quality.

Door Trim

Door Trim

●
The upper skin on all models is soft leather.
●
The ornament skin when the "Dark Blue" interior color is provided has been changed from suede-like to soft leather.
●
The ornamentation color of the door assist grips and door inside handles is high-brightness silver, improving marketability.

Combination Meter

Combination Meter

●
Ring illumination has been adopted on the rings of the 4-dial analog meter, improving visibility and the sense of quality. Blue ring illumination is provided on 2AZ-FE engine models (except sports package), and red illumination on 2AZ-FE engine models (sports package) and 2GR-FE engine models.
