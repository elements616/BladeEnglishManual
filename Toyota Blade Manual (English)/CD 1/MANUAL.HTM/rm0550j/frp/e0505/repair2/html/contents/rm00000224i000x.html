Oil Pump Assembly (2AZ-FE) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Oil Pump Assembly

  1. Inspect Oil Pump Relief Valve




    1. Coat the oil pump relief valve with engine oil and check that it falls smoothly into the valve hole of the oil pump body by its own weight.

  2. Remove Oil Pump Cover




    1. Remove the 5 bolts and remove the oil pump cover.

  3. Side Clearance Inspection




    1. Using a precision straightedge and feeler gauge, inspect the side clearance.

      Standard:
      0.030-0.085mm
      Limit:
      0.16mm

      □ HINT □
      If the clearance is more than the maximum, replace the oil pump assembly.

  4. Tip Clearance Inspection




    1. Using a feeler gauge, inspect the clearance between the drive rotor and driven rotor.

      Standard:
      0.08-0.16mm
      Limit:
      0.35mm

      □ HINT □
      If the clearance is more than the maximum, replace the oil pump assembly.

  5. Body Clearance Inspection




    1. Using a feeler gauge, inspect the clearance between the driven rotor and the case.

      Standard:
      0.10-0.17mm
      Limit:
      0.325mm

      □ HINT □
      If the clearance is more than the maximum, replace the oil pump assembly.

  6. Remove Oil Pump Rotor Set




    1. Remove the drive rotor and driven rotor from the oil pump cover.

  7. Install Oil Pump Rotor Set

    1. Apply engine oil to the drive rotor and driven rotor.




    2. Assemble the rotors with the matchmarks facing the cover side.

  8. Install Oil Pump Cover




    1. Install the oil pump cover with the 5 bolts.

      Standard
      T = 8.8 N/m{ 90 kgf/cm }
