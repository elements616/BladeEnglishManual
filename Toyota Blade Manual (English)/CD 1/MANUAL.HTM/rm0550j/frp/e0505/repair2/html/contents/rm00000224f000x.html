Water Pump Assembly (2AZ-FE) Inspection

Print


1. Inspect Water Pump Assembly



  1. Check that coolant is not leaking from the drain hole.

    □ HINT □
    If coolant is leaking, replace the water pump assembly.




  2. Turn the pulley and check that the bearing is not noisy and does not bind.

    □ HINT □
    If the bearing is noisy or binds, replace the water pump assembly.
