Water Pump Assembly (2GR-FE) Inspection

Print


1. Inspect Water Pump Assembly



  1. Check that coolant is not leaking from the drain hole.

    □ HINT □
    If coolant is leaking, replace the water pump assembly.

  2. Turn the pulley by hand and check that the bearing has no abnormal noise or binding.

    □ HINT □
    If there is abnormal noise or binding, replace the water pump assembly.
