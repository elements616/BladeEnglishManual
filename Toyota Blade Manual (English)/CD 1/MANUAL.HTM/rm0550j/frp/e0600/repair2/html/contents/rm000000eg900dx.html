  1. Scope of Repair Work Explanations

    1. Repair work can be broadly divided into 3 processes: 'diagnosis', 'removal/installation, replacement, disassembly/reassembly and inspection/adjustment', and 'final inspection'.

    2. This manual explains process 2, 'removal/installation, replacement, disassembly/reassembly and inspection/adjustment'; explanations of process 1, 'diagnosis' (covered in a separate section), and process 3, 'final inspection', are omitted.

    3. The following basic operations are omitted from this manual, but must be performed in actual work.


      1. Jack and lift operations

      2. Cleaning and washing of removed parts as necessary

      3. Visual inspection



    1. Preparation

      1. Items to be prepared before work, such as SST, tools, gauges and lubricants, are listed in the Preparation section. However, items that are considered standard equipment in a general service shop, such as Toyota tool stands, jacks and rigid racks, are omitted from the list.

      2. Tools and equipment other than SST listed in the Preparation list are given as examples only and do not specify a particular supplier, product name or part number.



      1. Work Procedure

        1. Component illustrations are provided for each section or title as necessary.



        2. The component illustrations indicate non-reusable parts, grease application points, precoated bolts and torque specifications.



        3. Torque specifications, the plastic region tightening method, lubricant application points and non-reusable parts are described as key points of the procedure.

          ■ NOTICE ■
          When the above items can be shown with illustrations alone, component illustrations including the procedure are provided. In such cases, all information such as torque and lubricants is included in the illustrations.

        4. Only items with procedures are listed; the work area and work details are shown in the illustrations, and the text gives detailed work methods, standard values and notices.



        5. Some illustrations are shared with similar models. Therefore, details may differ from the actual vehicle.





        1. How to Read Service Specifications

          1. Standard values and limits are shown in bold in the text.



          1. Definition of Terms

            Standard
            		Indicates the allowable range for inspection and adjustment.
            Limit
            		Indicates the maximum or minimum value that must not be exceeded during inspection and adjustment.
            <WARNING>
            		Describes items and procedures that may cause injury to the technician or others, and items and procedures that may lead to injury or accidents if not observed.
            <NOTICE>
            		Describes items and procedures that must not be performed and that require particular attention, since failure to observe them will damage the vehicle or components.
            <HINT>
            		Supplementary explanations to make work easier are given separately from the main text.
