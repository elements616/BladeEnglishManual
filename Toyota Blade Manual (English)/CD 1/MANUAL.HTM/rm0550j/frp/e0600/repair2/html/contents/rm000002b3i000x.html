Alternator Assembly (2GR-FE) Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Alternator Pulley with Clutch



  1. Using a small screwdriver, remove the alternator pulley cap from the alternator pulley with clutch.




  2. Install SST1 to SST2.

    SST
    09820-63020  

  3. Secure the width across flats of SST1 in a vise.




  4. Install SST1 to the end of the alternator rotor shaft.




  5. Install SST2 to the alternator pulley with clutch.




  6. Turn SST2 clockwise to loosen it, and remove the alternator pulley with clutch from the alternator assembly.



2. Remove Alternator Rear End Cover



  1. Place the alternator assembly on the alternator pulley with clutch.




  2. Remove the 3 nuts and the alternator rear end cover from the alternator coil.



3. Remove Terminal Insulator



  1. Remove the alternator terminal insulator from the alternator coil.



4. Remove Alternator Brush Holder Assembly



  1. Remove the 2 screws and the alternator brush holder from the alternator coil.



5. Remove Alternator Coil



  1. Remove the 4 through bolts.




  2. Using SST, remove the alternator coil.

    SST
    09950-40011   (09951-04020, 09952-04010, 09953-04020, 09954-04010, 09955-04071, 09957-04010, 09958-04011)



6. Remove Alternator Rotor Assembly



  1. Remove the alternator washer.




  2. Remove the alternator rotor assembly from the alternator drive end frame.



7. Remove Alternator Drive End Frame Bearing



  1. Remove the 4 screws and the bearing retainer from the alternator drive end frame assembly.




  2. Using SST and a hammer, remove the alternator drive end frame bearing from the alternator drive end frame assembly.

    SST
    09950-60010   (09951-00250)
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)
