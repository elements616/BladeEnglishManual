Oil Pump Assembly (2GR-FE) Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Oil Pump Cover



  1. Remove the 8 bolts and remove the oil pump cover.



2. Remove Oil Pump Rotor Set

  1. Remove the oil pump rotor set from the timing chain (belt) cover.



3. Remove Oil Pump Relief Valve



  1. Using a socket wrench (27mm), remove the oil pump relief valve plug.

  2. Remove the oil pump relief valve spring and oil pump relief valve.
