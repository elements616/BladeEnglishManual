WARNING!

    To ensure safe work and to prevent damage to customers' vehicles, strictly observe the following precautions.

    1. The Electronic Technical Manual is intended for qualified service engineers who have acquired professional technical skills.
    2. Servicing performed by unqualified or inadequately trained persons, servicing without the proper tools and equipment, or servicing by methods other than those described in the Electronic Technical Manual may not only damage the vehicle but also cause injury to the service engineer and people nearby.

      • Proper servicing and repair are essential to ensure the safety of the service engineer as well as the safety and reliability of the vehicle. Also, when replacing parts, always use genuine Toyota parts with the same part number. Do not use deteriorated parts.
      • The contents of the Electronic Technical Manual are procedures that must be followed when performing servicing and repair. To perform servicing and repair according to these procedures, correctly use the SST (Special Service Tools) designed specifically for each purpose.
      • Never use work methods or tools that are not recommended, as doing so may compromise the safety of the service engineer and the safety and reliability of the vehicle.
      • The Electronic Technical Manual contains various items, described as <WARNING> and <NOTICE>, that must be observed to prevent accidents during servicing and repair work and to prevent vehicle damage and loss of safety and reliability due to improper work methods (refer to the Introduction). However, as these instructions cannot warn of every possible hazard, please understand in advance that the items described as <WARNING> and <NOTICE> are not absolute rules guaranteeing that following them alone will avoid all danger.
      • This vehicle is equipped with SRS airbags and seat belt pretensioners. Before servicing the components or wiring of the SRS airbags and seat belt pretensioners, refer to "Precautions" in the SRS airbag section and perform servicing and repair work in accordance with the instructions. Failure to follow the instructions may cause the SRS airbags and seat belt pretensioners to malfunction, cause serious or fatal personal injury, damage the vehicle or surrounding objects, or affect the system. Read the instructions carefully and perform the work using the correct procedures and methods.
