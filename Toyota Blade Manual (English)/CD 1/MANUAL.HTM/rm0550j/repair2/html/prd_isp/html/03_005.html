Periodic Inspection Procedure 2

Replacement (1/2)
1. Private Passenger, etc.

(1) "●◆" indicates intervals specified by law, of which "◆" indicates distance items.

Distance items at 12 months may be skipped if the distance driven since the vehicle registration date or the previous periodic inspection is 5,000km or less, and distance items at 24 months may be skipped if the distance driven since the vehicle registration date or the previous periodic inspection is 1,0000km or less (15,000km or less for the first time), limited to 1 time. However, the inspection cannot be skipped 2 times in a row.

Daily inspection should be performed at an appropriate time determined by distance driven, operating conditions, etc.

(2) ○ indicates inspection intervals specified by Toyota.

(3) △ indicates inspection intervals specified by Toyota for severe conditions (severe use). (Except Cavalier)

(4) "Replace every... km" and "Replace every... years" indicate replacement intervals specified by Toyota; where both are given, replace at whichever comes first.

[] indicates the 1 replacement interval.

" " indicates the replacement interval under severe conditions.

(5) At 24 months after registration, perform the 12-month inspection items; at 36 months and every 24 months thereafter, perform the 24-month inspection items.

(6) Perform the new vehicle 1-month inspection at 1 months after registration or 1,000km driven, whichever comes first.

Perform the new vehicle 6-month inspection at 6 months after registration.
