Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3 Removal

Print

■ WARNING ■
Some of these service operations affect the SRS airbag system, so be sure to read the precautions for the SRS airbag system before starting work. (see)



1. Remove Instrument Panel Airbag Assembly LWR NO.1

□ HINT □
The procedure up to removing instrument panel airbag assembly LWR No.1 isfor reference.



2. Loosen Wire Adjusting Nut NO.1



  1. Remove the lock nut.

  2. Loosen wire adjusting nut No.1.



3. Remove Console Box Assembly RR

□ HINT □
The procedure up to removing the console box assembly RR isfor reference.



4. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.1



  1. Disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.1 from parking brake pull rod sub-assembly No.1 as shown in the illustration.



5. Remove Parking Brake Pull Rod Sub-assembly NO.1



  1. Disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from parking brake pull rod sub-assembly No.1 as shown in the illustration.

  2. Using the same procedure as for parking brake cable assembly No.3, disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.2 from parking brake pull rod sub-assembly No.1, and remove parking brake pull rod sub-assembly No.1.



6. Remove Front Floor Brace Center (Engine Type 2AZ-FE (FF))



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the front floor brace center.



7. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly CTR (Engine Type 2AZ-FE (FF))



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts.

  3. Disconnect the 3 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



8. Remove Front Floor Brace Center (Engine Type 2GR-FE)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the front floor brace center.



9. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly CTR (Engine Type 2GR-FE)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.

  3. Remove the 4 exhaust pipe supports and exhaust center pipe sub-assembly CTR NO.2.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



10. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.1 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 3 nuts and front floor heat insulator No.1.



11. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.2 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 3 nuts and front floor heat insulator No.2.



12. Remove Propeller Shaft with Center Bearing Assembly (Engine Type 2AZ-FE (4WD))

□ HINT □
The procedure up to removing the propeller shaft with center bearing assembly isfor reference.



13. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector



  1. Remove the parking brake lever protector from the parking brake cable assembly No.3.



14. Remove Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Detach the clamp and disconnect the parking brake cable support clamp sub-assembly.




  2. Disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the disc brake cylinder assembly RR LH.




  3. Using an offset wrench (14mm), remove parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the disc brake cylinder assembly RR LH.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the disc brake cylinder assembly RR LH.



  4. Remove the 5 bolts and parking brake cable assembly No.3.





15. Remove Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3 (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the disc brake cylinder assembly RR LH.




  2. Using an offset wrench (14mm), remove parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the disc brake cylinder assembly RR LH.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the disc brake cylinder assembly RR LH.



  3. Remove the 5 bolts and parking brake cable assembly No.3.



    □ HINT □
    The bolt positions of parking brake cable assembly No.2 are symmetrical to those of parking brake cable assembly No.3, except for part [A].



16. Remove Parking Brake Cable Support Clamp Sub-assembly (Drive Type FF)



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the parking brake cable support clamp sub-assembly from parking brake cable assembly No.3.
