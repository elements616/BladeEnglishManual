Stabilizer Bar Rear (4WD) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Rear Stabilizer Link Assembly LH Inspection

■ NOTICE ■
The nut, rear stabilizer link assembly LH and rear stabilizer cushions cannot be reused, so install new ones.




  1. Inspect Ball Joint Rotation Condition

    1. Secure the rear stabilizer link assembly LH in a vise using aluminum plates.

    2. Install the nut to the stud of the rear stabilizer link assembly LH.

    3. Swing the stud back and forth 5 times, then turn the nut continuously at a rate of 3 - 5 seconds per turn and measure the turning torque on the 5 turn.

      Standard
      T = 0.05 to 1.96 N*m{ 0.5 to 20 kgf*cm } (while turning)

    4. Turn the stud.

      Specified Condition
      No abnormal catching or play during rotation

  2. Dust Cover Inspection

    1. Check that the dust cover is not cracked and that there is no grease leakage.
