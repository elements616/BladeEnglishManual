Lighting System Precaution

Print




    1. Precautions for Handling Discharge Headlights


    1. When the headlight dimmer switch assembly is in HEAD, touching the high-voltage socket of the discharge headlight may instantaneously generate a high voltage of approximately 20000V, which could lead to a serious accident.

    2. Never take measurements by connecting a tester to the high-voltage socket of the discharge headlight, as the high voltage could lead to a serious accident.

    3. When performing work related to the discharge headlights, observe the following conditions.


      1. Perform the work in a place not exposed to rain or other water. (To prevent electric shock)

      2. Turn the headlight dimmer switch assembly OFF.

      3. Disconnect the battery terminal and disconnect the 3-pin connector clamped to the headlight assembly. (This cuts off the power supply to the headlight light control computer assembly)

    4. When illuminating the bulb during work related to the discharge headlights, do so only after installation is completely finished. Never illuminate the light without the bulb installed.

    5. Do not illuminate the discharge headlights using a power source other than the vehicle.

    6. If the discharge headlight (especially the headlight light control computer assembly) has any of the following damage, replace it with a new one.


      1. If the computer has obvious deformation (warping, twisting, dents, impact marks, cracks or chips). (*)

      2. If the resin part of the connector or high-voltage socket is cracked or chipped, or the terminal part is deformed. (*)

      3. If the contact with the reflector side ground is unstable due to deformation of the socket cover. (*)

      4. If the high-voltage harness or power source wire harness is damaged. (*)

        □ HINT □
        If there is a malfunction even though it temporarily operates normally, the fail-safe function operates and illumination is interrupted.

    7. If the headlight light control computer assembly has no external damage such as (*), or if it illuminates normally even though it has received an impact, perform the headlight illumination diagnosis using the following method. If all items are normal as a result of the diagnosis, it can be reused.


      1. Perform a cold start (illumination after being off for 10 minutes or more) and a hot start (illuminated for 15 minutes or more → off for 1 minutes → illuminated again) several times; if it illuminates reliably, judge that it can be reused.

      2. Monitor the illumination status for approximately 5 minutes from immediately after the cold start until it becomes stable. If there is no unstable condition such as flickering, judge that it can be reused.

      3. With bulbs of similar usage time installed, illuminate the RH and LH headlights for approximately 30 minutes; if there is no obvious difference in brightness between the RH and LH headlights and illumination is stable, judge that they can be reused.

    8. The emitted color of discharge headlights changes as usage time elapses.



      2. Precautions When Replacing Bulbs of Each Light

      1. Discharge headlights and halogen lights become hot during use, so if oil or grease adheres to the bulb surface, bulb life will be shortened. When replacing a bulb, hold it by the flange and do not let your hands touch the glass part.

      2. The pressure inside the glass bulb of discharge headlight and halogen light bulbs is high, so if dropped, struck or scratched they may break and scatter glass.

      3. If the bulb is left removed for a long time, dirt or moisture may enter the lens, so prepare a new bulb before replacing the bulb.

      4. Always use a replacement bulb of the same standard and wattage.

      5. If a bulb is replaced on one side only, there may be a difference in brightness and emitted color between the left and right.

      6. Improper socket installation can cause lens fogging or water entry, so install it securely after replacing the bulb.

      7. Glass fragments may scatter and cause injury, so dispose of used discharge headlight bulbs without breaking them.

      8. Whether a discharge headlight bulb is defective cannot be determined visually. Therefore, inspect it by swapping it with a discharge headlight bulb that illuminates normally and illuminating it to determine whether it is OK or NG.



        3. Precautions for Handling Vehicles with Auto Leveling

        1. Initialization is necessary after performing work that changes the vehicle height, such as removal/installation or replacement of the height control sensor or replacement of the suspension, and after replacing the headlight leveling computer assembly.
