Knock Control Sensor (2AZ-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Fuel Spill Prevention Procedure

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Perform the work with the engine coolant temperature at 60°C or less.
  2. Before removing fuel system parts, be sure to perform the fuel spill prevention procedure.
  3. Even after the fuel spill prevention procedure, some pressure remains in the fuel line, so when disconnecting the fuel line, cover it with a shop rag etc. to prevent fuel from spraying.

  1. Remove the rear floor service hole cover. (Drive Type FF)

  2. Remove the rear floor service hole cover. (Drive Type 4WD)




  3. Disconnect the fuel pump connector from the fuel suction tube assembly.

  4. Start the engine.

  5. After the engine stops by itself, turn the IG OFF.

    □ HINT □
    Lean malfunction DTCs "P0171/25" and "P0174/25" may be detected.

  6. Crank the engine again and check that it does not start.

  7. Remove the fuel tank cap and release the pressure in the fuel tank.

  8. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.
    For the procedure, seeReference

  9. Connect the fuel pump connector to the fuel suction tube assembly.

  10. Install the rear floor service hole cover with new butyl tape. (Drive Type FF)

  11. Install the rear floor service hole cover with new butyl tape. (Drive Type 4WD)



2. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal


3. Remove Front Wiper Arm Head Cap



  1. Remove the 2 front wiper arm head caps.



4. Remove Front Wiper Arm & Blade Assembly RH



  1. Remove the front wiper arm nut and front wiper arm & blade assembly RH.



5. Remove Front Wiper Arm & Blade Assembly LH



  1. Remove the front wiper arm nut and front wiper arm & blade assembly LH.



6. Remove Hood to Cowl Top Seal



  1. Disengage the 7 clips and remove the hood to cowl top seal.



7. Remove Front Side Panel Protector NO.1 LH



  1. Disengage the pin and remove front side panel protector NO.1 LH.



8. Remove Cowl Top Ventilator Louver LH



  1. Disengage the 11 claws and remove the cowl top ventilator louver LH as shown in the illustration.



9. Remove Front Side Panel Protector NO.1 RH



  1. Disengage the pin and remove front side panel protector NO.1 RH.



10. Remove Cowl Top Ventilator Louver RH



  1. Disengage the 5 claws and remove the cowl top ventilator louver RH as shown in the illustration.



11. Remove Windshield Wiper Motor & Link Assembly



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the windshield wiper motor & link assembly.

  2. Disconnect the connector and clamp, and remove the windshield wiper motor & link assembly as shown in the illustration.



12. Remove Cowl Body Mounting Reinforcement RH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the cowl body mounting reinforcement RH from the cowl top panel outer.



13. Remove Cowl Top Panel Outer



  1. Disconnect the windshield deicer wire harness connector and disengage the 3 clamps, and detach them from the cowl top panel outer. (w/ Cold Area Specification)




  2. Disengage the clamps of the heater air duct splash shield seal No.2 and water guard plate RH, and bend each as shown in the illustration.




  3. Remove the 8 bolts and the cowl top panel outer.



14. Remove Engine Cover Sub-assembly NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and engine cover assembly No.1.



15. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Hose

  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose.




  2. Disconnect the vacuum switching valve connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.

  3. Disconnect the fuel vapor feed hose and fuel vapor feed hose No.2.




  4. Disconnect the intake air flow meter connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness from the air cleaner cap.




  5. Lock air cleaner hose clamp No.1.

  6. With the clamp locked, remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



16. Remove Throttle Body Assembly



  1. Disconnect the throttle body connector.




  2. Disconnect the water bypass hose, water bypass hose No.2, throttle body hose and purge line hose.




  3. Remove the 4 bolts and remove the throttle body assembly.




  4. Remove the gasket from the intake manifold.



17. Disconnect Fuel Tube Sub-assembly



  1. Remove the EFI fuel pipe clamp No.2.




  2. Using SST, disconnect the fuel tube (see ).

    SST
    09268-21010  



18. Disconnect Oxygen Sensor



  1. Disconnect the oxygen sensor connector and clamp.



19. Remove Fuel Delivery Pipe w/ Injector

■ NOTICE ■
Do not drop the fuel injectors when removing and installing the delivery pipe.




  1. Disconnect the throttle body connector.




  2. Disconnect the 4 injector connectors.

  3. Disconnect the oil control valve connector. (*1)

  4. Disengage the 3 clamps and disconnect the wire harness from the delivery pipe. (*2)




  5. Remove the 2 bolts and remove the fuel delivery pipe w/ injector.




  6. Remove the 2 spacers from the cylinder head.

  7. Remove the 4 insulators from the cylinder head.



20. Remove Fuel Injector Assembly



  1. Remove the fuel injectors from the fuel delivery pipe.




  2. Remove the O-rings from the injectors.



21. Disconnect Check Valve to Connector Tube Hose



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the check valve to connector tube hose.



22. Disconnect Ventilation Hose NO.2



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect ventilation hose No.2.



23. Remove Intake Manifold



  1. Disconnect the check valve to connector tube hose from the vacuum hose clamp. (*1)

  2. Remove the 5 bolts and 2 nuts and remove the intake manifold. 。




  3. Remove the gasket from the intake manifold.



24. Remove Knock Control Sensor

  1. Disconnect the knock control sensor connector.




  2. Remove the nut and remove the knock control sensor.
