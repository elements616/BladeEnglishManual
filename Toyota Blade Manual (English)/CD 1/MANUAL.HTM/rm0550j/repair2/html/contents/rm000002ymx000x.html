Meter & Gauge System Vehicle Speed Signal Circuit

Preparation List


Function Description


The combination meter assembly uses a direct line to read the vehicle speed signal from the ABS & traction actuator assembly, and acts as a relay that transmits it to other systems. To receive the vehicle speed signal, the combination meter assembly applies a voltage of 11 to 14V from the SI terminal to the ABS & traction actuator assembly, and reads the vehicle speed signal converted inside the ABS & traction actuator assembly. Also, the combination meter assembly receives a voltage of 4.5 to 14V at the +S terminal from each control ECU that requires the vehicle speed signal, and outputs the vehicle speed signal converted from the signal received at the SI terminal.

□ HINT □
The vehicle speed signal in this circuit is not used for speed control of the combination meter assembly. This circuit is for systems that require the vehicle speed signal, which is supplied to them via the combination meter assembly.
