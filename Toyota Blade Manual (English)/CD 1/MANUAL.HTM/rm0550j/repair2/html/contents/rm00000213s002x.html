Cooler Condenser Assembly (Engine Type 2AZ-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Discharge Refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a)

  1. Operate the A/C under the following conditions.

    Condition
    		Description
    Engine Speed
    		Idling
    Operating time
    		3 minutes or more
    A/C Switch
    		ON
    Blower level
    		HI
    Set Temperature
    		MAX COOL

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If the A/C cannot be operated due to compressor lock etc., operation is not necessary.

  2. Stop the engine.

  3. Connect a refrigerant recovery/recycling machine and recover the refrigerant gas.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When using a refrigerant recovery/recycling machine, use it correctly according to its instruction manual.



2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Remove Radiator Grille Protector



  1. Remove the 2 radiator grille protectors.



4. Remove Front Bumper Assembly



  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, turn the pin hold clip 90° and remove the pin hold clip.

    □ HINT □
    Perform the same procedure on the RH side.




  2. Apply protective tape around the front bumper assembly.

  3. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 upper clips.

  4. Remove the 6 screws, 2 clips and 2 bolts.




  5. Pull the front bumper assembly outward from the vehicle to disengage the 8 claws (4 each on LH/RH).

  6. Disconnect the connectors.

  7. Pull the front bumper assembly toward the front of the vehicle and remove the front bumper assembly.



5. Disconnect Water Bypass Hose



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 clamps, and disconnect the water bypass hose from the radiator support assembly.



6. Disconnect Hood Lock Assembly



  1. Detach the hood lock control assembly from the 2 clamps.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the hood lock assembly.



7. Remove Radiator Support Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 horn assembly connectors.




  2. Remove the 4 bolts and radiator support assembly.



8. Remove Fan Shroud NO.2



  1. Remove the clamp and disconnect the water bypass hose No.3 from the fan shroud No.2.

  2. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the fan shroud No.2 from the radiator assembly.

  3. Remove the 2 radiator support cushions from fan shroud No. 2.



9. Air Conditioner Tube & Accessory Assembly Disconnection



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the air conditioner tube & accessory assembly.

  2. Remove the O-ring from the air conditioner tube & accessory assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Wrap vinyl tape around the fitting portions of the disconnected pipe and cooler condenser assembly to prevent dirt, moisture, etc. from entering.



10. Disconnect Discharge Hose Sub-assembly



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the discharge hose.

  2. Remove the O-ring from the discharge hose.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Wrap vinyl tape around the fitting portions of the disconnected pipe and cooler condenser assembly to prevent dirt, moisture, etc. from entering.



11. Condenser Assembly (with Receiver) Removal



  1. Pull out the condenser assembly (with receiver) upward.
