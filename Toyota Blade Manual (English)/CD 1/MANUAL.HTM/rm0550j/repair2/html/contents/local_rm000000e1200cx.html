Continuously Variable Transaxle Assembly Precautions



  1. Before disassembly, clean off sand, mud, etc. adhering to the outside of the transaxle so that it does not enter the transaxle during removal, installation and disassembly.

  1. Until the oil pan is removed, do not turn the transaxle with the oil pan side up, as foreign matter may enter the valve body.

  1. When separating light alloy parts such as the case, do not pry them with a screwdriver, etc.; tap them lightly with a plastic hammer to separate them.

  1. During removal, installation and disassembly, work with bare hands or vinyl gloves. Do not use work gloves, shop rags, etc., to prevent lint from entering.

  1. Always keep disassembled parts in order and cover them with a shop rag to protect them from dust, etc.

  1. Before installation, thoroughly clean each part and, after drying, apply Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC. However, do not clean aluminum and rubber parts with alkaline chemicals. Also, never clean rubber parts such as O-rings, gaskets and oil seals with cleaning solvent (white gasoline, etc.).

  1. Apply Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC to sliding and rotating surfaces before installation.

  1. When installing O-rings and oil seal rings, apply sufficient Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC and install them without damaging them.

  1. Do not secure parts directly in a vise; always place aluminum plates between the part and the vise.

  1. Be very careful not to scratch the contact surfaces, as this may cause oil leaks.

  1. Before applying seal packing, completely remove the old seal packing from the sealing surfaces and clean them with white gasoline.

  1. Use Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC when filling fluid.
