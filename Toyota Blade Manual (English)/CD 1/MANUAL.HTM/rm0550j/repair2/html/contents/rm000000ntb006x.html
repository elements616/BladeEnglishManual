4 Wheel Drive Control Switch (4WD) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. 4 Wheel Drive Control Switch



  1. Resistance Inspection

    1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), inspect the resistance between the terminals of the 4 wheel drive control switch.

      SST
      09082-00030  
      09083-00170  
      Resistance: 
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      4(B) - 1(GND)
      		Switch ON
      		1 Ω or less
      4(B) - 1(GND)
      		Switch OFF
      		10k Ω or higher

  2. Illumination Inspection

    1. Check that the illumination comes on when the battery positive is connected to terminal 3 (ILL+) and the negative to terminal 2 (ILL-) of the 4 wheel drive control switch.

      Specified Condition
      Comes on
