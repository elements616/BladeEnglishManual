How to Troubleshoot ECU Controlled Systems Description of Basic Diagnosis Procedure



  1. Malfunctioning Vehicle Brought to Workshop

  2. Customer Problem Analysis and Malfunction Analysis

  3. Confirm Problem Symptom and Check DTCs (Including Freeze Frame Data)

  4. Understand Vehicle Condition

  5. Circuit Inspection or Parts Inspection

  6. Repair Malfunctioning Area

  7. Confirmation Test

  8. Restore Vehicle
