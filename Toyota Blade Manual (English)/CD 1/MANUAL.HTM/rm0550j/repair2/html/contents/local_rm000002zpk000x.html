Brake Precaution



  1. Precautions for Handling and Work

  1. The front speed sensors of 2WD and the front and rear speed sensors of 4WD are sensitive to magnetism, so take sufficient care when performing work that uses magnets, etc.

  1. Replace each part properly, as improper replacement may affect the performance of the brake system and result in danger while driving. Use the same parts or equivalent parts for replacement.

  1. Keep parts and the surrounding area clean when servicing the brake system.

  1. If the vehicle is equipped with a mobile communication system, etc., refer to the precautions in the Introduction section.
