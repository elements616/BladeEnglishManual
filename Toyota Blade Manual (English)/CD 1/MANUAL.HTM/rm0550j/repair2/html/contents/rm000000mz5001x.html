Television Camera Controller Removal

Print


1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Front Seat Headrest Assembly


3. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Outer Front RH (Power Seat Type)

  1. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover outer front RH.



4. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner Front RH (Power Seat Type)



  1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover inner front RH.



5. Remove Seat Track Cover Outer RH (Power Seat Type)



  1. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.

  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the seat track cover outer RH.



6. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner RH (Power Seat Type)



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the seat track cover inner RH.



7. Remove Front Seat Assembly RH (Power Seat Type)



  1. Remove the 2 rear side bolts of the front seat assembly RH.

  2. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  3. Remove the 2 front side bolts.

  4. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) and the power seat switch knob (for reclining) to set the seat slide position of the front seat assembly RH to the center and the seatback to vertical.

  5. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
    (see)

  6. Disconnect each connector and remove the front seat assembly RH.



8. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Outer Front RH (Manual Seat Type)

  1. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover outer front RH.



9. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner Front RH (Manual Seat Type)



  1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover inner front RH.



10. Remove Seat Track Cover Outer RH (Manual Seat Type)

  1. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.




  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the seat track cover outer RH.



11. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner RH (Manual Seat Type)



  1. Disengage the claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the seat track bracket cover inner RH.



12. Remove Front Seat Assembly RH (Manual Seat Type)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts on the rear side of the front seat RH.

  2. Operate the seat track handle to move the seat slide position of the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts on the front side of the front seat assembly RH.

  4. Operate the seat track handle and reclining adjuster handle to move the seat slide position of the front seat assembly RH to the center and set the seatback upright.

  5. Disconnect each connector and remove the front seat assembly RH.



13. Remove Television Camera Controller



  1. Disconnect the connectors.

  2. Remove the 2 bolts and the television camera controller.
