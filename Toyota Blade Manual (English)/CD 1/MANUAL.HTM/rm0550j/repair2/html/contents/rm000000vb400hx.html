Preparation List



1. Dispose of Front Shock Absorber Assembly LH



  1. Disposal by Drilling

    1. With the piston rod extended, place the shock absorber horizontally, and using a hacksaw or equivalent, make a hole between A and B as shown in the illustration to release the gas.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. The gas released is colorless, odorless and non-toxic.
      2. As chips may fly out due to the force of the gas, cover the hacksaw with a shop rag or equivalent when making the hole.
