Headlight Dimmer Switch Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List


■ WARNING ■
Some of these service operations affect the SRS airbag system, so be sure to read the precautions for the SRS airbag system before starting work. (see)



1. Check Front Wheels Straight-ahead Position


2. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


3. Remove Steering Wheel Cover Lower NO.3



  1. Disengage the claws and remove the steering wheel cover lower No.3.



4. Remove Steering Wheel Cover Lower NO.2



  1. Disengage the claws and remove the steering wheel cover lower No.2.



5. Remove Horn Button Assembly



  1. Using a torx socket wrench (T30), loosen the 2 torx bolts until they turn freely.

  2. Using needle-nose pliers, pull out the 2 torx bolts.




  3. Pull the horn button assembly toward the rear of the vehicle.




  4. Using a thin-bladed screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, release the lock of the airbag connector.

  5. Disconnect the airbag connector and horn connector, and remove the horn button assembly.



6. Remove Steering Wheel Assembly

  1. Operate the tilt and telescopic mechanisms and fix the steering column assembly in its most extended and highest position.




  2. Remove the nut and put matchmarks on the steering wheel assembly and steering column assembly.




  3. Using SST, remove the steering wheel assembly.

    SST
    09950-50013   (09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05070)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Apply a small amount of grease to the threads and tip of the SST center bolt (09953-05020) before use.



7. Remove Steering Column Cover

■ NOTICE ■
If the removal procedure is incorrect, the claws may be damaged.

  1. Release the tilt lever.




  2. Push the steering column cover (lower side) from the left and right to disengage the 4 claws.




  3. Insert a finger through the tilt lever opening of the steering column cover (lower side) and disengage the claw.

    □ HINT □
    Open the claw to disengage it.




  4. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the steering column cover (lower side).




  5. Disengage the 2 claws and pins, and remove the steering column cover (upper side).



8. Remove Spiral Cable w/ Steering Sensor



  1. Disconnect each connector from the spiral cable and steering sensor.

  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the spiral cable w/ steering sensor.



9. Remove Windshield Wiper Switch Assembly



  1. Disconnect the connectors.

  2. Disengage the claws and remove the windshield wiper switch assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pushing too hard will break the claws.



10. Remove Headlight Dimmer Switch Assembly



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the band clamp as shown in the illustration.




  3. Disengage the claws and remove the headlight dimmer switch assembly.
