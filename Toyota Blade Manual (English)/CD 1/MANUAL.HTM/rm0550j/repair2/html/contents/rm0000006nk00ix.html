Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.1 Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



2. Remove Console Box Carpet



  1. Remove the console box carpet.



3. Remove Console Box Assembly RR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the console box assembly RR.



4. Remove Indoor Electrical Key Oscillator



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Detach the clamp and remove the indoor electrical key oscillator.



5. Remove Console Box Mounting Bracket NO.2 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Turn back the floor carpet.

  2. Remove the 4 bolts and console box mounting bracket No.2.



6. Remove Console Box Mounting Bracket NO.2 (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Turn back the floor carpet.

  2. Remove the 3 bolts and console box mounting bracket No.2.



7. Remove Parking Brake Lever Assembly



  1. Detach the wire harness clamp from the console box mounting bracket UPR LH.




  2. Detach the 2 wire harness clamps from the console box mounting bracket UPR RH.




  3. Remove the lock nut and adjusting nut from parking brake cable assembly No.1.




  4. Disconnect the parking brake switch connector.




  5. Remove the 3 bolts and parking brake lever assembly.




  6. Raise the claw of the parking brake lever assembly and remove parking brake cable assembly No.1 from the parking brake lever assembly.



8. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3



  1. Disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the parking brake equalizer.



9. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.2

□ HINT □
Perform the same procedure as for parking brake cable assembly No.3.



10. Remove Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.1



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove parking brake cable assembly No.1 from the parking brake cable end stopper.



11. Remove Parking Brake Cable End Stopper



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the parking brake cable end stopper from the parking brake equalizer.
