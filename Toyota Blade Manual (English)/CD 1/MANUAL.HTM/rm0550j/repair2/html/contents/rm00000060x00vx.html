Inspection Procedure

Step 1Preliminary Inspection

  1. Inspect Brake Pedal

    1. Operate the brake pedal and inspect for mechanical malfunctions in pedal operation.

    2. Inspect the brake pedal height, freeplay and reserve distance. (see)

      □ HINT □
      To identify the malfunctioning part, adjust the pedal height after completing each inspection.

  1. Inspect Wheel Brakes

    1. Inspect that the disc brake pads of each wheel brake are installed correctly.

    2. Inspect for loose bleeder plugs and union bolts, damaged gaskets, and fluid leakage from the wheel cylinders.

  1. Inspect for Brake Fluid Leakage and Air Entry

    1. Check the fluid level in the brake master cylinder reservoir.

    2. Operate the brake pedal repeatedly and check that there is no abnormal feel (spongy feel from air) when operating the pedal.

    3. Inspect for changes in the fluid level in the brake master cylinder reservoir.

    4. If the fluid level decreases, inspect for fluid leakage due to damaged flexible hoses or brake tubes, loose union nuts, or a faulty master cylinder seal.

  1. Bleed Brake Line

    1. Bleed the air from the brake pipelines and actuator assembly. (see)
