DTC B2784 Antenna Coil Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The certification ECU (smart key computer assembly) outputs this code when a malfunction such as an open or short circuit is detected in the antenna coil in the engine switch, or when the engine switch is not connected. Also, past DTCs are not stored.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
Inspection Area
B2784
  1. Open or short circuit in antenna coil
  2. Engine switch not connected
  1. Engine Switch
  2. Certification ECU (Smart Key Computer Assembly)
