Rear Bumper Installation

Print


1. Install Rear Bumper Assembly

  1. Connect the connector.




  2. Lift the rear bumper assembly from under the vehicle and pass the license plate through from the lower side of the rear bumper assembly.




  3. As shown in the illustration, while pulling the lower side of the rear bumper assembly toward the rear of the vehicle, push it toward the front of the vehicle until the rear bumper assembly passes over the upper side of the license plate.




  4. Install the 2 clips of the rear bumper assembly




  5. Engage the 12 claws (6 each on LH/RH).




  6. Install the rear bumper assembly with the 6 clips and 2 bolts.



2. Install Rear Bumper Plate NO.1



  1. Engage the 2 claws and install the rear bumper plate No.1.

    □ HINT □
    Install the LH side using the same procedure.
