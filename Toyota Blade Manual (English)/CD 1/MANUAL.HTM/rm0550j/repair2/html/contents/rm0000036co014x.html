Step 7Customer Problem Analysis

  1. Perform a customer problem analysis using the check sheet to hear about the toll gate where the problem occurred, the route driven and the customer's usage conditions

    1. "ETC gate bar does not open" can have various causes, and an inquiry to the road operator is necessary

      Driving condition factors
      1. Driving speed is high
      2. Driving at an angle
      3. Driving immediately behind a large vehicle
      Roadside radio equipment factors
      1. Improper radio wave characteristics (directivity, leakage from adjacent lanes)
      2. Multipath in the surrounding environment
      3. Roadside unit not operating or switching operation
      Customer's method of use
      1. ETC Card Not Inserted
      2. Different cards used at entrance and exit for distance-based tolls
      3. Card removed immediately after passing entrance gate for distance-based tolls
      4. Setup not performed again after license plate number change
      5. Vehicle not set up for towing attempted to pass while towing
      6. Multiple ETC on-board units installed in vehicle
      External disturbance factors
      1. Interference from illegal radio
      2. Multipath due to large vehicle passing simultaneously in adjacent lane
