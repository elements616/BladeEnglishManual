DTC B2070/41 VSC Buzzer Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. This DTC is output when a malfunction occurs in the skid control buzzer.
  2. The buzzer sounding command from the seat belt control computer is sent to the ABS & traction actuator assembly via CAN communication, and the ABS & traction actuator assembly sounds the skid control buzzer.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
Inspection Area
B2070/41
Skid control buzzer malfunction
  1. ABS with EBD & TRC & VSC & BA system
  2. Seat Belt Control Computer
