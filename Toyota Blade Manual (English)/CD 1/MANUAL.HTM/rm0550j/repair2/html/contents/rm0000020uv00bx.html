Camshaft Timing Oil Control Valve Assembly (2GR-FE) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Camshaft Timing Oil Control Valve Assembly

  1. Resistance Inspection




    1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the resistance between the terminals.

      SST
      09082-00030  

      Resistance
      Tester Connection
      		Measurement Condition
      		Standard
      1 - 2
      		20°C
      		6.9 to7.9Ω

  2. Operation Check




    1. Apply battery voltage between the terminals and check that the spool valve operates.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Check that the spool valve is not stuck.

      □ HINT □
      Poor return of the spool valve caused by foreign matter such as sludge being caught in it results in a slight pressure leak to the advance side, which may cause a DTC to be detected.
