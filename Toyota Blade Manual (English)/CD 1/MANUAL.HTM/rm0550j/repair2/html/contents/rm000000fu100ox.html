Function Description


The engine control computer controls the inflow and outflow of line pressure to and from the primary pulley according to vehicle speed and accelerator opening, using signals to solenoid DS1 (for inflow to the primary pulley) and solenoid DS2 (for outflow from the primary pulley). As a fail-safe function, if a solenoid is short-circuited or open, the engine control computer stops energizing the malfunctioning solenoid.
