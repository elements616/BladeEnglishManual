Step 3Microphone Check (Recording and Playback of Spoken Voice)



  1. Microphone Check (Recording and Playback of Spoken Voice)

    1. After pressing the record switch, speak into the microphone and record the voice.

    2. Press the playback switch and check the recorded voice.

      Specified Condition
      The spoken voice is played back normally without noise, distortion, etc.

      □ HINT □
      1. During playback of the recorded voice, the microphone input level gauge does not respond to the playback sound.
      2. The content spoken during recording is optional.
      3. Recording stops after 5 seconds elapse or when the stop switch is pressed.
      4. During recording and playback, all switch operations except the stop switch are disabled.
      5. When nothing has been recorded, the playback switch is grayed out.
      6. Playback ends when playback of the recorded content is finished.
