Circuit Description


The intake air temperature sensor is built into the intake air flow meter sub-assembly and detects the intake air temperature. The resistance of the thermistor built into the intake air temperature sensor changes according to changes in the intake air temperature. The lower the intake air temperature, the greater the thermistor resistance; conversely, the higher the temperature, the lower the resistance. The intake air temperature sensor is connected to the engine control computer, and a power source voltage of 5V is supplied to the intake air temperature sensor from the THA terminal of the engine control computer via resistor R. In other words, since resistor R and the intake air temperature sensor are connected in series, the resistance changes according to changes in the intake air temperature, and the potential of the THA terminal also changes. Based on this signal, the engine control computer increases the fuel injection volume when the engine is cold to improve driveability.
