3. Adjust Front Door Panel Sub-assembly LH



  1. Using SST, loosen the body side bolts and adjust the door in the forward/rearward and vertical directions.

    SST
    09812-00010  
    Standard
    T = 26 N/m{ 265 kgf/cm }

  2. Loosen the door side bolts and adjust the door in the vertical direction and the front end of the door in the left/right direction.

    Standard
    T = 26 N/m{ 265 kgf/cm }

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When adjusting, use a service bolt to adjust the fit.




  3. Using a torx socket wrench T40, loosen the striker installation torx screws just enough to move the striker. Using a brass bar, lightly tap the striker to adjust the rear end of the door in the vertical and left/right directions and the front door lock striker plate assembly.

    Standard
    T = 23 N/m{ 235 kgf/cm }
