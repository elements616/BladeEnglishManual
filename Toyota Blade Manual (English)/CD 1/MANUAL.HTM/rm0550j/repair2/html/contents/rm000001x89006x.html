Headlight Assembly LH Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Headlight Cover



  1. Turn the headlight cover in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove it.



2. Remove Headlight NO.2 Bulb (Halogen Headlight Type)



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Turn the headlight No.2 bulb in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove it.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not touch the glass portion of the bulb.



3. Remove Discharge Headlight Bulb (Discharge Headlight Type)



  1. Turn in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and disconnect the socket of the headlight light control computer assembly LH.




  2. In the order shown in the illustration, release the set spring lock and remove the discharge headlight bulb.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not touch the glass portion of the bulb.



4. Remove Headlight NO.1 Bulb



  1. Turn the headlight No.1 bulb in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove it.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not touch the glass portion of the bulb.



5. Remove Front Turn Signal Light Bulb



  1. Turn in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove the front turn signal light bulb together with the front turn signal light socket.

  2. Remove the front turn signal light bulb from the front turn signal light socket.



6. Remove Front Turn Signal Light Socket


7. Remove Headlight Light Control Computer Assembly LH (Discharge Headlight Type)



  1. Remove the 4 screws and separate the headlight light control computer assembly LH.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the headlight light control computer assembly LH.



8. Remove Headlight Gasket (Discharge Headlight Type)

  1. Remove the headlight gasket.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Once removed, be sure to replace it with a service headlight gasket, as it may cause water leakage.



9. Remove Headlight Unit LH
