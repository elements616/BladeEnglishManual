EFI System (2GR-FE) Function Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Throttle Body Function Inspection

■ NOTICE ■
Be sure to perform a function inspection after assembling the throttle body or removing and installing its components.

  1. Remove the EFI fuse and ETCS fuse at the same time, and connect the fuses after 60 seconds or more have elapsed.

  2. After installing the throttle body to the vehicle, turn the IG ON without operating the accelerator pedal.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If the accelerator pedal is operated, start over from removal of the EFI fuse and ETCS fuse above.

  3. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

    SST
    09991-70200  

  4. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3 and clear the DTCs.

  5. Start the engine, and check that the check engine warning light is not illuminated and that the idle speed is within the standard when the A/C is turned ON → OFF after warm-up.

    Standard
    A/C OFF 680 to 780r/min

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Perform with no electrical load.
    2. Perform in the N or P position.

  6. When the accelerator pedal is quickly depressed to near full open, check that the value of [ECU Data Monitor] - [Throttle Sensor No.1 Opening (THP1)] is 60% or more.

  7. Perform a road test and check that there is nothing unusual.



2. Camshaft Oil Control Valve Assembly Operation Inspection

  1. Camshaft Timing Oil Control Valve Assembly (Intake Side)

    1. Warm up the engine.

    2. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    3. Following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display, select [Active Test] - [VVT Control].

    4. With the engine idling, check the idle speed for [Not Performed/Performed].

      Specified Condition

      Active Test (Oil Control Valve Condition)
      		Engine Condition
      Not performed (not operating)
      		Normal idle speed
      Performed (operating)
      		Engine stalls or idles roughly

  2. Camshaft Timing Oil Control Valve Assembly (Exhaust Side)

    1. Warm up the engine.

    2. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    3. Following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display, select [Active Test] - [Exhaust VVT Linear Drive Bank 1].

    4. With the engine idling, check the idle speed when the exhaust side VVT is advanced and retarded.

      Specified Condition

      Active Test (Oil Control Valve Condition)
      		Engine Condition
      Not advanced or retarded
      		Normal idle speed
      Advanced and retarded between -100 (advance side) ←→ 100% (retard side)
      		Engine stalls or idles roughly



3. Intake Air Flow Meter Sub-assembly Function Inspection

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Inspect the intake air flow meter sub-assembly according to the following procedure.
  2. If [ECU Data Monitor] - [Intake Air Amount] is outside the standard with the engine stopped, or if foreign matter is found on the platinum filament (heater) portion of the intake air flow meter sub-assembly, replace the intake air flow meter sub-assembly.

  1. Intake Air Amount Inspection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Inspect indoors with the vehicle level and the engine stopped.
    2. Perform the inspection with the intake air flow meter sub-assembly installed to the air cleaner case (installed on the vehicle).
    3. Do not extract exhaust from the exhaust tail pipe with an exhaust duct.


    1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. Turn the IG ON and wait for 30 seconds. (Engine stopped)

    3. Following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display, select [ECU Data Monitor] - [Intake Air Amount] and read the intake air amount.

      Standard
      0.55g/s or less



4. Accelerator Pedal Sensor Assembly Function Inspection

  1. Accelerator Sensor No.1 Voltage Inspection

    1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. Turn the IG ON with the engine stopped.

    3. Following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display, select [ECU Data Monitor] - [Accelerator Sensor No.1 Voltage (VPA1)] and check the voltage with the accelerator fully closed.

      Standard
      0.5 to 1.1V

  2. Throttle Sensor Opening No.1 Inspection

    1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. Turn the IG ON with the engine stopped.

    3. Following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display, select [ECU Data Monitor] - [Throttle Sensor No.1 Opening (THPS)] and check the opening.

      Standard
      Condition
      		Throttle SensorNo.1 Opening
      Accelerator pedal fully closed
      		10 to 24%
      Accelerator pedal fully open
      		64 to 96%
      Accelerator pedal fully closed → fully open
      		Changes continuously
