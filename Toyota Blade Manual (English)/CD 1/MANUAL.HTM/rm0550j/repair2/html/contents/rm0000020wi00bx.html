Thermostat (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly



  1. Place both hands on the front of the cover and lift it upward to disengage the 2 front clips, then continue lifting upward to disengage the 1 rear clips, and remove the V-bank cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pulling up the front and rear at the same time may cause damage.



2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal


4. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.

  3. Remove the insulator.

  4. Remove the battery.



5. Remove Radiator Grille Protector



  1. Remove the 2 radiator grille protectors.



6. Remove Engine Under Cover

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 4 screws, and remove the engine under cover.



7. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove the engine under cover No.1.



8. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear RH

  1. Remove the 5 clips and the engine under cover rear RH.



9. Remove Front Bumper Assembly



  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, turn the pin hold clip 90° and remove the pin hold clip.

    □ HINT □
    Perform the same procedure on the RH side.




  2. Apply protective tape around the front bumper assembly.

  3. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 upper clips.

  4. Remove the 6 screws, 2 clips and 2 bolts.




  5. Pull the front bumper assembly outward from the vehicle to disengage the 8 claws (4 each on LH/RH).

  6. Disconnect the connectors.

  7. Pull the front bumper assembly toward the front of the vehicle and remove the front bumper assembly.



10. Millimeter Wave Radar Sensor Assembly Removal (w/ Cruise Control (w/ Radar Cruise))



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the 3 bolts and the millimeter wave radar sensor assembly.



11. Millimeter Wave Radar Sensor Bracket NO.1 Removal (w/ Cruise Control (w/ Radar Cruise))



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and millimeter wave radar sensor bracket NO.1.



12. Remove Radiator Side Deflector LH



  1. Disengage the 3 clips and guide, and remove the radiator side cover LH.



13. Remove Radiator Side Cover RH



  1. Disengage the 2 clips and remove the radiator side cover RH.



14. Drain Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)


15. Disconnect Water Bypass Hose



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 clamps, and disconnect water bypass hose NO.2 from the radiator support assembly.



16. Disconnect Hood Lock Assembly



  1. Detach the hood lock control assembly from the 2 clamps.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the hood lock assembly.



17. Remove Radiator Support Upper

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and the UPR2 radiator supports.



18. Remove Radiator Support Cushion

  1. Remove the 2 radiator support cushions from fan shroud No. 2.



19. Remove Radiator Support Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 horn assembly connectors.




  2. Remove the 4 bolts and radiator support assembly.



20. Disconnect Water Bypass Hose



  1. Slide the 2 clamps and disconnect water bypass hose No.1 and water bypass hose No.3.



21. Remove Fan Shroud NO.2



  1. Detach the 2 clamps and disconnect the 2 horn assembly connectors from fan shroud No.2.

  2. Remove the 2 bolts and separate fan shroud No.2 from the radiator assembly.



22. Disconnect Radiator Hose NO.2



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect radiator hose NO.2.



23. Disconnect Radiator Hose NO.1



  1. Remove the clamp and disconnect the radiator hose NO.1.

    □ HINT □
    Pinch the claws at the clamp joint to remove it.

  2. Slide the clamp and disconnect radiator hose NO.1.



24. Disconnect Oil Cooler Inlet Hose NO.1



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the oil cooler inlet hose NO.1.



25. Disconnect Oil Cooler Outlet Hose NO.1



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the oil cooler outlet hose NO.1.



26. Disconnect Wire Harness



  1. Disconnect the cooling fan motor connector and 2 wire harness clamps.



27. Disconnect Thermistor Assembly



  1. Detach the clip and disconnect the thermistor assembly.



28. Remove Radiator Assembly

  1. Separate the cooler condenser assembly from the fan shroud.




  2. Remove the radiator assembly together with the fan shroud from the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When removing the radiator assembly, do not apply excessive force to the cooler condenser assembly and pipes.

  3. Remove the 2 radiator supports lower.



29. Disconnect Radiator Hose NO.2



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the radiator outlet hose.



30. Remove Fan & Alternator V Belt



  1. Put a tool on the pulley set bolt of the tensioner, and turn the tensioner counterclockwise to loosen the tension.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Performing this from under the vehicle may damage the tensioner.
    2. The pulley set bolt has a left-hand thread, so turning it clockwise loosens the pulley set bolt.

  2. Using a 5mm hexagon wrench, fix the tensioner as shown in the illustration and remove the belt.



31. Remove Idler Pulley Sub-assembly NO.2



  1. Remove the bolt and idler pulley No.2.



32. Remove Water Inlet



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and remove the water inlet.



33. Thermostat Removal
