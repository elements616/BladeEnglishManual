Wiper Rubber LH Removal

Print


1. Remove Front Wiper Blade LH

  1. Release the holder of the front wiper blade LH as shown in the illustration.



  2. Remove the front wiper blade LH from the front wiper arm LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    To prevent damage to the windshield glass, place a cloth or similar between the windshield glass and the front wiper arm LH.






2. Remove Wiper Rubber LH



  1. Remove the wiper rubber LH together with the wiper rubber backing plate.




  2. Remove the wiper rubber LH from the wiper rubber backing plate.
