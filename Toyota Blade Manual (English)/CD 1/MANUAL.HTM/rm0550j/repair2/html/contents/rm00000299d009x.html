3. Remove Instrument Cluster Finish Panel Assembly

  1. Operate the tilt lever to lower the steering wheel.




  2. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.




  3. Disengage the 9 clips and 2 claws, and remove the instrument cluster finish panel.



4. Remove Combination Meter Assembly



  1. Remove the 4 screws.




  2. Pull the combination meter assembly toward the rear of the vehicle, disconnect the connectors, and remove the combination meter assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When removing the combination meter assembly, be careful not to damage the instrument panel safety pad and combination meter assembly.



5. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a moulding remover and move it to near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the moulding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



6. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a moulding remover and move it to near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the moulding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



7. Remove Instrument Panel Register Assembly CTR



  1. Pull the instrument panel register assembly CTR horizontally toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 clips, 5 claws and 3 guides.




  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the instrument panel register assembly CTR.



8. Remove Front Pillar Garnish Corner Piece RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 5 claws and 2 guides A.

  3. Disengage the 2 guides B and remove the front pillar garnish corner piece RH.



9. Remove Front Pillar Garnish RH



  1. Pull the upper part of the garnish in the direction of arrow (1) shown in the illustration to disengage the clip and front pillar garnish clip.

    □ HINT □
    Leave the front pillar garnish clip caught on the front pillar garnish RH.




  2. Using needle-nose pliers wrapped with protective tape, turn the front pillar garnish clip 90° and disconnect it from the front pillar garnish RH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. If the front pillar garnish clip is not removed from the vehicle and is not damaged, it can be reused.
    2. If the front pillar garnish clip is removed from the vehicle, be sure to replace it with a new one.

  3. Pull the garnish in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration, disengage the guide and remove the front pillar garnish RH.




  4. Curtain Shield Airbag Protection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Immediately after removing the front pillar garnish RH, install a protective cover onto the curtain shield airbag.


    1. Using cloth or nylon, wrap the airbag portion as shown in the illustration and fix both ends of the cloth or nylon with packing tape.

13. Remove Box Panel NO.2



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

    □ HINT □
    Apply protective tape on the RH side in the same way.

  2. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 clips.




  3. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 2 claws.

  4. Disconnect each connector and remove box panel No.2.



14. Remove Position Indicator Housing Assembly



  1. As shown in the illustration, using a moulding remover, disengage the 3 claws.




  2. Disengage the 6 claws.

  3. Disconnect the connector and remove the position indicator housing assembly.



15. Remove Instrument Cluster Finish Panel Assembly Center NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 6 clips and 4 claws, and remove instrument cluster finish panel assembly center No.1.



16. Remove Glove Compartment Door Assembly



  1. Disengage the claw and disconnect the glove compartment door stopper sub-assembly.

  2. Slightly deform parts A and B shown in the illustration in the direction of the arrows, release the 2 stoppers and pull out the glove compartment door assembly until it is horizontal.




  3. Pull the glove compartment door assembly horizontally toward the rear of the vehicle, disengage the 2 hinge claws and remove the glove compartment door assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Always pull horizontally to remove the glove compartment door assembly, as pulling it upward causes looseness in the hinge.



17. Remove Instrument Panel Box Door Sub-assembly NO.1

  1. w/ Stereo Jack Adapter Assembly No.1:

    1. Remove the instrument panel finish plate. (see)




  2. Remove the 2 screws <B>.




  3. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  4. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the clips and claws.




  5. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips and 2 claws.




  6. Pull in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration to pull out the upper part of instrument panel box sub-assembly No.1.

  7. Pull in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration and remove instrument panel box sub-assembly No.1.



18. Disconnect Instrument Panel Passenger Airbag Assembly Connector



  1. Disconnect the instrument panel passenger airbag assembly connector.



19. Remove Instrument Panel UPR w/ Instrument Panel Passenger Airbag Assembly



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Operate the tilt lever to lower the steering wheel.

  3. Disconnect the connectors.

  4. Remove the 2 screws <B>.



  5. Remove the 2 instrument panel passenger airbag assembly installation bolts <A>.





  6. Disengage the 4 clips and 3 claws.





  7. Pull toward the rear of the vehicle, disengage the 5 claws and remove the instrument panel UPR w/ instrument panel passenger airbag assembly.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    When removing the instrument panel UPR w/ instrument panel passenger airbag assembly, work so as not to damage the instrument panel UPR w/ instrument panel passenger airbag assembly and steering wheel assembly.

69. Remove Assist Grip



  1. Using a clip remover wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 4 claws of the assist grip cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not pry forcibly, as the assist grip cover may be deformed.

    □ HINT □
    It is sufficient for the assist grip cover to lift slightly as shown in the illustration.

  2. Pull out the 2 assist grip covers by hand to remove them.




  3. Disengage the 2 springs and remove the assist grip.

  4. Remove the 2 springs from the vehicle.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the LH side using the same procedure.

71. Remove Room Light Assembly NO.1



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, pry the cutout of the lens cover to disengage the 4 claws and remove the room light lens.




  2. Disengage the claws.

  3. Disengage the guide and remove the room light cover.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the RH side in the same way.




  4. Using a flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, push the 2 levers in the direction of the arrow so that the 2 claws A are disengaged.

  5. Disengage the 2 claws B.




  6. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver, disengage the 4 claws and remove room light assembly No.1 from the room light switch base.

73. Remove Roof Headlining Assembly

  1. Disconnect antenna cord sub-assembly No.2:

    1. Remove the protective cover installed on the curtain shield airbag.

    2. Disconnect the connector at the front pillar RH area.

    3. Using a clip remover, disengage the 4 clamps and disconnect antenna cord sub-assembly No.2.

    4. Install the protective cover onto the curtain shield airbag.

    5. Disconnect each connector at the rear pillar RH area.

    6. Remove the bolt and disconnect antenna cord sub-assembly No.2.

  2. Disconnect roof wire No.1:

    1. Disconnect each connector at the instrument panel J/B area.

    2. Remove the protective cover installed on the curtain shield airbag.

    3. Disconnect the connector. (w/ Telephone Microphone Assembly)

    4. Using a clip remover, disengage the 5 clamps and disconnect roof wire No.1.

    5. Install the protective cover onto the curtain shield airbag.



  3. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips.






  4. Take the roof headlining assembly out through the back door.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the roof headlining assembly and body interior.
