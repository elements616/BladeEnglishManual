      2. Precautions for Removal/Installation and Replacement of Electric Power Steering Components

      1. When removing or installing the steering gear assembly, always set the front wheels in the straight-ahead position.

      2. When disconnecting the sliding yoke of the steering column from the pinion shaft of the steering gear, always place matchmarks on the sliding yoke and pinion shaft before starting work.
