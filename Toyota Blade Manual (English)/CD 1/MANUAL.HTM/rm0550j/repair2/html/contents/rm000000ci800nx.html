Preparation List



1. Discharge Refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a)

  1. Operate the A/C under the following conditions.

    Condition
    		Description
    Engine Speed
    		Idling
    Operating time
    		3 minutes or more
    A/C Switch
    		ON
    Blower level
    		HI
    Set Temperature
    		MAX COOL

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If the A/C cannot be operated due to compressor lock etc., operation is not necessary.

  2. Stop the engine.

  3. Connect a refrigerant recovery/recycling machine and recover the refrigerant gas.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When using a refrigerant recovery/recycling machine, use it correctly according to its instruction manual.



2. Fuel Spill Prevention Procedure

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Perform the work with the engine coolant temperature at 60°C or less.
  2. Before removing fuel system parts, be sure to perform the fuel spill prevention procedure.
  3. Even after the fuel spill prevention procedure, some pressure remains in the fuel line, so when disconnecting the fuel line, cover it with a shop rag etc. to prevent fuel from spraying.

  1. Remove the rear seat assembly LH.
    (see)

  2. Remove the rear floor service hole cover.




  3. Disconnect the fuel pump connector from the fuel suction tube assembly.

  4. Start the engine.

  5. After the engine stops by itself, turn the IG OFF.

    □ HINT □
    Lean malfunction DTCs "P0171/25" and "P0174/25" may be detected.

  6. Crank the engine again and check that it does not start.

  7. Remove the fuel tank cap and release the pressure in the fuel tank.

  8. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.
    (see )

  9. Connect the fuel pump connector to the fuel suction tube assembly.

  10. Install the rear floor service hole cover with new butyl tape.

25. Remove Brake Master Cylinder Sub-assembly



  1. Disconnect the warning switch connector from the reservoir and disengage the clamp.




  2. Using a union nut wrench (10mm), disconnect the 2 brake tubes from the brake master cylinder assembly.




  3. Remove the 2 nuts and the brake master cylinder assembly.

  4. Remove the O-ring from the brake master cylinder assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. The master cylinder piston comes out easily, so do not hold the piston portion or pull it out. If the master cylinder piston comes out, the master cylinder function may be reduced; do not reuse it, and replace the assembly.
    1. Do not do anything that could damage the master cylinder piston, such as applying impact to it or clamping it in a vise.
    1. Do not remove the master cylinder while vacuum is applied to the brake booster.
    1. Do not point the piston side of the master cylinder downward below horizontal.
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to adhere to the master cylinder piston. If foreign matter adheres, remove it with a shop rag etc., and apply rubber grease to the entire circumference of the outer surface of the piston (sliding part).
    1. Do not use any grease or oil other than that specified.

41. Remove Fan & Alternator V Belt



  1. Put a tool on the pulley set bolt of the tensioner, and turn the tensioner counterclockwise to loosen the tension.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Performing this from under the vehicle may damage the tensioner.
    2. The pulley set bolt has a left-hand thread, so turning it clockwise loosens the pulley set bolt.

  2. Using a 5mm hexagon wrench, fix the tensioner as shown in the illustration and remove the belt.



42. Drain Engine Oil

  1. Remove the drain plug and drain the engine oil.

  2. After draining the oil, install the drain plug with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 25 N/m{ 250 kgf/cm }



43. Drain Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)

■ WARNING ■
To avoid the danger of being burned, do not remove the reservoir tank cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Liquid and steam will spray out under pressure.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  2. Remove the reserve tank cap and drain the coolant.



  3. Loosen the cylinder block drain cock plug and drain the coolant from the engine.





44. Drain Automatic Transaxle Fluid



  1. Remove the refill plug and gasket.




  2. Using a socket hexagon wrench 6, remove the overflow plug and gasket.




  3. Using a socket hexagon wrench 6, remove transmission oil filler tube NO.1 and drain the automatic transmission fluid.




  4. Using a socket hexagon wrench 6, install transmission oil filler tube NO.1.

    Standard
    T = 1.7 N/m{ 17 kgf/cm }




  5. Install the overflow plug with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 40 N/m{ 408 kgf/cm }




  6. Install the refill plug with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 49 N/m{ 500 kgf/cm }

71. Disconnect Fuel Main Tube



  1. Disengage the claw and remove EFI fuel pipe clamp No.1.




  2. Pinch the retainer of the fuel tube connector from both sides with your fingers, pull out the fuel tube connector and disconnect the fuel tube from the pipe.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. If the fuel tube is stuck and cannot be disconnected, do not use tools; pinch the quick connector and push and pull it to release the connection.
    2. When disconnecting, cover the connection with a shop rag or equivalent to prevent fuel from splashing.

  3. Cover the fuel connector and pipe connection with plastic bags to prevent foreign matter from entering.

75. Remove Front Axle Shaft Nut LH



  1. Set the SST aligned with the groove of the front drive shaft assembly LH, and using a hammer, unstake the front axle hub nut LH.

    SST
    09930-00010  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set SST in the groove with its flat surface facing up.
    2. Never grind the tip of SST with a grinder or similar tool.
    3. When removing the front axle hub nut LH, completely unstake it.
    4. Do not damage the threads of the front drive shaft assembly LH.

  2. Using a socket wrench (30mm), remove the front axle hub nut LH.

85. Disconnect Front Axle Assembly LH



  1. Using a plastic hammer, lightly tap the end of the front drive shaft assembly LH to disengage the front drive shaft assembly LH from the front axle assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    If the engagement is tight, tap the end of the front drive shaft assembly LH using a brass bar and hammer.

  2. Push the front axle assembly LH toward the outside of the vehicle and pull the front drive shaft assembly LH out of the front axle assembly LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not push the front axle assembly LH out farther than necessary.
    2. Do not damage the front axle outboard joint boot.
    3. Do not damage the speed sensor rotor.
    4. Suspend the front drive shaft assembly LH with wire or similar.



86. Disconnect Front Axle Assembly RH

□ HINT □

95. Remove Engine Assembly w/ Transaxle



  1. Set the height-adjustable attachment and plate lift attachment, and set the engine lifter.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set the engine assembly w/ transaxle so that it is level.
    2. Securely support the engine assembly w/ transaxle so that it does not fall over.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator RH from the engine mounting bracket RH.




  3. Remove the bolt and nut, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator LH from the engine mounting bracket LH.

  4. Operate the engine lifter and slowly remove the engine assembly w/ transaxle from the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Remove it while taking care not to damage the wire harnesses and steering shaft.

  1. Disengage the claws and remove steering wheel cover lower NO.2.



98. Remove Horn Button Assembly


  1. Using a torx socket wrench (T30), loosen the 2 torx bolts until they turn freely.



  2. Using needle-nose pliers, pull out the 2 torx bolts.




  3. Pull the horn button assembly toward the rear of the vehicle.




  4. Using a thin-bladed screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, release the lock of the airbag connector.

  5. Disconnect the airbag connector and horn connector, and remove the horn button assembly.



99. Remove Steering Wheel Assembly

  1. Operate the tilt and telescopic mechanisms and fix the steering column assembly in its most extended and highest position.




  2. Remove the nut and put matchmarks on the steering wheel assembly and steering column assembly.




  3. Using SST, remove the steering wheel assembly.

    SST
    09950-50013   (09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05070)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Apply a small amount of grease to the threads and tip of the SST center bolt (09953-05020) before use.



100. Remove Steering Column Cover

■ NOTICE ■
If the removal procedure is incorrect, the claws may be damaged.

  1. Release the tilt lever.




  2. Push the steering column cover (lower side) from the left and right to disengage the 4 claws.




  3. Insert a finger through the tilt lever opening of the steering column cover (lower side) and disengage the claw.

    □ HINT □
    Open the claw to disengage it.




  4. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the steering column cover (lower side).

    □ HINT □
    As shown in the illustration, rotate the steering column cover (lower side) around the 2 claws to remove it.




  5. Disengage the 2 claws and pins, and remove the steering column cover (upper side).

103. Remove Brake Pedal Return Spring



  1. Remove the brake pedal return spring from the brake pedal support.



104. Disconnect Brake Master Cylinder Push Rod Clevis

  1. Remove the clip and push rod clevis pin, and disconnect the brake master cylinder push rod clevis from the brake pedal.



105. Remove Brake Master Cylinder Push Rod Clevis

  1. Loosen the lock nut, and remove the brake master cylinder push rod clevis and lock nut.

    □ HINT □
    Measuring the push rod protrusion from the brake master cylinder push rod clevis beforehand makes it easier to adjust the brake pedal height after installing the brake booster assembly.



106. Disconnect Check Valve to Connector Tube Hose



  1. Slide the clip and disconnect the check valve to connector tube hose from the brake booster assembly.



107. Disconnect Brake Tube



  1. Using a union nut wrench (10mm), disconnect the brake tube from the front brake flexible hose No.1 (RH side).




  2. Using a union nut wrench (10mm), disconnect the 2 brake tubes from the brake actuator.




  3. Disengage the 2 clamps and disconnect the brake tube from the body.



108. Remove Brake Booster Assembly



  1. Remove the 4 nuts and the brake booster assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not apply excessive force to the brake tubes on the engine room side.

  2. Remove the brake booster gasket from the brake booster assembly.
