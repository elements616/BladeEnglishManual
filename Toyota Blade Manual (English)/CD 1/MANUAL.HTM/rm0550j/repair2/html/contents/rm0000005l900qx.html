DTC B1443/43 Mode Damper Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The mode damper servo sends the damper position to the air conditioner amplifier assembly by pulse signals. Based on this signal, the air conditioner amplifier assembly rotates the motor (forward, reverse) and moves the mode damper to the desired position. This performs switching control of the air outlets.
