Circuit Description



  1. A three-way catalytic converter is used to efficiently purify the CO, HC and NOx in the exhaust gas. This three-way catalytic converter functions most efficiently near the stoichiometric air-fuel ratio. For this reason, CO, HC and NOx cannot be purified efficiently unless the engine is precisely controlled near the stoichiometric air-fuel ratio. The air fuel ratio sensor (A/F sensor) is installed for the purpose of this control. The air fuel ratio sensor (A/F sensor) outputs a voltage proportional to the air-fuel ratio, and the engine control computer controls the air-fuel ratio based on that voltage. A zirconium element is used for the sensor element. The zirconium element is made of platinum-plated zirconia and contains an integrated heating element. The inside of the zirconium element is exposed to the outside air and the outside is exposed to the exhaust gas, and it generates a small voltage when there is a large difference between the oxygen concentrations of the exhaust gas and outside air. When heated, the sensor becomes very efficient. When the exhaust temperature is low, the sensor does not generate a useful voltage signal without supplemental heating. For this reason, the air fuel ratio sensor has a heater to warm the zirconium element, and the engine control computer regulates supplementary heating using a duty cycle approach to adjust the average current to the zirconium element.
