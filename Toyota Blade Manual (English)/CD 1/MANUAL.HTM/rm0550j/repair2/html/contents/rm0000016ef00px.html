Shift Lock Control Unit Assembly (U660E) Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Position Indicator Housing UPR



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the position indicator housing UPR from the shift lock control unit assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the position indicator housing UPR.



2. Remove Position Indicator Slide Cover



  1. Remove the position indicator slide cover together with position indicator slide cover No.2 as a unit from the shift lock control unit assembly.



3. Remove Position Indicator Slide Cover NO.2



  1. Remove position indicator slide cover No.2 from the position indicator slide cover.



4. Remove Position Indicator Housing LWR



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the position indicator housing LWR from the shift lock control unit assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the position indicator housing LWR.



5. Remove Shift Lock Control Computer



  1. Disconnect the connector from the shift lock control computer.




  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the shift lock control computer from the shift lock control unit assembly.



6. Remove Indicator Light Wire



  1. Remove the clamp and disconnect the indicator light wire from the position indicator housing assembly.




  2. Remove the 3 screws and remove the position indicator light guide together with the indicator light wire from the position indicator housing assembly.




  3. Remove the bulb socket and indicator light wire from the position indicator light guide.
