Circuit Description



  1. The intake air flow meter sub-assembly uses a platinum filament. It consists of a heater made of platinum filament, a temperature sensor and a control circuit built into the housing. In operation, the heater in the bypass passage of the housing is cooled by the passing intake air, and the temperature sensor detects that temperature change. In the circuit, the heater and temperature sensor form a bridge circuit, and the action of the power transistor that controls the heater to maintain a set temperature (potential difference between A and B) is measured as the output voltage of the intake air flow meter sub-assembly.
