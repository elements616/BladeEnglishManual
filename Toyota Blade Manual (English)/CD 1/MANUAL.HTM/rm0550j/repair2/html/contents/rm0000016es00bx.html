Torque Converter & Drive Plate (U660E) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Torque Converter Assembly



  1. Inspect Torque Converter One-way Clutch

    1. Insert SST (tool) into the stator shaft engagement area.

      SST
      09350-32014   (09351-32010, 09351-32020)

    2. Insert SST (stopper) from the sleeve portion of the torque converter and engage it with the notch of the stator thrust washer.

    3. Stand the torque converter upright and turn SST (tool).

      SST
      09350-32014   (09351-32010, 09351-32020)

      Specified Condition
      Turns freely clockwise and locks counterclockwise




  2. Torque Converter Assembly Acceptance Judgment

    1. If the torque converter inspection results meet the criteria, replace the torque converter.

      Specified Condition
      Metallic noise is heard from the torque converter during the stall test or in neutral
      1-way clutch is free or locked in both directions
      Fine particles mixed in the ATF exceed the limit sample (refer to the limit sample)

      □ HINT □
      1. The limit sample for fine particles in ATF is approximately 25ml of ATF (1 35mm film case(s)) drained into a petri dish from a removed torque converter.
      2. For the limit sample for fine particles in ATF, refer to the 'Notice packed with the rebuilt A/T' or the 'A/T Rebuilt Parts Handling Manual (Pub. No. 63264)' issued in 1/1998.

  3. Replace ATF in Torque Converter

    1. If the ATF has an abnormal odor or is discolored, thoroughly stir the ATF inside the torque converter, and drain the ATF with the installation surface facing up.




  4. Clean and Inspect Oil Cooler and Oil Pipeline

    1. If the torque converter has been inspected or the ATF has been replaced, clean the oil cooler and pipeline.

      □ HINT □
      1. From the inlet hose 196kPa{2kgf/cm2Clean by blowing in } of compressed air.
      2. If a large amount of fine particles, etc. are mixed in the ATF, add new ATF using a bucket pump, etc. and clean again.

    2. If the ATF is cloudy, inspect the oil cooler (radiator).




  5. Preventing Torque Converter Deformation and Oil Pump Gear Damage

    1. If there are interference marks between the tip of a torque converter installation bolt and the bottom of the torque converter installation hole, replace the bolts and torque converter.

    2. All installation bolts must be the same length (length difference within 0.5mm) (no spring washers missing).



2. Inspect Drive Plate & Ring Gear Sub-assembly

  1. Inspect Drive Plate




    1. Using a dial indicator, inspect the drive plate runout.

      Standard
      0.20mm

      □ HINT □
      If it is not within the standard, replace the drive plate & ring gear.

  2. Inspect Ring Gear

    1. Inspect the ring gear for damage.

      □ HINT □
      If it is damaged, replace the drive plate & ring gear.
