Smart Entry & Start System (Smart Entry) Precautions

Print



    Precautions when Using 1.TaSCAN

    1. When troubleshooting the Smart Entry & Start System (smart entry) using SST (TaSCAN) with the IG OFF, repeatedly turn any of the door courtesy light switches ON and OFF until communication between SST (TaSCAN) and the vehicle starts.

      □ HINT □
      The interval for turning the door courtesy light switch ON and OFF is within 1.5 seconds.



      Precautions when Replacing 2.ECU

      1. If any of the immobiliser code computer (ID code box), smart key computer assembly (certification ECU), steering lock actuator (steering lock ECU), engine control computer (engine ECU) or electrical key transmitter has been replaced, refer to the registration procedures in the reference section.

      2. When the smart key computer assembly (certification ECU) has been replaced, the engine may not start due to a mismatch in the stored lock position with the steering lock actuator (steering lock ECU). After replacement, perform the following procedure.


        1. Move the shift lever to the P position.

        2. With the IG OFF, open and close the door, then start the engine.



        3.Precautions when Disconnecting/Reconnecting the Battery Negative Terminal

        1. Immediately after disconnecting/reconnecting the battery negative terminal, smart unlock may not operate. In that case, perform a door lock → unlock operation once by wireless operation, and smart unlock operation will be restored.



          4.Precautions for Key Built-in Battery and Vehicle Battery

          1. When the doors are locked, radio waves are transmitted from the vehicle and the vehicle battery is constantly consumed, so if the vehicle is left for a long time, the vehicle's power saving control will operate.

          2. When the doors are locked, if the electrical key transmitter is within the key detection area of the outside transmitters, periodic communication occurs between the electrical key transmitter and the vehicle, consuming the battery. When the vehicle is not in use, do not leave the key near the vehicle (within 2m).



            5.Precautions for Electrical Key Transmitter

            1. The electrical key transmitter is a precision device, so observe the following precautions.


              1. Do not subject it to strong impacts.

              2. Do not leave it in a hot place for a long time.

              3. Do not clean it with an ultrasonic cleaner or similar device.

              4. Do not use it together with a magnetized key holder (magnets, etc.).

              5. Do not attach stickers or other items to the surface of the electrical key transmitter.



              6.Precautions when Inspecting the Smart Entry & Start System (Smart Entry)

              1. Smart Entry & Start System (Smart Entry) General


                1. Always carry the electrical key transmitter.

              2. Under the following conditions, the key detection area described above may become smaller, or the key may not be detected normally (because weak radio waves are used to detect the electrical key transmitter).


                1. When the battery is depleted.

                2. When there are facilities that generate strong radio waves nearby, such as TV towers, power plants or broadcasting stations.

                3. When the electrical key transmitter is carried together with wireless communication devices such as two-way radios, cellular phones or cordless phones.

                4. When the electrical key transmitter is covered by or in contact with a metal object.

                5. When a radio-wave type keyless entry system is being operated nearby.

                6. When it is placed near equipment that generates high voltage or noise.

              3. Smart Entry & Start System (Smart Entry) Operating Range


                1. Depending on the body shape of the vehicle, there are locations where smart entry may not operate easily.

                2. Even if the electrical key transmitter is within the key detection area outside the vehicle, it may not be detected normally if it is near the window, door handle or center of the bumper.

                3. Even if the electrical key transmitter is within the key detection area inside the cabin, it may not be detected normally if it is on the instrument panel, on the rear package tray, in the glove box or on the floor.

                4. Depending on how the electrical key transmitter is held, it may not operate easily.

              4. As the electrical key transmitter emits weak radio waves, persons using a pacemaker should check the contents of the pacemaker instruction manual.

              5. The smart function does not operate under the following conditions.


                1. When smart cancel has been performed.

                2. When the electrical key transmitter battery is depleted (the electrical key transmitter indicator does not blink when any of the transmitter buttons is pressed).



                7.Precautions for Smart Door Unlock Function

                1. When performing smart door unlock, securely touch the back side of the door handle (touch sensor).

                2. If the door handle is touched while wearing gloves or work gloves, the response may be delayed.

                3. When performing smart door unlock, check that the door has been unlocked before pulling the door handle to open the door.

                4. The door may not be unlocked if the vehicle is approached suddenly or the handle is operated suddenly. If the door could not be unlocked, door unlock is performed 4 times, but if the door handle is kept pulled at this time, the door may not be unlocked due to mechanical factors. If the door does not open after pulling the handle 1 time, return the door handle to its original position once and the door can be unlocked.

                5. If the electrical key transmitter is brought close to the door handle, smart door unlock may not be possible.

                6. When the electrical key transmitter is within the outside detection area, the door unlocks even if a person not carrying the electrical key transmitter grips the door handle. However, doors other than the door for which verification is OK cannot be unlocked. (When the electrical key transmitter is within the driver side outside detection area, anyone gripping the driver door handle can unlock the door. However, gripping a door handle other than the driver door will not unlock the door.)

                7. If no operation to open the door is performed after the door is unlocked, the doors are automatically locked after approximately 30 seconds.

                8. When the electrical key transmitter is within the outside detection area, if a large amount of water splashes on the door handle due to car washing or heavy rain, the human sensor may react and perform smart door unlock. However, if no operation to open the door is performed, the doors are automatically locked after approximately 30 seconds.

                9. If the smart function compatible electrical key transmitter of another vehicle is carried together with the smart function compatible electrical key transmitter of this vehicle, the time from touching the door handle until smart door unlock may be slightly longer than usual.



                  8.Precautions for Smart Door Lock Function

                  1. When performing smart door lock, if the lock switch on the door handle is pressed quickly, the doors may not lock.

                  2. If the electrical key transmitter is brought close to the cabin (window or door handle), smart door lock may not be possible. Also, at that time, the [key left-behind warning] may sound and smart unlock may become impossible. In that case, move the electrical key transmitter away from the cabin (window or door handle), perform smart door lock, and then perform smart unlock again.

                  3. If the lock switch is pressed while opening (or closing) the door while carrying the electrical key transmitter, the door ajar warning (outside the vehicle [beep (10 seconds)]) sounds.

                  4. If the electrical key transmitter is placed on the instrument panel, on the rear package tray, in the glove box or on the floor, the electrical key transmitter left-behind prevention function does not operate, and the doors may lock by a door lock operation, locking the electrical key transmitter inside the cabin. Always carry the electrical key transmitter.

                  5. If wireless door lock, manual door lock or key-linked door lock is performed with the electrical key transmitter in the inside detection area or the trunk detection area, smart door unlock cannot be performed afterwards. Also, for the above reason, if wireless door lock is performed near the window or door handle, smart door unlock may not be possible. To unlock the doors, perform wireless door unlock.

                  6. For approximately 3 seconds after door lock (all door lock operations including smart and wireless), smart door unlock cannot be performed.
