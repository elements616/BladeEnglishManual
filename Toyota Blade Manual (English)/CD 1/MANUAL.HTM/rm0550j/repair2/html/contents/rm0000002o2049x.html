    Step 3Basic Inspection

      SST
      09082-00030  

    1. Basic Inspection

      1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the battery voltage.

        Standard
        11 to 14 V (IG OFF)

      2. Inspect areas that can be checked visually, such as blown fuses, open or short circuits in the wire harness and poor connector connections.

      2. Check the screen display for approximately 1 minutes, and check the display of all ECUs and sensors connected to CAN communication.

      1. Start the engine, select "System Diagnosis/Body" → "System Name/Navigation System" → "DTC" on the SST (TaSCAN) screen, and check the DTCs. (see)

        ■ NOTICE ■
