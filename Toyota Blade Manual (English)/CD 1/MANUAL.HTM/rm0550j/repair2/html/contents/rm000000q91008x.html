Power Outlet Socket Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Box Panel NO.2



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

    □ HINT □
    Apply protective tape on the RH side in the same way.

  2. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 clips.




  3. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 2 claws.

  4. Disconnect each connector and remove box panel No.2.



2. Remove Power Outlet Socket Cover



  1. Insert a flat-tip screwdriver into the claw portion of the power outlet socket cover and break off the claw.

  2. Break off the claw on the opposite side of the power outlet socket cover in the same way.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the power point socket assembly and box panel No.2.




  3. Remove the power outlet socket cover and power point socket assembly together from box panel No.2.



3. Remove Power Point Socket Assembly



  1. Using nippers, etc., cut off the area around the 2 claws of the power outlet socket cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the power point socket assembly.

  2. Remove the power point socket assembly from the lid side of the power outlet socket cover.
