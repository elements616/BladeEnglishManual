Function Description


The ABS motor relay and motor fail-safe relay are built into the actuator assembly.

If a motor system malfunction DTC is stored, the motor fail-safe relay cuts off the power supply to the ABS motor relay as a fail-safe.

The skid control computer (built into actuator assembly) turns the ABS motor relay ON to operate the pump motor in the actuator assembly while ABS, TRC, VSC or brake assist is operating.

If the voltage to the relay power source (+BM terminal) drops below the DTC detection condition due to insufficient battery or alternator output, this code may be stored.

DTC Output Related Items
C0273/13
  1. When the ABS motor relay is ON during the initial check or control operation with IG1 terminal voltage at 9.5to17.2V, the ABS motor relay contact remains OFF for 0.1 seconds or more
  2. When the relay is ON with IG terminal voltage below 9.5V, 0.1 seconds or more elapse without the relay contact turning ON
  1. ABS motor relay malfunction
  2. Low voltage to ABS motor relay power source (+BM)
  3. Open in wire harness
  4. Poor connector contact pressure, terminal deformation or corrosion
C0274/14
When the ABS motor relay is OFF, the ABS motor relay contact remains ON for 4 seconds or more
  1. ABS motor relay internal malfunction
  2. Short in motor relay monitor circuit (inside actuator)
  3. Short in wire harness
C1361/91
Immediately after the IG is turned ON, when the motor fail-safe relay is OFF, the contact in the relay remains ON for 4 seconds or more
  1. Motor fail-safe relay internal malfunction
  2. Short in motor relay monitor circuit inside actuator
  3. Short in wire harness
