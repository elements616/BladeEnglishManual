Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding Center LH Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding Center LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the body panel around the roof drip side finish moulding center LH.

  2. Disengage the 2 clips and remove the roof drip finish moulding center LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not remove the clips.
    2. If a clip is damaged, replace it with a supply clip.
