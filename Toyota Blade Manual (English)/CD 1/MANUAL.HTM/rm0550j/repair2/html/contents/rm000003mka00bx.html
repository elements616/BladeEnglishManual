Parking Brake Control Pedal Assembly Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Parking Brake Switch Assembly



  1. Remove the screw and remove the parking brake switch assembly from the parking brake control pedal assembly.



2. Remove Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.1



  1. Remove wire adjusting nut No.1 from parking brake cable assembly No.1.




  2. Raise the claw of the parking brake control pedal assembly.




  3. Remove the clip from parking brake cable assembly No.1.

  4. Remove parking brake cable assembly No.1 from the parking brake control pedal assembly.



3. Remove Parking Pedal Pad

  1. Remove the parking pedal pad from the parking brake pedal.
