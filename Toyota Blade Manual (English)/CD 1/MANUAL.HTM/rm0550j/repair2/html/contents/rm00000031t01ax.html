Hands-free System Precaution

Print



    1. When Hands-free Calls Cannot Be Made

    1. Hands-free cannot be used to make calls in the following cases.


      1. When Bluetooth Connected

        1. When outside the service area
        2. When outgoing calls are restricted, such as when lines are congested
        3. During an HELPNET emergency call, automatic maintenance check or manual maintenance check
        4. When phonebook data is being transferred from the phone
        5. When the phone is dial-locked
        6. When the phone is in use, such as during data communication
        7. When the phone is malfunctioning
        8. When the phone is not connected
        9. When the phone battery charge is insufficient
        10. When the phone power is OFF
        11. While using i-mode, Vodafone live! or EZweb
        12. When the Bluetooth setting of the cellular phone is not in hands-free mode
        13. When the phone itself cannot be used for other reasons

    2. If call waiting or three-way calling is subscribed to, cancel call waiting or three-way calling on the phone itself before use.

    3. When HELPNET is used, hands-free cannot be used while HELPNET is operating. Also, while HELPNET is operating, the line cannot be disconnected by hanging up. Hands-free calls can be made while waiting for automatic incoming calls.
