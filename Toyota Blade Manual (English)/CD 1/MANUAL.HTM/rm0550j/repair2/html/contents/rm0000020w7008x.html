Exhaust Pipe Assembly (2AZ-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Exhaust Tail Pipe Assembly



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.

  2. Disconnect the 2 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust tail pipe assembly.

  3. Remove the gasket.



2. Remove Front Floor Brace Center (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the front floor brace center.



3. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly Center



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  2. Disconnect the 3 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.

  3. Remove the gasket.



4. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly Front



  1. Disconnect the oxygen sensor connector.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs, and remove the exhaust pipe assembly FR.

  3. Remove the gasket.



5. Oxygen Sensor Removal



  1. Using SST, remove the oxygen sensor from the front exhaust pipe assembly.
