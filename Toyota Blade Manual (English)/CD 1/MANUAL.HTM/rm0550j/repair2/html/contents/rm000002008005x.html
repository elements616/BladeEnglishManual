Power Door Lock System Function Inspection

Print


1. Power Door Lock Basic Inspection

□ HINT □
"All doors" in this text refers to "the driver side door, front passenger side door, rear RH door, rear LH door and back door".

  1. Check that all doors lock when the door control switch (for manual operation) is operated to the lock side, and that all doors unlock when it is operated to the unlock side.

  2. Check that all doors lock when the driver side key-linked operation is performed to the lock side from outside the vehicle, and that all doors unlock when it is performed to the unlock side.

    □ HINT □
    With the IG ON and the tongue plate of the seat belt inserted into the buckle of the driver seat belt, the doors cannot be locked or unlocked by key-linked operation.

  3. Key Confinement Prevention Function Inspection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    To prevent the key from being locked in, perform the inspection with the driver side door glass open.


    1. Place the electrical key transmitter (electronic key) inside the vehicle.

    2. With the driver side door open, even if the door lock knob of the driver side door is operated to the lock side, it unlocks immediately.

    3. With the driver side door open, even if the door control switch (for manual operation) is operated to the lock side, it unlocks immediately.

    4. With the driver side door open, if the door lock knob of the driver side door is held to the lock side for 2 seconds or more to lock it and the driver side door is closed, all doors unlock.

  4. Security Function Inspection

    1. Open the driver side door glass and close all doors so that the door control switch can be operated from outside the vehicle.

    2. With the electrical key transmitter (electronic key) outside the vehicle, open the driver side door and close the door with keyless lock; in this state, check that the doors do not unlock even if the door control switch (for manual operation) is operated to the unlock side from outside the vehicle.

    3. With the electrical key transmitter (electronic key) outside the vehicle, close the driver side door and lock the doors by key-linked operation; in this state, check that the doors do not unlock even if the door control switch (for manual operation) is operated to the unlock side from outside the vehicle.

    4. With the electrical key transmitter (electronic key) outside the vehicle, close the driver side door and lock the doors by wireless operation; in this state, check that the doors do not unlock even if the door control switch (for manual operation) is operated to the unlock side from outside the vehicle.

      □ HINT □
      Check that the security function is canceled under the following conditions.
      1. When the IG is turned ON.
      2. When the driver side door is unlocked by key-linked operation.
      3. When the door control knob is unlocked manually and then the door control switch (for manual operation) is operated to unlock.
      4. When the doors are unlocked by wireless operation or smart operation.

  5. Driver Side Key-linked Door Lock/Unlock Prohibition Control Function Inspection

    1. With the IG ON and the tongue plate of the seat belt inserted into the buckle of the driver seat belt, check that key-linked operation does not occur even if the door is locked or unlocked with the key at the driver side door from outside the vehicle.
