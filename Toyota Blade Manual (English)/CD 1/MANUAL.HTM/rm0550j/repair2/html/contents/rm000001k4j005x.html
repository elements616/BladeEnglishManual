Inner Rear View Mirror Assembly Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Inner Rear View Mirror Assembly (Inner Mirror EC Mirror)



  1. Apply black vinyl tape to the ambient light sensor area of the inner rear view mirror assembly.

  2. Connect the battery positive terminal to terminal 2 and the negative terminal to terminal 5 of the inner rear view mirror assembly connector.

  3. Check that the mirror surface changes when light is shone on the mirror surface of the inner rear view mirror assembly.

    Specified Condition
    Light Condition
    		Mirror Surface Condition
    Light not shining on mirror surface
    		No change
    Light shining on mirror surface
    		Mirror surface dims
