Rear Seat Belt Assembly Inner (w/ Center) LH Removal

Print


1. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



2. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



3. Disconnect Rear Seat Cushion Assembly



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.




  2. Flip up the rear seat cushion assembly as shown in the illustration.



4. Remove Rear Seat Inner Belt Assembly (Center) LH



  1. Remove the bolt and remove the rear seat inner belt assembly (center) LH.
