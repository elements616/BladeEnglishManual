Pre-crash Safety System Precaution

Print



    1. Precautions for Troubleshooting

    1. If the malfunction is caused by poor terminal contact or improper part installation, reconnecting the connector or removing and installing the relevant part may return the system to normal, either permanently or temporarily.

    2. To clearly identify the malfunctioning area, check and record the operating conditions at the time of the malfunction, such as DTC output, before disconnecting connectors or removing and installing parts.

    3. The system may be affected by malfunctions other than in the pre-crash safety system, so check and record all current and past DTCs output due to malfunctions in other systems.



      2. Precautions for Handling and Operation

      1. Unless otherwise specified in the inspection procedure, always remove and install each computer, sensor, etc. with the ignition switch OFF.

      2. When any computer, sensor, etc. has been removed and installed, after installing all parts, perform a DTC output check and make sure that no DTCs are output.
