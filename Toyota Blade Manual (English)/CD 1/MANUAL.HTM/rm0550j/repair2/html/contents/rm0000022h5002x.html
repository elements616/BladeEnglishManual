Headlight NO.1 Bulb Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



2. Air Cleaner Inlet NO.1 Removal



  1. Remove the clip.

  2. Disengage the claw and remove the air cleaner inlet NO.1.



3. Remove Headlight NO.1 Bulb



  1. Turn the headlight bulb NO.1 in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove it.
