Front Suspension Crossmember Sub-assembly Removal


  1. Inspect Front Tires for Straight-ahead Position

  2. Secure Steering Wheel

  3. Remove Column Hole Cover Silencer Sheet

  4. Disconnect Steering Intermediate Shaft Assembly NO.2

  5. Disconnect Steering Column Hole Cover Sub-assembly NO.1

  6. Remove Front Tire

  7. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  8. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.2

  9. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear LH

  10. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear RH

  11. Disconnect Tie Rod End Sub-assembly LH

  12. Disconnect Tie Rod End Sub-assembly RH

  13. Disconnect Front Stabilizer Link Assembly LH

  14. Disconnect Front Stabilizer Link Assembly RH

  15. Disconnect Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 LH

  16. Disconnect Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 RH

  17. Loosen Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 LH

  18. Loosen Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 RH

  19. Remove Engine Front Mounting Bracket Reinforcement Lower

  20. Remove Front Suspension Member Reinforcement LH

  21. Remove Front Suspension Member Reinforcement RH

  22. Remove Front Suspension Member Brace Rear LH

  23. Remove Front Suspension Member Brace Rear RH

  24. Remove Front Suspension Crossmember Sub-assembly

  25. Remove Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 LH

  26. Remove Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 RH

  27. Remove Front Suspension Member Brace Front LH

  28. Remove Front Suspension Member Brace Front RH

  29. Remove Stabilizer Bar Front

  30. Remove Steering Link Assembly
