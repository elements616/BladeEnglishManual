DTC P2714/77 Line Pressure Control Linear Solenoid Circuit

Print

Preparation List


Function Description


Based on the signals from the accelerator position sensor and transmission revolution sensors, the transmission control computer controls the line pressure control solenoid assembly (SLT) to a predetermined duty ratio. As a result, the line pressure is regulated to a pressure that matches the throttle opening angle and engine output.

The transmission control computer calculates the heat generated by the friction material from the speeds of the transmission revolution sensors (NT and NC), and detects slipping of the clutches, etc. As a fail-safe, the operation of the throttle motor is limited and shifting to 5 and 6 is prohibited on the 2 trip.

■ NOTICE ■
DTC P2714/77 indicates that the solenoid (SLT) is [stuck ON (malfunction)]. If the solenoid (SLT) remains ON, the line pressure and clutch pressure drop, which may cause the friction material to burn and the vehicle to become undrivable.

Inspection Area
P2714/77
  1. ATF temperature is -10°C or higher and the vehicle is being driven in D range (however, no malfunction is detected in SL1, SL2, SL3, SL4 solenoids, transmission revolution sensors (NT and NC) or fluid temperature sensor)
  2. In any gear position, the transmission revolution sensor (NC) speed is lower than normal relative to the transmission revolution sensor (NT) speed
  3. 2 seconds or more
  4. 2 trip
  1. Line Pressure Control Solenoid Assembly (SLT)
  2. Torque Converter Assembly
  3. Transaxle Assembly
  4. Transmission Control Computer Assembly
