Front Bumper Assembly Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Front Spoiler Cover LH (w/ Front Spoiler)



  1. Remove the 5 front spoiler retainers and front spoiler cover LH.



2. Remove Front Spoiler Cover RH (w/ Front Spoiler)

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



3. Remove Front Spoiler Side Protector LH (w/ Front Spoiler)



  1. Remove the front spoiler side protector LH.



4. Remove Front Spoiler Side Protector LH (w/ Front Spoiler)

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



5. Remove Fog Light Assembly LH



  1. Remove the screw and remove the fog light assembly LH.



6. Remove Fog Light Assembly RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



7. Remove Hood to Front End Panel Seal



  1. Disengage the 5 clips.

  2. Peel off the double-sided tape and remove the hood to front end panel seal.



8. Remove Radiator Grille Molding UPR RH (Engine Type 2GR-FE)



  1. Remove the 4 screws and radiator grille molding UPR.



9. Remove Radiator Grille Sub-assembly (Radiator Grille Bar Type)



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  2. Disengage the 12 claws and remove the radiator grille sub-assembly.



10. Remove Radiator Grille (Radiator Grille Bar Type)



  1. Disengage the 6 claws and remove the radiator grille.



11. Remove Radiator Grille Sub-assembly (Radiator Grille Mesh Type)



  1. Disengage the 6 claws and remove the radiator grille sub-assembly.



12. Remove Radiator Grille Emblem Assembly (w/o Radar Cruise)



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and 2 guides, and remove the radiator grille emblem assembly.



13. Remove Radiator Grille Emblem Assembly (w/ Radar Cruise)



  1. Remove the 2 spring nuts.

  2. Disengage the claws and remove the radiator grille emblem assembly.



14. Remove Front Bumper Hole Cover LH



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front bumper hole cover LH.



15. Remove Front Bumper Hole Cover RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



16. Remove Radiator Grille LWR LH



  1. Disengage the 6 claws and remove the radiator grille LWR LH.



17. Remove Radiator Grille LWR RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



18. Remove Front Bumper Hole Cover Assembly LH



  1. Remove the screw.




  2. Disengage the 8 claws and remove the front bumper hole cover assembly LH.



19. Remove Front Bumper Hole Cover Assembly RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



20. Remove Front Bumper Side Retainer LH



  1. Remove the bolt and screw.

  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front bumper side retainer LH.



21. Remove Front Bumper Side Retainer RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



22. Remove Front Bumper Energy Absorber



  1. Disengage the 3 fitting points of the front bumper energy absorber, and remove the front bumper energy absorber from the front bumper reinforcement.



23. Remove Front Bumper Reinforcement Sub-assembly



  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 8 clamps and disconnect the harness.




  2. Remove the 8 bolts and front bumper reinforcement.
