Clutch Control Solenoid NO.2 (U660E) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Operation Inspection



  1. Check that the valve in the solenoid operates when the battery positive is connected to terminal 2 and the battery negative is connected to terminal 1 of clutch control solenoid No.2 (SL2, SL4) through the 12V-21W valve.

    Specified Condition
    Operates (operating sound heard)



2. Resistance Inspection

  1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), check the resistance between the terminals.

    SST
    09082-00030  
    09083-00170  

    Resistance
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Standard
    1 - 2
    		Always
    		5.0 to 5.6Ω(20°C)
