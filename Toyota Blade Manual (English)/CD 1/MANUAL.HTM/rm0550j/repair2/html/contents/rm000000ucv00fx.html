      2.Steering Column Handling and Work Precautions

      1. Do not subject the steering column assembly (especially the motor and torque sensor) to impacts. If it has been subjected to a severe impact (such as being dropped on the floor), replace it with a new part.

      2. When carrying the steering column assembly, do not pull on the harness.
