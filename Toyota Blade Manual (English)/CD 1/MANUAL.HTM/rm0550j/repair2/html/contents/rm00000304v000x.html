ABS & Traction Actuator Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Front Wiper Arm Head Cap



  1. Remove the 2 front wiper arm head caps.



3. Remove Front Wiper Arm & Blade Assembly RH



  1. Remove the front wiper arm nut and front wiper arm & blade assembly RH.



4. Remove Front Wiper Arm & Blade Assembly LH



  1. Remove the front wiper arm nut and front wiper arm & blade assembly LH.



5. Remove Hood to Cowl Top Seal



  1. Disengage the 7 clips and remove the hood to cowl top seal.



6. Remove Front Side Panel Protector NO.1 LH



  1. Disengage the pin and remove front side panel protector NO.1 LH.



7. Remove Cowl Top Ventilator Louver LH



  1. Disengage the 11 claws and remove the cowl top ventilator louver LH as shown in the illustration.



8. Remove Front Side Panel Protector NO.1 RH



  1. Disengage the pin and remove front side panel protector NO.1 RH.



9. Remove Cowl Top Ventilator Louver RH



  1. Disengage the 5 claws and remove the cowl top ventilator louver RH as shown in the illustration.



10. Remove Windshield Wiper Motor & Link Assembly



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the windshield wiper motor & link assembly.

  2. Disconnect the connector and clamp, and remove the windshield wiper motor & link assembly as shown in the illustration.



11. Remove Windshield Wiper Motor Assembly



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disconnect the rod of the windshield wiper link assembly from the crank arm pivot of the windshield wiper motor assembly as shown in the illustration.




  2. Put matchmarks on the crank arm assembly of the front wiper link assembly and the windshield wiper motor assembly as shown in the illustration.

  3. Remove the nut and separate the crank arm assembly of the front wiper link assembly from the windshield wiper motor assembly.




  4. Disconnect the connector.

  5. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the windshield wiper motor assembly from the front wiper link assembly.



12. Remove Cowl Body Mounting Reinforcement RH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the cowl body mounting reinforcement RH from the cowl top panel outer.



13. Remove Cowl Top Panel Outer



  1. Disconnect the windshield deicer wire harness connector and disengage the 3 clamps, and detach them from the cowl top panel outer. (w/ Cold Area Specification)




  2. Disengage the clamps of the heater air duct splash shield seal No.2 and water guard plate RH, and bend each as shown in the illustration.




  3. Remove the 8 bolts and the cowl top panel outer.



14. Remove Engine Cover Sub-assembly NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and engine cover assembly No.1.



15. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose

  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose.




  2. Disconnect the vacuum switching valve connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.

  3. Disconnect the fuel vapor feed hose and fuel vapor feed hose No.2.




  4. Disconnect the intake air flow meter connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness from the air cleaner cap.




  5. Lock air cleaner hose clamp No.1.

  6. With the clamp locked, remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



16. Remove Air Cleaner Case

  1. Remove the air cleaner filter element.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and clamp, and remove the air cleaner case.



17. Disconnect Wire Harness



  1. Remove the clamp, raise the lock lever and disconnect the engine control computer connector.




  2. Remove the 2 nuts from the engine room junction block, disconnect the 2 connectors and 2 clamps, and disconnect the wire harness.




  3. Remove the bolt and clamp, and disconnect the ground wire of the engine room wire harness.




  4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground wire of the engine room wire harness.



18. Drain Brake Fluid


19. Remove ABS & Traction Actuator Assembly w/ Bracket



  1. Move the lock lever as shown in the illustration to release the connector lock, and disconnect the connector from the actuator assembly.




  2. Using a union nut wrench 10mm, disconnect the 6 brake tubes.




  3. Disconnect the brake tube w/ tube clamp and fuel tube w/ fuel tube clamp No.3 from the actuator bracket assembly.

  4. Remove the 3 nuts and remove the ABS & traction actuator assembly together with the bracket.



20. Remove ABS & Traction Actuator Assembly



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and brake actuator assembly from the brake actuator bracket assembly.
