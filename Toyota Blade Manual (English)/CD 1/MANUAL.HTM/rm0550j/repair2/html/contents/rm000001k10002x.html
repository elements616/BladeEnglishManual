Television Amplifier Antenna Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Rear Door Scuff Plate RH



  1. Disengage the 8 claws and remove the rear door scuff plate RH.



3. Remove Rear Door Opening Trim Weatherstrip RH



  1. Remove the rear door opening trim weatherstrip RH.



4. Remove Package Tray Trim Panel Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 suspenders from the package tray trim garnish hooks.

  2. Disengage the 2 pins and remove the package tray trim panel assembly.



5. Remove Rear Floor Mat Rear



  1. Remove the rear floor mat rear.



6. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly


7. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly CTR


8. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



9. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



10. Disconnect Rear Seat Outer Belt Assembly Center



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear seat outer belt assembly center together with the rear seat inner belt assembly.




  2. Remove the washer and rear seat outer belt assembly center from the rear seat inner belt assembly.



11. Remove Rear Seat Assembly RH



  1. Remove the 2 front side bolts of the rear seat assembly RH.




  2. Fold the rear seat assembly RH forward.

  3. Remove the 2 rear side bolts and remove the rear seat assembly RH.



12. Remove Deck Floor Box RH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the deck floor box RH.



13. Remove Luggage Hold Belt Striker Assembly (Rear Side Installation Position)



  1. Remove the bolt and the trunk (luggage compartment) hold belt striker.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the LH side using the same procedure.



14. Remove Deck Trim Cover Rear



  1. Disengage the 10 claws and remove the deck trim cover rear.



15. Remove Rear Seatback Hinge Sub-assembly (Installation Position RH Side)



  1. Remove the bolt and the rear seatback hinge.



16. Remove Rear Seat Side Garnish RH



  1. Disengage the 6 claws and 2 clips.

  2. Remove the rear seat outer belt assembly RH from the guide and remove the rear seat side garnish RH.



17. Remove Luggage Hold Belt Striker Assembly (Installation Position RH Side)



  1. Remove the bolt and the trunk (luggage compartment) hold belt striker.



18. Remove Deck Trim Side Panel Assembly RH



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and 4 clips.

  2. Remove the rear seat outer belt assembly RH from the guide and remove the deck trim side panel assembly RH.



19. Remove Roof Side Garnish Assembly INN RH



  1. Disengage the 4 clips and remove the roof side garnish assembly inner RH.

    □ HINT □
    The clips remain on the body side.

  2. Remove the clips from the body.



20. Remove Amplifier Antenna Assembly



  1. Disconnect the connectors.

  2. Remove the bolt and remove the amplifier antenna assembly.
