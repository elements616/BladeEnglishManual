Back Door Stay Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Back Door Stay Assembly LH

■ NOTICE ■
  1. When removing and installing, hold the cylinder and avoid touching the rod stroke area as much as possible to prevent foreign matter from adhering.
  2. Do not touch the rod with work gloves or similar, as fibers may adhere and cause gas leakage.
  3. Do not apply a load in the lateral direction of the cylinder, to prevent the rod from deforming.




  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, remove the stop ring along the groove.

  2. Disengage the ball joint and remove the back door stay assembly LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Remove the back door stay assembly LH while holding the back door so that it does not lower.



2. Remove Back Door Stay Bolt



  1. Remove the back door stay bolt.



3. Remove Back Door Damper Stay Bracket Upper LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and back door damper stay bracket upper LH.
