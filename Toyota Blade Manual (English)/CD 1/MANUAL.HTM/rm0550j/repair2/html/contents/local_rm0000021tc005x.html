Rear Drive Shaft (4WD) Disassembly


  1. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Inboard Joint Boot Clamp LH NO.2

  2. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Inboard Joint Boot Clamp LH

  3. Disconnect Drive Shaft Inboard Joint Boot

  4. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Inboard Joint Assembly LH

  5. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Inboard Joint Snap Ring LH

  6. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Dust Cover LH
