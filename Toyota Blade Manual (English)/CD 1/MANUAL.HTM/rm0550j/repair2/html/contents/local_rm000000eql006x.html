Automatic Transaxle Assembly (U660E) Precautions



  1. Before disassembly, clean off sand, mud, etc. adhering to the outside of the transmission so that foreign matter does not enter the transmission during removal and installation.

  1. Until the oil pan is removed, do not turn the transmission with the oil pan side up, as foreign matter may enter the valve body.

  1. During removal and installation, work with bare hands or vinyl gloves. Do not use work gloves, shop rags, etc., as lint may enter.

  1. Always keep removed parts in order and protect them from dust, etc.

  1. Apply Toyota Genuine ATF WS to sliding and rotating surfaces before installation.

  1. Be very careful not to scratch the contact surfaces of the case, as this may cause oil leaks.

  1. Before applying seal packing, completely remove the old seal packing from the sealing surfaces and clean them with white gasoline.

  1. After installing the sealed parts, do not fill with fluid immediately; leave them for at least 1 hours.

  1. Use Toyota Genuine ATF WS when filling fluid.
