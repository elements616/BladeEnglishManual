Front Lower Ball Joint Assembly LH Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Front Lower Ball Joint Assembly LH



  1. Lower Ball Joint Turning Condition Inspection

    1. Install the nut to the stud of the front lower ball joint assembly LH.

    2. Move the stud back and forth 5 times, then turn it continuously at a rate of 3 to 5 seconds per turn, and measure the turning torque on the 5th turn.

      Standard
      T = 0.98 to 3.43 N/m{ 10 to 35 kgf/cm }

    3. Turn the stud.

      Specified Condition
      No abnormal catching or play during rotation

  2. Dust Boot Inspection

    1. Check that the dust boot has no cracks or grease leaks.
