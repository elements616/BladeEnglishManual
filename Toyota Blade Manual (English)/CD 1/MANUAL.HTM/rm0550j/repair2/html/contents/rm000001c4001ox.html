    Step 2Customer Problem Analysis and Symptom Check

    1. Customer Problem Analysis and Symptom Check (see)

      1. The hands-free system provides services using communication. Therefore, it is important to determine whether the problem is caused by the communication system, including radio wave conditions, or by something else. Perform thorough customer problem analysis according to the questionnaire, and ask about the conditions of occurrence carefully to determine whether the problem may be temporary due to poor radio wave conditions.

        □ HINT □
        If conditions such as being unable to make hands-free calls occur temporarily, this is due to radio wave conditions or base station congestion and is not a malfunction. (Explain to the customer and have them continue using it)
