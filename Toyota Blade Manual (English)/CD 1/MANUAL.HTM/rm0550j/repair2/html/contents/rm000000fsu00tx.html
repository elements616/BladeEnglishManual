Preparation List



1. Time Lag Test

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Be sure to perform this after inspecting and adjusting the engine.
  2. Perform the test at normal operating CVT fluid temperature (50 to 100°C).
  3. Perform the test with the A/C OFF.

  1. Apply the parking brake and chock the wheels.

  2. Start the engine.

  3. While depressing the brake pedal, shift from the N position to the D or R position at idle speed, and measure the time until a slight shock is felt.

    Standard
    N → D position 1.2 seconds or less
    N → R position 1.5 seconds or less

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Take several measurements and use the average value.
    2. When measuring repeatedly, allow an interval of 1 minutes.



2. Stall Speed Test

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Be sure to perform this after inspecting and adjusting the engine.
  2. Perform the test at normal operating CVT fluid temperature (50 to 100°C).
  3. Perform the test with the A/C OFF.
  4. Do not continuously run the test for 5 seconds or more.
  5. Perform the test in a place with a high μ (coefficient of friction), such as on asphalt, so that the tires do not spin.

  1. Apply the parking brake and chock the wheels.

  2. Using SST (TaSCAN), following the screen display, display the [ECU Data Monitor] screen and select [Engine Speed].

    SST
    09991-70200  

  3. Start the engine.

  4. Firmly depress the brake pedal with your left foot, shift to the D position, fully depress the accelerator pedal with your right foot, and quickly read the highest engine speed.

4. Shift Schedule

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Be sure to perform this after inspecting and adjusting the engine.
  2. Perform the test at normal operating CVT fluid temperature (50 to 100°C).
  3. Perform the test with no electrical load, the A/C OFF and the brake OFF.

□ HINT □
  1. Lock-up control is not performed when the CVT fluid temperature is 40°C or less and the vehicle speed is 60km/h or less, or when the CVT fluid temperature is 5°C or less.
  2. Lock-up control is not performed when the engine coolant temperature is 30°C or less.
  3. The lock-up OFF vehicle speed varies according to the ON/OFF status of the electrical load, A/C and brake.

5. Road Test

■ WARNING ■
Make sure that the road is safe before performing the test.

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Be sure to perform this after inspecting and adjusting the engine.
  2. Perform the test at normal operating CVT fluid temperature (50 to 100°C).
  3. Perform the test with the A/C OFF.


□ HINT □

Road Test List 
Inspection Item
Inspection and Check Procedure
D Position Shift Function
Check that the transmission shifts during normal driving (typical city driving). (Inspect based on the shift schedule)
Transmission Control Switch Operation
(D ←→ M Selector Switch Operation)
Move the shift lever to the M position and check that gears can be changed with sequential shifting.
Kick-down Function
Check that kick-down occurs according to the shift schedule.
Engine Braking
  1. While driving in 7 gear in the M position (approximately 90 to 100km/h), shift to "-" on the sequential gate and check engine braking in 6 gear.
  2. While driving in 6 gear in the M position (approximately 70 to 80km/h), shift to "-" on the sequential gate and check engine braking in 5 gear.
  3. While driving in 5 gear in the M position (approximately 50 to 60km/h), shift to "-" on the sequential gate and check engine braking in 4 gear.
  4. While driving in 4 gear in the M position (approximately 40 to 50km/h), shift to "-" on the sequential gate and check engine braking in 3 gear.
  5. While driving in 3 gear in the M position (approximately 30 to 40km/h), shift to "-" on the sequential gate and check engine braking in 2 gear.
  6. While driving in 2 gear in the M position (approximately 20 to 30km/h), shift to "-" on the sequential gate and check engine braking in 1 gear.
P Position Operation
Stop the vehicle on a slope (approximately 5° or more), apply the parking brake in the P position, and check that the vehicle does not move when the parking brake is released.
Abnormal Noise, Vibration
Check for abnormal noise and vibration while driving and during shifting.
