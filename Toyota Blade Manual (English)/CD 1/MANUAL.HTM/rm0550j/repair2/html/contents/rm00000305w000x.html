Exhaust Manifold (2AZ-FE) Removal

Print


1. Remove Front Tire RH


2. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear RH

  1. Remove the 5 clips and remove the RH rear engine cover.



3. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove the engine under cover No.1.



4. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



5. Remove Fan & Alternator V Belt



  1. Using SST, slowly turn the V-belt tensioner clockwise.

    SST
    09961-01270  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Check that the SST and tools are securely set.
    2. When compressing the V-belt tensioner, slowly apply force perpendicular to the tool, taking 3 seconds or more.

  2. Remove the V-belt from each pulley and slowly return the tensioner.



6. Remove Alternator Assembly



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the terminal cap.

  3. Remove the nut and disconnect the harness from the +B terminal.

  4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the clamp bracket.

  5. Disconnect the harness clamp.




  6. Remove the 2 bolts and the Alternator assembly.



7. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly Front



  1. Disconnect the oxygen sensor connector.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the exhaust center pipe assembly.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs, and remove the exhaust pipe assembly FR.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



8. Remove Oil Level Gauge Sub-assembly

  1. Remove the oil level gauge.



9. Remove Oil Level Gauge Guide



  1. Remove the bolt and remove the oil level gauge guide.

  2. Remove the O-ring from the oil level gauge guide.



10. Remove Exhaust Manifold Heat Insulator NO.1



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and remove the exhaust manifold heat insulator No.1.



11. Air Fuel Ratio Sensor Removal



  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.




  2. Using SST, disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor from the exhaust manifold.

    SST
    09224-00010  



12. Remove Manifold Stay



  1. Remove the bolt and nut and remove the manifold stay.



13. Remove Manifold Stay NO.2



  1. Remove the bolt and nut and remove manifold stay No.2.



14. Remove Exhaust Manifold Converter Sub-assembly



  1. Remove the 5 nuts and remove the exhaust manifold converter.

  2. Remove the exhaust manifold to head gasket from the cylinder head.
