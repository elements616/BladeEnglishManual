Cooler Compressor Assembly (Engine Type 2GR-FE) Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Magnetic Clutch Assembly



  1. Secure the cooler compressor w/ magnetic clutch assembly in a vise with aluminum plates.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Be careful not to pinch the harness.

  2. Using vise pliers with a shop rag in between, hold the magnetic clutch hub.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Use a shop rag or equivalent so as not to damage the clutch hub.

  3. Remove the bolt, and remove the magnetic clutch hub and magnetic clutch washers.

    □ HINT □
    There are 3 or fewer magnetic clutch washers. They are used for adjustment, so the number is not fixed.




  4. Disconnect the magnetic clutch assembly connector.

  5. Remove the screw and disconnect the ground wire.

  6. Disengage the clamp and remove the bracket.




  7. Using SST, remove the snap ring and remove the clutch rotor from the cooler compressor assembly.

    SST
    09904-00010   (09904-00050)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When removing the snap ring, do not damage the seal cover of the bearing.




  8. Using SST, remove the snap ring and remove the magnetic clutch stator from the cooler compressor assembly.

    SST
    09904-00010   (09904-00050)
