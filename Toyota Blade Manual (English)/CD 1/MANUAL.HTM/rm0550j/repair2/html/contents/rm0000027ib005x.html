2. Check Front Wheels Straight-ahead Position

4. Remove Front Tire


5. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove the engine under cover No.1.



6. Remove Engine Under Cover

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 4 screws, and remove the engine under cover.



7. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.2

  1. Remove the 5 clips and engine under cover No.2.



8. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear LH

  1. Remove the 5 clips and the engine under cover rear LH.



9. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



10. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



11. Drain Engine Oil

  1. Remove the drain plug and drain the engine oil.

  2. After draining the oil, install the drain plug with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 25 N/m{ 250 kgf/cm }



12. Drain Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)

■ WARNING ■
To avoid the danger of being burned, do not remove the reservoir tank cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Liquid and steam will spray out under pressure.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  2. Remove the radiator cap and drain the coolant.



  3. Loosen the cylinder block drain cock plug and drain the coolant from the engine.





13. Drain CVT Fluid

  1. Using a socket hexagon wrench 10, remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the fluid.

  2. Using a socket hexagon wrench 10, tighten the drain plug with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 47 N/m{ 479 kgf/cm }



14. Drain Transfer Oil (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Using straight hexagon 10mm and an offset wrench (10X12 long), remove transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) and the gasket.

  2. Using socket hexagon 10mm, remove the transfer drain plug and gasket, and drain the transfer oil.

  3. Install the drain plug with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 39 N/m{ 398 kgf/cm }



15. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.




  3. Disconnect the current sensor connector.

  4. Remove the insulator.

  5. Remove the battery.



16. Remove Engine Cover Sub-assembly NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and engine cover assembly No.1.



17. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose

  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose.




  2. Disconnect the vacuum switching valve connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.

  3. Disconnect the fuel vapor feed hose and fuel vapor feed hose No.2.




  4. Disconnect the intake air flow meter connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness from the air cleaner cap.




  5. Lock air cleaner hose clamp No.1.

  6. With the clamp locked, remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



18. Remove Air Cleaner Case

  1. Remove the air cleaner filter element.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and clamp, and remove the air cleaner case.



19. Remove Battery Carrier



  1. Remove the battery tray.

  2. Detach the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness from the battery carrier.

  3. Remove the 4 bolts and battery carrier.



20. Remove Battery Carrier NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and battery carrier No.2.



21. Remove Fan & Alternator V Belt



  1. Using SST, slowly turn the V-belt tensioner clockwise.

    SST
    09961-01270  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Check that the SST and tools are securely set.
    2. When compressing the V-belt tensioner, slowly apply force perpendicular to the tool, taking 3 seconds or more.

  2. Remove the V-belt from each pulley and slowly return the tensioner.



22. Remove Alternator Assembly



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the terminal cap.

  3. Remove the nut and disconnect the harness from the +B terminal.

  4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the clamp bracket.

  5. Disconnect the harness clamp.




  6. Remove the 2 bolts and the Alternator assembly.



23. Disconnect Radiator Outlet Hose



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the radiator outlet hose.



24. Disconnect Radiator Inlet Hose



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the radiator inlet hose.



25. Disconnect Heater Water Outlet Hose



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the heater outlet water hose.



26. Disconnect Heater Water Inlet Hose A



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect heater water inlet hose A.



27. Disconnect Check Valve to Connector Tube Hose



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the check valve to connector tube hose.



28. Disconnect Transmission Control Cable Assembly



  1. Remove the nut and clip, and disconnect the transmission control cable.




  2. Remove the clamp and disconnect the transmission control cable.




  3. Remove the bolt and disconnect the transmission control cable support from the engine mounting insulator RR.



29. Disconnect Engine Wire



  1. Remove the clamp, raise the lock lever and disconnect the engine control computer connector.




  2. Remove the 2 nuts from the engine room junction block, disconnect the 2 connectors and 2 clamps, and disconnect the wire harness.




  3. Remove the bolt and clamp, and disconnect the ground wire of the engine room wire harness.




  4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground wire of the engine room wire harness.



30. Disconnect Compressor Assembly (with Pulley)





    1. Disconnect the compressor assembly connector.

    2. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts.

    3. Using a torx socket wrench (E8), remove the 2 stud bolts and disconnect the compressor assembly.

      □ HINT □
      Without disconnecting the hoses, suspend the compressor with rope, etc.



31. Disconnect Fuel Tube Sub-assembly



  1. Disengage the claw and remove EFI fuel pipe clamp No.1.




  2. Pinch the retainer of the fuel tube connector from both sides with your fingers, pull out the fuel tube connector and disconnect the fuel tube from the pipe.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Before removal, check for mud or other dirt around the pipe and connector, and wipe it off if present.
    2. If the connector and pipe are stuck, pinch the retainer by hand and push and pull to release the connection and pull it out. Do not use tools at this time.
    3. When disconnecting, cover the connection with a shop rag or equivalent to prevent fuel from splashing.
    4. Inspect the sealing surface of the removed pipe for mud, etc., and wipe it off if present.



32. Remove Fuel Pipe Support



  1. Remove the bolt and clamp, and remove the fuel pipe support.



33. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly Front



  1. Disconnect the oxygen sensor connector.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the exhaust center pipe assembly.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs, and remove the exhaust pipe assembly FR.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



34. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly CTR (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.

  2. Disconnect the 3 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.

  3. Remove the gasket.



35. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.2 (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Remove the 4 nuts and remove the front floor heat insulator No.2.



36. Remove Propeller Shaft with Center Bearing Assembly (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and center bearing washers, and disconnect the propeller with center bearing shaft assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. When removing, do not apply excessive force to the universal joints.
    2. During removal, transport and storage, do not allow the No. 2 joint angle to be 20° or more.




  2. Put matchmarks on the differential carrier assembly and propeller shaft assembly.

  3. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers, and disconnect the propeller shaft assembly from the differential carrier assembly.




  4. Put matchmarks on the transfer assembly and propeller shaft assembly.

  5. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers, and disconnect the propeller shaft assembly from the transfer assembly.



37. Remove Column Hole Cover Silencer Sheet



  1. Turn back the floor carpet, remove the 2 clips, and remove the column hole cover silencer sheet.



38. Disconnect Steering Intermediate Shaft Assembly NO.2



  1. Remove the bolt from steering intermediate shaft assembly No.2.

  2. Slide steering intermediate shaft assembly No.2 toward the steering column assembly until the serrations of the steering intermediate shaft are visible.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not disconnect steering intermediate shaft assembly No.2 from the steering intermediate shaft.




  3. Put matchmarks on steering intermediate shaft assembly No.2 and the steering intermediate shaft.

  4. Disconnect steering intermediate shaft assembly No.2 from the steering intermediate shaft.



39. Disconnect Speed Sensor Front LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the speed sensor wire and flexible hose.




  2. Remove the bolt and disconnect the speed sensor front LH from the steering knuckle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not allow foreign matter to adhere to the tip and installation part of the speed sensor front LH.



40. Disconnect Speed Sensor Front RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



41. Remove Front Axle Shaft Nut LH



  1. Set the SST aligned with the groove of the front drive shaft assembly LH, and using a hammer, unstake the front axle hub nut LH.

    SST
    09930-00010  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set SST in the groove with its flat surface facing up.
    2. Never grind the tip of SST with a grinder or similar tool.
    3. When removing the front axle hub nut LH, completely unstake it.
    4. Do not damage the threads of the front drive shaft assembly LH.

  2. Using a socket wrench (30mm), remove the front axle hub nut LH.



42. Remove Front Axle Shaft Nut RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



43. Disconnect Front Stabilizer Link Assembly LH



  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the front stabilizer link assembly LH from the front shock absorber w/ coil spring.

    □ HINT □
    Using socket hexagon wrench 6, hold the stud bolt so that it does not spin.



44. Disconnect Front Stabilizer Link Assembly RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



45. Disconnect Tie Rod End Sub-assembly LH

  1. Remove the cotter pin and castle nut.




  2. Using SST, disconnect the tie rod end LH from the steering knuckle.

    SST
    09960-20010   (09961-02010, 09961-02050)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not damage the front disc brake dust cover.
    2. To prevent SST from falling, be sure to suspend it with a string or similar.
    3. Do not damage the ball joint dust cover.
    4. Do not damage the steering knuckle.



46. Disconnect Tie Rod End Sub-assembly RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



47. Disconnect Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 LH



  1. Remove the 1 bolts and 2 nuts.

  2. Push down the front suspension lower arm No.1 LH and disconnect it from the front lower ball joint LH.



48. Disconnect Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



49. Disconnect Front Axle Assembly LH



  1. Using a plastic hammer, lightly tap the end of the front drive shaft assembly LH to disengage the front drive shaft assembly LH from the front axle assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    If the engagement is tight, tap the end of the front drive shaft assembly LH using a brass bar and hammer.

  2. Push the front axle assembly LH toward the outside of the vehicle and pull the front drive shaft assembly LH out of the front axle assembly LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not push the front axle assembly LH out farther than necessary.
    2. Do not damage the front axle outboard joint boot.
    3. Do not damage the speed sensor rotor.
    4. Suspend the front drive shaft assembly LH with wire or similar.



50. Disconnect Front Axle Assembly RH

□ HINT □
Use the same procedure for the RH side as for the LH side.



51. Remove Front Drive Shaft Assembly LH



  1. Using SST, remove the drive shaft assembly LH.

    SST
    09520-00031  
    09520-01010  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not damage the oil seal.
    2. Do not damage the boot.
    3. Do not drop the drive shaft.



52. Remove Front Drive Shaft Assembly RH (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and drive shaft assembly RH from the bearing bracket.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not damage the oil seal.
    2. Do not damage the boot.
    3. Do not drop the drive shaft assembly.

    □ HINT □
    If the fit is tight, lightly tap the inboard joint assembly using a brass bar and hammer.



53. Remove Front Drive Shaft Assembly RH (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, compress the drive shaft bearing bracket hole snap ring and remove it from the bearing bracket.

  2. Remove bearing bracket bolt No.1 and remove the front drive shaft assembly RH from the drive shaft bearing bracket.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not damage the oil seal.
    2. Do not damage the boot.
    3. Do not drop the drive shaft assembly.
    4. When removing/installing the drive shaft assembly RH, drain all the transaxle oil and transfer oil.
    5. If the drive shaft assembly RH is removed/installed without draining the oil, transfer oil will flow into the transaxle side. As the transaxle and transfer use different oils, the 2 types of oil will mix, and internal cleaning of the transaxle and transfer will be necessary.

59. Remove Engine Assembly w/ Transaxle



  1. Set the height-adjustable attachment and plate lift attachment, and set the engine lifter.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set the engine assembly w/ transaxle, front crossmember and front suspension crossmember so that they are level.
    2. Securely support the engine assembly w/ transaxle, front crossmember and front suspension crossmember so that they do not fall over.




  2. Remove the bolt and 2 nuts, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator RH from the engine mounting bracket RH.




  3. Remove the bolt and nut, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator LH from the engine mounting bracket LH.




  4. Remove the 12 bolts and operate the engine lifter to slowly remove the engine assembly w/ transaxle, front crossmember and front suspension crossmember from the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Remove it while taking care not to damage the wire harnesses and steering shaft.

  2. Check that the timing marks of the camshaft timing gears and the front marks of the bearing caps are at the positions shown in the illustration.

    □ HINT □
    If the marks are not aligned, turn the crankshaft one revolution and align them again.

89. Remove Crankshaft Pulley

    09213-54015   (91651-60855)

90. Remove Crankshaft Position Sensor

91. Remove Oil Pan Sub-assembly



  1. Remove the 12 bolts and 2 nuts.




  2. Using an oil pan seal cutter, remove the oil pan.

    ■ NOTICE ■

92. Remove Timing Chain or Belt Cover Sub-assembly

  1. Using a torx socket wrench (E10), remove the stud bolt.




  2. Remove the 12 bolts and 2 nuts.




  3. Using a screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, pry at the location shown in the illustration and remove the timing chain cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the timing chain cover, cylinder block or cylinder head.



93. Remove Timing Gear Case or Timing Chain Case Oil Seal



  1. Using a utility knife, cut off the oil seal lip.

  2. Using a screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, pry out the oil seal.

    □ HINT □
    After removing the oil seal, check that the crankshaft is not damaged. If there are scratches, correct them with sandpaper (#400).
