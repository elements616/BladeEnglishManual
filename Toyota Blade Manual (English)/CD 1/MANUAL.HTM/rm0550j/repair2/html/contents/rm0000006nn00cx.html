Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3 Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



2. Remove Console Box Carpet



  1. Remove the console box carpet.



3. Remove Console Box Assembly RR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the console box assembly RR.



4. Remove Indoor Electrical Key Oscillator



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Detach the clamp and remove the indoor electrical key oscillator.



5. Remove Console Box Mounting Bracket NO.2 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Turn back the floor carpet.

  2. Remove the 4 bolts and console box mounting bracket No.2.



6. Remove Console Box Mounting Bracket NO.2 (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Turn back the floor carpet.

  2. Remove the 3 bolts and console box mounting bracket No.2.



7. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3



  1. Loosen the lock nut and adjusting nut of parking brake cable assembly No.1.




  2. Disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the parking brake equalizer.



8. Remove Front Floor Brace Center (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the front floor brace center.



9. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly CTR (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts.

  3. Disconnect the 3 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



10. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly CTR (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts.

  3. Disconnect the 3 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



11. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.1 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 3 nuts and front floor heat insulator No.1.



12. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.2 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 3 nuts and front floor heat insulator No.2.



13. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.2 (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Remove the 4 nuts and remove the front floor heat insulator No.2.



14. Remove Propeller Shaft with Center Bearing Assembly (Drive Type 4WD)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and center bearing washers, and disconnect the propeller with center bearing shaft assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. When removing, do not apply excessive force to the universal joints.
    2. During removal, transport and storage, do not allow the No. 2 joint angle to be 20° or more.




  2. Put matchmarks on the differential carrier assembly and propeller shaft assembly.

  3. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers, and disconnect the propeller shaft assembly from the differential carrier assembly.




  4. Put matchmarks on the transfer assembly and propeller shaft assembly.

  5. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers, and disconnect the propeller shaft assembly from the transfer assembly.



15. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector



  1. Remove the parking brake lever protector from the parking brake cable assembly No.3.



16. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Support Clamp Sub-assembly (Drive Type FF)



  1. Detach the clamp and disconnect the parking brake cable support clamp sub-assembly.



17. Remove Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3 (Drive Type FF)




  1. Remove the 5 set bolts of parking brake cable assembly No.3.




  2. Pull out the nipple end of parking brake cable assembly No.3.




  3. Disconnect the cable end from the operating lever of the disc brake cylinder.




  4. Using an offset wrench (14mm), remove parking brake cable assembly No.3.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the disc brake cylinder.



18. Remove Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3 (Drive Type 4WD)


  1. Remove the 5 set bolts of parking brake cable assembly No.3.

    □ HINT □
    The bolt positions of parking brake cable assembly No.2 are symmetrical to those of parking brake cable assembly No.3, except for part [A].







  2. Pull out the nipple end of parking brake cable assembly No.3.




  3. Disconnect the cable end from the operating lever of the disc brake cylinder.




  4. Using an offset wrench (14mm), remove parking brake cable assembly No.3.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the disc brake cylinder.



19. Remove Parking Brake Cable Support Clamp Sub-assembly (Drive Type FF)

  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the parking brake cable support clamp sub-assembly from parking brake cable assembly No.3.
