Refrigerant HFC-134a [R134a] Adjustment

Print

Preparation List



1. Discharge Refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a)

  1. Operate the A/C under the following conditions.

    Condition
    		Description
    Engine Speed
    		Idling
    Operating time
    		3 minutes or more
    A/C Switch
    		ON
    Blower level
    		HI
    Set Temperature
    		MAX COOL

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If the A/C cannot be operated due to compressor lock etc., operation is not necessary.

  2. Stop the engine.

  3. Connect a refrigerant recovery/recycling machine and recover the refrigerant gas.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When using a refrigerant recovery/recycling machine, use it correctly according to its instruction manual.



2. Charge Refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a)

  1. Evacuate the system using a vacuum pump.



  2. Charge with refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a).

    Standard
    440±50g

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Never operate the cooler compressor before charging refrigerant, as operating it without refrigerant may cause it to seize due to insufficient lubrication.

    □ HINT □
    When reusing recovered refrigerant gas, the recovery rate of the refrigerant recovery/recycling machine is approximately 90%, so additional refrigerant from a service can is required when recharging; prepare it in advance.






3. Warm Up Engine

■ NOTICE ■
After charging with refrigerant, perform a break-in operation at 2000r/min or less for 2 minutes or more.



4. Inspect for Refrigerant Gas Leaks

  1. After charging the refrigerant gas, inspect for refrigerant gas leaks using a halogen leak detector.
