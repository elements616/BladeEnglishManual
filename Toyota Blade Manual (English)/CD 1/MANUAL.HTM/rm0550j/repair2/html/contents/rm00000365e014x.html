DTC B15F2 ETC Wireless Communication Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Function Description


This DTC is output when a malfunction occurs in wireless road-to-vehicle communication at a toll gate


DTC No.
Diagnosis Description
Inspection Area
B15F2
  1. Communication error other than authentication error detected in DSRC communication
  2. Signal from roadside unit can no longer be received normally, and a timeout occurs
  3. Release requested during communication with roadside unit, and communication disconnected
  4. RF module malfunction (PLL lock loss) detected
  5. Malfunction detected during RF module initialization
Toll Collection (ETC) Computer Assembly

Step 3Inspect with ETC On-board Unit Tester

  1. Inspect Area Around Antenna

    1. Check for any obstructions around the navigation antenna assembly (top surface of the instrument panel), and remove them if present.

    2. Check for any film, stickers, etc. affixed to the windshield glass, and remove them if present.




  1. Inspect with ETC On-board Unit Tester

    1. Using an ETC on-board unit tester, check whether the toll collection (ETC) computer assembly is performing wireless communication normally.

      Result
      Result
      		Proceed to
      Abnormal operation
      		A
      Normal operation
      		B

      □ HINT □
      1. Operate the ETC on-board unit tester at a position approximately 1 m from the windshield glass, on a line extending upward and forward of the vehicle from the navigation antenna assembly at an elevation angle of 67±15°. (The distance between the ETC on-board unit tester and the antenna is only a guide)
      2. Point the communication surface of the ETC on-board unit tester toward the navigation antenna assembly.
      3. Do not bring the ETC on-board unit tester too close to the navigation antenna assembly, as communication with the tester may be established even if the antenna is disconnected.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      When making an inquiry to the road operator, ask the customer for the toll gate, date and time of the occurrence, etc., obtain the customer's consent to inquire about the travel record with the road operator, and then make the inquiry, explaining that there was a malfunction in the wireless communication.

Step 5Operation Check

  1. Check Area Around Antenna

    1. Check for any obstructions around the navigation antenna assembly (top surface of the instrument panel), and remove them if present.

    2. Check for any film, stickers, etc. affixed to the windshield glass, and remove them if present.




  1. Inspect with ETC On-board Unit Tester

    1. Using an ETC on-board unit tester, check that the toll collection (ETC) computer assembly is performing wireless communication normally.

      □ HINT □
      1. Operate the ETC on-board unit tester at a position approximately 1m from the windshield glass, on a line extending upward and forward of the vehicle from the navigation antenna assembly at an elevation angle of 67±15°. ((The distance between the ETC on-board unit tester and the antenna is only a guide)
      2. Point the communication surface of the ETC on-board unit tester toward the navigation antenna assembly.
      3. Do not bring the ETC on-board unit tester too close to the navigation antenna assembly, as communication with the tester may be established even if the antenna is disconnected.

  1. Setup

    1. Perform setup.

  1. Check Using ETC Card

    1. Insert an ETC card into the toll collection (ETC) computer assembly and check that the card is authenticated normally.
