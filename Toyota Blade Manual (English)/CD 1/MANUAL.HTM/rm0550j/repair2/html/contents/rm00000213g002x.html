Air Conditioner Radiator Assembly Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Mode Damper Servo Sub-assembly Removal



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the 3 screws and remove the mode damper servo.



2. Air Mix Damper Servo Sub-assembly Removal



  1. Automatic air conditioning (driver side)

    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove the 3 screws and remove the air mix damper servo.




  2. Dual mode automatic air conditioning (driver side)

    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove the 3 screws and remove the air mix damper servo.




  3. Dual mode automatic air conditioning (front passenger side)

    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove the 3 screws and remove the air mix damper servo.



3. Air Conditioner Harness Assembly Removal



  1. Disengage each clamp and remove the air conditioner harness assembly.



4. Upper Console Mounting Bracket Removal



  1. Disengage the claw and remove the upper console mounting bracket.



5. Console Mounting Bracket LH Removal



  1. Remove the screw and remove the console mounting bracket LH.



6. Air Conditioner Amplifier Assembly Removal



  1. Remove the screw, disengage the claw and remove the air conditioner amplifier assembly.



7. Air Conditioner Duct Removal



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the air conditioner duct.



8. Cooler Drain Hose Removal



  1. Remove the cooler drain hose.



9. Radiator Heater Unit Removal



  1. Remove the screw and remove the clamp.

  2. Remove the radiator heater unit from the air conditioner unit assembly.



10. Cooler Expansion Valve Removal



  1. Using a hexagon wrench, remove the 2 hexagon bolts and remove the cooler expansion valve.



11. Cooler Evaporator NO.1 Removal



  1. Remove the 4 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws as shown in the illustration and remove the lower heater case.




  3. Remove the clamp and remove the cooler evaporator NO.1 together with the cooler thermistor NO.1.

  4. Remove the 2 O-rings from the cooler evaporator NO.1.



12. Cooler Thermistor NO.1 Removal



  1. Remove the cooler thermistor NO.1 from the cooler evaporator NO.1.
