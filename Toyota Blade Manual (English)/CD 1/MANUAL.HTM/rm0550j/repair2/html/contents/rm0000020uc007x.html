Transmission Control Cable Assembly Removal

Print


1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal


2. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



3. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



4. Remove Shift Lever Knob



  1. Turn the shift lever knob in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration and remove it.



5. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



6. Remove Position Indicator Housing Assembly



  1. Disengage the 7 claws.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the instrument panel finish panel LWR assembly and position indicator housing assembly.




  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the position indicator housing assembly.



7. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel LWR CTR



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 6 clips and 4 claws, and remove the instrument panel finish panel LWR CTR.



8. Remove Console Box Carpet



  1. Remove the console box carpet.



9. Remove Console Box Assembly RR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the console box assembly RR.



10. Remove Console Box Insert FR NO.2



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the console box insert.



11. Remove Console Box Insert



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guides and remove console box insert front No.2.



12. Remove Box Bottom Mat


13. Remove Instrument Panel Under Tray



  1. Using a flat-tipped screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 4 claws and remove the instrument panel under tray.



14. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel Lower NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and 2 guides.




  3. Pull in the direction of the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove instrument panel finish panel lower No.1.



15. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel Lower NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Pull in the direction of the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove instrument panel finish panel lower No.2.



16. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.

  3. Remove the insulator.

  4. Remove the battery.



17. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose

  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose.




  2. Disconnect the vacuum switching valve connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.

  3. Disconnect the fuel vapor feed hose and fuel vapor feed hose No.2.




  4. Disconnect the intake air flow meter connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness from the air cleaner cap.




  5. Lock air cleaner hose clamp No.1.

  6. With the clamp locked, remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



18. Remove Air Cleaner Case

  1. Remove the air cleaner filter element.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and clamp, and remove the air cleaner case.



19. Remove Battery Carrier



  1. Remove the battery tray.

  2. Detach the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness from the battery carrier.

  3. Remove the 4 bolts and battery carrier.



20. Remove Battery Carrier NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and battery carrier No.2.



21. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove the engine under cover No.1.



22. Remove Front Floor Brace Center (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the front floor brace center.



23. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly CTR (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts.

  3. Disconnect the 3 exhaust pipe supports and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.

  4. Remove the 2 gaskets.



24. Remove Front Floor Heat Insulator NO.1 (Drive Type FF)



  1. Remove the 3 nuts and front floor heat insulator No.1.



25. Remove Transmission Control Cable Assembly

  1. Move the shift lever to the N position.




  2. Disconnect the cable end from the shift lock control unit.




  3. Using a thin-bladed screwdriver, pull out the stopper of the transmission control cable, turn the nut approximately 180° counterclockwise, and while holding the nut, disconnect the transmission control cable from the shift lever retainer.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not turn the nut too far, as the internal spring will come off the nut and the transmission control cable cannot be reused.
    2. Do not remove the stopper. If it has been removed, reinstall it in the same position.




  4. Remove the nut and clip, and disconnect the transmission control cable from the control shaft lever and control cable bracket.




  5. Remove the bolt and disconnect the transmission control cable support from the engine mounting insulator rear.




  6. Remove the 2 nuts and remove the transmission control cable.
