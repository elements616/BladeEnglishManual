Brake Line Precaution



  1. As these are important safety parts, if a brake fluid leak is found, always disassemble the parts and replace them with new ones if there is any abnormality.

  1. When removing each brake component, cover the connections to prevent foreign matter such as dirt and dust from entering.

  1. Do not damage or deform the brake tubes during removal and installation.

  1. When installing the grommet to the body, check that the brake tube passes through the center of the grommet.

  1. When installing the brake tubes and flexible hoses, check that they are not twisted or bent.

  1. If the flexible hose fitting does not align with the groove in the bracket, twist the flexible hose by the minimum angle and insert it.

  1. Do not reuse the flexible hose installation clips.

  1. Do not allow shock absorber oil, grease or other oils to adhere to the flexible hoses.

  1. When installing a brake tube to a resin clamp, make sure that the brake tube is securely fitted into the clamp and that the brake tube is not loose or pinched.

  1. Do not reuse the flexible hose clips and resin clamps.

  1. When installing the brake tubes and flexible hoses, make sure that they do not interfere with other parts.

  1. Do not allow brake fluid to adhere to painted surfaces such as the body. If brake fluid adheres, wipe it off immediately and wash it off with water.
