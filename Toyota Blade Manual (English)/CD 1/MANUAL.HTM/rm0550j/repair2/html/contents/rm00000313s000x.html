Center Pillar Stripe LH Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Center Pillar Stripe LH



  1. Using an infrared lamp, loosen the center pillar stripe LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Be careful of burns from the heated body or the heat of the infrared lamp.

    □ HINT □
    Heat at 40-60°C for 3-5 minutes.

  2. Raise the end of the center pillar stripe LH with a fingernail and peel it off parallel to the applied surface.
