Intelligent Parking Assist System Function Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Wheel Speed Sensor Travel Distance Information

  1. Pre-inspection Preparation

    1. On a level surface, stop the vehicle with the steering wheel in the straight-ahead position.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      As this inspection involves moving the vehicle, secure a wide area where the surroundings can be safely monitored.

    2. Mark the road surface at the vehicle's stop position with chalk or similar.

  2. Inspect Wheel Speed Sensor Travel Distance Information

    1. Turn the IG OFF and connect the SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. Start the engine and display the "ECU Data Monitor" screen following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display.

      [Diagnosis Menu Screen: Chassis → IPA → ECU Data Monitor] 
      Item Name
      [Symbol]
      		Item Description/Display Range
      		Standard Value
      		Remarks
      RH Wheel Travel Distance Information
      [RWSD]
      		Right wheel travel distance information (wheel speed pulses) displayed as a number from "0" to "255"
      		0 to 255 [pulses]
      		1 pulses = 20.14 mm
      (Varies depending on tire size, pressure and wear condition.)
      LH Wheel Travel Distance Information
      [LWSD]
      		Left wheel travel distance information (wheel speed pulses) displayed as a number from "0" to "255"
      		0 to 255 [pulses]
      		1 pulses = 20.14 mm
      (Varies depending on tire size, pressure and wear condition.)

    3. Read the right and left wheel travel distance information before driving.

      Standard
      0

    4. With the steering wheel kept straight, drive forward approximately 2 m and stop.

    5. Mark the road surface at the vehicle's stop position with chalk or similar, and measure the distance between the marks made before and after driving.

    6. After driving, read the right and left wheel travel distance information again, and check that it nearly matches the distance traveled.

      Standard
      Nearly matches the distance traveled

      □ HINT □
      1. If the travel distance information (wheel speed pulses) read with SST (TaSCAN) is "100", the vehicle has moved forward 100 x 20.14mm = 2.014 m (this varies with tire size, inflation pressure and wear condition.).
      2. If the travel distance information (wheel speed pulses) exceeds "255", it returns to "0" (0 = 256), so add 256 to the value after driving forward and calculate as follows. Pulse change = After driving forward + 256 - Before driving forward
      3. If the right side differs from the actual distance traveled, replace the speed sensor front RH. (see)
      4. If the left side differs from the actual distance traveled, replace the speed sensor front LH. (see)
