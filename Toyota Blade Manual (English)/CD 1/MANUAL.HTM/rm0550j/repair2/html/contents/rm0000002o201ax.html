      Step 11Work after Replacement

        ■ NOTICE ■
        1. When the battery terminal is disconnected, perform the setting operations required when the battery terminal is removed and installed. (see)
        2. Depending on the parts replaced or the work performed during vehicle inspection and maintenance, registration and setting operations for other systems as well as the intelligent parking assist system may be necessary. (see)

        □ HINT □
        1. When replacing the navigation receiver assembly on a vehicle not subscribed to the G-BOOK service, perform the vehicle linking setting.
        2. When replacing the navigation receiver assembly on a vehicle subscribed to the G-BOOK service, perform the vehicle linking setting, then perform the G-BOOK use resumption operation and HELPNET service start operation.
        3. If the navigation receiver assembly is replaced while a purchased G-SOUND unlimited license exists, perform G-SOUND license recovery.
