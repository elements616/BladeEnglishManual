Skid Control Sensor (FF) Removal

Print

Preparation List


□ HINT □
When replacing the sensor rotor, replace it together with the rear axle hub & bearing assembly, as the sensor rotor is built into the rear axle hub & bearing assembly.



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal


2. Remove Rear Tire


3. Remove Skid Control Sensor Wire



  1. Using a screwdriver, disengage the connector claw and disconnect the skid control sensor wire connector.



4. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector



  1. Remove the parking brake lever protector from the parking brake cable assembly No.3.



5. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3



  1. Disconnect the cable end from the operating lever of the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.




  2. Using an offset wrench (14mm), disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.



6. Disconnect Rear Disc Brake Caliper Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and separate the rear disc brake caliper assembly LH from the rear axle carrier.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Suspend the caliper with wire or equivalent.



7. Remove Rear Disc



  1. Put matchmarks on the disc and axle hub, and remove the disc.



8. Remove Rear Axle Hub & Bearing Assembly LH w/ Skid Control Sensor



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and the rear axle hub & bearing assembly LH w/ skid control sensor from the rear axle carrier.



9. Remove Skid Control Sensor

  1. Install 3 hub nuts to the rear axle hub bolts, and secure the rear axle hub & bearing assembly LH w/ skid control sensor in a vise with aluminum plates.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If it has been dropped or subjected to a strong impact, replace it together with the rear axle hub & bearing.

  2. Using a pin punch (3mm) and hammer, remove the 2 pins from SST (09520-00031) and remove the attachment (09521-00010).




  3. Using SST and 2 bolts (90101-12007), remove the skid control sensor from the rear axle hub & bearing assembly LH.

    SST
    09520-00031   (09520-00040, 09521-00020)
    09950-00020  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Pull out the skid control sensor straight so that it does not contact the skid control sensor rotor.
    2. If the skid control sensor rotor is damaged or deformed, replace it together with the rear axle hub & bearing assembly LH.
    3. Do not scratch the contact surface between the skid control sensor and rear axle hub.
    4. Do not allow foreign matter to enter the skid control sensor rotor.

    □ HINT □
    When replacing the sensor rotor, replace it together with the rear axle hub & bearing assembly, as the sensor rotor is built into the rear axle hub & bearing assembly.
