Upper Control Arm Assembly Rear LH Installation

Print

Preparation List



1. Temporarily Tighten Upper Control Arm Assembly Rear LH



  1. Install the upper control arm assembly rear LH to the suspension member and axle carrier, and temporarily tighten it with the 2 bolts and 2 nuts.



2. Fully Tighten Upper Control Arm Assembly Rear LH



  1. With a block of wood in between, jack up the rear suspension arm assembly No.2 LH to the standard vehicle height condition as shown in the illustration to apply load to the rear suspension.




  2. Tighten the upper control arm assembly rear LH with the 2 bolts and 2 nuts.

    Standard
    T = 90 N/m{ 918 kgf/cm }

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Hold the nut and tighten with the bolt.



3. Install Rear Tire
Standard
T = 103 N/m{ 1050 kgf/cm }


4. Stabilize Suspension

  1. Lower the vehicle and bounce it up and down several times to stabilize the rear suspension.
