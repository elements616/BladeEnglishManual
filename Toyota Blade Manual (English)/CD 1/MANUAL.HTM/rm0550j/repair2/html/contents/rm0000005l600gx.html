DTC B1424/24 D Seat Solar Sensor Circuit Malfunction (Indoor Output Normal)

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The cooler thermistor (solar sensor) is installed on the top of the instrument panel, detects the amount of sunlight and performs "AUTO" control of the heater & air conditioner. The output voltage of the cooler thermistor (solar sensor) changes in accordance with changes in the amount of sunlight: as sunlight increases, the output voltage increases, and as sunlight decreases, the output voltage decreases. The air conditioner amplifier assembly reads the voltage output from the cooler thermistor (solar sensor).
