Preparation List



1. Inspect Steering Pad Switch Assembly


  1. Resistance Inspection




    1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the resistance between the terminals of the steering pad switch assembly connector when each switch is operated.

      SST
      09082-00030  
      09083-00170

      Tester Connection (Tester Connection)

      		Standard
      7(DISP) - 8(EAU)

      Display switch pressed
      		Below 1 Ω

  2. Illumination Inspection (Night Illumination)

    1. Check that the illumination (LED) in the steering pad switch assembly comes on when battery positive voltage is connected to terminal 5 (IL+2) and battery negative to terminal 8 (EAU).
