Front Flexible Hose NO.1 Removal

Print

Preparation List




1. Precautions for Brake Line Work
(see)


2. Remove Front Tire


3. Drain Brake Fluid


4. Remove Front Flexible Hose NO.1



  1. Hold front brake flexible hose No.1 with a wrench and, using a union nut wrench (10mm), disconnect front brake flexible hose No.1 from the brake tube.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not bend or damage the brake tube.
    2. Do not allow foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the brake tube connections.

  2. Remove the clip and disconnect front brake flexible hose No.1.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts, and remove the speed sensor bracket and then the flexible hose bracket from the absorber bracket.




  4. Remove the union bolt, and remove front brake flexible hose No.1 and the gasket.
