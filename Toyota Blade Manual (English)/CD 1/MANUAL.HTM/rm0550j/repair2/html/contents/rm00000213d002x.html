76. Inspect Horn Button Assembly

  1. Vehicles not involved in a collision




    1. If any of the following applies, replace the horn button assembly with a new one.

      Specified Condition
      There are cracks, dents or chips on the pad surface of the horn button assembly




  2. Vehicles involved in a collision in which the airbag did not deploy

    ■ WARNING ■
    Replace the horn button assembly using the correct procedure.


    1. Remove the horn button assembly, and if any of the following applies, replace the horn button assembly with a new one.

      Specified Condition
      There are cracks, dents or chips on the pad surface of the horn button assembly
      There are scratches, cracks or damage on the connector and wire harness
      The horn button assembly has been dropped




    2. If any of the following applies, replace the horn button assembly and steering wheel assembly together with new ones.

      Specified Condition
      The horn button assembly interferes with the steering wheel assembly, making installation of the horn button assembly difficult
      The clearance between the horn button assembly and steering wheel assembly when installed is not uniform
      The bracket portion of the steering wheel assembly is deformed

      ■ NOTICE ■
      If the bracket portion of the steering wheel assembly is deformed, do not repair it; replace it with a new one.



77. Inspect Airbag Warning Light



  1. Check that the warning light comes on when the IG is turned ON.

  2. Check that the warning light goes off approximately 6 seconds after the IG is turned ON.

    □ HINT □
    1. If the warning light remains on even after approximately 6 seconds have elapsed after the IG is turned ON, there is a malfunction in the airbag/pretensioner system.
    2. If the light comes on occasionally even after approximately 6 seconds have elapsed after the IG is turned ON, a short to +B, short to ground or open circuit in the airbag warning light circuit may be the cause.
    3. If the light comes on even with the IG OFF or ACC ON, a malfunction in the power supply system or combination meter assembly may be the cause.

82. Add Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)

  1. Close the radiator drain cock plug.

  2. Close the cylinder block drain cock plug.

    Standard
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }




  3. Fill coolant of the specified concentration up to the B line of the radiator reserve tank.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not add water as coolant

    □ HINT □
    Squeeze the radiator inlet hose and radiator outlet hose by hand several times, and if the level in the coolant filler drops, add coolant again.

  4. After installing the reservoir tank cap, warm up the engine until the thermostat opens. (A)

    □ HINT □
    1. During warm-up, squeeze the radiator inlet hose and radiator outlet hose several times.
    2. The thermostat opening timing can be checked by gripping the radiator outlet hose by hand.

    ■ WARNING ■
    1. Wear protective gloves when working
    2. Be careful of cooling fan operation
    3. The engine and radiator become hot, so avoid the risk of burns.

  5. After warm-up, repeat the cycle of 3000rpm engine speed for 5 seconds ←→ idling for 45 seconds 8 times or more (7 minutes or more). (B)

  6. Stop the engine and wait until the coolant cools down, and if the level is below LOW, add coolant of the specified concentration through the filler again and adjust it to between FULL and LOW. (C)

  7. Repeat steps (A) to (C) until the coolant level in the radiator reservoir tank stops dropping.

  8. When the level stops dropping, add coolant of the specified concentration through the filler again to adjust the coolant level in the radiator reservoir tank to between FULL and LOW.



83. Inspect for Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant) Leaks

■ WARNING ■
To avoid the danger of being burned, do not remove the reservoir tank cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Liquid and steam will spray out under pressure.




  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and install a radiator cap tester.

  2. Warm up the engine.

  3. 118kPa{1.2kgf/cm2Pump it to } and check that the pressure does not drop.

    □ HINT □
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks. If there are no external leaks, check the heater core, cylinder block and cylinder head.



84. Charge Refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a)

  1. Evacuate the system using a vacuum pump.



  2. Charge with refrigerant HFC-134a (R134a).

    Standard
    440±50g

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Never operate the cooler compressor before charging refrigerant, as operating it without refrigerant may cause it to seize due to insufficient lubrication.

    □ HINT □
    When reusing recovered refrigerant gas, the recovery rate of the refrigerant recovery/recycling machine is approximately 90%, so additional refrigerant from a service can is required when recharging; prepare it in advance.






85. Warm Up Engine

■ NOTICE ■
