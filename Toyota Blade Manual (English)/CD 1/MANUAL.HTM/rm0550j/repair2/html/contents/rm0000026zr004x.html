Circuit Description



  1. A three-way catalytic converter is used to efficiently purify the CO, HC and NOx in the exhaust gas. This three-way catalytic converter functions most efficiently near the stoichiometric air-fuel ratio. For this reason, CO, HC and NOx cannot be efficiently purified unless the engine is accurately controlled near the stoichiometric air-fuel ratio. The air fuel ratio sensor (A/F sensor) is installed for the purpose of this control. The air fuel ratio sensor (A/F sensor) outputs a voltage proportional to the air-fuel ratio, and the engine control computer controls the air-fuel ratio based on that voltage.
