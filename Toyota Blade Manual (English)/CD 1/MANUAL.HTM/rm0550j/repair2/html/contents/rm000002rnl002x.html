Horn Button Assembly Disposal

Print

Preparation List



1. Dispose of Horn Button Assembly (When Scrapping Vehicle)

■ WARNING ■
  1. Perform deployment outdoors on a flat surface where safety can be ensured. Also, avoid deployment in residential areas as much as possible.
  2. The deployment noise is loud, so thoroughly notify people in advance that deployment will be performed.

  1. Remove Steering Column Lower Cover

    1. Remove Horn Button Assembly (see)

    2. Remove Steering Wheel Assembly (see)

    3. Release the tilt lever, disengage the 7 claws and remove the steering column cover (lower side). (see)

  2. Install Steering Wheel Assembly (see)

  3. Install Horn Button Assembly (see)




  4. Disconnect the airbag connector from the spiral cable.




  5. Airbag (Horn Button Assembly) Deployment

    1. Connect the following 2 SSTs together.

      SST
      09082-00711  
      09082-00780  

    2. Short the alligator clip and terminal of the SST.

    3. Connect the SST to the airbag connector of the spiral cable and lock it securely.

      ■ WARNING ■
      Check that there is no looseness in the steering wheel assembly and horn button assembly.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Lock the SST connector securely.




    4. Extend the SST fully from the vehicle and place the battery 5m or more away from the vehicle.

      ■ WARNING ■
      Close all doors and door glass.

    5. After confirming the safety of the vehicle interior and surroundings, connect the SST alligator clip to the battery negative.

    6. Connect the SST terminal to the battery positive and deploy the airbag (horn button assembly).

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Before deployment, always make sure that there is no one inside the cabin or in the surrounding area.
      2. Before deployment, always warn people around the vehicle being disposed of in a loud voice.

  6. Airbag (Horn Button Assembly) Disposal Procedure




    1. Place the deployed airbag (horn button assembly) in a strong, transparent plastic bag, seal it and dispose of it.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. As some parts of the deployed airbag (horn button assembly) become extremely hot (several hundred °C or more), leave it for 30 minutes or more after deployment.
      2. Do not apply water, etc. to the deployed airbag (horn button assembly).
      3. When handling the deployed airbag (horn button assembly), wear dustproof protective glasses and gloves.
      4. Never dispose of the airbag (horn button assembly) in an undeployed state.
      5. Always wash your hands with water after completing work.



2. Dispose of Horn Button Assembly (When Disposing of Part Alone)

■ WARNING ■
  1. Perform deployment outdoors on a flat surface where safety can be ensured. Also, avoid deployment in residential areas as much as possible.
  2. The deployment noise is loud, so thoroughly notify people in advance that deployment will be performed.

  1. Fix Horn Button Assembly



    1. Fully tighten by hand 2 bolts with plate washers in the 2 installation holes of the horn button assembly.






    2. Connect the following 3 SSTs together.

      SST
      09082-00711  
      09082-00802   (09082-10801, 09082-40800)

    3. Connect the SST to the airbag (horn button assembly) and lock it securely.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Lock the SST connector securely.



    4. After passing the SST through the disc wheel, use wire harnesses (core wire cross-sectional area 1.25mm2or more) to fix the horn button assembly to the disc wheel.



      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Strictly observe the following 2 instructions. Failure to follow the instructions may result in serious injury, as the deployed airbag (horn button assembly) or tires may be thrown.
      2. As a force of approximately 1 tons is applied to the wire harness when the airbag (horn button assembly) deploys, wrap the wire harness 3 times and fix it securely without slack.
      3. As shown in the illustration, always install the airbag (horn button assembly) with the deployment surface facing up.




  2. Airbag (Horn Button Assembly) Deployment

    1. Pull out the SST from the underside of the disc wheel.

    2. Short the alligator clip and terminal of the SST.

    3. As shown in the illustration, stack 3 or more tires on the disc wheel to which the horn button assembly is fixed, and place a tire with a disc wheel on top.

    4. Using wire with a diameter of 1.8mm or more (general-purpose ordinary iron wire: equivalent to JIS SWM-B), wrap the tires together 2 times or more and tie them securely with no slack.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Secure them so that the total load capacity is 1 tons or more.



    5. Extend SST fully from the airbag (horn button assembly), and place the battery 5m or more away from the tire.






    6. Surround the area within a radius of 5m from the outside of the tires with pylons and rope, etc. so that no one can enter.

    7. After confirming the safety of the surroundings, connect the SST alligator clip to the battery negative.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Perform deployment outdoors on a flat surface where safety can be ensured. Also, avoid deployment in residential areas as much as possible.
      2. The deployment noise is loud, so thoroughly notify people in advance that deployment will be performed.

    8. Connect the SST terminal to the battery positive and deploy the airbag (horn button assembly).

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Before deployment, always make sure that there is no one in the surrounding area.
      2. Before deployment, always warn people around the work area in a loud voice.

      □ HINT □
      The connector portion of the SST (wire D) connected directly to the inflator melts after 1 deployment due to the inflator heat.

  3. Airbag (Horn Button Assembly) Disposal Procedure




    1. Place the deployed airbag (horn button assembly) in a strong, transparent plastic bag, seal it and dispose of it.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. As some parts of the deployed airbag (horn button assembly) become extremely hot (several hundred °C or more), leave it for 30 minutes or more after deployment.
      2. Do not apply water, etc. to the deployed airbag (horn button assembly).
      3. When handling the deployed airbag (horn button assembly), wear dustproof protective glasses and gloves.
      4. Never dispose of the airbag (horn button assembly) in an undeployed state.
      5. Always wash your hands with water after completing work.
