Hood Sub-assembly Adjustment

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Hood Sub-assembly


  1. Check that each clearance dimension is within the reference value.




    Reference Value

    A
    		6.2±1.5 mm
    		D
    		3.8±1.5 mm
    B
    		4.3±1.5 mm
    		E
    		0±1.0 mm
    C
    		0.1±1.5 mm



2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Disconnect Water Hose Clamp Bracket NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect water hose clamp bracket No.1 from the radiator support UPR.



4. Adjust Hood Sub-assembly

  1. Adjustment in forward/rearward direction of hood and left/right direction at rear end




    1. Loosen the bolts of the bonnet (hood) hinge assembly and adjust.

      Standard
      T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }

      ■ NOTICE ■
      When adjusting, use a service bolt to adjust the fit.

  2. Vertical adjustment of left and right front end of hood




    1. Turn the 2 cushions on each of the left and right sides to adjust the height.

  3. Vertical and lateral adjustment of front end of hood




    1. Loosen the 3 bolts of the bonnet (hood) lock assembly and adjust.

      Standard
      T = 7.5 N/m{ 77 kgf/cm }

      ■ NOTICE ■
      When adjusting, use a service bolt to adjust the fit.



5. Connect Water Hose Clamp Bracket NO.1



  1. Connect water hose clamp bracket No.1 to the radiator support UPR with the 2 bolts.

    Standard
    T = 5.0 N/m{ 51 kgf/cm }
