Engine Mounting Insulator Rear (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.2

  1. Remove the 5 clips and engine under cover No.2.



2. Disconnect Transmission Control Cable Assembly



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the transmission control cable support from the engine mounting insulator rear.



3. Disconnect Engine Mounting Insulator Rear



  1. Support the engine assembly w/ transaxle with a transmission jack using a wooden block or similar.




  2. Remove the bolt and disconnect the engine mounting insulator rear from the engine mounting bracket rear.



4. Remove Engine Mounting Insulator Rear



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts, and remove the engine mounting insulator rear from the front suspension crossmember.
