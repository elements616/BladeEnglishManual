Fusible Link Block Assembly (Engine Type 2AZ-FE) Removal

Print


1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Engine Cover Sub-assembly NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and engine cover assembly No.1.



3. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose

  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose.




  2. Disconnect the vacuum switching valve connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.

  3. Disconnect the fuel vapor feed hose and fuel vapor feed hose No.2.




  4. Disconnect the intake air flow meter connector and clamp, and disconnect the wire harness from the air cleaner cap.




  5. Lock air cleaner hose clamp No.1.

  6. With the clamp locked, remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



4. Remove Air Cleaner Case

  1. Remove the air cleaner filter element.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and clamp, and remove the air cleaner case.



5. Remove Engine Control Computer



  1. While pushing the lock on the lever, lift the lever and disconnect the 2 engine control computer connectors.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When disconnecting the connectors, be careful not to allow foreign matter such as mud or water to adhere to the connecting parts.




  2. Remove the clamp and 2 bolts, and remove the engine control computer.




  3. Remove the 4 screws and remove the 2 engine control computer brackets.



6. Remove Relay Block Cover UPR NO.1


7. Disconnect Wire Harness



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  2. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

  3. Detach the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness.



8. Remove Front Fender Liner LH



  1. Using a screwdriver, turn the pin hold clip 90° and remove the pin hold clip.

  2. Detach the 4 clips and turn over the front fender liner LH.



9. Remove Engine Room Relay Block



  1. Detach the harness clamp.

  2. Remove the 3 bolts.

  3. Disengage the claw and remove the relay block toward the inside of the engine room.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    The wire harness is connected, so do not pull it forcefully.



10. Remove Fusible Link Block Assembly



  1. Disengage the 10 claws and separate the relay block cover into the upper side and lower side.



  2. Disengage the 4 claws of the fuse holder and fusible link block assembly, and pull them out from the separated relay block cover.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    The wire harness is connected, so do not pull it forcefully.




  3. Remove the bolt.

  4. Disengage the claw and separate the fuse holder from the fusible link block assembly.




  5. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 wire harnesses.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When disconnecting the wire harnesses from the fusible link block assembly, put marks on the fusible link block assembly, wire harnesses and bolts to avoid mistakes when connecting.




  6. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the 3 wire harnesses.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When disconnecting the wire harnesses from the fusible link block assembly, put marks on the fusible link block assembly, wire harnesses and bolts to avoid mistakes when connecting.

  7. Disconnect the 2 connectors and remove the fusible link block assembly.
