Differential (4WD) Precautions

Print



    1. Before disassembly, clean off any sand, mud, etc. adhering to the outside of the rear differential assembly so that it does not enter the rear differential during disassembly and installation.


      2. When separating joined light alloy parts such as the differential side bearing retainer, do not pry them with a screwdriver, etc.; separate them by lightly tapping with a plastic hammer.


        3. Always keep disassembled parts arranged in order and cover them with a shop rag to protect them from dust, etc.


          4. Thoroughly clean and dry each part before installation, then apply grease or oil to the required areas. Do not clean aluminum parts, rubber parts, bolts with pre-coated adhesive, etc. with alkaline chemicals. Also, do not clean rubber parts such as O-rings and oil seals with cleaning solvent (white gasoline, etc.).


            5. New differential case bolts are coated with heat-treatment coating oil, so never wash it off.


              6. Apply sufficient hypoid gear oil SX to sliding surfaces and rotating parts before installation.


                7. When securing a part in a vise, do not clamp it directly in the vise; always use aluminum plates.


                  8. Replace the snap rings with new ones every time.


                    9. Handle the case carefully, as damage to the case mating surfaces will cause oil leaks.


                      10. Before applying seal packing, thoroughly remove any old seal packing from the sealing surfaces and clean the sealing surfaces with white gasoline.


                        11. After assembling parts to which seal packing has been applied, do not add oil immediately; leave them for at least 1 hours.


                          12. Handle the parts carefully, as damage to the contact surfaces of oil seals, O-rings and gaskets will cause oil leaks.


                            13. When press-fitting an oil seal, be careful not to damage the oil seal lip and outer circumference and not to tilt the oil seal.


                              14. When replacing a bearing, replace the outer race and inner race as a set.


                                15. When replacing a bearing, do not apply load to the rollers, cage or rolling surfaces.


                                  16. When replacing the hypoid gear, replace the ring gear and drive pinion as a set.


                                    17. New bearings are coated with rust preventive oil, so never wash it off.


                                      18. Replace bolts if there is significant rust, etc. on the seating surfaces or threads.
