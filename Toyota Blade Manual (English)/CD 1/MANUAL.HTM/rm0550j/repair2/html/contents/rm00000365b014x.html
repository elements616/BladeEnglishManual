Step 5Operation Check

  1. Check Area Around Antenna

    1. Check for any obstructions around the navigation antenna assembly (top surface of the instrument panel), and remove them if present.

    2. Check for any film, stickers, etc. affixed to the windshield glass, and remove them if present.




  1. Inspect with ETC On-board Unit Tester

    1. Using an ETC on-board unit tester, check that the toll collection (ETC) computer assembly is performing wireless communication normally.

      □ HINT □
      1. Operate the ETC on-board unit tester at a position approximately 1m from the windshield glass, on a line extending upward and forward of the vehicle from the navigation antenna assembly at an elevation angle of 67±15°. ((The distance between the ETC on-board unit tester and the antenna is only a guide)
      2. Point the communication surface of the ETC on-board unit tester toward the navigation antenna assembly.
      3. Do not bring the ETC on-board unit tester too close to the navigation antenna assembly, as communication with the tester may be established even if the antenna is disconnected.

  1. Setup

    1. Perform setup.

  1. Check Using ETC Card

    1. Insert an ETC card into the toll collection (ETC) computer assembly and check that the card is authenticated normally.
