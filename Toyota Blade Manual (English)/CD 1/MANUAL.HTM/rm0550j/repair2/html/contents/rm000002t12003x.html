Circuit Description


The side collision sensor RH (for side airbag and curtain shield airbag deployment judgment) circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly, side airbag sensor assembly RH and airbag sensor rear right.

The side airbag sensor assembly RH detects impacts and sends signals for airbag ignition judgment to the airbag computer assembly.

When a malfunction is detected in the side collision sensor (for side airbag and curtain shield airbag deployment judgment) circuit, DTC B1622 is stored in the airbag computer assembly.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Item
Trouble Area
B1622

Inspection Procedure

Step 1Connector Inspection

□ HINT □
For the inspection procedure and precautions, seeReference

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    Make sure the ignition switch is OFF before disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.

  1. Inspect the condition of the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly, side airbag sensor assembly RH and airbag sensor rear RH.

    Specified Condition
    The connector is connected properly

  1. Disconnect the connectors from the airbag computer assembly, side airbag sensor assembly RH and airbag sensor rear RH, and inspect the condition of the terminals of each connector.

    Specified Condition
    The terminals are not deformed or damaged

  1. Turn IG OFF.

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

  1. Connect the airbag computer assembly and airbag sensor rear RH connectors.

  1. Interchange the side airbag sensor assembly RH and side airbag sensor assembly LH, connect the connectors and install them securely.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. When installing, do not subject the side airbag sensor assembly RH and side airbag sensor assembly LH to strong impacts such as dropping them.
    2. If a sensor has been subjected to a strong impact such as being dropped, replace it with a new one.
