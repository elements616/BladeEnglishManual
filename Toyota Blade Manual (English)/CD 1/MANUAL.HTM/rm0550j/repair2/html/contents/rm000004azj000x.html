Multimedia Interface ECU Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a moulding remover and move it to near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the moulding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



2. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a moulding remover and move it to near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the moulding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



3. Remove Instrument Panel Register Assembly CTR



  1. Pull the instrument panel register assembly CTR horizontally toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 clips, 5 claws and 3 guides.




  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the instrument panel register assembly CTR.



4. Remove Shift Lever Knob



  1. Turn the shift lever knob in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration to remove it.



5. Remove Box Panel NO.2



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

    □ HINT □
    Apply protective tape on the RH side in the same way.

  2. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 clips.




  3. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 2 claws.

  4. Disconnect each connector and remove box panel No.2.



6. Remove Position Indicator Housing Assembly



  1. As shown in the illustration, using a moulding remover, disengage the 3 claws.




  2. Disengage the 6 claws.

  3. Disconnect the connector and remove the position indicator housing assembly.



7. Remove Instrument Cluster Finish Panel Assembly Center NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 6 clips and 4 claws, and remove instrument cluster finish panel assembly center No.1.



8. Remove Navigation Receiver Assembly w/ Bracket



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.




  2. Disengage the 4 clips and pull the navigation receiver assembly w/ bracket toward the rear of the vehicle.

  3. Disconnect each connector and remove the navigation receiver assembly w/ bracket.



9. Air Conditioner Control Assembly Removal



  1. Disengage the 4 clips.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the air conditioner control assembly.



10. Remove Multimedia Interface Computer Assembly w/ Bracket



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  2. Disengage the 2 guides.

  3. Disconnect each connector and remove the multimedia interface computer assembly w/ bracket.



11. Remove Multimedia Interface Bracket NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws and remove multimedia interface bracket No.1.



12. Remove Multimedia Interface Computer
