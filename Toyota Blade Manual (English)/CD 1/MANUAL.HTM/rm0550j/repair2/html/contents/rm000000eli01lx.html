DTC B1806 Front Passenger Side Airbag Squib Circuit (Open)

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The front passenger side squib circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly and instrument panel passenger airbag assembly. When the activation conditions are met, the instrument panel passenger airbag assembly (front passenger side airbag) is activated.

When an open is detected in the front passenger side squib circuit (P+, P- lines), DTC B1806 is stored in the airbag computer assembly.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Item
Trouble Area
B1806
  1. Open in P+, P- lines of the wire harness between the airbag computer assembly and instrument panel passenger airbag assembly (front passenger side airbag)
  2. Instrument panel passenger airbag assembly (front passenger side airbag) malfunction
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly Malfunction
  1. Instrument panel wire (between airbag computer assembly and junction connector)
  2. Instrument panel wire assembly (between junction connector and instrument panel passenger airbag assembly)
  3. Instrument Panel Passenger Airbag Assembly
  4. Airbag Computer Assembly

Inspection Procedure


□ HINT □
When performing the simulation method, use TaSCAN to select check mode first. (see)


Step 1Connector Inspection

□ HINT □
For the inspection procedure and precautions, seeReference

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    Make sure the ignition switch is OFF before disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.

  1. Check the connection condition of the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and instrument panel passenger airbag assembly, and of the junction connectors (instrument panel wire and instrument panel wire assembly).

    Specified Condition
    The connector is connected properly

  1. Disconnect the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and instrument panel passenger airbag assembly, and the junction connectors (instrument panel wire and instrument panel wire assembly), and check the condition of the terminals of each connector.

    Specified Condition
    The terminals are not deformed or damaged

  1. Check the condition of the short pins of the connectors with a short mechanism on the instrument panel wire and instrument panel wire assembly.

    Specified Condition
    The short pins are not deformed or damaged
