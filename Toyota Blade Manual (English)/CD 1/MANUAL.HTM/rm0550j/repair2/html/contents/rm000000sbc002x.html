Wiper & Washer System Precautions

Print



    1. Handling Precautions for Washer Nozzle (Diffusion Type)

    1. As the spray angle of the washer nozzle cannot be adjusted with a tool, adjust by replacing it with a nozzle with a spray angle of 0° or ±2°.

    2. When the washer nozzle is removed from the cowl top ventilator louver, replace it if the claws are deformed and there is looseness after installation.

    3. If the washer nozzle is clogged, do not use metal objects such as safety pins; clean it with a soft resin brush, etc.
