  1. TaSCAN ECU Data Monitor

    ■ NOTICE ■
    For subtle symptoms such as hesitation or rough idle, it is necessary to use a method of collecting data from a vehicle of the same model under the same conditions and comparing it, and make an overall judgment from all items of computer data.

    1. TaSCAN Active Test

      1. Using SST (TaSCAN), display the [Active Test] screen according to the screen display, and perform the Active Test.

        SST
        09991-70200  

        [Diagnosis menu screen: Powertrain → TCCS → Active Test] 
        Item Name
        		Test Details
        		Restrictions
        Shift Position
        		Can be set to any shift position
        [1(2.4), 2(1.5), 3(1.0), 4(0.7), 5(0.43)]
        		Test is canceled when vehicle speed is less than 20km/h or 45km/h or more
        Lock-up SOL
        		Lock-up can be turned ON/OFF
        		Possible in a forward driving shift position and at vehicle speed of 40km/h or more
        CVT Upshift SOL
        		Solenoid DS1 can be switched 100%/0%
        		Possible in P or N position and at vehicle speed of 0km/h
        CVT Downshift SOL
        		Solenoid DS2 can be switched 100%/0%
        		Possible in P or N position and at vehicle speed of 0km/h
        Lock-up Control SOL (DSU)
        		Solenoid DSU can be switched 100%/0%
        		Possible in P or N position and at vehicle speed of 0km/h
        Lock-up Control SOL (SL)
        		Solenoid SL can be switched between MIN current/MAX current
        		Possible in P or N position and at vehicle speed of 0km/h
        SLS
        		Belt clamping force control solenoid valve (SLS) can be switched between high pressure/low pressure
        (duty ratio 48 ←→ 88%)
        		Possible in P or N position, with throttle fully closed (at idle) and at vehicle speed of 0km/h
        SLS Drive
        		Solenoid SLS can be switched between MIN/MAX
        		Possible in P or N position, with throttle fully closed (at idle) and at vehicle speed of 0km/h
        TC Terminal ON
        		Combination meter warning diagnosis display all at once
        		-

        □ HINT □
        When performing the lock-up SOL test, monitor the engine speed (ESPD) and turbine speed (NT) data.
