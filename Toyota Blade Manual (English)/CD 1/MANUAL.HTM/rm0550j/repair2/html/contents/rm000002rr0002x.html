Preparation List



1. Handling and Work Precautions
(see)


2. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


3. Remove Shift Lever Knob



  1. Turn the shift lever knob in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration and remove it.



4. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



5. Remove Console Box Carpet



  1. Remove the console box carpet.



6. Remove Console Box Assembly RR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the console box assembly RR.



7. Remove Box Bottom Mat


8. Remove Instrument Panel Under Tray



  1. Using a flat-tipped screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 4 claws and remove the instrument panel under tray.



9. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



10. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



11. Remove Position Indicator Housing Assembly



  1. Disengage the 7 claws.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the position indicator housing assembly.



12. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel LWR CTR



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 6 clips and 4 claws, and remove the instrument panel finish panel LWR CTR.



13. Remove Console Box Insert FR NO.2



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guides and remove console box insert front No.2.



14. Remove Console Box Insert



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the console box insert.



15. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel Lower NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and 2 guides.




  3. Pull in the direction of the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove instrument panel finish panel lower No.1.



16. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel Lower NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Pull in the direction of the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove instrument panel finish panel lower No.2.



17. Remove Airbag Computer Assembly

  1. Make sure that the ignition switch is OFF.

  2. Check that the battery negative terminal is disconnected.

    ■ WARNING ■
    1. If work is performed without disconnecting the negative battery terminal, the airbag may deploy even if only the airbag computer assembly itself receives an impact, so be sure to check that the cable is disconnected from the negative battery terminal before performing the work.
    2. After disconnecting the battery negative terminal, do not start work for 90 seconds.




  3. Disconnect the connector from the airbag computer assembly.

  4. Remove the 3 bolts and airbag computer assembly.



18. Inspect Airbag Computer Assembly

  1. Vehicles not involved in a collision, or vehicles involved in a collision in which the airbag did not deploy

    1. Check the diagnostic trouble codes.

  2. Vehicles involved in a collision in which the airbag deployed

    1. Replace the airbag computer assembly with a new one.
