Shock Absorber Assembly Rear LH Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1 (LH Seat Position)



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



2. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2 (LH Seat Position)



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



3. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly


4. Remove Rear Seat Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 front side bolts.




  2. Fold the rear seat assembly LH forward.

  3. Remove the 2 rear side bolts and remove the rear seat assembly LH.



5. Remove Rear Door Scuff Plate LH
Remove the LH side using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)


6. Remove Rear Door Opening Trim Weatherstrip LH
Remove the LH side using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)


7. Remove Package Tray Trim Panel Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 suspenders from the package tray trim garnish hooks.

  2. Disengage the 2 pins and remove the package tray trim panel assembly.



8. Remove Rear Floor Mat Rear



  1. Remove the rear floor mat rear.



9. Remove Deck Floor Box RH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the deck floor box RH.



10. Remove Deck Floor Box LH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and the deck floor box LH.



11. Remove Luggage Hold Belt Striker Assembly (Rear Side Installation Position)



  1. Remove the bolt and the trunk (luggage compartment) hold belt striker.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the LH side using the same procedure.



12. Remove Deck Trim Cover Rear



  1. Disengage the 10 claws and remove the deck trim cover rear.



13. Remove Rear Seatback Hinge Sub-assembly (LH Side Installation Position)
Remove the LH side using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)


14. Remove Rear Seat Side Garnish LH
Remove the LH side using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)


15. Remove Luggage Hold Belt Striker Assembly (LH Side Installation Position)
Remove the LH side using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)


16. Remove Room Light Assembly NO.2



  1. Using a screwdriver with a thin blade wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claws.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove room light assembly No.2.



17. Remove Deck Trim Side Panel Assembly LH
Remove the LH side using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)


18. Remove Rear Tire


19. Remove Height Control Sensor Sub-assembly Rear (w/ Discharge Headlight)



  1. Disconnect the connector and disengage the clamp.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and remove the height control sensor assembly (rear).



20. Remove Rear Stabilizer Link Assembly LH



  1. Support rear suspension arm assembly No. 2 LH with a jack using a wooden block.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not raise the rear axle beam assembly too far with the jack.

    □ HINT □
    Support it at a position where the rear shock absorber assembly LH is compressed approximately 20 to 30mm.




  2. Remove the nut and the rear stabilizer cushion.




  3. Remove the nut and remove the rear stabilizer link assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    Using socket hexagon wrench 6, hold the stud bolt so that it does not spin.



21. Remove Rear Shock Absorber Cushion Retainer



  1. Using socket hexagon wrench 6, hold the rear shock absorber assembly LH and remove the lock nut.




  2. Remove the rear shock absorber cushion retainer.



22. Remove Rear Suspension Support



  1. Remove the rear suspension support.



23. Remove Rear Shock Absorber Assembly LH



  1. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the rear shock absorber assembly LH.



24. Remove Rear Spring Bumper NO.1 LH
