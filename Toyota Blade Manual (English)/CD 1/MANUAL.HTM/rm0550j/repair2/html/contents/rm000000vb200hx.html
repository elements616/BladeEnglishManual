  1. Front Shock Absorber Assembly LH Operation Inspection

    1. Compress and extend the front shock absorber assembly LH 4 times or more.

    2. Inspect the front shock absorber assembly LH.

      Specified Condition
      When compressed and extended at a constant speed, the resistance is constant throughout the entire stroke, and there is no abnormal resistance or noise. Also, when extending, it returns at a constant speed without noise.
