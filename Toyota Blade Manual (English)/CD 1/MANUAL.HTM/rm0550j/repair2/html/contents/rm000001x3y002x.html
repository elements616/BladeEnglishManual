Upper Control Arm Assembly Rear LH Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Rear Tire


2. Remove Upper Control Arm Assembly Rear LH



  1. Using a wooden block, jack up the rear axle carrier to the standard vehicle height as shown in the illustration, and support the rear suspension arm assembly No.2 LH.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts, and remove the upper control arm assembly rear LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Hold the nut and loosen the bolt.
