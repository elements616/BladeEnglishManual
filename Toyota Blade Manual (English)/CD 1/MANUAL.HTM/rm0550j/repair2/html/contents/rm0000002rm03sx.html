HDD Navigation System Precaution

    1. Disconnecting the Battery Negative Terminal

    1. The HDD navigation system stores various memories and settings after the ignition is turned OFF. Therefore, always wait 1 minutes after turning the ignition OFF before disconnecting the battery negative terminal.

        2. On this model, the navigation receiver assembly functions as a one-touch type HELPNET terminal and holds the HELPNET on-board unit ID.

            5. Precautions Regarding the Hard Disk Drive

            1. The navigation receiver assembly has a built-in hard disk drive (hereafter HDD) for storing map data, etc. used by the navigation system. Therefore, observe the following precautions when handling it.


              1. Do not subject it to vibration or impact

                ■ NOTICE ■
                Do not drop the navigation receiver assembly or subject it to strong impact. Do not use a unit that has been dropped or subjected to strong impact.

              2. Do not allow condensation to form

                ■ NOTICE ■
                If the navigation receiver assembly is subjected to sudden temperature changes, condensation may form inside and cause HDD failure. Therefore, store a navigation receiver assembly removed from the vehicle inside the vehicle or in an environment with equivalent temperature, humidity, etc.

              3. Do not apply static electricity

                ■ NOTICE ■
                Touching the connector terminals may apply static electricity to the internal circuits and cause HDD failure.

              4. Keep magnetized objects away

                ■ NOTICE ■
                Do not bring speakers, motors, magnetized tools, etc. near the navigation receiver assembly.
