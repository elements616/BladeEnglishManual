Engine Mounting Insulator Front (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Check Front Wheels Straight-ahead Position


2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly



  1. Place both hands on the front of the cover and lift it upward to disengage the 2 front clips, then continue lifting upward to disengage the 1 rear clips, and remove the V-bank cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pulling up the front and rear at the same time may cause damage.



4. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


5. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.




  3. Disconnect the current sensor connector.

  4. Remove the insulator.

  5. Remove the battery.



6. Remove Battery Carrier



  1. Remove the battery tray.

  2. Disengage the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness from the battery carrier.

  3. Remove the 4 bolts and the battery carrier.



7. Remove Battery Carrier NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and battery carrier NO.2.



8. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove engine under cover No.1.



9. Remove Engine Under Cover

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 4 screws, and remove the engine under cover.



10. Remove Front Suspension Member Reinforcement LH



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and front suspension member reinforcement LH.



11. Remove Front Suspension Member Reinforcement RH



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and front suspension member reinforcement RH.



12. Support Engine Assembly



  1. Support the oil pan with a transmission jack using a wooden block or similar.



13. Disconnect Engine Mounting Insulator Front



  1. Remove the bolt and nut, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator front from the engine mounting bracket front.



14. Remove Front Cross Member Sub-assembly



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and front cross member.



15. Remove Engine Mounting Insulator Front



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the engine mounting insulator front from the front crossmember.
