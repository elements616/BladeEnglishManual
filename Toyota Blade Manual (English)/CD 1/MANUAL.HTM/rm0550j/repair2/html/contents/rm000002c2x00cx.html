Front Seat Assembly RH (Power Seat Type) Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Operation Inspection

  1. Slide Motor Operation Check




    1. Check that the adjuster operates smoothly when the battery is connected to the slide motor connector terminals.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      Battery positive - Battery negative
      		Operating Direction
      2(FWD) - 1(BACK)
      		Slide adjuster forward
      1(BACK) - 2 (FWD)
      		Slide adjuster backward

  2. Front Vertical Motor Operation Check




    1. Check that the adjuster operates smoothly when the battery is connected to the front vertical motor connector terminals.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      Battery positive - Battery negative
      		Operating Direction
      1(DOWN) - 2(UP)
      		Cushion frame front upward
      2(UP) - 1(DOWN)
      		Cushion frame front downward

  3. Rear Lifter Motor Operation Check




    1. Check that the adjuster operates smoothly when the battery is connected to the rear lifter motor connector terminals.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      Battery positive - Battery negative
      		Operating Direction
      2(DOWN) - 1(UP)
      		Cushion frame rear upward
      1(UP) - 2(DOWN)
      		Cushion frame rear downward

  4. Reclining Motor Operation Check




    1. Check that the adjuster operates smoothly when the battery is connected to the reclining motor connector terminals.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      Battery positive - Battery negative
      		Operating Direction
      2(FWD) - 1(BACK)
      		Back frame forward
      1(BACK) - 2(FWD)
      		Back frame backward
