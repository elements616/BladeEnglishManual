Step 1Inspect Speaker Installation Condition

  1. Inspect Speaker Installation Condition

    1. Inspect each speaker installation for looseness, play or other abnormalities.

    2. Inspect each speaker for foreign matter inside or for abnormalities such as a torn speaker cone.

Step 2Identify Cause of Noise

  1. Identify the cause of the noise based on the symptom chart below.

    □ HINT □
    1. First, check that there is no external noise. If this step is neglected, the source of the noise may be unclear and misidentified, so always perform it.
    2. When eliminating noise, eliminate the strongest noise first and proceed in order.
    3. Detuning the radio (not tuned to a broadcast) makes the noise more noticeable and the symptom easier to judge.
