Brake Function Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Simple Inspection of Brake Booster

  1. Brake Booster Airtightness Inspection

    1. Start the engine, let it idle for 1-2 minutes, then stop it.

    2. Depress the brake pedal several times with the force used in normal braking, and check the change in brake pedal height.

      Specified Condition
      The brake pedal height on depressions 2 and 3 becomes higher than on depression 1 (interval between depressions 1 and 2, and between depressions 2 and 3: 5 seconds or more)

  2. Brake Booster Operation Inspection

    1. With the engine stopped, depress the brake pedal several times with the same force, and check that the brake pedal height does not change.

    2. Start the engine with the brake pedal depressed, and check the change in brake pedal height.

      Specified Condition
      When the engine is started with the brake pedal depressed, the brake pedal moves down slightly

  3. Brake Booster Airtightness Under Load Inspection

    1. Depress the brake pedal while the engine is running, stop the engine in that condition and hold for 30 seconds, then check the change in brake pedal height.

      Specified Condition
      No change in pedal height



2. Inspect Brake Booster Function


  1. Pre-inspection Preparation




    1. Remove the bleeder plug of the front brake cylinder, and install an LSPV gauge via nipple No.3.

    2. Bleed the air from the LSPV gauge.

    3. Disconnect the vacuum hose from the brake booster, and install a MityVac via a 3-way union.

      □ HINT □
      Use the MityVac as a vacuum gauge.

    4. Install a vacuum hose of the same diameter to the other side of the 3-way union and connect it to the brake booster.

    5. Install a pedal force gauge to the brake pedal.

  2. Airtightness Inspection

    1. Start the engine, stop the engine at a vacuum of 66.7kPa{500mmHg}, and check the change in vacuum.

      Specified Condition
      No drop in vacuum for 15 seconds

  3. Airtightness Under Load Inspection

    1. Start the engine, depress the brake pedal with a pedal force of 196N{20kgf}, stop the engine at a vacuum of 66.7kPa{500mmHg}, and check the change in vacuum.

      Specified Condition
      Drop in vacuum in 15 seconds is within 3.3kPa{25mmHg}

  4. Operation Inspection Without Power Assist

    1. With the engine stopped, operate the brake pedal several times, and check the fluid pressure at each pedal force with no vacuum.

      Standard

      Pedal Force [N{kgf}]
      		Fluid Pressure [MPa{kgf/cm2}]
      200{20.4}
      		0.5{5.1}
      300{30.6}
      		1.1{11.2}

  5. Operation Inspection With Power Assist

    1. Start the engine, set the vacuum in the brake booster to 66.7kPa{500mmHg}, and check the fluid pressure at each pedal force.

      Standard

      Pedal Force [N{kgf}]
      		Fluid Pressure [MPa{kgf/cm2}]
      50{5.1}
      		1.3 to 2.3{13.3 to 23.5}
      100{10.2}
      		3.5 to 4.5{35.7 to 45.9}
      150{15.3}
      		5.7 to 6.7{58.1 to 68.3}
      200{20.4}
      		8.0 to 9.0{81.6 to 91.8}

      □ HINT □
      If the brake booster assembly is faulty, inspect the check valve. If the check valve is normal, replace the brake booster assembly.

  6. Remove the LSPV gauge, pedal force gauge and MityVac.

  7. Bleed Brake System (see)



3. Inspect Check Valve

  1. Slide the clip and disconnect the vacuum hose.

  2. Remove the vacuum check valve from the brake booster assembly.




  3. Check that air flows from the brake booster side to the engine side, and that air does not flow from the engine side to the brake booster side.

    □ HINT □
    If it is faulty, replace the vacuum check valve.

  4. Install the vacuum check valve to the brake booster assembly.

  5. Connect the vacuum hose with the clip.
