Continuously Variable Transaxle Assembly Precautions

Print



    1. Before disassembly, clean off any sand, mud, etc. adhering to the outside of the transaxle so that it does not enter the inside during removal, installation and disassembly.


      2. Until the oil pan is removed, do not turn the oil pan side upward, as foreign matter may enter the valve body.


        3. When disassembling light alloy parts such as the case, do not pry them with a screwdriver, etc.; separate them by lightly tapping with a plastic hammer.


          4. During removal, installation and disassembly, work with bare hands or vinyl gloves. Do not use work gloves, shop rags, etc., to prevent lint from entering.


            5. Always keep disassembled parts arranged in order and cover them with a shop rag to protect them from dust, etc.


              6. Thoroughly clean each part before installation, and after drying, apply Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC. However, do not clean aluminum and rubber parts with alkaline chemicals. Also, never clean rubber parts such as O-rings, gaskets and oil seals with cleaning solvent (white gasoline, etc.).


                7. Apply Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC to sliding and rotating surfaces before installation.


                  When installing 8.O rings and oil seal rings, apply sufficient Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC and install them without damaging them.


                    9. Do not clamp parts directly in a vise; always use aluminum plates.


                      10. Be very careful not to damage the mating surfaces, as this will cause oil leaks.


                        11. Before applying seal packing, thoroughly remove any old seal packing from the sealing surfaces and clean them with white gasoline.


                          12. Use Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid TC when filling fluid.
