2. Manual Maintenance Check

□ HINT □
  1. The manual maintenance check performs a maintenance check of the in-vehicle unit (in-vehicle unit repair check and maintenance check after vehicle repair) and checks whether the member is registered with the center (at subscription, cancellation and contract change), and can switch between the emergency call enabled/disabled states.
  2. The screen is an example display and may differ from the actual screen.

  1. Check GPS Reception

    1. Check that the GPS mark is displayed on the current position screen.

      □ HINT □
      The manual maintenance check confirms that position information can be sent accurately. Therefore, avoid locations between buildings and indoors such as in a factory, and perform it in an open location where GPS can be received.

  2. Manual Maintenance Check

    1. Press the "Info G" button to display the information screen.




    2. Press "Setup" on the information screen.




    3. On the setup screen, press and hold "HELPNET Maintenance Check" for 10 seconds or more.




    4. Connection to the center starts.




    5. When connected to the center, data communication is performed first, and the current position of the vehicle, the fact that a manual maintenance check is being performed, and member registration with the center are checked.




    6. After data communication is completed, the system enters call mode.




    7. Talk with the center through the microphone, and check that the call can be made normally and that the reported position is correct.




    8. After the conversation ends, finish the check.

    9. If the "Confirm" button is displayed, press "Confirm".

    10. Then, display the current position screen.

    11. Press the "Info G" button.




    12. Check that the "HELPNET" switch is displayed on the information screen.



3. Check HELPNET Call Result

□ HINT □
For diagnosis screen transitions, refer to HDD Navigation System - Diagnosis System ().

  1. Diagnosis Startup

    1. Start diagnosis and display the diagnosis inspection menu screen. (see)

    2. Select "HELPNET Check" on the diagnosis check menu screen to display the HELPNET check menu screen.




    3. Select "Log" on the HELPNET check menu screen to display the HELPNET call log screen.
