  1. Instrument Panel Junction Block Assembly (Main Body ECU)


    1. Inspect Main Body ECU






      1. Disconnect the main body ECU connector to be inspected.

      2. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the voltage and resistance between the terminals of the vehicle side wire harness connector.

        SST
        09082-00030  
        09083-00170  

        Specified Condition

        Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
        		Condition
        		Standard
        A3 (GND) ←→ Body ground
        		Always
        		Below 1 Ω
        A5 (E) ←→ Body ground
        		Always
        		Below 1 Ω
        U4 (GND2) ←→ Body ground
        		Always
        		Below 1 Ω
        V6 (AM2) ←→ Body ground
        		Always
        		11 to 14V
        U1 (AM1) ←→ Body ground
        		Always
        		11 to 14V
        S7 (E-IG) ←→ Body ground
        		IG OFF → IG ON
        		1V or less → 11 to 14V
        V4 (ACCD) ←→ Body ground
        		ACC OFF → ACC ON
        		1V or less → 11 to 14V

      3. Connect the connector.

      4. Using SST (TaSCAN), check the pulses between the terminals of the vehicle side wire harness connector.

        SST
        09991-70200  

        Specified Condition

        Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
        		Condition
        		Standard
        V23(LIN2)←→P1(GND1)
        		IG ON
        		Pulse Generation
