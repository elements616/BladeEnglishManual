Electronically Controlled Automatic Transaxle [ECT] (U660E) List by Symptom

Print


■ NOTICE ■
Check the DTCs; if the problem symptom occurs with normal DTC output (no DTCs stored), inspect each part according to the list below.


□ HINT □
  1. When the ATF temperature is 0°C or less, shifting to 5 and 6 is not possible.
  2. Lock-up is prohibited when the engine coolant temperature is 60°C or less, the stop light switch is ON, or the throttle is fully closed.
  3. Acceleration flex lock-up control is not performed when the ATF temperature is 40°C or less or 110°C or higher, or when the stop light switch is ON.
  4. Deceleration flex lock-up control is not performed when the ATF temperature is 20°C or less or 110°C or higher, or when the engine coolant temperature is 70°C or less.
