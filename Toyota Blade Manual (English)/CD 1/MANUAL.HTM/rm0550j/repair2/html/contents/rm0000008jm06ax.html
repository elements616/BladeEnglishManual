Step 2Inspect Aftermarket Parts (Sunshade Film, Telephone Antenna, etc.)

  1. Inspect aftermarket parts

    1. Check whether aftermarket parts such as sunshade film or a telephone antenna are installed.

      Specified Condition
      No aftermarket parts (including non-genuine wiring)

      □ HINT □
      Noise entering the radio is suppressed to a level that causes no practical problems, so extremely loud noise is not expected. If loud noise occurs, check that the ground at the antenna installation area is normal, that all genuine noise suppression parts are installed, and that there is no non-genuine wiring.

Step 3Inspect for Antenna Noise

  1. With ACC ON, turn on the radio and set it to AM mode.

  1. Remove the roof antenna pole.

  1. Check that noise is produced from the speakers when a screwdriver touches the antenna pole mounting thread of the amplifier antenna assembly.

  1. Inspect ground installation condition

      Specified Condition

    2. Check that the ground section of antenna cord sub-assembly NO.2 is securely attached to the body.

      Specified Condition

Step 5Inspect Antenna Cord Sub-assembly NO.2
SST
09082-00030  
09083-00170  



  1. Pre-inspection Preparation

    1. Make the connection between the amplifier antenna assembly and antenna cord sub-assembly NO.2 accessible.

    2. Disconnect the connector at the connection between the amplifier antenna assembly and antenna cord sub-assembly NO.2.

  1. Noise generation check

    1. With the navigation receiver assembly connector connected, turn the ignition switch to ACC.

    2. Turn on the radio and set it to AM mode.

    3. Check that noise is produced from the speakers when a metal object such as a thin flat-tip screwdriver or thin wire touches antenna jack A of antenna cord sub-assembly NO.2.

      Specified Condition
      Noise (scratching sound) produced

  1. Voltage Inspection

    1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the voltage between terminal 1 (ANT) and body ground from the back side of the antenna cord sub-assembly NO.2 connector.

      Voltage
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Standard
      1 (ANT) ←→ Body ground
      		Receiving radio (AM/FM)
