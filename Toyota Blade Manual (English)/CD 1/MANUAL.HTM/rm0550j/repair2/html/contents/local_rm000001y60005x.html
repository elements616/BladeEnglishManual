Engine Mounting Insulator LH (2GR-FE) Removal


  1. Check Front Wheels Straight-ahead Position

  2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  3. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly

  4. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal

  5. Remove Battery

  6. Remove Battery Carrier

  7. Remove Battery Carrier NO.2

  8. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose

  9. Remove Air Cleaner Filter Element Sub-assembly

  10. Remove Air Cleaner Case

  11. Remove Engine Mounting Insulator LH
