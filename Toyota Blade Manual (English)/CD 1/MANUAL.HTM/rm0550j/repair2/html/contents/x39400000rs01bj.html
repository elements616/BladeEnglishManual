Circuit Description


When the engine is cranked, a signal is input from the passenger side J/B (main body ECU) to the STSW terminal of the engine control computer, and current flows from the NSW terminal through the neutral switch to the ST relay coil. The signal from the NSW terminal (STA signal) is also input to the STA terminal. When the STA signal and NE signal are input to the engine control computer, current flows to the coil of the C/OPN relay. This turns on the switch of the C/OPN relay, and the fuel pump operates via the F/PMP relay. While the NE signal is being output (while the engine is running), the engine control computer keeps the circuit opening relay ON, so the fuel pump continues to operate. The fuel pump speed is controlled in 2 steps (high speed and low speed) according to the engine condition (starting, light load, heavy load). The switch of the F/PMP relay is changed by the FPR signal from the engine control computer. During idling or under light load, current flows to the fuel pump through the fuel pump resistor, and the fuel pump operates at low speed.
