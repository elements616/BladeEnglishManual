ABS with EBD & TRC & VSC & BA System Function Inspection

Preparation List



1. Brake System Indicator Light Initial Check

  1. Turn IG ON.

  2. Check that the ABS warning light, brake warning light and slip indicator light come on and go off after approximately 3 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    If a light does not come on, check for a burnt-out bulb and inspect the communication line between the skid control computer (built into the actuator) and the meter.



2. Brake Actuator Operation Inspection

  1. Pre-inspection Preparation

    1. With the IG OFF, connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3 and start the engine.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. Turn on the SST power and select "Menu (Diagnosis)" → "Chassis" → "ABS/VSC" → "Active Test".

  2. Actuator Motor Operation Inspection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    To protect the motor, do not operate the motor for 5 seconds or more. When operating it continuously, allow an interval of 20 seconds or more.


    1. Turn the motor relay ON and check the motor operating sound.

      Specified Condition
      Motor operating sound is heard

    2. Turn the motor relay OFF.

    3. Keep the brake pedal depressed with a constant force for 15 seconds and check the brake pedal condition.

      Specified Condition
      Pedal does not go down

    4. With the brake pedal depressed, turn the motor relay ON and check the brake pedal condition.

      Specified Condition
      No pulsation in the pedal

    5. Turn the motor relay OFF and release the brake pedal.

  3. Inspect Solenoid Operation

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. To protect the motor and solenoids, do not operate the motor and solenoids for 5 seconds or more. When operating it continuously, allow an interval of 20 seconds or more.
    2. The Active Test operating time for each solenoid is approximately 2 seconds, after which it turns OFF automatically.
    3. Do not depress the brake pedal with only the pressure reduction valve operating by itself.
    4. Do not turn ON any solenoid other than those instructed.


    1. Depress the brake pedal and hold it.

    2. Turn the SFRH and SFRR solenoids ON at the same time and check the brake pedal condition.

      Specified Condition
      Pedal does not go down

    3. Check the brake pedal condition when the SFRH and SFRR solenoids turn OFF.

      Specified Condition
      Pedal goes down

    4. Turn the motor relay ON and check the brake pedal condition.

      Specified Condition
      Pedal returns

    5. Check the solenoids for the other wheels in the same way.

      □ HINT □
      Front right (SFRH, SFRR), front left (SFLH, SFLR), rear right (SRRH, SRRR), rear left (SRLH, SRLR)
