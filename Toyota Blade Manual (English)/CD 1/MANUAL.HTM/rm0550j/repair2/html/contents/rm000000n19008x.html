Print


1. Rear Shock Absorber Assembly LH Inspection



  1. Inspect the rear shock absorber assembly LH by compressing it fully and letting it extend naturally 4 times or more.

    Specified Condition
    When compressed and extended at a constant speed, the resistance must be constant throughout the entire stroke, with no abnormal resistance or noise. Also, when extending, it must return at a constant speed without noise.
