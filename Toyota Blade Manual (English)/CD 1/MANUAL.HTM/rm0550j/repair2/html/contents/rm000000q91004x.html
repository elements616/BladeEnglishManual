Power Outlet Socket Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



2. Remove Power Outlet Socket Cover



  1. Insert a flat-tip screwdriver into the claw portion of the power outlet socket cover and break off the claw.

  2. Tilt the power outlet socket cover and power point socket assembly to create a gap between the power outlet socket cover and the support (steel part).

  3. Break off the claw on the opposite side of the power outlet socket cover in the same way.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not move the support (steel part) too much, as it may be damaged.
    2. Do not damage the power point socket assembly and console box rear cover.




  4. Remove the power outlet socket cover and power point socket assembly together from the console box rear cover.



3. Remove Power Point Socket Assembly



  1. Using nippers, etc., cut off the area around the 2 claws of the power outlet socket cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the power point socket assembly.

  2. Remove the power point socket assembly from the lid side of the power outlet socket cover.
