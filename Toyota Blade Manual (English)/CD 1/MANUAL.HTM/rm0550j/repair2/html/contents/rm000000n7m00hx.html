Fan & Alternator V-belt (2GR-FE) Removal

Print


1. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly



  1. Place both hands on the front of the cover and lift it upward to disengage the 2 front clips, then continue lifting upward to disengage the 1 rear clips, and remove the V-bank cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pulling up the front and rear at the same time may cause damage.



2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Remove Front Tire RH


4. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear RH

  1. Remove the 5 clips and the engine under cover rear RH.



5. Remove Fan & Alternator V Belt



  1. Put a tool on the pulley set bolt of the tensioner, and turn the tensioner counterclockwise to loosen the tension.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Performing this from under the vehicle may damage the tensioner.
    2. The pulley set bolt has a left-hand thread, so turning it clockwise loosens the pulley set bolt.

  2. Using a 5mm hexagon wrench, fix the tensioner as shown in the illustration and remove the belt.
