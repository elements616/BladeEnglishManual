Back Door Stay Assembly Disposal

Print

Preparation List



1. Dispose of Back Door Stay Assembly LH

  1. With the piston rod extended, secure the back door stay assembly LH in a vise.




  2. Using a hacksaw, cut between A and B shown in the illustration to release the gas.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Wear protective goggles when performing this work.
    2. The enclosed gas is colorless, odorless and harmless, but cover the area with a shop rag or similar, as hacksaw chips may fly.
