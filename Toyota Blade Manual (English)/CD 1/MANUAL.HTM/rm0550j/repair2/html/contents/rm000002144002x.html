Air Conditioner Control Panel Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



3. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



4. Remove Instrument Panel Register Assembly CTR



  1. Pull the instrument panel register assembly CTR horizontally toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 clips, 5 claws and 3 guides.




  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the instrument panel register assembly CTR.



5. Remove Shift Lever Knob



  1. Turn the shift lever knob in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration to remove it.



6. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



7. Remove Position Indicator Housing Assembly



  1. Disengage the 7 claws.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the position indicator housing assembly.



8. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel LWR CTR



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 6 clips and 4 claws, and remove the instrument panel finish panel LWR CTR.



9. Instrument Cluster Finish Panel Sub-assembly Center Removal (w/o Radio Receiver)



  1. Disengage the 4 clips and remove the instrument cluster finish panel center.



10. Radio Tuner Opening Cover w/ Bracket Removal (w/o Radio Receiver)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts <G> and remove the radio tuner opening cover w/ bracket.



11. Radio Receiver Assembly w/ Bracket Removal (w/o HDD Navigation System)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.




  2. Disengage the 4 clips and pull the radio receiver assembly w/ bracket toward the rear of the vehicle.

  3. Disconnect each connector and remove the radio receiver assembly w/ bracket.



12. Navigation Receiver Assembly w/ Bracket Removal (w/ HDD Navigation System)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.




  2. Disengage the 4 clips and pull the navigation receiver assembly w/ bracket toward the rear of the vehicle.

  3. Disconnect each connector and remove the navigation receiver assembly w/ bracket.



13. Air Conditioner Control Assembly Removal



  1. Disengage the 4 clips.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the air conditioner control assembly.
