Speed Sensor Rear LH (4WD) Removal

Print

Preparation List


□ HINT □
When replacing the sensor rotor, replace it together with the rear axle hub & bearing assembly, as the sensor rotor is built into the rear axle hub & bearing assembly.



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Rear Door Scuff Plate LH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)



3. Remove Rear Door Opening Trim Weatherstrip LH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)



4. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly (LH Seat Position)


5. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1 (LH Seat Position)



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



6. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2 (LH Seat Position)



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



7. Remove Rear Seat Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 front side bolts.




  2. Fold the rear seat assembly LH forward.

  3. Remove the 2 rear side bolts and remove the rear seat assembly LH.



8. Remove Package Tray Trim Panel Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 suspenders from the package tray trim garnish hooks.

  2. Disengage the 2 pins and remove the package tray trim panel assembly.



9. Remove Rear Floor Mat Rear



  1. Remove the rear floor mat rear.



10. Remove Deck Floor Box RH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the deck floor box RH.



11. Remove Deck Floor Box LH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and the deck floor box LH.



12. Remove Luggage Hold Belt Striker Assembly (Rear Side Installation Position)



  1. Remove the bolt and the trunk (luggage compartment) hold belt striker.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the LH side using the same procedure.



13. Remove Deck Trim Cover Rear



  1. Disengage the 10 claws and remove the deck trim cover rear.



14. Remove Rear Seatback Hinge Sub-assembly

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)



15. Remove Rear Seat Side Garnish LH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)



16. Remove Luggage Hold Belt Striker Assembly (LH Side Installation Position)

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side.



17. Remove Room Light Assembly NO.2



  1. Using a screwdriver with a thin blade wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claws.

  2. Disconnect the connector and remove room light assembly No.2.



18. Remove Deck Trim Side Panel Assembly LH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side. (see)



19. Remove Rear Tire


20. Remove Speed Sensor RR LH



  1. Disconnect the speed sensor connector.

  2. Remove the grommet and pull it out toward the outside of the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not pull out the sensor wire harness by grasping it.



  3. Remove bolt A and disconnect sensor clamp No.3 from the body.



  4. Remove bolt B and disconnect sensor clamp No.2 from the trailing arm bracket.

  5. Disconnect sensor clamp No.1 from the slotted hole of the trailing arm.

  6. Remove bolt C and the speed sensor RR LH.



21. Remove Rear Axle Shaft Nut LH



  1. Set the SST aligned with the groove of the rear drive shaft assembly LH, and using a hammer, unstake the rear axle shaft nut.

    SST
    09930-00010  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set SST in the groove with its flat surface facing up.
    2. Do not grind the tip of the SST with a grinder or equivalent.
    3. When removing the rear axle shaft nut, completely unstake it.
    4. Do not damage the threads of the drive shaft assembly.

  2. Using a socket wrench (30mm), remove the rear axle shaft nut.



22. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector



  1. Remove the parking brake lever protector from the parking brake cable assembly No.3.



23. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3



  1. Disconnect the cable end from the operating lever of the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.




  2. Using an offset wrench (14mm), disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.



24. Disconnect Rear Disc Brake Caliper Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the rear disc brake caliper assembly LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Suspend the caliper with wire or equivalent.



25. Remove Rear Disc



  1. Put matchmarks on the disc and axle hub, and remove the disc.



26. Remove Rear Axle Hub & Bearing Assembly LH

□ HINT □
When replacing the sensor rotor, replace it together with the rear axle hub & bearing assembly, as the sensor rotor is built into the rear axle hub & bearing assembly. (see)
