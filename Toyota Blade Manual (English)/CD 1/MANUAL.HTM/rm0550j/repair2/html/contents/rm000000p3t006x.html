1. Intelligent Parking Assist

■ NOTICE ■
If "System Initializing" is displayed on the intelligent parking assist screen, perform steering sensor steering angle neutral point memorization.

  1. Steering Sensor Steering Angle Neutral Point Memorization

    1. The steering angle neutral point is memorized by turning the steering wheel fully to the left and right on a level road.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Steering sensor neutral point memorization must be performed with the engine running. Therefore, apply the parking brake, depress the brake pedal, move the shift lever to P, and take sufficient care that the vehicle does not move off.

      □ HINT □
      Selecting "Help", displayed together with "System Initializing", displays the above correction method.
