Function Description


The speed sensors detect the wheel speeds and send signals to the skid control computer. These signals are used for ABS control.

The speed sensor generates vehicle speed pulses by detecting changes in the magnetic field of a magnetic rotor with N/S poles evenly distributed around its circumference.

From these vehicle speed pulses, the skid control computer detects each wheel speed.

  1. At a vehicle speed of 10km/h or more, an open or short in the speed sensor signal circuit continues for 1 seconds or more
  2. When an open in the sensor signal of the malfunctioning wheel occurs 255 times or more
  3. An open in the speed sensor signal circuit continues for 0.5 seconds or more
  4. When IG1 terminal voltage is 9.5 V or higher and the sensor power source voltage remains low for 0.5 seconds or more
  1. Open or short in speed sensor
  2. Speed sensor improperly installed or abnormal clearance with sensor rotor
  3. Open or short in wire harness
  4. Poor connector contact pressure, terminal deformation or corrosion
  5. Foreign matter adhering to speed sensor
  6. Sensor rotor malfunction (foreign matter adhesion, damage)
  7. Wiring resistance of speed sensor circuit
  8. Actuator assembly
