E.F.I. Water Temperature Sensor (2AZ-FE) Removal


  1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal

  2. Drain Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)

  3. Remove Engine Cover Sub-assembly NO.1

  4. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose

  5. Remove Air Cleaner Case

  6. Remove E.F.I. Water Temperature Sensor
