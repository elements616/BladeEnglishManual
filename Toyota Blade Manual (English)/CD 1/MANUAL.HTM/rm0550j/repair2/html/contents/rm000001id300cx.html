  1. Smart Entry Cancel Function

    1. Smart Entry Cancel


      1. When cancel is set, the following functions stop operating.

        1. Smart entry control
        2. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) lock-in prevention (cabin and trunk) control
        3. Smart Back Door Open Control
        4. Smart warning control

    2. Smart Cancel Setting Procedure


      1. Set the cancel with the following operation. To return from the cancel state, perform the same operation again.

        Procedure
        		Operation
        Prerequisites
        		Power OFF, driver door closed, driver door unlocked
        Operation 1
        		Perform wireless unlock 1 times
        Operation 2
        		Within 5 seconds of performing [Operation 1], open the driver door
        Operation 3
        		Within 5 seconds of performing [Operation 2], perform wireless unlock 2 times
        Operation 4
        		Within 30 seconds of performing [Operation 3], open → close the driver door 2 times, then open it again (driver door open → close → open → close → open)
        Operation 5
        		Within 30 seconds of performing [Operation 4], perform wireless unlock 2 times
        Operation 6
        		Within 30 seconds of performing [Operation 5], open → close the driver door 1 times, then open it again (driver door open → close → open)
        Operation 7
        		Within 5 seconds after performing [Operation 6], close the driver door
        Complete
        		When smart cancel is set: wireless door lock buzzer sounds 2 times "beep, beep"
        When returning from smart cancel: wireless door lock buzzer sounds 1 time "beep"

        ■ NOTICE ■
        If the driver door is opened → closed during [Operation 3] or [Operation 5], the cancel operation is aborted.
