Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding Center LH Installation

Print

Preparation List



1. Install Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding Clip NO.1

■ NOTICE ■
  1. When reusing the clip, do not peel off the double-sided tape remaining on the clip installation position on the body side or on the clip.
  2. When installing a new clip, peel off the double-sided tape remaining on the installation surface on the body side, and clean the body side using white gasoline.
  3. Use the primer supplied with the Diabond. (Although labeled as primer, it is a 2-part type adhesive, so do not allow this primer to dry)

□ HINT □
  1. Since it is a 2-part type adhesive, it is also possible to mix the 2 parts in a separate container and then apply it to the bonding surface for bonding.
  2. Mixing ratio adhesive:primer = 10:1

  1. Apply protective tape.

  2. Apply Diabond in a bead of 2-3mm diameter from the tube onto a new roof drip side finish moulding.

  3. Apply primer with the supplied brush to the body side at the roof drip side finish moulding clip installation position.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not allow the primer to dry.

  4. Press and install the roof drip side finish moulding clip.




  5. Immediately after applying primer, attach the clip to the roof panel referring to the position shown in the illustration. Lightly rub them together to blend the adhesive and primer, then position the clip and press it firmly.

  6. Install the roof drip side finish moulding center LH 40 minutes or more after pressing.

    □ HINT □
    1. Initial curing time 40 minutes
    2. Complete curing time 24 hours



2. Install Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding Center LH



  1. Engage the 2 clips to install the roof drip side finish moulding center LH.
