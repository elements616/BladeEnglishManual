Front Power Seat System Function Inspection

Print


1. Basic Operation Inspection (Driver Side)

  1. Check that the front seat assembly RH slides forward and backward when the front power seat switch RH (slide) is operated.

  2. Check that the front part of the front seat cushion assembly RH moves up and down when the front power seat switch RH (front vertical) is operated.

  3. Check that the entire front seat cushion assembly RH moves up and down when the front power seat switch RH (rear lifter) is operated.

  4. Check that the front seatback assembly RH moves forward and backward when the front power seat switch RH (reclining) is operated.



2. Basic Operation Inspection (Front Passenger Side)

  1. Check that the front seat assembly LH slides forward and backward when the front power seat switch LH (slide) is operated.

  2. Check that the front seatback assembly LH moves forward and backward when the front power seat switch LH (reclining) is operated.



3. Inspect Front Seat Assembly (Driver Side Power Seat Motor)

  1. Power Seat Motor Internal PTC Operation Inspection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Inspect with the seat installed on the vehicle.


    1. Move the seat fully to the front or rear, or fully up or down, and wait for approximately 60 seconds.

    2. Check the time until the current is cut off (motor operating sound stops) when the front power seat switch RH is operated further in the same direction.

      Specified Condition
      Cut off in approximately 4 to 90 seconds

    3. After the cutoff inspection, turn the switch OFF, wait for approximately 60 seconds, and check that the motor operates when the front power seat switch RH is operated in the opposite direction.



4. Inspect Front Seat Assembly (Front Passenger Side Power Seat Motor)

  1. Power Seat Motor Internal PTC Operation Inspection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Inspect with the seat installed on the vehicle.


    1. Move the seat fully to the front or rear and wait for approximately 60 seconds.

    2. Check the time until the current is cut off (motor operating sound stops) when the front power seat switch LH is operated further in the same direction.

      Specified Condition
      Cut off in approximately 4 to 90 seconds

    3. After the cutoff inspection, turn the switch OFF, wait for approximately 60 seconds, and check that the motor operates when the front power seat switch LH is operated in the opposite direction.
