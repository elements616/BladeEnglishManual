Circuit Description


The airbag computer assembly consists of a G sensor, safing sensor, drive circuit, diagnosis circuit, ignition control, etc. The airbag computer assembly receives signals from the G sensor, determines whether to activate the airbags (horn button assembly, instrument panel passenger airbag assembly, curtain shield airbags RH and LH, front seat airbag assemblies RH and LH) and pretensioners (front seat outer belt assemblies RH and LH), and detects malfunctions in the airbag system.

When a malfunction is detected in the airbag computer assembly, DTC B1000 is stored.
