Print



    1. Precautions for Front Drive Shaft Assembly Removal/Installation

    1. When removing/installing the front drive shaft assembly RH on Drive Type 4WD models, drain all of the transaxle oil and transfer oil. If the drive shaft assembly RH is removed/installed without draining the oil, transfer oil will flow into the transaxle side, and since the transaxle and transfer use different oils, 2 types of oil will be mixed. If the oils mix, the inside of the transaxle and transfer must be cleaned.
