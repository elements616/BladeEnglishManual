Rear Drive Shaft (4WD) Removal


  1. Remove Rear Tire

  2. Remove Rear Axle Shaft Nut LH

  3. Remove Rear Axle Shaft Nut RH

  4. Disconnect Speed Sensor Rear LH

  5. Disconnect Speed Sensor Rear RH

  6. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector

  7. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3

  8. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.2

  9. Disconnect Rear Disc Brake Caliper Assembly LH

  10. Disconnect Rear Disc Brake Caliper Assembly RH

  11. Remove Rear Disc LH

  12. Remove Rear Disc RH

  13. Remove Rear Axle Hub & Bearing Assembly LH

  14. Remove Rear Axle Hub & Bearing Assembly RH

  15. Remove Height Control Sensor Sub-assembly Rear

  16. Remove Rear Suspension Arm Assembly NO.1 LH

  17. Remove Rear Suspension Arm Assembly NO.1 RH

  18. Loosen Trailing Arm Assembly Rear LH

  19. Loosen Trailing Arm Assembly Rear RH

  20. Loosen Rear Suspension Arm Assembly NO.2 LH

  21. Loosen Rear Suspension Arm Assembly NO.2 RH

  22. Remove Rear Axle Carrier LH

  23. Remove Rear Axle Carrier RH

  24. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Assembly LH

  25. Remove Rear Drive Shaft Assembly RH
