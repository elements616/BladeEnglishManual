      1. Engine Speed Measurement by Cylinder

        □ HINT □
        1. If the engine speed of any cylinder is higher than that of the other cylinders, the compression pressure of that cylinder may be abnormal.
        2. If a malfunction occurs, perform a compression inspection. (see)

        1. Warm up the engine.

        2. Turn IG OFF.

        3. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

          SST
          09991-70201  

        4. Turn IG ON.

        5. Following the SST (TaSCAN) screen display, select [Active Test] - [Check the Cylinder Compression].

        6. In the items to monitor, select [Engine Speed of Cylinder Cut #1 - #4] and [All Cylinders Average Engine Speed].

        7. With the engine stopped, switch [Check the Cylinder Compression] to [ON].

          □ HINT □
          1. After the above procedure, [Active Test] - [Check the Cylinder Compression] can be performed.
          2. Fuel injection of all cylinders is prohibited, and the engine speed of each cylinder can be measured.
          3. The engine speed displayed first is the maximum value of the SST (TaSCAN). [Engine Speed of Cylinder Cut #1 - #4] displayed during cranking for approximately 10 seconds are the measured values.

        8. Crank the engine for approximately 10 seconds.

          □ HINT □
          Continue cranking until [Engine Speed of Cylinder Cut #1 - #4] and [All Cylinders Average Engine Speed] are displayed on the SST (TaSCAN).

        9. Read [Engine Speed of Cylinder Cut #1 - #4] and [All Cylinders Average Engine Speed] displayed on the SST (TaSCAN).

          ■ NOTICE ■
          1. If the engine is cranked with [Active Test] - [Check the Cylinder Compression] not performed, the engine will start.
          2. To perform the test again after performing the Active Test, return to the Active Test menu screen once, and then perform it again.
          3. Use a fully charged battery.
          4. When the Active Test is performed, DTC P1604 (starting malfunction) etc. may be detected.
          5. After performing the Active Test, be sure to check and clear the DTCs.
