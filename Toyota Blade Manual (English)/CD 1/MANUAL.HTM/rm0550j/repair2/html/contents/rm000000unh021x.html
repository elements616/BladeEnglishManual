Power Steering Computer Assembly Removal

Print


1. Check Front Wheels Straight-ahead Position


2. Remove Instrument Panel UPR w/ Instrument Panel Passenger Airbag Assembly

□ HINT □
For the procedure up to removal of the instrument panel upper w/ instrument panel passenger airbag assembly,for reference.



3. Remove Power Steering Computer Assembly



  1. Detach the wire harness clamp from the power steering computer assembly.

  2. Disconnect the connector from the power steering computer assembly.

    □ HINT □
    As shown in the illustration, pull out the lock lever lock, then lower the lock lever and disconnect the connector.




  3. Disconnect the 3 connectors from the power steering computer assembly.




  4. Remove the 2 bolts and nuts, and remove the power steering computer assembly.
