Transfer Oil (4WD) Adjustment

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Transfer Oil



  1. Using straight hexagon wrench 10mm and an offset wrench (10X12 long), remove transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) and the gasket.




  2. Check that the oil is within 0-5 mm of the bottom edge of the transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) hole.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Too much or too little oil can cause problems.
    2. Stop the vehicle on a level surface.

  3. If the oil level is low, check for oil leaks, then add oil.

  4. Using straight hexagon wrench 10mm and an offset wrench (10X12 long), install transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 39 N/m{ 398 kgf/cm }



2. Add Transfer Oil

  1. Using straight hexagon wrench 10mm and an offset wrench (10X12 long), remove transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) and the gasket.

  2. Fill with hypoid gear oil SX.

    Reference Value
    0.45L

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Point the nozzle tip toward the front of the vehicle.
    1. If the nozzle is pointed in the wrong direction, oil may spurt out.
    1. Fill the oil slowly.
    1. Repeat the oil filling several times at intervals of a few minutes.

  3. Check that the oil level is within 0-5 mm of the bottom edge of the transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) hole.

  4. Using straight hexagon wrench 10mm and an offset wrench (10X12 long), install transfer case plug No.2 (filler plug) with a new gasket.

    Standard
    T = 39 N/m{ 398 kgf/cm }

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When replacing the oil, recheck the oil level after driving.
