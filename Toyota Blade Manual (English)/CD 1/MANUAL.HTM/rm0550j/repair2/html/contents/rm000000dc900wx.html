Function Description


The transmission control computer shifts from 1 to 6 by combining the ON-OFF operation of the SL1, SL2, SL3 and SL4 solenoids.

If an open or short occurs in any solenoid valve circuit, the transmission control computer turns other solenoid valves ON or OFF as a fail-safe function. Also, if all solenoid valves malfunction, only the mechanical hydraulic circuit operates and the transmission is shifted manually. (In the case of an open or short, the transmission control computer stops current flow to the malfunctioning solenoid valve.)
