Stop Light Control Relay Assembly On-vehicle Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Stop Light Control Relay Assembly



  1. Input/Output Operation Inspection

    1. Disconnect the stop light control relay connector.

    2. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the voltage between each terminal of the vehicle side wire harness connector and body ground.

      SST
      09082-00030  
      09083-00170  

      Voltage
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      		Condition
      		Standard
      6 (+B) - Body ground
      		Always
      		11 to 14 V

    3. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the resistance between each terminal of the vehicle side wire harness connector and body ground.

      SST
      09082-00030  
      09083-00170  

      Resistance
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      		Condition
      		Standard
      4 (GND) - Body ground
      		Always
      		Below 1 Ω

    4. Connect the stop light control relay connector.




    5. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the voltage between each connector terminal and body ground.

      SST
      09082-00030  
      09083-00170  

      Voltage
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      		Condition
      		Standard
      2 (STP) - Body ground
      		Brake pedal not depressed → depressed
      		Below 1 V → 11 to 14 V
      8 (OUT) - Body ground
      		Brake pedal not depressed → depressed
      		Below 1 V → 11 to 14 V
