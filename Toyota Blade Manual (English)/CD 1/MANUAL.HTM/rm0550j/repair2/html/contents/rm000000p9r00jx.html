Preparation List



1. Spiral Cable Sub-assembly Inspection

  1. Set the spiral cable to the center position. (see)




  2. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the resistance between the terminals of the spiral cable under the following conditions.

    1. After setting the spiral cable to the center position, measure the resistance at the position turned 2.5 turns clockwise and at the position turned 2.5 turns counterclockwise.

    2. Set the spiral cable to the position turned 2.5 turns clockwise from the center position, and measure the resistance while turning it 5.0 turns counterclockwise.
