How to Troubleshoot ECU Controlled Systems Malfunction Analysis from Customer Problem Analysis Check Sheet

Print


  1. When troubleshooting, do not have preconceptions, in order to accurately confirm the problem symptom and make accurate judgments. To confirm the problem symptom, it is very important to ask the customer about the conditions when the problem occurred.
  2. The following 5 items are important points for problem analysis. Past problems that seem unrelated, repair history, etc. can sometimes be useful, so it is necessary for troubleshooting to collect as much information as possible and accurately understand its relationship with the problem symptom. A customer problem analysis check sheet is provided in the diagnosis section for each system.

  1. What - Vehicle model, system name
  2. When - Date, time, frequency
  3. Where - Road conditions
  4. Conditions - Driving conditions, weather

  5. How did it occur - Problem symptom
