Audio & Visual Precautions

Print



    1. Precautions for Handling and Servicing

    1. If the negative terminal is disconnected from the battery during work, explain to the customer that the AM/FM station channel information and any other registered information stored in the radio receiver assembly will be erased. If the settings need to be restored, record the stored channel information beforehand and set it again after the negative terminal is reconnected.

    2. Eject all music discs, etc. from the radio receiver assembly, etc. before removal/installation.

      □ HINT □
      If a music disc, etc. cannot be ejected due to a malfunction, do not force it out; send the unit for repair as is.

    3. Do not touch the cone paper of the speaker assembly.
