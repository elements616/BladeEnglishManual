Engine Mounting Insulator Rear (2AZ-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove the engine under cover No.1.



2. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.2

  1. Remove the 5 clips and engine under cover No.2.



3. Disconnect Transmission Control Cable Assembly



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the transmission control cable support from the engine mounting insulator RR.



4. Remove Engine Mounting Insulator Rear



  1. Support the oil pan with a transmission jack using a wooden block or similar.




  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator RR from the engine mounting bracket RR.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts, and remove the engine mounting insulator RR.
