Smart Entry & Start System (Smart Entry) Function Description

Print





  1. Vehicle Battery Power Saving Function

    1. In the smart entry & start system (smart entry), a request signal is periodically transmitted every 0.25 seconds during standby to form the detection area, so the battery may discharge if the vehicle is left for a long time. To prevent battery discharge, the following control is set.


      1. If there is no response from the electrical key transmitter for 5 days or more, the periodic request signal transmission interval is extended to 0.75 seconds.

      2. If there is no response from the electrical key transmitter for 14 days or more, and 10 minutes or more have elapsed with the electrical key transmitter in the detection area (including inside the vehicle), smart entry & start system control is stopped.

    2. The smart entry function is restored when any of the following operations is performed.


      1. When locking/unlocking by wireless operation.

      2. When locking by pressing the lock switch of the door outside handle assembly RH or LH.

      3. When locking/unlocking by inserting the mechanical key into the driver side door key cylinder.



    1. Smart Warning Function

      1. Shift Not in P (Power Left ON) Exit Warning


        1. The driver opened the door and attempted to exit the vehicle with the shift lever in a position other than P. [A]

          Warning Purpose
          1. Prevention of sudden start
          2. Vehicle theft prevention
          3. Rollaway prevention
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Shift position other than P
          2. ACC ON or IG ON
          3. Driver side door open
          4. Vehicle speed 0 km/h
          Warning Stop Conditions
          (Warning stops when any condition is met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. Shift position P
          3. Vehicle speed above 0 km/h
          4. Driver side door closed
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Continuous sounding "beep"
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Shift to P"
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		-

        2. From state [A] above, the driver further took the electrical key transmitter out, closed the driver side door and attempted to leave the vehicle.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Prevention of sudden start, vehicle theft and roll-away
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Shift position other than P
          2. ACC ON or IG ON
          3. Driver side door open → closed
          4. Vehicle speed 0 km/h
          5. No electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification NG]
          Warning Stop Conditions
          (Warning stops when any condition is met)
          1. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification OK]
            (Buzzer in meter and wireless door lock buzzer stop sounding; only "Shift to P" is displayed)
          2. Vehicle speed above 0km/h
            (Buzzer in meter and wireless door lock buzzer stop sounding, "Key not detected")
          3. Power OFF
            (All warnings stop)
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Continuous sounding "beep"
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Shift to P" and "Key not detected" displayed alternately
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		Continuous sounding "beep"

      2. Key Reminder Warning


        1. The driver opened the driver side door and attempted to leave the vehicle with ACC ON.

        2. The driver opened the driver side door and attempted to leave the vehicle with the steering lock unlocked.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Vehicle theft prevention/Regulatory compliance
          Prerequisites
          		Remote engine starter not operating
          Warning Start Conditions (1)
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. ACC ON
          2. Driver side door open
          Warning Start Conditions (2)
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. Steering lock unlocked
          3. Driver side door open
          Warning Stop Conditions (1)
          (Warning stops when any condition is met)
          1. IG ON
          2. Driver side door closed
          Warning Stop Conditions (2)
          (Warning stops when all conditions are met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. Steering lock locked
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Intermittent sounding "beep, beep, beep..."
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		-
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		-

      3. P Position Power Left ON Exit Warning


        1. With the shift in P, the driver took the electrical key transmitter out, closed the driver side door and attempted to leave the vehicle. [A]

          Meaning of Warning
          		Prevention of vehicle theft, inability to restart the engine and battery discharge
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Shift position P
          2. ACC ON or IG ON
          3. Driver side door open → closed
          4. No electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification NG]
          Warning Stop Conditions
          (Warning stops when any condition is met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification OK]
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Sounds "pip" 1 time(s)
          (If cabin verification NG continues, sounds 1 time(s) again when driving first starts)
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Key not detected"
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		Sounds 3 times (pip, pip, pip)

        2. From state [A] above, the lock switch of the door handle was pressed to perform smart door lock.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Prevention of vehicle theft and battery discharge
          Prerequisites
          		Door handle lock switch OFF → ON performed
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Shift position P
          2. ACC ON or IG ON
          3. All doors closed
          4. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) outside vehicle [smart outside verification OK]
          Warning Stop Conditions (1)
          (Warning stops when all conditions are met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. No electrical key transmitter (electronic key) outside vehicle [smart outside verification NG]
          Warning Stop Conditions (2)
          (Warning stops when all conditions are met)
          1. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification OK]
          2. No electrical key transmitter (electronic key) outside vehicle [smart outside verification NG]
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		-
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Key not detected" (not displayed when [A] warning stop conditions are met)
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		Sounds for 2 seconds (beeeep)

      4. Smart Entry Door Ajar Warning


        1. Smart lock was attempted with one of the vehicle doors open.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Vehicle theft prevention
          Prerequisites
          		Power OFF and any door open
          Warning Start Condition
          		Smart lock operation performed after preconditions are met
          Warning Stop Conditions
          (Warning stops when any condition is met)
          1. ACC ON or IG ON
          2. All doors closed
          3. Wireless unlock performed
          4. 10 seconds elapsed after the warning started
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		-
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		-
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		Continuous sounding "beep"

      5. Passenger Key Take-out Warning


        1. With ACC ON or IG ON, a passenger took out the electrical key transmitter that had been placed in a bag, etc.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Prevention of inability to restart
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. ACC ON or IG ON
          2. Door other than driver side door open → closed
          3. Vehicle speed 0 km/h
          4. No electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification NG]
          Warning Stop Conditions
          (Warning stops when any condition is met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. Vehicle speed above 0 km/h
          3. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification OK]
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Sounds "beep" 1 time(s)
          (If cabin verification NG continues, sounds 1 time(s) again when driving first starts)
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Key not detected"
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		Sounds 3 times "pip, pip, pip"

      6. Electrical Key Transmitter Out of Area Warning


        1. An attempt was made to start the engine or turn ACC ON or IG ON with the electrical key transmitter (electronic key) not in the cabin, or with the electrical key transmitter battery depleted.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Prevention of user confusion
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Engine switch ON
          2. No electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification NG]
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Sounds "beep" 1 time(s)
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Key not detected" (displayed for up to 8 seconds)
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		-

      7. Electrical Key Transmitter Left Behind Warning


        1. The driver exited the vehicle with the electrical key transmitter in the cabin and pressed the door handle lock switch to perform smart door lock.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Prevention of vehicle theft due to electrical key transmitter (electronic key) being left behind
          Warning Start Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. Power OFF
          2. All doors closed
          3. Door handle lock switch ON
          4. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification OK]
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		-
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		-
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		Sounds "beep" for 2 seconds

      8. Electrical Key Transmitter Battery Low Warning


        1. The vehicle was driven with an electrical key transmitter whose battery charge was low.

          Meaning of Warning
          		Convenience function
          Warning Conditions
          (Warning starts when all conditions are met)
          1. IG ON → OFF after IG ON has continued for 20 minutes or more
          2. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) battery charge is low
          3. Electrical key transmitter (electronic key) in cabin [smart cabin verification OK]
          Warning Type
          1. Buzzer in meter
          		Sounds "beep" 1 time(s)
          1. Multi-information display in meter
          		"Key battery low"
          1. Wireless door lock buzzer (outside vehicle)
          		-
