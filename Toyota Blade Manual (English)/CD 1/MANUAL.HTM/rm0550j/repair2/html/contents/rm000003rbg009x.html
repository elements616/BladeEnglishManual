DTC P2757/64 SLU/DSU Solenoid Circuit (Functional Malfunction)

Preparation List


Function Description


The engine control computer uses the lock-up control solenoids (SL and DSU) to perform lock-up control according to driving conditions.

□ HINT □
The solenoid (DSU) controls the lock-up clutch pressure according to the input torque.


During torque converter lock-up control (ON/OFF), the engine control computer compares the turbine speed (NT) and engine speed (NE) to detect mechanical failure of the lock-up control solenoids (SL and DSU).

If DTCs for other lock-up control solenoid (SL and DSU) circuits are output at the same time, it is necessary to troubleshoot the DTCs other than P0741/64 and P2757/64 first.

Step 3Road Test

■ WARNING ■
Carefully check road safety.

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Be sure to perform this after inspecting and adjusting the engine.
  2. Perform the test at normal operating CVT fluid temperature (50 to 100°C).
  3. Perform the test with no electrical load, the A/C OFF and the brake OFF.

□ HINT □
  1. Lock-up control is not performed when the CVT fluid temperature is 40°C or less and the vehicle speed is 60km/h or less, or when the CVT fluid temperature is 5°C or less.
  2. Lock-up control is not performed when the engine coolant temperature is 30°C or less.
  3. The lock-up OFF vehicle speed varies according to the ON/OFF status of the electrical load, A/C and brake.

  1. Using SST (TaSCAN), read the Data Monitor items [Engine Speed], [Lock-up Status] and [Turbine Speed] during the drive test.
