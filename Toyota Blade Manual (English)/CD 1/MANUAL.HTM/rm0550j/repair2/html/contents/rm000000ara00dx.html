Front Seat Assembly RH (Power Seat Type) Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Power Seat Switch Knob (for Reclining)



  1. Using a screwdriver with protective tape, remove the power seat switch knob (for reclining).



2. Remove Power Seat Switch Knob (for Slide & Vertical)



  1. Using a screwdriver with protective tape, remove the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical).



3. Remove Front Seat Cushion Shield RH



  1. Disconnect the rubber band at the rear of the separate type front seatback cover with pad.




  2. Remove the 5 screws.

  3. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the front seat cushion shield RH together with the front seat cushion shield inner NO.1 RH.



4. Remove Front Seat Cushion Shield Inner NO.1 RH



  1. Remove the screw, disengage the 3 claws and remove the front seat cushion shield inner NO.1 RH.



5. Remove Front Seat Inner Belt Assembly RH

  1. Disconnect the connector and each clamp.




  2. Remove the nut and the front seat inner belt assembly RH.



6. Remove Front Seat Cushion Shield Inner RH



  1. Remove the screw and disengage the 3 claws.




  2. Slide the front seat cushion shield inner RH in the direction of the arrow in the illustration, disengage the clip guide and remove it.

    □ HINT □
    The clip remains on the seat frame side.



7. Remove Front Power Seat Switch RH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the 3 screws and the front power seat switch RH.



8. Remove Separate Type Front Seat Cushion Cover with Pad

  1. w/ Seat Heater




    1. Disconnect each connector and each clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.




    2. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.

    3. Disengage the 3 J-hooks.




    4. Disengage each J-hook and remove the separate type front seat cushion cover with pad.




  2. w/o Seat Heater

    1. Disconnect each connector and each clamp, and disconnect the wire harness.

    2. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.

    3. Disengage the 3 J-hooks.




    4. Disengage each J-hook and remove the separate type front seat cushion cover with pad.



9. Remove Separate Type Front Seat Cushion Cover RH



  1. Remove the 12 hog rings and the separate type front seat cushion cover RH.



10. Remove Front Seat Cushion Heater Assembly RH



  1. Cut off the 8 tack pins and remove the front seat cushion heater RH.



11. Remove Separate Type Front Seatback Cover with Pad

  1. w/ Seat Heater




    1. Remove the 3 hog rings.




    2. Remove the 3 hog rings.




    3. Disengage the 2 J-hooks.




    4. Remove the nut at the position shown in the illustration, and disconnect the bracket of the separate type front seatback cover RH.




    5. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the 2 front seat headrest supports.

    6. Remove the separate type front seatback cover with pad from the front seat frame & adjuster RH.

  2. w/o Seat Heater




    1. Remove the 3 hog rings.




    2. Remove the 3 hog rings.




    3. Remove the nut at the position shown in the illustration, and disconnect the bracket of the separate type front seatback cover RH.




    4. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the 2 front seat headrest supports.

    5. Remove the separate type front seatback cover with pad from the front seat frame & adjuster RH.



12. Remove Separate Type Front Seatback Cover RH



  1. Remove the 8 hog rings and the separate type front seatback cover RH.



13. Remove Front Seatback Heater Assembly RH



  1. Cut off the 4 tack pins and remove the front seatback heater RH.



14. Remove Reclining Adjuster Inside Cover RH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  2. Disengage the clamp and guide, and remove the reclining adjuster inside cover RH.



15. Remove Reclining Adjuster Inside Cover RH



  1. Remove the screw and the reclining adjuster inside cover RH.



16. Remove Reclining Adjuster Inside Cover LH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  2. Disengage the clamp and guide, and remove the reclining adjuster inside cover LH.



17. Remove Reclining Adjuster Inside Cover LH



  1. Remove the screw and the reclining adjuster inside cover LH.



18. Remove Front Seat Cushion Shield Lower RH

  1. Driver side




    1. Remove the screw and the front seat cushion shield lower RH.

  2. Front passenger side




    1. Remove the screw and the front seat cushion shield lower RH.



19. Remove Front Seat Cushion Shield Lower LH

  1. Driver side




    1. Remove the screw and the front seat cushion shield lower LH.

  2. Front passenger side




    1. Remove the screw and the front seat cushion shield lower LH.
