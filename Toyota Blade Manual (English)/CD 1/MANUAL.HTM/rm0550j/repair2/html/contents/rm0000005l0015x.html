DTC B1413/13 Front Evaporator Temperature Sensor Circuit Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The cooler thermistorNO.1(evaporator temperature sensor) is installed on the evaporator in the air conditioner unit, detects the temperature of the cool air after it passes through the evaporator, and is used for air conditioner control. The resistance of the cooler thermistorNO.1(evaporator temperature sensor) changes according to the temperature of the cool air after passing through the evaporator: as the cool air temperature decreases, the resistance increases, and as the cool air temperature increases, the resistance decreases. The air conditioner amplifier assembly outputs a voltage (5V) to the cooler thermistorNO.1(evaporator temperature sensor) and reads the voltage changes that accompany changes in the resistance of the cooler thermistorNO.1(evaporator temperature sensor).
