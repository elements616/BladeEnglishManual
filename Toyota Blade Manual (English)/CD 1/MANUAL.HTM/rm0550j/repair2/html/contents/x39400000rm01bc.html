DTC P2112/41 Throttle Body Stuck Closed

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. The throttle valve is operated by the throttle motor through gears. The throttle valve opening angle is detected by the throttle position sensor and fed back to the engine control computer. When a DTC is stored, the engine control computer cuts off the current to the throttle motor. The throttle valve is returned to a specified opening angle (6.5°) by the return spring.

Inspection Procedure


□ HINT □
Using SST (TaSCAN), read the freeze data. Freeze data records part of the engine operating condition when a malfunction occurs, and this information is useful when troubleshooting.


Step 1Inspect Throttle Body Assembly (Throttle Valve)

  1. Remove air cleaner hose No.1.

  1. Check for foreign matter between the throttle valve and housing; if any, remove it and clean.

  1. Check that the throttle valve moves smoothly when moved by hand to the fully open position.

  1. Check that the throttle valve moves smoothly when moved by hand to the fully closed position.

  1. Check that the throttle valve returns smoothly to the opener position when released.
