Circuit Description


The side collision sensor RH (for side airbag and curtain shield airbag deployment judgment) circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly, side airbag sensor assembly RH and airbag sensor rear RH.

The airbag sensor rear RH detects impacts and sends signals to the airbag computer assembly for airbag ignition judgment.

When a malfunction is detected in the side collision sensor RH (for side airbag and curtain shield airbag deployment judgment) circuit, DTCs B1623, B1632, B1633, B1642 and B1643 are recorded in the airbag computer assembly.
