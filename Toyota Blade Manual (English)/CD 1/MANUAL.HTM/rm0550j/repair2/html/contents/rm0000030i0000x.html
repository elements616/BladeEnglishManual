1. Precautions for Overhaul of Seats with SRS Side Airbag

  1. For seats with SRS side airbag, read the SRS airbag system precautions before starting work, and perform the work correctly. (see)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If the side airbag of a vehicle with side airbags has deployed, replace the front seat assembly RH.

3. Remove Front Seat Headrest Assembly


4. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Outer Front RH

  1. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover outer front RH.



5. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner Front RH



  1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover inner front RH.



6. Remove Seat Track Cover Outer RH

  1. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.




  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the seat track cover outer RH.



7. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner RH



  1. Disengage the claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the seat track bracket cover inner RH.



8. Remove Front Seat Assembly RH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts on the rear side of the front seat RH.

  2. Operate the seat track handle to move the seat slide position of the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts on the front side of the front seat assembly RH.

  4. Operate the seat track handle and reclining adjuster handle to move the seat slide position of the front seat assembly RH to the center and set the seatback upright.

  5. Disconnect each connector and remove the front seat assembly RH.
