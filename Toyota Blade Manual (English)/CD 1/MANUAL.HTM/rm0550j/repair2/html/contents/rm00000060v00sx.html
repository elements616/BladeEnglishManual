Inspection Procedure

Step 1Customer Problem Analysis

  1. Through customer problem analysis, check the illumination status of the VSC warning light when VSC did not operate.

    □ HINT □
    When the VSC warning light is on (malfunction DTC being output), VSC does not operate due to the fail-safe function.

  1. Through customer problem analysis, check whether the vehicle was being driven at 15km/h or less when VSC did not operate.

    □ HINT □
    VSC does not operate at vehicle speeds of 15km/h or less.
