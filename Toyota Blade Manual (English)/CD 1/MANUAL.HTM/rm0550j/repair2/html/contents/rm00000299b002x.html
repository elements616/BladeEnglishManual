Preparation List



1. Install Roof Wire NO.1 (w/ Interior Illumination Light)

  1. Align roof wire No.1 with the markings on the roof headlining and temporarily attach it.

  2. Turn the visor connector approximately 90° clockwise and install it to the roof headlining assembly.



  3. Apply the fixing tape aligned with the markings on the roof headlining assembly, and install roof wire No.1.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    Apply it securely so that it does not peel off, as this may cause abnormal noise.

    □ HINT □
    1. Align the roof wire No.1 marking shown in part B of the illustration with the reference position on the roof headlining assembly.
    2. Align the junction box with the roof headlining marking.
    3. Position roof wire No.1, the roof wire No.1 markings, the roof headlining assembly markings and the fixing tape as shown in the illustration.



2. Install Roof Wire NO.1 (w/o Interior Illumination Light)

  1. Align roof wire No.1 with the markings on the roof headlining and temporarily attach it.

  2. Turn the visor connector approximately 90° clockwise and install it to the roof headlining assembly.



  3. Apply the fixing tape aligned with the markings on the roof headlining assembly, and install roof wire No.1.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    Apply it securely so that it does not peel off, as this may cause abnormal noise.

    □ HINT □

    2. Align the junction box with the roof headlining marking.
    3. Position roof wire No.1, the roof wire No.1 markings, the roof headlining assembly markings and the fixing tape as shown in the illustration.



3. Install Antenna Cord Sub-assembly NO.2

  1. Align antenna cord NO.2 with the markings on the roof headlining assembly and temporarily attach it.



  2. Apply the fixing tape at the positions shown in the illustration, aligned with the markings on the roof headlining assembly.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Apply it securely so that it does not peel off, as this may cause abnormal noise.
    2. Packing tape may be used as a substitute.
    3. Apply the fixing tape so that antenna cord NO.2 is approximately in its center.

  3. Insert antenna cord NO.2 into the cutout in the roof headlining assembly, and install antenna cord NO.2 to the roof headlining assembly.



4. Install Roof Headlining Pad (w/ Interior Illumination Light)

10. Install Roof Headlining Assembly (w/ Interior Illumination Light)



  1. Bring the roof headlining assembly in through the back door.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the roof headlining assembly and body interior.

  2. Install the 3 clips.

  3. Connect roof wire No.1:

    1. Remove the protective cover installed on the curtain shield airbag.

    2. Connect the connector. (w/ Telephone Microphone Assembly)

    3. Connect the connector. (Inner Mirror EC Mirror)

    4. Engage the 5 clamps and install roof wire No.1 to the front pillar LH area.

    5. Connect the connector to the instrument panel J/B area.

    6. Install the protective cover onto the curtain shield airbag.

  4. Connect antenna cord No.2:

    1. Connect each connector at the rear pillar RH area.

    2. Connect antenna cord No.2 with the bolt.

      Standard
      T = 7.0 N/m{ 71 kgf/cm }

    3. Remove the protective cover installed on the curtain shield airbag.

    4. Engage the 4 clamps and install antenna cord No.2 to the front pillar RH area.

    5. Connect the connector.



    6. Install the protective cover onto the curtain shield airbag.



      ■ NOTICE ■
      After installation, check that the back door weatherstrip is not caught in the roof headlining assembly.

53. Install Front Seat Assembly RH (Manual Seat Type)

  1. Place the front seat assembly RH in the vehicle, and align the adjuster pins with the holes on the vehicle side.

  2. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  3. Temporarily install the front side of the front seat assembly RH with the 2 bolts.

  4. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.




  5. Temporarily install the rear side of the front seat assembly RH with the 2 bolts.

  6. Tighten the 2 rear side bolts of the front seat assembly RH in the order of inner → outer.

    Standard
    T = 37 N/m{ 377 kgf/cm }

  7. Operate the seat track handle to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.

  8. Tighten the 2 front side bolts of the front seat assembly RH.

    Standard
    T = 37 N/m{ 377 kgf/cm }

  9. Connect the connectors.



54. Install Front Seat Assembly RH (w/ Power Seat)

  1. Place the front seat assembly RH in the vehicle, and align the adjuster pins with the holes on the vehicle side.

  2. Connect the connectors.

  3. Connect the battery negative terminal.

    Standard
    T = 5.4 N/m{ 55 kgf/cm }

  4. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Check that it is locked.




  5. Temporarily install the front side of the front seat assembly RH with the 2 bolts.

  6. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Check that it is locked.




  7. Temporarily install the rear side of the front seat assembly RH with the 2 bolts.

  8. Tighten the 2 rear side bolts of the front seat assembly RH in the order of inner → outer.

    Standard
    T = 37 N/m{ 377 kgf/cm }

  9. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Check that it is locked.

  10. Tighten the 2 front side bolts of the front seat assembly RH.

    Standard
    T = 37 N/m{ 377 kgf/cm }

  11. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Check that it is locked.

90. Inspect Airbag Warning Light



  1. Check that the warning light comes on when the IG is turned ON.

  2. Check that the warning light goes off approximately 6 seconds after the IG is turned ON.

    □ HINT □
    1. If the warning light remains on even after approximately 6 seconds have elapsed after the IG is turned ON, there is a malfunction in the airbag/pretensioner system.
    2. If the light comes on occasionally even after approximately 6 seconds have elapsed after the IG is turned ON, a short to +B, short to ground or open circuit in the airbag warning light circuit may be the cause.
    3. If the light comes on even with the IG OFF or ACC ON, a malfunction in the power supply system or combination meter assembly may be the cause.
