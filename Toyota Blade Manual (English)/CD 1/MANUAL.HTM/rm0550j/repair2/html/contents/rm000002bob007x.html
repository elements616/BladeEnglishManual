DTC P0741/64 SL Solenoid Circuit (Functional Malfunction)

Print

Preparation List


Function Description


The transmission control computer controls the hydraulic pressure to the lock-up relay valve by the ON or OFF signal of solenoid SL, turning lock-up ON or OFF.


DTC No.
SAE/TCCS
DTC Detection Conditions
1. Diagnosis conditions 2. Malfunction status 3. Malfunction duration 4. Other
Inspection Area
P0741/64
  1. While driving in D range with engine coolant temperature 40°C or higher and ATF temperature -10°C or higher (no malfunction of SL1, SL2, SL3, SL4, transmission revolution sensor (NT, NC) or fluid temperature sensor is detected)
  2. When (a) and (b), engine speed is higher than turbine speed by 70rpm or more
    (a). When lock-up is requested
    (b). Hydraulic pressure switch No.3 is ON
  3. 3.5 seconds or more
  4. 2 trip
  1. Automatic transmission 3 way solenoid assembly (SL)
  2. Transmission wire
  3. Transmission Control Computer Assembly
  4. Transmission Valve Body Assembly
  5. Torque Converter Assembly
  6. Transaxle Assembly
