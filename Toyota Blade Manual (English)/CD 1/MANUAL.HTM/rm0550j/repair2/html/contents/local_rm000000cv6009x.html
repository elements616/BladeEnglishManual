Transfer System (4WD) Precautions



  1. Before disassembly, clean off sand, mud, etc. adhering to the outside of the transfer assembly so that it does not enter the transfer during disassembly and installation.

  1. When separating the joints of light alloy parts such as the transfer cover, do not pry them with a screwdriver, etc.; tap them lightly with a plastic hammer to separate them.

  1. Always keep disassembled parts in order and cover them with a shop rag to protect them from dust, etc.

  1. Before installation, thoroughly clean and dry each part, then apply grease or oil to the necessary areas. Do not clean aluminum parts, rubber parts, bolts with pre-coated adhesive, etc. with alkaline chemicals. Also, do not clean rubber parts such as O-rings and oil seals with cleaning solvent (white gasoline, etc.).

  1. Apply sufficient hypoid gear oil SX to sliding and rotating surfaces before installation.

  1. When securing a part in a vise, do not secure it directly; always place aluminum plates between the part and the vise.

  1. Replace damaged or deformed snap rings with new ones.

  1. Be careful not to scratch the contact surfaces of the case, as this may cause oil leaks.

  1. Before applying seal packing, completely remove the old seal packing from the sealing surfaces and clean the sealing surfaces with white gasoline.

  1. After assembling parts to which seal packing has been applied, do not fill with oil immediately; leave them for at least 1 hours.

  1. Be careful not to scratch the surfaces that contact oil seals, O-rings and gaskets, as this may cause oil leaks.

  1. When press-fitting an oil seal, be careful that the lip and outer circumference of the oil seal are not scratched and that the oil seal is not tilted.

  1. When replacing a bearing, replace the outer race and inner race as a set.

  1. When replacing a bearing, do not apply load to the rollers, cage or rolling surfaces.

  1. When replacing the hypoid gear, replace the ring gear and drive pinion as a set.

  1. New bearings are coated with rust preventive oil; never wash it off.

  1. Replace bolts if there is significant rust, etc. on the seating surfaces or threads.
