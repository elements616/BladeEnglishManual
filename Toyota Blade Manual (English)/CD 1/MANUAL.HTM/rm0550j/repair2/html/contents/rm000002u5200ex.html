EFI System (2GR-FE) Cooling Fan Circuit

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The engine control computer calculates the fan speed required for cooling based on the engine coolant temperature, A/C switch, A/C refrigerant pressure, engine speed and vehicle speed information, and controls the cooling fan by sending a signal to the cooling fan control computer. The cooling fan control computer controls the cooling fan speed steplessly according to the duty ratio of the signal sent from the engine control computer. The engine control computer sends a signal to the cooling fan computer according to the driving conditions and controls the fan to the optimum speed, balancing cooling performance and quietness.
