Rear Combination Light Assembly LH Removal

Print


1. Remove Rear Combination Light Cover LH



  1. Disengage the 4 claws.

  2. Disengage the 2 guides and remove the rear combination light cover LH.



2. Remove Rear Combination Light Assembly LH



  1. Disconnect the connector and disengage the clamp.




  2. Remove the 2 nuts.

  3. Pull in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove the rear combination light assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    1. The tail light (rear combination light) guide remains on the vehicle.
    2. Once removed, replace the clips with new ones.
