DTC B1905 Front Pretensioner LH Squib Circuit (Short)

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. The front pretensioner LH squib circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly and front seat outer belt assembly LH (front passenger side pretensioner). When the activation conditions are met, the front pretensioner LH is activated.
  2. When a short is detected in the front pretensioner LH squib circuit (PL+, PL- lines), DTC B1905 is stored in the airbag computer assembly.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Item
Trouble Area
B1905
  1. Short between PL+ and PL- lines of the wire harness between the airbag computer assembly and front seat outer belt assembly LH (front passenger side pretensioner)
  2. Front seat outer belt assembly LH (front passenger side pretensioner) malfunction
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly Malfunction
  1. Floor wire [between airbag computer assembly and front seat outer belt assembly LH (front passenger side pretensioner)]
  2. Front Seat Outer Belt Assembly LH (Front Passenger Side Pretensioner)
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly

Inspection Procedure


□ HINT □
When performing the simulation method, use TaSCAN to select check mode first. (see)


Step 1Connector Inspection

□ HINT □
For the inspection procedure and precautions, seeReference

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative battery terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    Before disconnecting the cable from the negative battery terminal, check that the ignition switch is off.

  1. Check the connection condition of the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and front seat outer belt assembly LH (front pretensioner LH).

    Specified Condition
    The connector is connected properly

  1. Disconnect the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and front seat outer belt assembly LH (front pretensioner LH), and check the condition of the terminals of each connector.

    Specified Condition
    The terminals are not deformed or damaged

  1. Check the condition of the short pins of the connectors with a short mechanism on the floor wire.

    Specified Condition
    The short pins are not deformed or damaged

  1. Check that the front seat outer belt assembly LH (front pretensioner LH) connector of the floor wire is not damaged.

    Specified Condition
    The connector locking button is not disengaged
    The claw of the locking button is not deformed or damaged
