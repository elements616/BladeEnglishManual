Air Fuel Ratio Sensor (2AZ-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



2. Remove Exhaust Manifold Heat Insulator NO.1



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and remove the exhaust manifold heat insulator No.1.



3. Air Fuel Ratio Sensor Removal



  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.




  2. Using SST, disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor from the exhaust manifold.
