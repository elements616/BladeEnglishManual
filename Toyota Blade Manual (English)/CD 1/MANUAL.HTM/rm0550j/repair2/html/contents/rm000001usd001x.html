ETC System Precautions

Print



    1.ETC System Precautions

    1. Handling of ETC Card


      1. ETC cards are made of a heat-resistant polyester resin developed so that the card can remain in use while inserted in the ETC on-board unit. If left in a vehicle under the hot sun, the card may be damaged.

      2. The ETC card is read and written when the ETC card is inserted, when passing through an ETC gate, when communicating with antennas installed on main lanes and ramps, and when checking the usage history. If the ETC card is removed from the on-board unit at this time, the data in the ETC card may be damaged.

      3. If the ETC card is left in the vehicle when leaving the vehicle, it may be stolen.

      4. Do not insert anything other than a properly issued ETC card into the on-board unit.

      5. If a problem occurs with the ETC card, contact the ETC card issuer.

      6. If communication with the roadside unit does not end normally when passing through an ETC gate, follow the instructions of the ETC gate attendant.

      7. If an expired ETC card is inserted into the ETC on-board unit, the on-board unit displays usable (*), but the vehicle cannot pass through the ETC gate.

        □ HINT □
        *: When usable, the green indicator on the ETC on-board unit comes on, and "ETC Card Authentication Complete" is displayed on the ETC menu screen of the HDD navigation system.

    2. ETC Card Processing


      1. On toll roads that use an entrance ticket system, if writing to the ETC card fails after passing through an ETC gate, the following occurs.

        When writing to the ETC card fails at the entrance gate
        		When writing to the ETC card fails at the exit gate
        The gate does not open at the exit gate, so the ETC card must be handed to the attendant at the toll booth (entrance, exit or ticket inspection booth) for processing
        		ETC can still be used afterwards, but the usage history for which writing failed is not recorded on the ETC card

    3. Setup


      1. When using ETC for the first time after purchase, the ETC on-board unit must be registered using a setup card.

      2. During setup, the information on the vehicle inspection certificate is written to the ETC on-board unit. Therefore, when the ETC on-board unit is installed in a different vehicle or the contents of the vehicle inspection certificate change, a new application must be made to ORSE (Organization for Road System Enhancement) and the unit must be set up again.

      3. A separate setup fee is required for setup.

    4. Muting Voice Messages When Not Set Up


      1. If the ETC on-board unit has not yet been set up, it announces by voice "Beep beep, not set up" when ACC is turned ON. The voice message when not set up can be muted by pressing the "VOL DOWN" switch several times to set the volume to "zero".

    5. Toll Collection (ETC) Antenna Assembly


      1. The toll collection (ETC) antenna assembly is built into the top of the instrument panel; if there is anything covering the area above the antenna, communication with the roadside unit may not be performed.

      2. If there is dirt such as snow, mud or bird droppings on the windshield, communication with the roadside unit may not be performed normally.

      3. If stickers or film are applied to the windshield, communication with the roadside unit may not be performed normally.



      2.ETC System Inspection Precautions

      1. ETC On-board Unit Tester


        1. The ETC on-board unit communicates and exchanges information with roadside units installed at toll gates (radio devices installed at toll road entrances/exits and on main lanes) at a very high frequency of 5.8GHz. Therefore, since it is difficult to inspect the communication operating state, use an ETC on-board unit tester to inspect the communication operation of the ETC on-board unit.

          ■ NOTICE ■
          The ETC on-board unit tester does not measure the transmitting/receiving sensitivity of the ETC on-board unit or roadside unit. Therefore, it does not guarantee communication between the ETC on-board unit and roadside unit at toll gates.

          □ HINT □
          Use an ETC on-board unit tester that conforms to the ETC on-board unit tester standard specifications of the JEITA (Japan Electronics and Information Technology Industries Association) ETC promotion committee.
