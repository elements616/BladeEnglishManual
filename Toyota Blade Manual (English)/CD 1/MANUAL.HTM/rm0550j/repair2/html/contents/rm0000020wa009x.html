Radiator Assembly (2AZ-FE) Removal

Print


1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal


2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Remove Radiator Grille Protector



  1. Remove the 2 radiator grille protectors.



4. Remove Front Bumper Assembly



  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, turn the pin hold clip 90° and remove the pin hold clip.

    □ HINT □
    Perform the same procedure on the RH side.




  2. Apply protective tape around the front bumper assembly.

  3. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 upper clips.

  4. Remove the 6 screws, 2 clips and 2 bolts.




  5. Pull the front bumper assembly outward from the vehicle to disengage the 8 claws (4 each on LH/RH).

  6. Disconnect the connectors.

  7. Pull the front bumper assembly toward the front of the vehicle and remove the front bumper assembly.



5. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove the engine under cover No.1.



6. Drain Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)

■ WARNING ■
To avoid the danger of being burned, do not remove the reservoir tank cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Liquid and steam will spray out under pressure.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  2. Remove the radiator cap and drain the coolant.



  3. Loosen the cylinder block drain cock plug and drain the coolant from the engine.





7. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.

  3. Remove the insulator.

  4. Remove the battery.



8. Disconnect Water Bypass Hose



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 clamps, and disconnect the water bypass hose from the radiator support assembly.



9. Disconnect Hood Lock Assembly



  1. Detach the hood lock control assembly from the 2 clamps.




  2. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the hood lock assembly.



10. Remove Radiator Support Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 horn assembly connectors.




  2. Remove the 4 bolts and radiator support assembly.



11. Remove Fan Shroud NO.2



  1. Remove the clamp and disconnect the water bypass hose No.3 from the fan shroud No.2.

  2. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the fan shroud No.2 from the radiator assembly.

  3. Remove the 2 radiator support cushions from fan shroud No. 2.



12. Disconnect Water Bypass Hose



  1. Slide the 2 clamps and disconnect the 2 water bypass hoses.



13. Disconnect Radiator Outlet Hose



  1. Slide the clamp and disconnect the radiator outlet hose.



14. Disconnect Radiator Inlet Hose



  1. Remove the clamp and disconnect the radiator inlet hose. (*1)

    □ HINT □
    Pinch the claws at the clamp joint to remove it.

  2. Slide the clamp and disconnect the radiator inlet hose.



15. Disconnect Wire Harness



  1. Disconnect the 2 cooling fan motor connectors and the wire harness clamp.



16. Disconnect Thermistor Assembly



  1. Detach the clip and disconnect the thermistor assembly.



17. Remove Radiator Assembly

  1. Separate the cooler condenser assembly from the fan shroud.




  2. Remove the radiator assembly together with the fan shroud from the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When removing the radiator assembly, do not apply excessive force to the cooler condenser assembly and pipes.

  3. Remove the 2 radiator supports lower.



18. Fan Shroud Removal



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and remove the fan shroud from the radiator assembly.
