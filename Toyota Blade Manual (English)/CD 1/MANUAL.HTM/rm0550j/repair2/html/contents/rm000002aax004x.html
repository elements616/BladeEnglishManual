  1. Resistance Inspection




    1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), measure the resistance between the terminals.

      SST
      09082-00030  

      Resistance
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Standard
      1 - 2
      		Injector temperature 20°C
      		11.6 to 12.4Ω

  2. Injection Volume Inspection

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Perform the inspection in a well-ventilated area and keep away from fire.


    1. Remove the fuel pipe clamp.




    2. Disconnect the fuel tube.

      □ HINT □
      For the fuel tube disconnection procedure,for reference.




    3. Connect SST to the fuel pipe (vehicle side).

      ■ NOTICE ■
      SST (connector) seals the fuel tube connector and pipe with an O-ring, so be careful not to damage the connection or allow foreign matter to adhere.

    4. Install the O-ring to the fuel injector.




    5. Install SST and a vinyl tube to the fuel injector.

      SST
      09268-41140  
      09268-41400  




    6. Set the fuel injector in a graduated cylinder.

    7. Connect SST (EFI inspection wire H) to the fuel injector.

      SST
      09842-30080  

    8. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    9. Following the screen display, select "Active Test" - "Circuit Relay" - "ON" to operate the fuel pump.

    10. Connect SST (EFI inspection wire H) to the battery, inject fuel (15 seconds) 2-3 times and calculate the average value.

      Standard
      Injection volume
      		Difference between each injector
      84 to 100 ml
      		16ml or less

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Always perform the switching at the battery terminal side.

      1. If the result is not as specified, replace with a known good part.

  3. Leak Inspection




    1. In the condition above, check that no fuel leaks from the injector when SST (EFI inspection wire H) is disconnected from the battery.

      Standard
      1 drops or less / 16 minutes

    2. Connect the fuel tube.

      □ HINT □
      For the fuel tube connection procedure,for reference.

    3. Install the fuel pipe clamp.
