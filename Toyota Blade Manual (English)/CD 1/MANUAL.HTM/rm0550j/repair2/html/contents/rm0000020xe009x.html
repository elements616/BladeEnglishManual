1. ST CUT Relay Inspection

  1. Resistance Inspection




    1. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), inspect the resistance between the relay terminals.

      SST
      09082-00030  

      Resistance
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Standard
      1 - 2
      		Always
      		130.4 to 230.8Ω(20°C)
      3 - 5
      		Always
      		10k Ω or higher

    2. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), check the resistance between the terminals when battery voltage is applied between terminals 1 and 2.
