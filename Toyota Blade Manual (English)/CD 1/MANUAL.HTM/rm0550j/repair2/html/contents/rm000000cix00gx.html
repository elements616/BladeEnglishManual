Rear Left Flexible Hose Removal

Print

Preparation List




1. Precautions for Brake Line Work
(see)


2. Remove Rear Tire


3. Drain Brake Fluid


4. Remove Rear Left Flexible Hose



  1. Hold the rear brake flexible hose LH with a wrench and, using a union nut wrench (10mm), disconnect the brake tube from the rear brake flexible hose LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not bend or damage the brake tube.
    2. Do not allow foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the brake tube connections.

  2. Remove the clip and disconnect the rear brake flexible hose LH.




  3. Remove the bolt and union bolt, and remove the rear brake flexible hose LH and gasket.
