Shift Lock Control Unit Assembly (U660E) Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Position Indicator Housing UPR



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the position indicator housing UPR from the shift lock control unit assembly.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the position indicator housing UPR.



2. Remove Position Indicator Slide Cover



  1. Remove the position indicator slide cover together with position indicator slide cover No.2 from the shift lock control unit.




  2. Remove position indicator slide cover No.2 from the position indicator slide cover.



3. Remove Indicator Light Wire



  1. Remove the 5 clamps and remove the indicator light wire.



4. Remove Position Indicator Light Bulb

  1. Remove the position indicator light bulb from the indicator light wire.

  2. Remove the bulb cap from the position indicator light bulb.
