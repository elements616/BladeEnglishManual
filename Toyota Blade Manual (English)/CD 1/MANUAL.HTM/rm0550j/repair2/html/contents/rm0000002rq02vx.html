    Step 2Customer Problem Analysis and Symptom Check

    1. Customer Problem Analysis and Symptom Check (see)

      1. Check on which screen the problem symptom occurs. (Occurs on map screen only/occurs on all screens/occurs elsewhere)

      2. In particular, problems related to TV images are greatly affected by the presence or absence of external factors.

        □ HINT □
        1. Location-related factors may include power transmission lines, overhead wires, neon signs, trucks and motorcycles, vehicle radios, building conditions, etc.
        2. Channel (broadcast station) related factors may include radio waves that are too strong, too weak, of poor quality, unstable, etc.

      2. Inspect areas that can be checked visually, such as blown fuses, open or short circuits in the wire harness and poor connector connections.
