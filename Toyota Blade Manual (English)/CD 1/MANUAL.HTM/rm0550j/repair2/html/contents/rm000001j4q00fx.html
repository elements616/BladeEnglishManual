Indoor Electrical Key Oscillator (Installation Position Rear Floor) Removal

Print


1. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly


2. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly CTR


3. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



4. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



5. Remove Rear Seat Assembly RH



  1. Remove the 2 front side bolts of the rear seat assembly RH.




  2. Fold the rear seat assembly RH forward.

  3. Remove the 2 rear side bolts and remove the rear seat assembly RH.



6. Remove Rear Door Scuff Plate RH



  1. Disengage the 8 claws and remove the rear door scuff plate RH.



7. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly


8. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



9. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



10. Remove Rear Seat Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 front side bolts.




  2. Fold the rear seat assembly LH forward.

  3. Remove the 2 rear side bolts and remove the rear seat assembly LH.



11. Remove Rear Door Scuff Plate LH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the RH side.



12. Remove Indoor Electrical Key Oscillator



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the bolt and remove the indoor electrical key oscillator.
