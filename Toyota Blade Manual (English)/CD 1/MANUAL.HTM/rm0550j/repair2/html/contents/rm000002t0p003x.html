Circuit Description


The front airbag sensor RH circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly and front airbag sensor RH.

The front airbag sensor RH detects impacts and sends signals to the airbag computer assembly to determine whether to deploy the airbags.

When a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor RH circuit, DTCs B1602, B1603, B1612 and B1613 are stored in the airbag computer assembly.

Inspection Procedure

Step 1Connector Inspection

□ HINT □
For the inspection procedure and precautions, seeReference

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    Make sure the ignition switch is OFF before disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.

  1. Check the connection condition of the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and front airbag sensor RH, and of the junction connectors (engine room main wire and instrument panel wire).

    Specified Condition
    The connector is connected properly

  1. Disconnect the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and front airbag sensor RH, and the junction connectors (engine room main wire and instrument panel wire), and check the condition of the terminals of each connector.

    Specified Condition
    The terminals are not deformed or damaged
