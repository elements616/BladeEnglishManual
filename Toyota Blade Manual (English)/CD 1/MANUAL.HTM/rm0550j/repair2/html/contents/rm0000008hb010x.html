Back Guide Monitor System Precaution

Print





    1. Precautions for Vehicle Servicing

    1. The maximum steering angle setting must be performed with the engine ON (engine running), so apply the parking brake and foot brake, move the shift lever to P, and take sufficient care that the vehicle does not move off.

    2. Pay attention to the following points when servicing the vehicle.


      1. When the battery terminal is disconnected, the back guide monitor screen displays "System Initializing" and steering angle neutral point correction is required. (see)

      2. When the navigation receiver assembly is replaced, "Initial Setting Not Performed" is shown on the displayed image (back guide monitor screen), and setting of the maximum steering angle and steering angle neutral point and camera optical axis adjustment are required. Camera optical axis adjustment is also required when the television camera is replaced and when the camera is misaligned. (see)

      3. If the lens of the television camera assembly is dirty with water droplets, snow, mud, etc., a clear image cannot be obtained, so rinse it with water and wipe it with a soft cloth. If it is very dirty, wash it off with a neutral detergent.

    3. The back guide monitor system is controlled by the navigation receiver assembly (built-in BGM). On vehicles with an intelligent parking assist system, the television camera controller performs control, so refer to the intelligent parking assist system ().
