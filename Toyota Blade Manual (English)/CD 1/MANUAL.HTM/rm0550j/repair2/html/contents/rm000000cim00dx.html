Rear Brake Removal

Print

Preparation List




1. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 clips.




  2. Place your hand at the position shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 clips.

  3. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console box rear cover.



2. Remove Console Box Carpet



  1. Remove the console box carpet.



3. Remove Console Box Assembly RR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the console box assembly RR.



4. Loosen Parking Brake Cable Assembly

  1. Fully release the parking brake lever.




  2. Loosen the lock nut and adjusting nut at the parking brake lever, and fully loosen the parking brake cable.



5. Remove Rear Tire


6. Drain Brake Fluid


7. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector



  1. Remove the parking brake lever protector from the parking brake cable assembly No.3.



8. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3



  1. Disconnect the cable end from the operating lever of the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.




  2. Using an offset wrench (14mm), disconnect parking brake cable assembly No.3 from the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    Insert the offset wrench all the way into the clip as shown in the illustration, flex the clip, and pull parking brake cable assembly No.3 out of the guide of the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.



9. Disconnect Rear Left Flexible Hose



  1. Remove the union bolt and gasket, and disconnect the rear brake flexible hose LH from the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.



10. Remove Rear Disc Brake Cylinder Assembly LH



  1. Hold the slide pin with a wrench, remove the 2 bolts and remove the rear disc brake cylinder assembly LH.



11. Remove Disc Brake Pad Kit Rear



  1. Remove the rear disc brake pads from the rear disc brake cylinder mounting LH.



12. Remove Rear Disc Brake Anti-squeal Shim Kit

  1. Remove the rear disc brake anti-squeal shims from the brake pads.



13. Remove Rear Disc Brake Pad Support Plate



  1. Remove the brake pad support plates from the rear disc brake cylinder mounting LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    The brake pad support plates have different shapes, so put identification marks on them to identify their positions when installing.



14. Remove Rear Disc Brake Cylinder Slide Pin



  1. Remove the rear disc brake cylinder slide pins from the rear disc brake cylinder mounting LH.



15. Remove Rear Disc Brake Bushing Dust Boot



  1. Remove the rear disc brake bushing dust boots from the rear disc brake cylinder mounting LH.



16. Remove Rear Disc Brake Cylinder Mounting LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and rear disc brake cylinder mounting LH.



17. Remove Rear Disc



  1. Put matchmarks on the disc and axle hub, and remove the disc.
