Instrument Panel Airbag Assembly Lower NO.1 Disposal

Print

Preparation List



1. Dispose of Instrument Panel Airbag Assembly LWR NO.1 (When Disposing of Vehicle)

■ WARNING ■
  1. Perform deployment outdoors on a flat surface where safety can be ensured. Also, avoid deployment in residential areas as much as possible.
  2. The deployment noise is loud, so thoroughly notify people in advance that deployment will be performed.

  1. Disconnect connector of instrument panel lower airbag No.1 (see)




  2. Deploy instrument panel lower airbag No.1

    1. Connect the following 2 SSTs.

      SST
      09082-00711  
      09082-00770  

    2. Short the alligator clip and terminal of the SST.

    3. Connect the SST to the connector (yellow 2P) of instrument panel lower airbag No.1 and lock it securely.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Check that instrument panel lower airbag No.1 has no looseness.
      2. Check that nothing is placed on top of the door of instrument panel lower airbag No.1.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Lock the SST connector securely.




    4. Extend the SST fully from the vehicle and place the battery at least 5m away from the vehicle.

      ■ WARNING ■
      Close all doors and door glass.

    5. After confirming safety inside the cabin and in the surrounding area, connect the alligator clip of the SST to the negative terminal of the battery.

    6. Connect the SST terminal to the positive terminal of the battery to deploy instrument panel lower airbag No.1.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Before deployment, always make sure that there is no one inside the cabin or in the surrounding area.
      2. Before deployment, always warn people around the vehicle being disposed of in a loud voice.




  3. Disposal Procedure for Instrument Panel Lower Airbag No.1

    1. Place the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1 in a strong, transparent plastic bag, seal it and dispose of it.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Parts of the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1 may be at a high temperature of several hundred °C or more, so leave it for 30 minutes or more after deployment.
      2. Do not apply water, etc. to the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1.
      3. When handling the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1, wear dust-proof protective glasses and gloves.
      4. Never dispose of instrument panel lower airbag No.1 without deploying it.
      5. Always wash your hands with water after completing work.



2. Dispose of Instrument Panel Airbag Assembly LWR NO.1 (When Disposing of Component Alone)

■ WARNING ■
  1. Perform deployment outdoors on a flat surface where safety can be ensured. Also, avoid deployment in residential areas as much as possible.
  2. The deployment noise is loud, so thoroughly notify people in advance that deployment will be performed.

  1. Secure instrument panel lower airbag No.1



    1. Using a wire harness (conductor cross-sectional area 1.25mm2or more), secure instrument panel lower airbag No.1 to a tire.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Strictly observe the following 2 instructions. If the instructions are not followed, the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1 or tires may be thrown about, which may result in serious damage.
      2. A force of approximately 1 tons is applied to the wire harness when instrument panel lower airbag No.1 deploys, so wrap the wire harness 3 times and secure it firmly with no slack.
      3. As shown in the illustration, always install instrument panel lower airbag No.1 with its deployment surface facing the center of the tire.







  2. Deploy instrument panel lower airbag No.1

    1. Connect the following 3 SSTs.

      SST
      09082-00711  
      09082-00770  

    2. Short the alligator clip and terminal of the SST.

    3. Connect the SST to instrument panel lower airbag No.1 and lock it securely.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Lock the SST connector securely.

    4. As shown in the illustration, stack 2 tires, place the tire with instrument panel lower airbag No.1 secured on top of them, then place 1 more tires on top and a tire with disc wheel at the very top.

    5. Using wire with a diameter of 1.8mm or more (general-purpose ordinary iron wire: equivalent to JIS SWM-B), wrap the tires together 2 times or more and tie them securely with no slack.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Secure them so that the total load capacity is 1 tons or more.



    6. Extend the SST fully from instrument panel lower airbag No.1 and place the battery at least 5m away from the tires.






    7. Surround the area within a radius of 5m from the outside of the tires with pylons and rope, etc. so that no one can enter.

    8. After confirming safety in the surrounding area, connect the alligator clip of the SST to the negative terminal of the battery.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Perform deployment outdoors on a flat surface where safety can be ensured. Also, avoid deployment in residential areas as much as possible.
      2. The deployment noise is loud, so thoroughly notify people in advance that deployment will be performed.

    9. Connect the SST terminal to the positive terminal of the battery to deploy instrument panel lower airbag No.1.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Before deployment, always make sure that there is no one in the surrounding area.
      2. Before deployment, always warn people around the work area in a loud voice.

      □ HINT □
      The connector of the SST (wire D) that connects directly to the inflator will melt after 1 deployment due to the heat of the inflator.




  3. Disposal Procedure for Instrument Panel Lower Airbag No.1

    1. Place the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1 in a strong, transparent plastic bag, seal it and dispose of it.

      ■ WARNING ■
      1. Parts of the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1 may be at a high temperature of several hundred °C or more, so leave it for 30 minutes or more after deployment.
      2. Do not apply water, etc. to the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1.
      3. When handling the deployed instrument panel lower airbag No.1, wear dust-proof protective glasses and gloves.
      4. Never dispose of instrument panel lower airbag No.1 without deploying it.
      5. Always wash your hands with water after completing work.
