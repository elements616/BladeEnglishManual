U1101
With the IG ON, the CAN gateway ECU cannot receive data from the ACC ECU
  1. Distance control computer (ACC ECU) branch line open or connector
  2. Distance control computer (ACC ECU) power source system malfunction
  3. Distance control computer (ACC ECU) internal malfunction

Inspection Procedure


■ NOTICE ■
  1. The following flowchart applies only when output by the CAN gateway ECU (V bus No.2 monitoring ECU). If an ECU other than the CAN gateway ECU outputs this code, it is necessary to identify the malfunctioning area using 'How to Proceed with Troubleshooting'.
  2. When measuring the resistance of the CAN bus, turn the IG OFF, confirm that warning functions such as the key reminder warning system are not operating, and leave the vehicle for 1 minutes or more without operating the key or switches or opening/closing the doors before starting measurement. If it is necessary to open a door during inspection, open it beforehand.


□ HINT □
  1. When inspecting each connector, before disconnecting it, push the connector case in the connecting direction and check that the connector is not loose or disconnected.
  2. When disconnecting a connector, check that the connector terminals and connector case are free of damage, deformation, corrosion, etc.
