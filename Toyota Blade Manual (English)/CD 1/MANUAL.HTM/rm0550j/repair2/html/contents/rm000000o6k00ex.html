  3. Depending on the parts replaced or the work performed during inspection, registration settings for other systems may be necessary in addition to the intelligent parking assist system. (see)

Step 2Inspect Tire Dynamic Loaded Radius


  1. Inspect Tire Dynamic Loaded Radius



    1. On a level surface, stop the vehicle with the steering wheel in the straight-ahead position.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      As this inspection involves moving the vehicle, secure a wide area where the surroundings can be safely monitored.

    2. Make marks on the tire circumference and the road surface with chalk, etc.

    3. Drive the vehicle straight forward and stop when the tire has made 5 revolutions.

    4. Mark the vehicle stop position with chalk, etc., measure the travel distance A mm and inspect that it is within the standard.

      Standard
      9466 to 9871mm

Step 4Symptom Check

  1. Garage Parking Operation

    1. Move the vehicle to a level place with a parking space.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      As this inspection involves moving the vehicle, secure a wide area where the surroundings can be safely monitored.

    2. Operate the intelligent parking assist system to perform a garage parking operation and check how the vehicle deviates from the set target parking position.

      □ HINT □
      Perform garage parking on both the left and right from a position angled approximately 45° to the target parking space.
