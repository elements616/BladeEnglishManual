Front Seat Assembly RH (Power Seat Type) Removal

Print


1. Precautions for Overhaul of Seats with SRS Side Airbag

  1. For the front seat assembly RH with SRS side airbag, read the SRS airbag system precautions before starting work, and perform the work correctly.
    (see)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If the side airbag of a vehicle with side airbags has deployed, replace it with a front seat assembly RH.



2. Remove Front Seat Headrest Assembly


3. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Outer Front RH

  1. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover outer front RH.



4. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner Front RH



  1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover inner front RH.



5. Remove Seat Track Cover Outer RH



  1. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the frontmost position.

  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the seat track cover outer RH.



6. Remove Seat Track Bracket Cover Inner RH



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the seat track cover inner RH.



7. Remove Front Seat Assembly RH



  1. Remove the 2 rear side bolts of the front seat assembly RH.

  2. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) to move the front seat assembly RH to the rearmost position.




  3. Remove the 2 front side bolts.

  4. Operate the power seat switch knob (for slide & vertical) and the power seat switch knob (for reclining) to set the seat slide position of the front seat assembly RH to the center and the seatback to vertical.

  5. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
    (see)

  6. Disconnect each connector and remove the front seat assembly RH.
