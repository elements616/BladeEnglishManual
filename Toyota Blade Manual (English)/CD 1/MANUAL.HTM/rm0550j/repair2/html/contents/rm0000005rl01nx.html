MS Bus DTC (Main Body ECU Output Code) List 
Symptom
Inspection Point
Reference Page
U0327
Smart ECU Not Connected
U1002
Sub-network Communication Malfunction

■ NOTICE ■
The reference pages for when the above codes are output apply only when they are output by the main body ECU (MS bus monitoring ECU). If they are output by an ECU other than the main body ECU, it is necessary to identify the malfunctioning area from "How to Proceed with Troubleshooting".


□ HINT □
If a communication stop or bus malfunction occurs in an ECU connected to the MS bus, the main body ECU detects the malfunction and outputs a DTC.

■ NOTICE ■
The reference pages for when the above codes are output apply only when they are output by the main body ECU (V bus No.2 monitoring ECU). If they are output by an ECU other than the CAN gateway ECU, it is necessary to identify the malfunctioning area from "How to Proceed with Troubleshooting".


□ HINT □
  1. If a communication stop or bus malfunction occurs in an ECU connected to V bus No.2, the CAN gateway ECU detects the malfunction and outputs a DTC.
  2. E grade 2WD models not equipped with Intelligent Parking Assist (combined with HDD navigation) do not have V bus No.2.
