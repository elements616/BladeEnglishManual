Step 2Inspect Aftermarket Parts (Sunshade Film, Telephone Antenna, etc.)

  1. Inspect aftermarket parts

    1. Check whether aftermarket parts such as sunshade film or a telephone antenna are installed.

      Specified Condition
      No aftermarket parts (including non-genuine wiring)

      □ HINT □
      Noise entering the radio is suppressed to a level that causes no practical problems, so extremely loud noise is not expected. If loud noise occurs, check that the ground at the antenna installation area is normal, that all genuine noise suppression parts are installed, and that there is no non-genuine wiring.
