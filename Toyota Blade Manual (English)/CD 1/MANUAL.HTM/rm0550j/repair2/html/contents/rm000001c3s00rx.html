  1. G-BOOK System

    1. G-BOOK Online Service


      1. The G-BOOK online service uses communication when acquiring data.

        □ HINT □
        The user's own Bluetooth cellular phone is used for data communication.



    1. One-touch Type HELPNET Service

      1. HELPNET Service


        1. Vehicles with navigation can use the one-touch type HELPNET service by subscribing to the G-BOOK service.

        2. The HELPNET service uses communication when making emergency calls.

          □ HINT □
          During an emergency call, calls and communication via hands-free, G-BOOK, etc. cannot be made.




      1. Communication Device

        1. w/ Navigation

          □ HINT □
          1. When replacing the navigation receiver assembly on a vehicle not subscribed to the G-BOOK service, perform the vehicle linking setting.
          2. To use the G-BOOK online service, a Bluetooth compatible cellular phone must be used.




        2. With Bluetooth Telephone Connected


          1. A Bluetooth cellular phone is used for data communication and HELPNET voice calls.

            □ HINT □
            1. When replacing the navigation receiver assembly, perform the vehicle association setting, and then perform the G-BOOK use resumption operation and HELPNET service start operation.
            2. If the navigation receiver assembly is replaced while a purchased G-SOUND unlimited license exists, perform G-SOUND license recovery.



        1. About G-BOOK Terminal Serial ID

          1. On this model, the navigation receiver assembly functions as the G-BOOK terminal.

          2. The navigation receiver assembly holds a serial No. (G-BOOK terminal ID) that identifies the terminal, which is used to identify the individual unit for the service.



          1. About HELPNET On-board Unit ID

            1. On this model (one-touch type HELPNET system), the navigation receiver assembly functions as the HELPNET in-vehicle unit.

            2. The HELPNET in-vehicle unit holds a serial No. (HELPNET in-vehicle unit ID) that identifies the terminal, which is used to identify the individual unit for the service.



            1. About Cellular Phone for Data Communication

              1. The user's own cellular phone is used for data communication.

              2. Use a model whose operation has been verified for data communication.

                  □ HINT □
                  1. When replacing the navigation receiver assembly on a vehicle not subscribed to the G-BOOK service, perform the vehicle linking setting.

                  6. G-BOOK Service Resumption Operation


                    1. Press the "Info G" switch to display the information screen.




                    2. Press the "Main Menu" switch on the information screen to start G-BOOK.

                      □ HINT □
                      The screen is an example display and may differ from the actual screen.




                    3. Press "G-BOOK.com" on the main menu screen.

                    4. Check that G-BOOK.com is displayed.

                    5. A registration procedure guide is displayed; follow the instructions on the screen to perform the G-BOOK use resumption procedure.

                  7. HELPNET Service Start Operation (Manual Maintenance Check)


                    1. After the G-BOOK use resumption operation is finished, perform a manual maintenance check as the service start operation. (see)

                      □ HINT □
                      The HELPNET service is provided from the time the service start operation (manual maintenance check) is completed normally.



                  1. G-SOUND License Recovery

                    □ HINT □
                    If the navigation receiver assembly has been replaced while there are purchased G-SOUND unlimited licenses, perform G-SOUND license recovery.

                    1. Contact G-BOOK Support Center


                      1. Notify the G-BOOK Support Center of the G-BOOK terminal IDs before and after replacement and the GAZOO ID, and request recovery processing for the applicable licenses.

                      2. After receiving contact from the G-BOOK Support Center, perform the G-SOUND license recovery procedure.



                    2. G-SOUND License Recovery

                      □ HINT □
                      The screen is an example display and may differ from the actual screen.


                      1. Press the "Info G" switch to display the information screen.




                      2. Press the "Amusement" switch on the information screen to display the amusement menu screen.




                      3. Press the "G-SOUND Catalog" switch on the amusement menu screen to display the G-SOUND catalog screen.




                      4. Press the "G Recommended" switch on the G-SOUND catalog screen. [*1]




                      5. Press the "Proceed" switch on the license recovery screen.




                      6. The license is downloaded.




                      7. After the license is acquired, "Play now?" is displayed; select "Yes" and check that the song can be played.

                      8. If there are multiple songs that require license recovery, repeat the same procedure from [*1].
