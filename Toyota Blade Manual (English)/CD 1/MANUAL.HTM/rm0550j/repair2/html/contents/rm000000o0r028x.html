EFI System (2GR-FE) Basic Inspection

Print

■ NOTICE ■
If the malfunctioning area cannot be identified by troubleshooting, it can be narrowed down by performing the basic inspection shown below.



1. Battery Inspection

  1. Inspect the condition of the battery. (see)



2. Cranking Operation Inspection

  1. Check that the engine cranks.

    □ HINT □
    If the engine does not crank, inspect the starter system. (see)



3. Engine Start Inspection

  1. Check that the engine starts.

    □ HINT □
    If the engine does not start, perform the fuel pressure inspection and spark inspection.



4. Air Filter Inspection

  1. Inspect the air filter.

    □ HINT □
    If the air filter is dirty, clean or replace it.



5. Inspect Idle Speed

  1. Inspect the idle speed. (see)



6. Ignition Timing Inspection

  1. Inspect the ignition timing. (see)



7. Fuel Pressure Inspection

  1. Inspect the fuel pressure. (see)



8. Spark Inspection

  1. Inspect the spark. (see)



9. Compression Inspection

  1. Inspect the compression. (see)
