3. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal

4. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



5. Remove Radiator Grille Protector



  1. Remove the 2 radiator grille protectors.



6. Remove Front Bumper Assembly



  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, turn the pin hold clip 90° and remove the pin hold clip.

    □ HINT □
    Perform the same procedure on the RH side.




  2. Apply protective tape around the front bumper assembly.

  3. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 upper clips.

  4. Remove the 6 screws, 2 clips and 2 bolts.




  5. Pull the front bumper assembly outward from the vehicle to disengage the 8 claws (4 each on LH/RH).

  6. Disconnect the connectors.

  7. Pull the front bumper assembly toward the front of the vehicle and remove the front bumper assembly.



7. Remove Headlight Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and screw.

  2. Disconnect each connector and remove the headlight assembly LH.



8. Remove Front Airbag Sensor LH

  1. Make sure that the ignition switch is OFF.

  2. Make sure that the negative battery terminal is disconnected.

    ■ WARNING ■
    1. If work is performed without disconnecting the negative battery terminal, the airbag may deploy even if an impact is applied only to the front airbag sensor LH, so be sure to confirm that the negative battery terminal is disconnected before starting work.
    2. After disconnecting the negative battery terminal, do not start work for 90 seconds.



  3. Remove the bolt and front airbag sensor LH from the radiator support.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Be careful not to damage the pin on the back of the front airbag sensor LH.
    2. Be careful not to drop it or subject it to strong impacts.

  4. Disconnect the connector from the front airbag sensor LH.

    1. While pushing the housing lock in the direction of arrow <A>, slide only the CPA rearward in the direction of arrow <B>.



    2. Move the CPA from the connected position to the 1 locked position.





    3. While pushing the housing lock in the direction of arrow <A> to release it, pull the entire connector in the direction of arrow <B> to disconnect the front airbag sensor LH connector.



    4. After disconnecting the connector, check that the CPA protrudes from the rear of the housing.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. Do not pull on the wire harness.
      2. Do not press on the top of the CPA when releasing the lock, as the lock cannot be released.



9. Inspect Front Airbag Sensor LH

  1. Vehicles not involved in a collision, or vehicles involved in a collision in which the airbag did not deploy

    1. Check the diagnostic trouble codes.

  2. Vehicles involved in a collision

    1. Check the diagnostic trouble codes.

    2. If the front fender or surrounding area is damaged, the airbag has not deployed and any of the following apply, replace the front airbag sensor with a new one.

      Specified Condition
      Deformation or peeling paint on the bracket or sensor body
      Scratches, cracks or damage on the front airbag sensor or connector
      Damaged serial number or peeling label
