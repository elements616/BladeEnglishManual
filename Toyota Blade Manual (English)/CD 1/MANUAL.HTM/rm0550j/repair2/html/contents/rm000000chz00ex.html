Brake Pedal Support Assembly Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Stop Light Switch Assembly



  1. Turn the stop light switch assembly 1/4 turn and remove it.



2. Remove Stop Light Switch Mounting Adjuster

  1. Remove the adjuster from the brake pedal support.



3. Remove Brake Pedal Sub-assembly



  1. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the brake pedal from the brake pedal support.



4. Remove Brake Pedal Bush

  1. Remove the 2 brake pedal bushes from the brake pedal.



5. Remove Brake Pedal Pad

  1. Remove the brake pedal pad.
