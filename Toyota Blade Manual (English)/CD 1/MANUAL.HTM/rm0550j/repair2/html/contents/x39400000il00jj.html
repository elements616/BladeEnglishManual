Smart Entry & Start System (Start System) Engine does not Start

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. When the electrical key transmitter (electronic key) is in the cabin, pressing the engine switch causes the instrument panel J/B assembly (main body ECU) to receive a signal and switch the power source mode. In addition, pushing the engine switch while depressing the brake pedal with the shift position in P or N starts the engine.

■ NOTICE ■
  1. When using the TaSCAN with the IG OFF, repeatedly turn any door courtesy light switch ON and OFF (at intervals of 1.5 seconds or less) until communication between the TaSCAN and the vehicle starts.
  2. The Smart Entry & Start System (start system) has functions that use multiplex communication. First, follow "How to Proceed with Troubleshooting" and confirm that there is no malfunction in the communication system before proceeding with troubleshooting.


□ HINT □
When the instrument panel J/B assembly (main body ECU) is replaced with a new ECU and the battery negative terminal is connected, it enters the IG ON state. Also, when the battery is removed and reinstalled, it returns to the state it was in when the battery was removed.
