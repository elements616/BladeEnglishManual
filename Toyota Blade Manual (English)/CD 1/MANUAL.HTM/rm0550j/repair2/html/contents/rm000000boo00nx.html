Seat Belt Warning System Function Inspection

Print



  1. Inspect Driver Side Seat Belt Warning Light

    1. Turn IG ON.

    2. Sit in the driver seat and check that the driver seat belt warning light in the combination meter assembly blinks when the seat belt tongue plate is not inserted into the buckle.

    3. While seated in the driver seat, check that the driver seat belt warning light in the combination meter assembly goes off when the seat belt tongue plate is inserted into the buckle.



    1. Inspect Front Passenger Side Seat Belt Warning Light

      1. Turn IG ON.

      2. Sit in the front passenger seat and check that the front passenger seat belt warning light blinks when the seat belt tongue plate is not inserted into the buckle.

      3. While seated in the front passenger seat, check that the front passenger seat belt warning light goes off when the seat belt tongue plate is inserted into the buckle.



      1. Inspect Seat Belt Warning Buzzer Sounding

        1. Start the engine.

        2. With the seat belt tongue plate not inserted into the buckle, check that the buzzer in the combination meter assembly sounds intermittently at 1.2-second intervals for 30 seconds when the vehicle speed reaches approximately 20 km/h or more.

        3. Check that approximately 30 seconds after the buzzer starts sounding, the interval changes and the buzzer sounds intermittently at 0.4-second intervals for approximately 90 seconds.

        4. Check that the buzzer stops approximately 120 seconds after it starts sounding.

        5. After the buzzer stops, turn the IG OFF and start the engine again, and check that the buzzer sounds again when the vehicle speed reaches approximately 20 km/h or more with the seat belt tongue plate not inserted into the buckle.

        6. After the buzzer stops, insert the seat belt tongue plate into the buckle, drive at approximately 20 km/h or more, and check that the buzzer sounds again when the seat belt is unfastened.

        7. After the buzzer stops, move the shift lever to the "R" position and then to the "D" or "M" position, and check that the buzzer sounds again when the vehicle speed reaches approximately 20 km/h or more with the seat belt tongue plate not inserted into the buckle.
