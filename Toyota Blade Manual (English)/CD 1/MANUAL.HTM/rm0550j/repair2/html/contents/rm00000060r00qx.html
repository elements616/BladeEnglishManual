Function Description



DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
DTC Output Related Items
C1246/46
  1. At a vehicle speed of 7km/h or more with PMC terminal voltage exceeding 0.86V, a condition with no change of 0.005V or more continues for 30 seconds
  2. At a vehicle speed of 10Km/h or more, noise to the PMC terminal occurs 7 times or more in 5 seconds
  3. When the stop light switch is OFF, PMC terminal voltage remains above 0.86V or below 0.27V for 5 seconds or more
  4. With IG1 terminal voltage at 9.5to17.2V, VCM terminal voltage remains outside 4.4to5.6V for 1.2 seconds or more
  5. With VCM terminal voltage at 4.8to5.2V, PMC terminal voltage remains outside 0.19to4.57V for 1.2 seconds or more
  1. Master pressure sensor malfunction in actuator
  2. Stop light circuit
  3. Stop Light Switch
  4. Open or short in wire harness
  5. Poor connector contact pressure, terminal deformation or corrosion
  6. Actuator assembly
