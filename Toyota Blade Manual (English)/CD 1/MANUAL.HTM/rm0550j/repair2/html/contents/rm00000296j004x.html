Ignition System (2GR-FE) Function Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Ignition Coil Assembly



  1. Input Voltage Inspection

    1. Disconnect the ignition coil assembly connector.

    2. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), check the voltage between the terminals of the vehicle side connector of the ignition coil assembly.

      SST
      09082-00030  

      Voltage
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Standard
      1(+B) - 4(GND)
      		IG ON
      		11 to 14V

    3. Turn IG OFF.



2. Inspect Ignition Coil and Spark

  1. DTC check (see)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    If a DTC is output, perform troubleshooting for the corresponding DTC.

  2. Inspect Spark

    1. Remove the V-bank cover. (see)

    2. Remove the windshield wiper motor & link assembly. (see)

    3. Remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose. (see)

    4. Remove the intake air surge tank. (see)




    5. Disconnect the 6 ignition coil assembly connectors.




    6. Remove the 6 bolts and the 6 ignition coil assemblies.




    7. Remove the 6 spark plugs.

    8. Install the spark plug to the ignition coil assembly and connect the connector.




    9. Ground the spark plug.

    10. Check that a spark occurs when the engine is cranked.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. When inspecting, be sure to ground the hexagon or threaded portion of the spark plug.
      2. If the ignition coil has been subjected to an impact such as being dropped, replace it with a new one.
      3. Do not crank the engine for 2 seconds or more.




    11. Install the 6 spark plugs.

      Standard
      T = 18 N/m{ 184 kgf/cm }




    12. Install the 6 ignition coil assemblies with the 6 bolts.

      Standard
      T = 10 N/m{ 102 kgf/cm }




    13. Connect the 6 ignition coil assembly connectors.

    14. Install the intake air surge tank. (see)

    15. Install the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose. (see)

    16. Remove the windshield wiper motor & link assembly. (see)

    17. Remove the V-bank cover. (see)

      ■ NOTICE ■
      After completing the work, be sure to clear the stored DTCs once and then check that the normal code is output.



3. Inspect Spark Plug



Spark Plug Type 
Manufacturer
Type
DENSO
SK20HR11

  1. Visual Inspection

    1. Inspect the spark plug for damaged threads and damaged insulator.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. Do not damage the iridium.
      2. If it is significantly dirty due to carbon fouling or similar, replace it with a new spark plug.
      3. When replacing with a new spark plug, do not remove the cap covering the tip for protection until it is installed to the engine.
      4. If a spark plug is dropped, replace it with a new one.

  2. Spark Plug Gap

    Standard
    1.1mm

    Limit
    1.3mm

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Because iridium is used, do not adjust the gap.
    2. Do not damage the iridium.
    3. If it is more than the limit, the iridium is worn, so replace the spark plug.
