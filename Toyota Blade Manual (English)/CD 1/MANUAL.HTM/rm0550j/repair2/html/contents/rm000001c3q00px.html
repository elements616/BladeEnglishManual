G-BOOK System Precautions

Print



    1. Manual Emergency Calls via HELPNET Strictly Prohibited

    1. Do not press the emergency call button unless there is an emergency. When inspecting, perform a "Manual Maintenance Check".

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. Connecting to the center with an emergency call when there is no emergency such as an accident places an unnecessary burden on the center and may delay responses to other emergency calls.
      2. If an emergency vehicle such as an ambulance is dispatched due to a false call, the related costs may be charged or the caller may be penalized under relevant laws.
      3. If a false call is made, the customer will incur unnecessary telephone charges.

    2. If a False Call Is Made


      1. If a call is made by mistake, inform the center during the call that it is a "false call".

      2. The call can be ended only by instruction from the center; it cannot be interrupted or ended from the vehicle.

      3. If it is not communicated that it is a "false call", the fire department and police may be notified and emergency vehicles may be dispatched according to arrangements with the center.



      2.G-BOOK Precautions

      1. Depending on the troubleshooting content, G-BOOK.com may be accessed. Therefore, explain to the customer that charges may be incurred when checking the online service during repair work, and be sure to obtain their consent before starting work.

      2. When replacing the navigation receiver assembly, perform the vehicle association setting, and then perform the G-BOOK use resumption operation and HELPNET service start operation. (see)

      3. On this model, the navigation receiver assembly functions as the G-BOOK terminal and holds the G-BOOK terminal ID.



        3. Precautions for One-touch Type HELPNET

        1. Depending on the troubleshooting content, a manual maintenance check may be performed. Since a Bluetooth cellular phone is used for communication, explain to the customer that call charges may be incurred during repair work, and be sure to obtain their consent before starting work.

        2. On this model (one-touch type HELPNET system), the navigation receiver assembly functions as the HELPNET in-vehicle unit and holds the HELPNET in-vehicle unit ID.



          4. HELPNET Service Automatic Maintenance Check

          1. The emergency call system performs communication tests with the center and automatic system checks so that it can always function correctly. No particular operation is necessary for this automatic maintenance check, but it is performed as follows.

            □ HINT □
            1. The automatic maintenance check is performed at intervals specified by Japan Mayday Service Co., Ltd.
            2. The automatic maintenance check finishes 1-2 minutes after it starts.
            3. During the automatic maintenance check, calls and communication via hands-free, G-BOOK, etc. cannot be made. In this case, wait a few minutes and then operate again.
            4. If communication is interrupted for some reason after the automatic maintenance check starts, the automatic maintenance check starts again the next time ACC is turned ON.
            5. Since a Bluetooth cellular phone is used for communication, the telephone usage charges for all data communication performed during the device check by the automatic maintenance check are borne by the customer.



            5. When Emergency Calls Cannot Be Made

            1. In cases such as the following, the HELPNET service may not be provided, or the quality of the HELPNET service may deteriorate. (For details, refer to the terms of use and the owner's manual for the in-vehicle unit)

              Cause of Unavailability
              		Details
              Communication not possible due to a malfunction of the in-vehicle unit or vehicle-related equipment
              		When the in-vehicle unit or telephone does not operate normally, such as when it is damaged or malfunctions due to an impact from a vehicle collision (*2)
              When function settings or telephone services that interfere with use of HELPNET are being used on the cellular phone
              		All services that restrict making and receiving calls, such as drive mode, dial lock, outgoing call restriction, answering machine and select call
              Communication not possible due to a center system malfunction
              1. When the center system requires periodic or emergency maintenance
              2. When emergency calls are temporarily concentrated at the center due to a large-scale accident or fire
              3. Center system failure, malfunction, etc.
              4. Damage to the center system due to a fire at the center
              5. Interruption or suspension of service provision for reasons of the emergency call service provider
              Communication not possible due to the communication network used by the emergency call system (*1)
              1. When the "registered vehicle" is located outside the service area of the cellular phone network used for this service
              2. When overseas
              3. When communication is not possible as a result, even within the service area of the cellular phone network, such as indoors, in underground parking lots, in mountainous areas, in tunnels or behind buildings
              4. When call use or call time is restricted, such as when the cellular phone network or general public fixed network (including relay networks) is extremely congested, and as a result communication by the emergency call system is not possible
              5. When calls are restricted by the carrier due to a large-scale disaster, etc., and as a result calls by the emergency call system are not possible
              6. When the service of the cellular phone network or general public fixed network (including relay networks) used for this service is interrupted or suspended due to an accident, maintenance, construction, etc.
              Center system positioning error
              1. When in a location where GPS (Global Positioning System) radio waves cannot be received, or where radio waves have difficulty reaching, such as indoors, in underground parking lots, in mountainous areas, in tunnels or behind buildings
              2. When positioning is not performed normally or has a large error, such as in tunnels or densely built-up areas, after disembarking from a ferry, or where GPS position calculation has a large error, or where the map database is old and map matching has a large error
              3. GPS system malfunction
              Others
              1. When the telephone cannot be used due to unpaid telephone charges, etc.
              2. When, for a call made by button operation, the caller does not respond to the center's questions and the center cannot grasp the situation
              3. When a G-BOOK contract has not been made or established, charges are unpaid, or membership has been canceled
              4. When the service start operation (manual maintenance check) has not been performed

              ■ NOTICE ■
              *1: Even within the cellular phone service area, it may be difficult to make emergency calls or talk due to radio wave conditions or line congestion. In this case, even if the line is connected to the center when an accident occurs, communication is not possible and calls requesting assistance cannot be made. If an emergency call cannot be made, report using the nearest public telephone, etc.

              □ HINT □
              *2: The one-touch type HELPNET in-vehicle unit installed on this model is not specially designed to withstand impacts.
