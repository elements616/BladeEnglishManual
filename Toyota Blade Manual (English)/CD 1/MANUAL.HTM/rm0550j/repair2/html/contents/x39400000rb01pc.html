Circuit Description


  1. The crankshaft position sensor system consists of a crankshaft timing pulley and a pickup coil. The crankshaft timing pulley, which is installed on the crankshaft, has 34 teeth with 2 teeth missing for top dead center detection. The NE signal sensor outputs 34 signals for every 1 engine revolutions. Based on the VV1 and VV2 signals, the engine control computer detects cylinder identification and camshaft phase or angle, and from the NE signal it detects crankshaft angle and engine speed.
