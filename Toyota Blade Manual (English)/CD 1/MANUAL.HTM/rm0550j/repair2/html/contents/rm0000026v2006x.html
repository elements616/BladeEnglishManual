Outer Rear View Mirror Assembly RH Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Front Door Inside Handle Bezel RH



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disengage the 3 claws and remove the front door inside handle bezel RH.



2. Remove Door Assist Grip Cover RH



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disengage the 4 claws and remove the door assist grip cover RH.



3. Remove Front Door Trim Board Sub-assembly RH



  1. Remove the 3 screws.




  2. Using a clip remover, disengage the 9 clips of the front door trim board RH.

  3. Disengage the 4 claws and disconnect the front door trim board RH from the front door glass weatherstrip inner RH.




  4. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front door trim board RH from the front door inside handle RH.

  5. Disconnect the connectors.



4. Remove Front Door Inside Handle Sub-assembly RH



  1. Disconnect the front door lock remote control cable RH and front door inside locking cable RH, and remove the front door inside handle RH.



5. Remove Power Window Regulator Master Switch Assembly w/ Front Armrest Base Panel UPR RH



  1. Disengage the 7 claws and remove the power window regulator master switch assembly w/ front armrest base panel UPR RH.



6. Remove Front Door Service Hole Cover RH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the front door service hole cover RH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Remove any butyl tape remaining on the door panel side.



7. Remove Outer Mirror Install Hole Cover RH



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the outer mirror install hole cover RH.



8. Remove Outer Rear View Mirror Assembly w/ Cover



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the outer rear view mirror assembly w/ cover.



9. Remove Outer Mirror RH (w/o Cold Area Specification)

  1. Push the upper part of the mirror surface to tilt the mirror.




  2. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  3. Using a roof molding remover, disengage the 2 claws.




  4. Disengage the 2 guides and remove the outer mirror RH.



10. Remove Outer Mirror RH (w/ Cold Area Specification)

  1. Push the upper part of the mirror surface to tilt the mirror.




  2. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  3. Using a roof molding remover, disengage the 2 claws.




  4. Disengage the 2 guides and separate the outer mirror RH.




  5. Disconnect each connector and remove the outer mirror RH.



11. Remove Outer Mirror Cover RH



  1. Push claws A, B, C, D and G from the back side of the outer mirror cover RH to lift it.

  2. Starting from the lifted area, disengage the 7 claws and remove the outer mirror cover RH.
