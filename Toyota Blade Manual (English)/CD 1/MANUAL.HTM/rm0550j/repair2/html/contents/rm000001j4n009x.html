Electrical Key Antenna Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Rear Bumper Plate NO.1



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disengage the 2 claws and remove the rear bumper plate No.1.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the LH side using the same procedure.



2. Remove Rear Bumper Assembly



  1. Apply protective tape around the rear bumper assembly and around the license plate.

  2. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips. (Lower part of bumper)

  3. Remove the 2 clips and 2 bolts.




  4. Pull the rear bumper assembly toward the outside of the vehicle to disengage the 10 claws (5 each on LH/RH).

  5. Pull the rear bumper assembly toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 claws.




  6. Disengage the 2 clips.




  7. Pull both ends of the rear bumper assembly toward the rear of the vehicle to secure space behind the rear combination lights.




  8. Lift the rear bumper assembly upward until it does not interfere with the upper side of the license plate.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the rear combination lights and the rear bumper assembly.




  9. As shown in the illustration, while pushing the lower side of the rear bumper assembly toward the front of the vehicle, pull it toward the rear of the vehicle until the rear bumper assembly passes over the upper side of the license plate.




  10. Pull the rear bumper assembly downward, pull it out from the license plate and remove the rear bumper assembly.

  11. Disconnect the connector.



3. Remove Electrical Key Antenna



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the 2 nuts and remove the electrical key antenna.
