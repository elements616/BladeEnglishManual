Front Bumper Assembly Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Hood to Front End Panel Seal



  1. Remove the 9 clips and hood to front end panel seal.



2. Remove Radiator Grille



  1. Detach the 4 clamps of the television camera wire. (w/ Intelligent Parking Assist System)




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the radiator grille.



3. Remove Ultrasonic Sensor NO.1 (w/ Intelligent Parking Assist System)



  1. Disconnect the connector.




  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove ultrasonic sensor NO.1.



4. Remove Ultrasonic Sensor Retainer NO.1 (w/ Intelligent Parking Assist System)



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove ultrasonic sensor retainer NO.1 from the front bumper assembly.



5. Remove Television Camera Wire (w/ Intelligent Parking Assist System)



  1. Detach the 2 clamps and remove the parking assist wire.

    □ HINT □
    Remove the RH side in the same way.



6. Remove Front Bumper Hole Cover LH



  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front bumper hole cover LH.



7. Remove Front Bumper Hole Cover RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



8. Remove Radiator Grille LWR LH



  1. Disengage the 6 claws and remove the radiator grille LWR LH.



9. Remove Radiator Grille LWR RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



10. Remove Fog Light Cover LH



  1. Disengage the 8 claws and remove the fog light cover LH.



11. Remove Fog Light Cover RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



12. Remove Fog Light Assembly LH



  1. Remove the screw and remove the fog light assembly LH.



13. Remove Fog Light Assembly RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



14. Remove Front Bumper Side Retainer LH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front bumper side retainer LH.



15. Remove Front Bumper Side Retainer RH

□ HINT □
Remove using the same procedure as for the LH side.



16. Remove Front Bumper Energy Absorber



  1. Disengage the 3 fitting points of the front bumper energy absorber, and remove the front bumper energy absorber from the front bumper reinforcement.



17. Remove Front Bumper Reinforcement Sub-assembly



  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 8 clamps and disconnect the harness.




  2. Remove the 8 bolts and front bumper reinforcement.
