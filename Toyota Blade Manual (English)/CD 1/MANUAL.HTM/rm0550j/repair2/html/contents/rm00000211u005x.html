Preparation List


Circuit Description


When the power window regulator master switch assembly (switch for each seat) is operated, the power window regulator motor assembly (each seat) is driven. The power window regulator motor assembly (each seat) has a motor function, regulator function and ECU function.

When the power window regulator switch assembly (front passenger side) or rear power window regulator switch assembly (rear RH/LH) is operated, the power window regulator motor assembly RH (rear RH) and power window regulator motor assembly LH (front passenger side, rear LH) are driven. The power window regulator motor assembly RH (rear RH) and power window regulator motor assembly LH (front passenger side, rear LH) have a motor function, regulator function and ECU function.

B2312 is output when the ECU judges that a switch of the power window regulator master switch assembly (each SW), power window regulator switch assembly (front passenger side) or rear power window regulator switch assembly (rear RH/LH) remains stuck.

When the power window regulator motor assembly RH (driver side, rear RH) or power window regulator motor assembly LH (front passenger side, rear LH) is replaced with a new one, perform registration setting of the power window system. (see)
