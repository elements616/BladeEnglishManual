Discharge Headlight Bulb Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Precautions for Handling Discharge Headlight
(see)


2. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


3. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector

  1. Remove the 8 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



4. Air Cleaner Inlet NO.1 Removal



  1. Remove the clip.

  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove air cleaner inlet NO.1.



5. Remove Headlight Cover



  1. Turn the headlight cover in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove it.



6. Remove Discharge Headlight Bulb



  1. Turn the socket of the headlight light control computer assembly LH in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration and disconnect it.




  2. In the order shown in the illustration, release the set spring lock and remove the discharge headlight bulb.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not touch the glass portion of the bulb.
