Fusible Link Block Assembly (Engine Type 2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly



  1. Place both hands on the front of the cover and lift it upward to disengage the 2 front clips, then continue lifting upward to disengage the 1 rear clips, and remove the V-bank cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pulling up the front and rear at the same time may cause damage.



2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.




  3. Disconnect the current sensor connector.

  4. Remove the insulator.

  5. Remove the battery.



4. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose



  1. Disconnect the air flow meter connector. (*1).

  2. Disconnect ventilation hose No.2. (*2).

  3. Disconnect the hose band (*3)

  4. Disengage the 3 clamps and remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



5. Remove Air Cleaner Case



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and clamp, and remove the air cleaner case.



6. Remove Engine Control Computer



  1. While pushing the lock on the lever, lift the lever and disconnect the 2 engine control computer connectors.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When disconnecting the connectors, be careful not to let mud, water or other foreign matter adhere to the connector connections.




  2. Remove the clamp and 2 bolts, and remove the engine control computer.




  3. Remove the 4 screws and 2 engine control computer brackets.



7. Remove Relay Block Cover UPR NO.1


8. Disconnect Wire Harness



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  2. Disconnect the 4 connectors.

  3. Detach the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness.



9. Remove Front Fender Liner LH



  1. Using a screwdriver, turn the pin hold clip 90° and remove the pin hold clip.

  2. Detach the 4 clips and turn over the front fender liner LH.



10. Remove Engine Room Relay Block



  1. Detach the harness clamp.

  2. Remove the 3 bolts.

  3. Disengage the claw and guide, and remove the relay block toward the inside of the engine room.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    The wire harness is connected, so do not pull it forcefully.



11. Remove Fusible Link Block Assembly



  1. Disengage the 10 claws and separate the relay block cover into the upper side and lower side.



  2. Disengage the 4 claws of the fuse holder and fusible link block assembly, and pull them out from the separated relay block cover.



    ■ NOTICE ■
    The wire harness is connected, so do not pull it forcefully.




  3. Remove the bolt.

  4. Disengage the claw and separate the fuse holder from the fusible link block assembly.




  5. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 wire harnesses.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When disconnecting the wire harnesses from the fusible link block assembly, put marks on the fusible link block assembly, wire harnesses and bolts to avoid mistakes when connecting.




  6. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the 3 wire harnesses.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    When disconnecting the wire harnesses from the fusible link block assembly, put marks on the fusible link block assembly, wire harnesses and bolts to avoid mistakes when connecting.

  7. Disconnect the 2 connectors and remove the fusible link block assembly.
