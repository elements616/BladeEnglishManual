Preparation List


Function Description


The slip indicator light blinks when slip control is operating to inform the driver of the occurrence of slip and system operation.

The slip indicator light comes on together with the respective warning lights while TRC and VSC control are prohibited due to an ABS or VSC system malfunction. Also, the slip indicator light blinks when hill-start assist control is operating, and comes on during maintenance mode.
