DTC B1830 Curtain Shield Airbag RH Squib Circuit (Short)

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. The curtain shield airbag RH squib circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag RH. When the activation conditions are met, the curtain shield airbag RH is activated.
  2. When a short is detected in the curtain shield airbag RH squib circuit (ICR+, ICR- lines), DTC B1830 is stored in the airbag computer assembly.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Item
Trouble Area
B1830
  1. Short between ICR+ and ICR- lines of the wire harness between the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag RH
  2. Curtain shield airbag RH malfunction
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly Malfunction
  1. Floor wire No.2 (between airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag RH)
  2. Curtain Shield Airbag RH
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly

Inspection Procedure


□ HINT □
When performing the simulation method, use TaSCAN to select check mode first. (see)


Step 1Connector Inspection

□ HINT □
For the inspection procedure and precautions, seeReference

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    Before disconnecting the cable from the negative battery terminal, check that the ignition switch is off.

  1. Check the connection condition of the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag RH.

    Specified Condition
    The connector is connected properly

  1. Disconnect the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag RH, and check the condition of the terminals of each connector.

    Specified Condition
    The terminals are not deformed or damaged

  1. Check the condition of the short pins of the connectors with a short mechanism on floor wire No.2.

    Specified Condition
    The short pins are not deformed or damaged

  1. Check that the curtain shield airbag RH connector of floor wire No.2 is not damaged.

    Specified Condition
    The connector locking button is not disengaged
    The claw of the locking button is not deformed or damaged
