Rear Brake Removal


  1. Remove Instrument Panel Airbag Assembly Lower NO.1

  2. Loosen Parking Brake Cable Assembly

  3. Remove Rear Tire

  4. Drain Brake Fluid

  5. Remove Parking Brake Lever Protector

  6. Disconnect Parking Brake Cable Assembly NO.3

  7. Disconnect Rear Left Flexible Hose

  8. Remove Rear Disc Brake Cylinder Assembly LH

  9. Remove Disc Brake Pad Kit Rear

  10. Remove Rear Disc Brake Anti-squeal Shim Kit

  11. Remove Rear Disc Brake Pad Support Plate

  12. Remove Rear Disc Brake Cylinder Slide Pin

  13. Remove Rear Disc Brake Bush Dust Boot

  14. Remove Rear Disc Brake Cylinder Mounting LH

  15. Remove Rear Disc
