Brake Booster Assembly (Engine Type 2GR-FE) Installation

Print

Preparation List



1. Install Brake Booster Assembly

  1. Install a new brake booster gasket to the brake booster assembly.




  2. Install the brake booster assembly with the 4 nuts.

    Standard
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not apply excessive force to the brake tubes on the engine room side.



2. Connect Brake Tube



  1. Using a union nut wrench (10mm), connect the 2 brake tubes to the brake actuator.

    Standard
    Specified torque
    T = 15 N/m{ 153 kgf/cm }
    When using union nut wrench + torque wrenchL155mm
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }




  2. Using a union nut wrench (10mm), connect the front brake flexible hose No.1 (RH side) to the brake tube.

    Standard
    Specified torque
    T = 15 N/m{ 153 kgf/cm }
    When using union nut wrench + torque wrenchL155mm
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }




  3. Install the brake tube to the body with the 2 new clamps.



3. Connect Check Valve to Connector Tube Hose



  1. Connect the check valve to connector tube hose to the brake booster assembly and secure it with the clip.



4. Temporarily Tighten Brake Master Cylinder Push Rod Clevis

  1. Temporarily install the brake master cylinder push rod clevis with the lock nut.

    □ HINT □
    Fully tighten the lock nut when inspecting and adjusting the brake pedal height.



5. Connect Brake Master Cylinder Push Rod Clevis



  1. Apply MP grease No.2 to the brake master cylinder push rod clevis.

  2. Connect the brake master cylinder push rod clevis to the brake pedal with the push rod clevis pin.

  3. Install a new clip to the push rod clevis pin.



6. Install Brake Pedal Return Spring



  1. Install the brake pedal return spring to the brake pedal support.



7. Install Brake Master Cylinder Sub-assembly

■ NOTICE ■
  1. The master cylinder piston comes out easily, so do not hold the piston portion or pull it out. If the master cylinder piston comes out, the master cylinder function may be reduced; do not reuse it, and replace the assembly.
  1. Do not do anything that could damage the master cylinder piston, such as applying impact to it or clamping it in a vise.
  1. Do not remove the master cylinder while vacuum is applied to the brake booster.
  1. Do not point the piston side of the master cylinder downward below horizontal.
  1. Do not allow foreign matter to adhere to the master cylinder piston. If foreign matter adheres, remove it with a shop rag etc., and apply rubber grease to the entire circumference of the outer surface of the piston (sliding part).
  1. Do not use any grease or oil other than that specified.

  1. Install a new O-ring to the brake master cylinder assembly.




  2. Insert the brake master cylinder assembly into the brake booster assembly, and install the brake master cylinder assembly with the 2 nuts.

    Standard
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }




  3. Using a union nut wrench (10mm), connect the 2 brake tubes to the brake master cylinder assembly.

    Standard
    Specified torque
    T = 15 N/m{ 153 kgf/cm }
    When using union nut wrench + torque wrenchL155mm
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }




  4. Connect the warning switch connector to the reservoir and engage the clamp.



8. Install Engine Assembly w/ Transaxle



  1. Set the engine assembly w/ transaxle on the engine lifter.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set the engine assembly w/ transaxle so that it is level.
    2. Securely support the engine assembly w/ transaxle so that it does not fall over.

  2. Operate the engine lifter and install the engine assembly w/ transaxle to the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Install it while being careful of the wire harnesses and steering shaft.




  3. Install the engine mounting insulator RH to the engine mounting bracket RH with the bolt and 2 nuts.

    Standard
    T = 95 N·m{ 969 kgf·cm } (Bolt)
    T = 95 N*m{ 969 kgf*cm } (Nut A)
    T = 52 N*m{ 530 kgf*cm } (Nut B)




  4. Install the engine mounting insulator LH to the engine mounting bracket LH with the bolt and nut.

    Standard
    T = 56 N/m{ 571 kgf/cm }

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Hold the bolt side and tighten the nut side.

15. Install Front Drive Shaft Assembly LH



  1. Apply Toyota Genuine ATF WS to the splines of the inboard joint assembly LH.

  2. Face the snap ring opening downward.

  3. Align the splines of the drive shaft and transaxle, and insert the drive shaft assembly LH using a brass bar and hammer.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not damage the oil seal.
    2. Do not damage the drive shaft boot.

    □ HINT □
    Determine that the drive shaft is fully driven in by a change in the reaction force of the brass bar or a change in the sound.



16. Install Front Drive Shaft Assembly RH



  1. Apply Toyota Genuine ATF WS to the splines of the inboard joint assembly RH.

  2. Align the splines of the drive shaft and transaxle, insert the drive shaft assembly, and tighten it with the 2 bolts.

    Standard
    T = 64 N/m{ 653 kgf/cm }

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the oil seal.



17. Install Front Axle Assembly LH

  1. Push the front axle assembly LH toward the outside of the vehicle, align the splines of the front drive shaft assembly LH with the front axle assembly LH, and insert it.

    2. Do not damage the oil seal.
    3. Do not damage the boot of the front drive shaft assembly LH.
    4. Do not damage the speed sensor rotor.
    5. Check that there is no foreign matter on the speed sensor rotor or insertion part.

59. Fill Automatic Transaxle Fluid

  1. Vehicle Condition Check

    □ HINT □
    1. Perform the work after the fluid temperature has dropped to room temperature.
    2. Leave the vehicle for a long time (overnight) after stopping the engine.

  2. Work Content Check



    1. Refer to the work flow below and follow the procedure.



      □ HINT □
      The fluid filling procedure differs depending on the work performed.

    2. If a large amount of fluid is to be added, go to step [*1]

    3. If a small amount of fluid is to be added, go to step [*2]

  3. Add fluid (when a large amount of fluid is to be added) [*1]

    1. Keeping the vehicle level, lift it up.

    2. Remove engine under cover NO.1.

    3. Remove the engine under cover rear LH.




    4. Using a socket hexagon wrench 6, remove the overflow plug and gasket.

    5. Remove the refill plug and gasket.




    6. Add fluid through the refill hole until fluid flows out of the overflow.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Use Toyota Genuine ATF WS as the fluid to be added.




    7. Using a socket hexagon wrench 6, temporarily tighten the overflow plug with the gasket.

    8. Add the specified amount of fluid through the refill hole.

      Related Work
      		Refill Amount (Reference)
      When removing/installing the oil pan or drive shaft, or replacing an oil seal
      		2.8L
      When removing/installing the torque converter assembly
      		4.8L
      When the transaxle assembly has been replaced (new torque converter assembly used)
      		5.3L
      When the transaxle assembly has been replaced (torque converter assembly reused)
      		3.4L

      ■ NOTICE ■
      The refill amount varies depending on the related work.




    9. Temporarily tighten the refill plug with the gasket.

    10. Lower the vehicle.

    11. Go to Oil Temperature Detection Mode Switching (When Terminal TC Is Shorted) [*3]

    12. Go to Oil Temperature Detection Mode Switching (When Using SST (TaSCAN)) [*4]

  4. Add fluid (when a small amount of fluid is to be added) [*2]

    1. Keeping the vehicle level, lift it up.

    2. Remove engine under cover NO.1.

    3. Remove the engine under cover rear LH.




    4. Remove the refill plug and gasket.

    5. Add the specified amount of fluid through the refill hole.

      Related Work
      		Refill Amount (Reference)
      When the oil cooler and cooler hoses have been removed
      		0.5L
      When oil seepage, etc. has been repaired
      		0.5L

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Use Toyota Genuine ATF WS as the fluid to be added.




    6. Temporarily tighten the refill plug with the gasket.

    7. Lower the vehicle.

    8. Go to Oil Temperature Detection Mode Switching (When Terminal TC Is Shorted) [*3]

    9. Go to Oil Temperature Detection Mode Switching (When Using SST (TaSCAN)) [*4]

  5. Switch to fluid temperature detection mode (when shorting TC terminal) [*3]

    1. Turn IG ON.

    2. Turn the A/C OFF.

    3. Turn IG OFF.




    4. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of DLC3.

      SST
      09843-18040  

    5. Start the engine.

    6. Slowly move the shift lever from the P range to the D range, and then shift back to the P range.




    7. Switch to oil temperature detection mode.

      Oil Temperature Detection Mode Switching Procedure
      Perform the N - D operation within 1.5 seconds
      Perform the above operation continuously for 6 seconds or more




    8. The meter indicator light [D] illuminates for 2 seconds and then goes off.




    9. Depending on the fluid temperature, the meter indicator light comes on, goes off or blinks.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. Add fluid while the meter indicator light is illuminated.
      2. If the meter indicator light is off, adjust the fluid temperature [*4] to between 40° and C-45°C.
      3. If the meter indicator light is blinking, check the vehicle condition and wait for the fluid temperature to drop before performing the work.

  6. Adjust fluid temperature (when shorting TC terminal) [*4]

    1. Move the shift lever to the P range.




    2. Disconnect terminals 13 (TC) ←→ 4 (CG) of DLC3.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Be sure to open terminal TC, as the fluid level cannot be adjusted accurately unless it is open.

    3. Warm up the engine at idle to raise the automatic transaxle fluid temperature.




    4. As soon as the meter indicator light [D] illuminates, lift up the vehicle.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Adjust the fluid level while the meter indicator light is illuminated.

    5. Go to Fluid Level Check [*7]

  7. Switch to fluid temperature detection mode (when using SST (TaSCAN)) [*5]

    1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. Start the engine with the A/C OFF.

    3. Select Active Test (TC terminal ON), and turn the TC terminal ON with the A/T fluid temperature displayed.

    4. Slowly move the shift lever from the P range to the D range, and then shift back to the P range.




    5. Switch to oil temperature detection mode.

      Oil Temperature Detection Mode Switching Procedure
      Perform the N - D operation within 1.5 seconds
      Perform the above operation continuously for 6 seconds or more




    6. The meter indicator light [D] illuminates for 2 seconds and then goes off.




    7. Depending on the fluid temperature, the meter indicator light comes on, goes off or blinks.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. Add fluid while the meter indicator light is illuminated.
      2. If the meter indicator light is off, adjust the fluid temperature [*6] to between 40° and C-45°C.
      3. If the meter indicator light is blinking, check the vehicle condition and wait for the fluid temperature to drop before performing the work.

  8. Adjust fluid temperature (when using SST (TaSCAN)) [*6]

    1. Move the shift lever to the P range.

    2. Turn the TC terminal from ON to OFF.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Be sure to open terminal TC, as the fluid level cannot be adjusted accurately unless it is open.

    3. Warm up the engine at idle to raise the automatic transaxle fluid temperature.




    4. As soon as the meter indicator light [D] illuminates, lift up the vehicle.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Adjust the fluid level while the meter indicator light is illuminated.

    5. Go to Fluid Level Check [*7]

  9. Check fluid level [*7]

    □ HINT □
    The fluid level adjustment method differs depending on whether or not the fluid overflows.





    1. Using a socket hexagon wrench 6, remove the overflow plug and gasket.

    2. Check the fluid condition.

      □ HINT □
      1. "Overflow" refers to the condition in which fluid flows out of the overflow tube.
      2. If only a small amount of fluid flows out, it may just be the fluid that had collected in the overflow tube (approximately 1cc), so judge that it is not overflowing.

    3. If fluid overflows, go to step [*8]

    4. If fluid does not overflow, go to step [*9]

  10. Adjust fluid level (when fluid overflows) [*8]

    ■ NOTICE ■
    The drained fluid is hot, so handle it with care.





    1. When the fluid flow becomes a trickle, fully tighten the overflow plug with a new gasket.

      Standard
      T = 40 N/m{ 408 kgf/cm }




    2. Fully tighten the refill plug with a new gasket.

      Standard
      T = 49 N/m{ 500 kgf/cm }

    3. Install the engine under cover rear LH.

    4. Install engine under cover NO.1.

    5. Lower the vehicle.

    6. Turn IG OFF and disconnect SST (TaSCAN).

  11. Refill fluid (when fluid does not overflow) [*9]




    1. Remove the refill plug and gasket.




    2. Add fluid through the refill hole until fluid flows out of the overflow.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      Use Toyota Genuine ATF WS as the fluid to be added.




    3. When the fluid flow becomes a trickle, fully tighten the overflow plug with a new gasket.

      Standard
      T = 40 N/m{ 408 kgf/cm }




    4. Fully tighten the refill plug with a new gasket.

      Standard
      T = 49 N/m{ 500 kgf/cm }

    5. Install the engine under cover rear LH.

    6. Install engine under cover NO.1.

    7. Lower the vehicle.

    8. Turn IG OFF and disconnect SST (TaSCAN).



60. Add Coolant (Toyota Genuine Super Long Life Coolant)

  1. Close the radiator drain cock plug.

  2. Close the cylinder block drain cock plug.

    Standard
    T = 13 N/m{ 133 kgf/cm }




  3. Fill coolant of the specified concentration up to the B line of the radiator reserve tank.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not add water as coolant

    □ HINT □
    Squeeze radiator hose NO.1 and radiator hose NO.2 by hand several times, and if the coolant level in the filler drops, add more coolant.

  4. After installing the reservoir tank cap, warm up the engine until the thermostat opens. (A)

    □ HINT □
    1. Squeeze radiator hose NO.1 and radiator hose NO.2 several times while warming up the engine.
    2. The valve opening timing can be checked by gripping radiator hose NO.2 by hand.

    ■ WARNING ■
    1. Wear protective gloves when working
    2. Be careful of cooling fan operation
    3. The engine and radiator become hot, so avoid the risk of burns.

  5. After warm-up, repeat the cycle of 3000rpm engine speed for 5 seconds ←→ idling for 45 seconds 8 times or more (7 minutes or more). (B)

  6. Stop the engine and wait until the coolant cools down, and if the level is below LOW, add coolant of the specified concentration through the filler again and adjust it to between FULL and LOW. (C)

  7. Repeat steps (A) to (C) until the coolant level in the radiator reservoir tank stops dropping.

  8. When the level stops dropping, add coolant of the specified concentration through the filler again to adjust the coolant level in the radiator reservoir tank to between FULL and LOW.

90. Bleed Brake Line

  1. Connect a vinyl tube to the bleeder plug.

  2. Depress the brake pedal several times, and loosen the bleeder plug with the brake pedal depressed. (*1)

  3. When fluid stops coming out, tighten the bleeder plug and release the brake pedal. (*2)

  4. Repeat (*1) to (*2) until there is no air in the brake fluid.

  5. Bleed the air from the brake line of each wheel in the same way.

    Standard

    T = 11 N/m{ 112 kgf/cm }



91. Bleed ABS & Traction Actuator Assembly

■ NOTICE ■
  1. If appropriate brake pedal height and feel cannot be obtained after performing normal brake line bleeding, air may have entered; perform the following bleeding again.
  2. Bleeding without using the SST (TaSCAN) may lead to malfunctions or accidents, so be sure to use the SST (TaSCAN).

  1. Turn the IG OFF and depress the brake pedal 20 times or more.

  2. With the IG still OFF, connect the SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

    SST
    09991-70200  

  3. Turn IG ON.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    The engine will not start.

  4. Turn the SST (TaSCAN) power switch ON, select "Menu (Work Support)" → "ABS/VSC/ECB" → "Air Bleeding" and execute it.

  5. Bleed Actuator Intake System

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Bleed the actuator intake system at the 2 right side wheels (front and rear wheels).


    1. On the SST (TaSCAN) menu screen, select intake system bleeding and execute it.

    2. Loosen the bleeder plug of the right front wheel. (*1)

    3. Operate the SST (TaSCAN) to operate the actuator.

      □ HINT □
      1. To protect the actuator, it automatically stops after 4 seconds.
      2. Leave the brake pedal released.

    4. Check on the SST (TaSCAN) screen that operation has stopped.

    5. Temporarily tighten the bleeder plug. (*2)

    6. Repeat the procedure from (*1) to (*2) until the air is completely bled.

    7. Tighten the bleeder plug.

      Standard
      T = 8.3 N/m{ 85 kgf/cm }

    8. Perform the same procedure for the right rear wheel as for the right front wheel.

      Standard
      T = 11 N/m{ 112 kgf/cm }

  6. Bleed Actuator Pressure Reduction System

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Bleed the actuator pressure reduction system at each of the 4 wheels.


    1. Execute pressure reduction system bleeding on the SST (TaSCAN) menu screen.

    2. Loosen the bleeder plug of the right front wheel. (*3)

    3. Operate the actuator using the SST (TaSCAN), and at the same time fully depress the brake pedal and hold it.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. The brake pedal will feel heavy at this time, but keep depressing it until brake fluid comes out of the bleeder plug.
      2. Hold the brake pedal depressed and do not pump it.

      □ HINT □
      To protect the solenoids, actuator operation automatically stops after 4 seconds. Also, when operating it continuously, wait at least 20 seconds between operations.

    4. Tighten the bleeder plug and release the brake pedal. (*4)

    5. Repeat the procedure from (*3) to (*4) until the air is completely bled.

    6. Tighten the bleeder plug.

    7. Perform the same procedure for the other 3 wheels as for the right front wheel.

      Standard
      Front Bleeder Plug
      T = 8.3 N/m{ 85 kgf/cm }
      Rear Bleeder Plug
      T = 11 N/m{ 112 kgf/cm }

  7. Perform normal brake line bleeding again by pumping the brake pedal.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Repeat bleeding for each of the 4 wheels until the air is completely bled.

      BTDC 8-18°
