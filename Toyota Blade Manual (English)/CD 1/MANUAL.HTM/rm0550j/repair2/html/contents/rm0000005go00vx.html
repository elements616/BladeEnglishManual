DTC B2781 Steering Lock ECU Driver Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


The steering lock ECU and steering lock motor are built into the steering lock actuator.

The steering lock ECU detects the lock/unlock position by means of the lock position switch of the steering lock motor.

The steering lock ECU receives lock/unlock command signals from the certification ECU and returns operation signals.

Since the steering lock ECU is not connected to CAN communication, responses to outside the steering lock system are made by the certification ECU via LIN communication.
