Steering Lock System DTC Check / Clear

Print



  1. Main Body ECU, Reading DTCs

    1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3 and turn the IG ON.

      SST
      09991-70200  

    2. On the SST (TaSCAN) screen, select [Diagnosis] → [Vehicle Diagnosis/ALL Diag] or [System Diagnosis/Body], and check the diagnostic trouble codes for [System Name/J/B Integration].



    1. Certification ECU (Steering Lock), Reading DTCs

      1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3 and turn the IG ON.

        SST
        09991-70200  

      2. On the SST (TaSCAN) screen, select [Diagnosis] → [Vehicle Diagnosis/ALL Diag] or [System Diagnosis/Body], and check the diagnostic trouble codes for [System Name/Certification ECU (Steering Lock)].

        ■ NOTICE ■
        1. Since the steering lock ECU does not store past DTCs, do not clear the codes until checking and recording of the codes is complete.
        2. When checking diagnostic trouble codes or data monitor related to the smart entry & start system from the IG OFF state, the body ECU may not respond unless any door is opened to cancel its sleep state.
        3. Diagnostic trouble codes may be stored due to malfunctioning areas other than the steering lock ECU system, so also check the diagnostic trouble codes of other systems.



      1. Clearing DTC Memory

        1. Connect SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3 and turn the IG ON.

          SST
          09991-70200  

        2. On the SST (TaSCAN) [Check DTC] screen, select [Clear] and clear the diagnostic trouble codes according to the screen.
