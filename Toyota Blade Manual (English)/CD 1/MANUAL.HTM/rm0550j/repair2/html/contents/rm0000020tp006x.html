Transmission Revolution Sensor Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Transmission Revolution Sensor (NT)



  1. Disconnect the transmission revolution sensor (NT) connector.

  2. Using SST (Toyota electrical tester), inspect the resistance between the connector terminals.

    SST
    09082-00030  
    09083-00170  

    Resistance
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Standard
    1 - 2
    		20°C
    		560 to 680Ω
