Navigation Receiver Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List


■ NOTICE ■
The navigation receiver assembly has a built-in hard disk drive (hereafter HDD) for storing map data, etc. used by the navigation system. Therefore, read the precautions carefully before performing the work. (see)



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



3. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a roof molding remover and move it near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the roof molding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



4. Remove Instrument Panel Register Assembly CTR



  1. Pull the instrument panel register assembly CTR horizontally toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 clips, 5 claws and 3 guides.




  2. Disconnect the connector and remove the instrument panel register assembly CTR.



5. Remove Navigation Receiver Assembly w/ Bracket



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.




  2. Disengage the 4 clips and pull the navigation receiver assembly w/ bracket toward the rear of the vehicle.

  3. Disconnect each connector and remove the navigation receiver assembly w/ bracket.



6. Remove Radio Bracket NO.1



  1. Remove the 4 screws and remove the radio bracket NO.1 from the navigation receiver assembly.



7. Remove Radio Bracket NO.2



  1. Remove the 4 screws and remove the radio bracket NO.2 from the navigation receiver assembly.



8. Remove Navigation Receiver Assembly
