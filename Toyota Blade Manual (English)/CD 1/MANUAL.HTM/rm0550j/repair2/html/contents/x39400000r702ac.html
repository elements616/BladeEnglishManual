Circuit Description


  1. Detected when the fuel correction amount by the upstream air fuel ratio sensor exceeds a specified value.
    There are 2 types of air-fuel ratio correction: normal operation air-fuel ratio correction (hereafter F/B correction) and operating condition memory air-fuel ratio correction (hereafter F/B learning).
    F/B correction is the fuel correction used to maintain the stoichiometric air-fuel ratio; the engine control computer receives the signal from the air fuel ratio sensor indicating whether the current air-fuel ratio is richer (hereafter rich) or leaner (hereafter lean) than the stoichiometric ratio, and decreases the injection volume when rich and increases it when lean.
    F/B learning is a control that, when F/B correction has been performed for a certain time, changes to match individual engine differences (caused by aging, changes in operating environment, etc.) to bring the F/B correction close to zero.
    If the condition in which the fuel correction amount (total of F/B correction value and F/B learning value) exceeds a specified value toward lean or rich continues for 2 consecutive trips, the engine control computer illuminates the check engine warning light.
    Also, when the vehicle runs out of fuel, the fuel correction amount becomes +35% or more, and if this continues (2 trips), lean malfunction P0171/25/P0174/25 may be output.
