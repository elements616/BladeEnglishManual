Speed Sensor Front LH Removal

Print

□ HINT □
When replacing the sensor rotor, replace it together with the front axle hub LH, as the sensor rotor is built into the front axle hub bearing LH.



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Front Tire


3. Remove Speed Sensor FR LH


  1. Remove the speed sensor clip from the body.



  2. Disconnect the connector.

  3. Remove bolt B and disconnect sensor clamp No.2 from the body.

  4. Remove bolt A and disconnect sensor clamp No.1 from the shock absorber.

  5. Remove the clip.

  6. Remove bolt C and the speed sensor FR LH from the steering knuckle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. The speed sensor FR LH is easily damaged, so do not apply excessive force or rotate it when pulling it out of the front axle hub LH.
    2. Do not allow foreign matter to adhere to the speed sensor tip and installation area.



4. Remove Front Axle Shaft Nut LH



  1. Set the SST aligned with the groove of the front drive shaft assembly LH, and using a hammer, unstake the front axle hub nut LH.

    SST
    09930-00010  

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Set SST in the groove with its flat surface facing up.
    2. Never grind the tip of SST with a grinder or similar tool.
    3. When removing the front axle hub nut LH, completely unstake it.
    4. Do not damage the threads of the front drive shaft assembly LH.

  2. Using a socket wrench (30mm), remove the front axle hub nut LH.



5. Disconnect Front Flexible Hose NO.1



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect front brake flexible hose No.1.



6. Disconnect Tie Rod End LH

  1. Remove the cotter pin and castle nut.




  2. Using SST, disconnect the tie rod end LH from the steering knuckle.

    SST
    09960-20010   (09961-02010, 09961-02050)

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not damage the front disc brake dust cover.
    2. To prevent SST from falling, be sure to suspend it with a string or similar.
    3. Do not damage the ball joint dust cover.
    4. Do not damage the steering knuckle.



7. Disconnect Front Disc Brake Caliper Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the front disc brake caliper assembly LH from the steering knuckle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Suspend the front disc brake caliper assembly LH with wire or equivalent.



8. Remove Front Disc



  1. Put matchmarks on the disc and axle hub, and remove the disc.



9. Disconnect Front Suspension Arm Sub-assembly Lower NO.1 LH



  1. Remove the 1 bolts and 2 nuts.

  2. Push down the front suspension lower arm No.1 LH and disconnect it from the front lower ball joint LH.



10. Remove Front Axle Assembly LH



  1. Using a plastic hammer, lightly tap the end of the front drive shaft assembly LH to disengage the front drive shaft assembly LH from the front axle assembly LH.

    □ HINT □
    If the engagement is tight, tap the end of the front drive shaft assembly LH using a brass bar and hammer.

  2. Push the front axle assembly LH toward the outside of the vehicle and pull the front drive shaft assembly LH out of the front axle assembly LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. Do not push the front axle assembly LH out farther than necessary.
    2. Do not damage the drive shaft outboard joint boot.
    3. Do not damage the speed sensor rotor.
    4. Suspend the front drive shaft assembly LH with wire or similar.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts, and remove the front axle assembly LH from the front shock absorber.



11. Remove Front Axle Hub LH w/ Sensor Rotor

  1. Secure the front axle assembly in a vise between aluminum plates.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not overtighten the vise.




  2. Remove the 4 bolts, and remove the front axle hub and dust cover.
