Rear Seat Assembly LH Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Rear Seatback Assembly LH



  1. Using a Torx socket wrench T45, remove the 2 Torx bolts, 2 bushings and rear seatback assembly LH.



2. Remove Rear Seatback Lock Control Lever w/ Base



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  2. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the rear seatback lock control lever w/ base.



3. Remove Rear Seat Lock Control Lever NO.1



  1. Slide rear seat lock control lever NO.1 as shown in the illustration to remove it.



4. Remove Rear Seat Lock Base



  1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove the rear seat lock base.



5. Remove Rear Seatback Lock Cover LH



  1. Remove the 2 screws and rear seatback lock cover LH.



6. Remove Rear Seatback Cover



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seatback cover.



7. Remove Rear Seatback Board Carpet Assembly LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.




  2. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.




  3. Detach each J hook and remove the rear seatback board carpet assembly LH.



8. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Support



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the 2 rear seat headrest supports.



9. Remove Separate Type Rear Seatback Cover LH

  1. Remove the separate type rear seatback cover w/ pad from the rear seatback frame sub-assembly LH.




  2. Remove the 10 hog rings and separate type rear seatback cover LH.



10. Remove Rear Seatback Lock Assembly LH



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear seatback lock assembly LH.



11. Remove Separate Type Rear Seat Cushion Cover LH



  1. Detach the 13 J hooks and remove the separate type rear seat cushion cover w/ pad.




  2. Remove the 6 hog rings and separate type rear seat cushion cover LH.
