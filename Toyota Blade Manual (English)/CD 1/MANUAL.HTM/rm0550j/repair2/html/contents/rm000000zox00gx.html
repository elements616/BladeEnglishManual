Electric Power Steering System Fail-safe List

Print



  1. Fail-safe List

    1. When the power steering computer assembly detects a malfunction, it stops power assist to protect the system, or continues control under specific conditions.



    Malfunction Description
    		Fail-safe
    1. Torque sensor malfunction
    2. Motor Rotation Angle Sensor Malfunction
    3. Power steering motor malfunction
    4. Power steering computer assembly malfunction (excluding temperature sensor system)
    		Power assist stopped
    1. Power steering computer assembly malfunction (temperature sensor system)
    		System overheat prevention control is switched to a specification that does not use the board temperature sensor, and control continues
    1. EEPROM Malfunction
    		Controlled with default map
    1. Speed sensor system malfunction
    2. ABS ECU communication malfunction
    		Characteristics fixed to vehicle speed 100km/h, and power steering control continues
    1. When ECU internal temperature is high (not a malfunction)
    2. Power supply voltage drop (not a malfunction)
    		Power assist amount gradually reduced

    □ HINT □
    If the steering wheel is repeatedly turned while stopped or driving at low speed, or held at full lock for a long time, the power assist amount may be reduced to prevent the motor and computer from overheating. In this case, the system returns to normal if the steering wheel is not operated for approximately 10 minutes with the IG ON.
