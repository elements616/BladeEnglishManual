Cooler Condenser Assembly (Engine Type 2AZ-FE) Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Cooler Condenser Cushion NO.1 Removal



  1. Remove the 4 cooler condenser cushions NO.1.



2. Cooler Dryer Removal



  1. Using a straight hexagon wrench 14mm, remove the cap from the modulator.




  2. Using needle-nose pliers, remove the cooler dryer from the modulator.
