Inspection Procedure

Step 1Preliminary Inspection

  1. Check that the following brake warning light off conditions are met.

    1. The ABS warning light is off.

      □ HINT □
      If the ABS warning light remains on, first resolve the ABS warning light remaining on.

    2. There is sufficient brake fluid in the brake master cylinder reservoir.

    3. The parking brake is fully released.


NEXT


Step 2Inspect Connection Condition of Each Part

  1. Lightly shake the connectors and wire harnesses of each part up and down and side to side, and inspect the brake warning light illumination status.

    Specified Condition
    Warning light display does not change

    □ HINT □
    If the warning light illumination changes when the actuator assembly connector and wires are vibrated, inspect the terminal contacts for malfunctions.



NG
Repair or Replace Wire Harness or Connector

Step 5Inspect Brake Master Cylinder Reservoir Sub-assembly (Fluid Level Warning Switch)

  1. With the fluid level sufficient, disconnect the fluid level warning switch connector and check the brake warning light display.

    Specified Condition
    Brake warning light does not go off

    □ HINT □
    If the light goes off, the level warning switch has an internal short.
