Starter Assembly (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



2. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal


3. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.




  3. Disconnect the current sensor connector.

  4. Remove the insulator.

  5. Remove the battery.



4. Remove Battery Carrier



  1. Remove the battery tray.

  2. Disengage the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness from the battery carrier.

  3. Remove the 4 bolts and the battery carrier.



5. Remove Battery Carrier NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and battery carrier NO.2.



6. Remove Starter Assembly



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Open the terminal cap cover.

  3. Remove the nut and disconnect terminal B.

  4. Remove the bolt (A in the illustration) and disconnect the wire harness clamp bracket.

  5. Remove the 2 bolts (B in the illustration) and the starter assembly.
