DTC B2311 Motor Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


When the power window regulator master switch assembly (all seats) is operated, the power window regulator motor assemblies (all seats) are driven. The power window regulator motor assemblies (all seats) have a motor function, regulator function and ECU function.

B2311 is output when an internal malfunction of the power window regulator motor assembly RH (driver side, rear right) or power window regulator motor assembly LH (front passenger side, rear left) is determined, or when the ECU in the power window regulator motor assembly RH (driver side, rear right) or power window regulator motor assembly LH (front passenger side, rear left) determines that there is a difference of approximately 2 cm or more from the normal fully closed window position when a window fully closing operation is performed.

■ NOTICE ■
  1. Identify the power window regulator motor assembly (ECU) at the location that output this DTC (B2311) before inspecting.
  2. If the power window regulator motor assembly RH (driver side, rear right) or power window regulator motor assembly LH (front passenger side, rear left) is replaced with a new one, perform power window system registration/setting (see) must be performed.
  3. If AUTO UP is operated after replacing the glass, glass run, etc., the jam protection function may operate. In that case, repeating AUTO UP hold ←→ AUTO DOWN operation 5 times or more returns it to normal operation.
