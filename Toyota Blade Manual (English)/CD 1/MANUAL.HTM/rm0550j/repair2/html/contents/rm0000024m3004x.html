Shift Lock Control Unit Assembly (U660E) On-vehicle Inspection

Print


1. Shift Lock Control Unit Assembly Inspection

  1. Inspect Shift Lock Operation

    1. Move the shift lever to P.

    2. Turn IG OFF.

    3. Check that the shift lever cannot be shifted to any position other than P when operated.

    4. With the IG ON and the brake pedal depressed, check that the shift lever can be shifted out of the P position.

  2. Inspect Shift Lock Release Button Operation

    1. Move the shift lever to P.

    2. Turn IG OFF.

    3. Check that the shift lever can be shifted out of the P position when operated while pressing the shift lock release button.
