Interior Illumination Light Assembly NO.1 Component Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Interior Illumination Light Assembly NO.1



  1. Inspect Light Illumination

    1. Connect a battery between the connector terminals and inspect the illumination condition of the lights.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → 5 (+B1)
      Battery negative (-) → 6 (GND)
      		All switches OFF
      		All lights off
      RH side switch ON
      		RH side light comes on
      LH side switch ON
      		LH side light comes on
      Dome light switch (ON/OFF switch) ON
      		Dome light on

    2. Connect a battery between the terminals of the connector, and check the lighting condition of the lights when the DOOR switch is turned OFF→ON.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive → 5 (+B1)
      Battery negative → 1 (CTY)
      		DOOR switch OFF
      		Front map light and dome light off
      DOOR switch ON
      		Front map light and dome light on

    3. Connect a battery between the terminals of the connector, and check the lighting condition of the LED when the ROOM switch is turned OFF→ON.

      Specified Condition
      Tester Connection (Terminal Symbol)
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive → 5 (+B1)
      Battery negative → 2 (CILL)
      		ROOM switch OFF
      		Indirect lighting off
      ROOM switch ON
      		Indirect lighting on
