Circuit Description



  1. The cranking hold control system energizes the starter until the engine control computer, having detected the starter signal (STSW) from the main body ECU, determines that the engine has started.
    The engine control computer, having received the starter signal (STSW), outputs the ACC cut relay signal (ACCR) to the main body ECU and turns the ST CUT relay ON to prevent flickering of the meter, clock, audio, etc., and the engine control computer also outputs a signal to the STAR terminal.
    The STAR output signal turns the ST relay ON via the neutral start switch, operating the starter. Also, while the engine is being cranked, the starter operation signal is also sent to the STA terminal of the engine control computer. The main body ECU monitors the voltage of the ST CUT relay output side terminal, and protects or drives the starter circuit if there is a malfunction.
