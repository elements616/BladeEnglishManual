Speaker Assembly Rear Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Rear Door Inside Handle Bezel RH



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disengage the 3 claws and remove the rear door inside handle bezel RH.



2. Remove Door Assist Grip Cover RH



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disengage the 4 claws and remove the door assist grip cover RH.



3. Remove Rear Door Trim Board Sub-assembly RH



  1. Remove the 3 screws.




  2. Using a clip remover, disengage the 9 clips.

  3. Disengage the 3 claws and disconnect the rear door trim board RH from the rear door glass weatherstrip inner RH.




  4. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the rear door trim board RH from the rear door inside handle RH.

  5. Disconnect the connector.



4. Remove Speaker Assembly RR



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Remove the 3 rivets and remove the speaker assembly RR from the rear door.
