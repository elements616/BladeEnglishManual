Rear Window Defogger System Function Inspection

Print


1. Rear Window Defogger Operation Inspection

  1. Turn IG ON.

  2. Check that the indicator comes on and the rear window defogger warms up when the rear window defogger switch is pressed.

  3. Check that the indicator goes off and rear window defogger operation stops after approximately 15 minutes.

    □ HINT □
    5 minutes after the indicator goes off, use a thermometer to check that the rear window defogger temperature has dropped from when the indicator went off.
