        1. Wiring Harness Connector (CAN J/C4)

          □ HINT □
          1. Connectors that connect to CAN J/C4 are identified by the wire color of the communication lines (bus) and differences in the connector mating surfaces.
          2. If a CAN J/C4 connector is disconnected, connecting it to any position causes no performance problem, but reconnect it to its original installation position in consideration of the effect of tension on the wiring and subsequent serviceability.
          3. If the connection destination of a connector is unknown, use the connected ECU check function of SST (TaSCAN) to check which ECUs and sensors disappear from the display when the connector is disconnected.
