Step 2Inspect Door Glass Sliding Resistance (Location where Jam Protection Function Operates)

  1. Remove the window regulator.

  1. Assemble the door glass to the door sash, raise it by hand and check that it slides smoothly without catching.

    □ HINT □
    1. The door glass should not contact the glass run unnaturally.
    2. The glass run should be free of deformation and trapped foreign matter.
