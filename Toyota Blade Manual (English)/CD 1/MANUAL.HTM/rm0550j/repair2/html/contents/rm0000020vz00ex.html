Oxygen Sensor (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Engine Under Cover

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 4 screws, and remove the engine under cover.



2. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove engine under cover No.1.



3. Oxygen Sensor NO.2 Removal

  1. Disconnect the oxygen sensor NO.2 connector.




  2. Using SST, remove the oxygen sensor NO.2 from the front exhaust pipe assembly.

    SST
    09224-00010  



4. Remove Exhaust Pipe Assembly Center

  1. Disconnect the oxygen sensor connector and clamp.




  2. Remove the 2 nuts and 2 bolts.




  3. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 compression springs, and remove the exhaust center pipe assembly.



5. Oxygen Sensor Removal



  1. Using SST, remove the oxygen sensor from the exhaust center pipe assembly

    SST
    09224-00010
