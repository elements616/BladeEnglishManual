Front Brake Disassembly

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Cylinder Boot



  1. Using a flat-head screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, remove the set ring.

  2. Remove the cylinder boot from the disc brake cylinder.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the inside of the disc brake cylinder and the cylinder boot installation groove.



2. Remove Front Disc Brake Piston



  1. Place a shop rag between the disc brake piston and disc brake cylinder to prevent damage.

  2. Using an air gun, gradually blow in air to remove the disc brake piston from the disc brake cylinder.

    ■ WARNING ■
    Do not get your fingers caught.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not splash the fluid.



3. Remove Piston Seal



  1. Using a flat-head screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, remove the piston seal.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not damage the inside of the disc brake cylinder and the piston seal installation groove.
