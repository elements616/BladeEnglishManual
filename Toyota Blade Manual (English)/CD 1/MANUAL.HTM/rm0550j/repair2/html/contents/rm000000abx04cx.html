(*1): Check mode

  1. In DTC inspection, it is very important to determine whether the malfunction indicated by the DTC is still occurring, or occurred in the past but has now returned to normal. It is also necessary to confirm through the problem symptom check whether the malfunction indicated by the DTC is directly related to the problem symptom. For this reason, it is necessary to check the DTCs before and after confirming the problem symptom in order to determine the current situation. If this is neglected, in some cases unnecessary troubleshooting will be performed on a normally operating system, making it difficult to locate the problem area and resulting in repairs unrelated to the problem. Therefore, always check the DTCs using the correct work procedure.
