Rear Combination Light Assembly LH Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Rear Combination Light Body Packing LH



  1. Remove the tail light (rear combination light) body packing LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. If it is stuck to the body, be sure to peel it all off.
    2. Once removed, be sure to replace it with a service tail light (rear combination light) body packing LH, as it may cause water leakage.
    3. Also replace the round packing.



2. Remove Back-up Light Bulb



  1. Turn in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove the back-up light bulb together with the tail light (rear combination light) socket & wire RH.

  2. Remove the back-up light bulb from the tail light (rear combination light) socket & wire RH.



3. Remove Rear Turn Signal Light Bulb



  1. Turn in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and remove the rear turn signal light bulb together with the tail light (rear combination light) socket & wire RH.

  2. Remove the rear turn signal light bulb from the tail light (rear combination light) socket & wire RH.



4. Remove Rear Combination Light Socket & Wire Sub-assembly RH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Disengage the claw and remove the tail light (rear combination light) socket & wire RH.



5. Remove Tail Light (Rear Combination Light) Lens & Body LH
