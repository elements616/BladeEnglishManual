Engine Mounting Insulator RH (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



2. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly



  1. Place both hands on the front of the cover and lift it upward to disengage the 2 front clips, then continue lifting upward to disengage the 1 rear clips, and remove the V-bank cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pulling up the front and rear at the same time may cause damage.



3. Remove Engine Mounting Stay NO.2 RH



  1. Remove the bolt and 2 nuts, and remove engine mounting stay NO.2 RH.



4. Disconnect Radiator Reserve Tank Assembly



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the radiator reserve tank assembly.

    □ HINT □
    Without disconnecting the hoses, suspend the radiator reserve tank assembly from the body with a string or similar.



5. Remove Front Tire RH


6. Remove Engine Under Cover Rear RH

  1. Remove the 5 clips and the engine under cover rear RH.



7. Disconnect Engine Mounting Insulator Sub-assembly RH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 clamps, and disconnect the ground wire.




  2. Remove the 2 bolts and nut, and remove the cooler pipe bracket.




  3. Support the engine assembly with a transmission jack using a wooden block or similar.




  4. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 nuts, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator RH from the engine mounting bracket RH.



8. Remove Engine Mounting Insulator Sub-assembly RH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the engine mounting insulator RH.
