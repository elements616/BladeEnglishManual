Engine Oil Pressure Switch Assembly (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Engine Under Cover NO.1

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 7 clips, and remove engine under cover No.1.



2. Remove Engine Oil Pressure Switch Assembly

  1. Disconnect the engine oil pressure switch assembly connector.




  2. Using a deep socket wrench 24mm, remove the engine oil pressure switch assembly.
