ABS with EBD & TRC & VSC & BA System DTC Check / Clear

Print



  1. Check DTC Output

    1. Reading DTCs Using the SST (TaSCAN)


      1. Turn the IG OFF and connect the SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

        SST
        09991-70200  

      2. Turn the IG ON and read the DTCs following the display screen of the SST (TaSCAN).

        ■ NOTICE ■
        If there is a malfunction, clear the stored DTCs after repair.




    2. Reading DTCs Using the Meter Display


      1. Turn IG OFF.

      2. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) ←→ 4 (CG) of DLC3.

        SST
        09843-18040  

        ■ NOTICE ■
        1. Do not connect the connector in the wrong position, as this will cause a malfunction.
        2. Perform this with the vehicle stopped.

      3. Turn IG ON.

      4. Read the DTCs displayed on the multi-information display.

      5. Turn the IG OFF and disconnect terminals 13 (TC) ←→ 4 (CG) of DLC3.

        ■ NOTICE ■
        If there is a malfunction, clear the stored DTCs after repair.



    1. Clear DTCs and Freeze Frame Data

      1. DTC Clearing Method (TaSCAN)


        1. Turn the IG OFF and connect the SST (TaSCAN) to DLC3.

          SST
          09991-70200  

        2. Turn the IG ON and clear the DTCs following the display screen of the SST (TaSCAN).

          □ HINT □
          If the malfunction persists, the DTCs may not be cleared.




      2. DTC Clearing Method (DLC3 Short)


        1. Turn IG OFF.

        2. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) ←→ 4 (CG) of DLC3.

          SST
          09843-18040  

          ■ NOTICE ■
          Do not connect the connector in the wrong position, as this will cause a malfunction.

        3. Turn the IG ON and depress the brake pedal 8 times or more within 5 seconds.

        4. Check that the normal code is output.

          □ HINT □
          If the malfunction persists, the DTCs may not be cleared.
