Circuit Description



  1. This VVT system performs advance and retard control of the intake and exhaust camshafts to provide the optimal valve timing for each driving condition. This control is performed based on conditions such as intake air volume, throttle position and engine coolant temperature. Based on the signals from these sensors, the engine control computer adjusts the oil control valves; as a result, the relative position of the camshafts is optimized, improving fuel economy, output and emission performance. The engine control computer also detects the actual valve timing using the signal from the camshaft position sensor and performs feedback control to achieve the target valve timing.
