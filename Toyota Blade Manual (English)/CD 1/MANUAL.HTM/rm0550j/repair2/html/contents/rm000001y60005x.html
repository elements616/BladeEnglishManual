Engine Mounting Insulator LH (2GR-FE) Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Check Front Wheels Straight-ahead Position


2. Remove Radiator Upper Air Deflector



  1. Remove the 9 clips and the radiator upper air deflector.



3. Remove V-bank Cover Sub-assembly



  1. Place both hands on the front of the cover and lift it upward to disengage the 2 front clips, then continue lifting upward to disengage the 1 rear clips, and remove the V-bank cover.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Pulling up the front and rear at the same time may cause damage.



4. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


5. Remove Battery



  1. Loosen the nut and disconnect the battery positive terminal. (*1)

  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and remove the battery clamp.




  3. Disconnect the current sensor connector.

  4. Remove the insulator.

  5. Remove the battery.



6. Remove Battery Carrier



  1. Remove the battery tray.

  2. Disengage the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness from the battery carrier.

  3. Remove the 4 bolts and the battery carrier.



7. Remove Battery Carrier NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and battery carrier NO.2.



8. Remove Air Cleaner Cap w/ Air Cleaner Hose



  1. Disconnect the air flow meter connector. (*1).

  2. Disconnect ventilation hose No.2. (*2).

  3. Disconnect the hose band (*3)

  4. Disengage the 3 clamps and remove the air cleaner cap w/ air cleaner hose.



9. Remove Air Cleaner Filter Element Sub-assembly

  1. Remove the air cleaner filter element from the air cleaner case.



10. Remove Air Cleaner Case



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and clamp, and remove the air cleaner case.



11. Remove Engine Mounting Insulator LH



  1. Support the engine assembly w/ transaxle with a transmission jack using a wooden block or similar.




  2. Remove the bolt and nut, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator LH from the engine mounting bracket LH.

  3. Operate the transmission jack to lower the engine assembly w/ transaxle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not lower the engine assembly w/ transaxle more than necessary.




  4. Remove the 4 bolts and remove the engine mounting insulator LH.
