DTC B1837 Curtain Shield Airbag LH Squib Circuit (Short to Ground)

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. The curtain shield airbag LH squib circuit consists of the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag LH. When the activation conditions are met, the curtain shield airbag LH is activated.
  2. When a short to ground is detected in the curtain shield airbag LH squib circuit (ICL+, ICL- lines), DTC B1837 is stored in the airbag computer assembly.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Item
Trouble Area
B1837
  1. Short to ground in ICL+, ICL- lines of the wire harness between the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag LH
  2. Curtain shield airbag LH malfunction
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly Malfunction
  1. Floor wire (between airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag LH)
  2. Curtain Shield Airbag LH
  3. Airbag Computer Assembly

Inspection Procedure


□ HINT □
When performing the simulation method, use TaSCAN to select check mode first. (see)


Step 1Connector Inspection

□ HINT □
For the inspection procedure and precautions, seeReference

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal and wait for 90 seconds.

    □ HINT □
    Before disconnecting the cable from the negative battery terminal, check that the ignition switch is off.

  1. Check the connection condition of the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag LH.

    Specified Condition
    The connector is connected properly

  1. Disconnect the connectors connected to the airbag computer assembly and curtain shield airbag LH, and check the condition of the terminals of each connector.

    Specified Condition
    The terminals are not deformed or damaged

  1. Check the condition of the short pins of the connectors with a short mechanism on the floor wire.

    Specified Condition
    The short pins are not deformed or damaged
