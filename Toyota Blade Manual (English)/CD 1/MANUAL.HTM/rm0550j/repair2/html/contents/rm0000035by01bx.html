DTC B15E7 Hard Disk Read/Write Malfunction (Low Temperature)

Print

DTC B15E8 Hard Disk Read/Write Malfunction (High Temperature)

Preparation List


Function Description


  1. This DTC is recorded when read/write errors for the HDD occur a specified number of times or more in a temperature environment above (below) the HDD operating range.

    DTC.No.
    		Diagnosis Description
    		Inspection Point
    B15E7
    		HDD read/write malfunction due to low temperature
    		Navigation Receiver Assembly
    B15E8
    		HDD read/write malfunction due to high temperature


Inspection Procedure


□ HINT □
Depending on the part replaced, setting work may be necessary. (see)


Step 1Check Environment

  1. Place the vehicle in a normal temperature environment and bring the cabin temperature to -20°C or higher and 65°C or lower.

    □ HINT □
    1. The HDD may not be usable at high or low temperatures.
    2. Clear and recheck the DTCs in a normal temperature environment.
