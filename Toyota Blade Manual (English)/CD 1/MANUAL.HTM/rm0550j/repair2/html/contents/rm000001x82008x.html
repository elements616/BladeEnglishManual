Rear Combination Light Assembly LH Disassembly

Print


1. Remove Rear Combination Light Body Packing LH



  1. Remove the rear combination light body packing LH.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. If it is stuck to the body, be sure to peel it all off.
    2. Once removed, be sure to replace it with a service rear combination light body packing LH, as it may cause water leakage.
    3. Also replace the round packing.



2. Remove Back-up Light Bulb



  1. Turn in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and separate the back-up light bulb together with the rear combination light socket & wire sub-assembly LH.

  2. Remove the back-up light bulb from the rear combination light socket & wire sub-assembly LH.



3. Remove Rear Turn Signal Light Bulb



  1. Turn in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration and separate the rear turn signal light bulb together with the rear combination light socket & wire sub-assembly LH.

  2. Remove the rear turn signal light bulb from the rear combination light socket & wire sub-assembly LH.



4. Remove Rear Combination Light Socket & Wire Sub-assembly LH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  2. Disengage the claw and clamp, and remove the rear combination light socket & wire sub-assembly LH.



5. Remove Tail Light (Rear Combination Light) Lens & Body LH
