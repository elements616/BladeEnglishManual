Function Description


The skid control computer (built into the actuator) receives the signals of the engine control computer, steering sensor and yaw rate sensor via CAN communication.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
DTC Output Related Items
U0073/94
  1. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, a delay in receiving data from the deceleration sensor, yaw rate sensor or steering sensor continues for 1 seconds or more
  2. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, a condition in which a delay in receiving data from the deceleration sensor, yaw rate sensor or steering sensor occurs 1 times or more within 5 seconds occurs 10 times or more within 60 seconds
  3. Transmission is not completed for 5 seconds or more after output from the skid control computer is completed
  4. A condition in which a bus off state occurs 1 times or more within 100ms occurs 10 times consecutively
Skid control computer CAN communication line
U0100/65
With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher and a vehicle speed of 15km/h or more, data cannot be sent to or received from the engine control computer for 2 seconds or more
Communication line between skid control computer and engine control computer
(CAN communication line)
U0123/62
  1. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, data cannot be received from the yaw rate sensor for 1 seconds or more
  2. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, a condition in which data cannot be received from the yaw rate sensor 1 times or more within 5 seconds occurs 10 times consecutively
Communication line between skid control computer and yaw rate sensor
(CAN communication line)
U0124/95
  1. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, data cannot be received from the deceleration sensor for 1 seconds or more
  2. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, a condition in which data cannot be received from the deceleration sensor 1 times or more within 5 seconds occurs 10 times consecutively
Communication line between skid control computer and deceleration sensor
(CAN communication line)
U0126/63
  1. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, data cannot be received from the steering sensor for 1 seconds or more
  2. With IG1 terminal voltage at 10to17.4V or higher, a condition in which data cannot be received from the steering sensor 1 times or more within 5 seconds occurs 10 times consecutively
Communication line between skid control computer and steering sensor
(CAN communication line)
