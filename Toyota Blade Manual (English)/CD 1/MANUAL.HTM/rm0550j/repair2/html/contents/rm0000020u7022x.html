Shift Lock Control Unit Assembly Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Disconnect Battery Negative Terminal
(see)


2. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End RH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a moulding remover and move it to near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the moulding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.




  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end RH.



3. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel End LH



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

  2. Insert a moulding remover and move it to near the clip engagement as shown in the illustration.




  3. As shown in the illustration, pull the moulding remover with both hands in the direction of the arrow to disengage the clip.

  4. Disengage the 3 clips and remove the instrument panel finish panel end LH.



4. Remove Shift Lever Knob



  1. Turn the shift lever knob in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration to remove it.



5. Remove Position Indicator Housing Assembly



  1. As shown in the illustration, using a moulding remover, disengage the 3 claws.




  2. Disengage the 6 claws.

  3. Disconnect the connector and remove the position indicator housing assembly.



6. Remove Box Panel NO.2



  1. Apply protective tape to the positions shown in the illustration.

    □ HINT □
    Apply protective tape on the RH side in the same way.

  2. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 clips.




  3. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 2 claws.

  4. Disconnect each connector and remove box panel No.2.



7. Remove Instrument Cluster Finish Panel Assembly Center NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 6 clips and 4 claws, and remove instrument cluster finish panel assembly center No.1.



8. Remove Console Box Cover RR



  1. Disengage the 4 clips and 2 guides, and remove the console box cover rear.



9. Remove Console Box Carpet



  1. Remove the console box carpet.



10. Remove Console Box Assembly RR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.




  2. Disengage the 4 guides and remove the console box assembly rear.



11. Remove Console Box Insert FR NO.2



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove console box insert FR No.2.



12. Remove Console Box Insert



  1. Disengage the 3 claws.

  2. Disengage the guide and remove the console box insert.



13. Remove Box Bottom Mat


14. Remove Instrument Panel Under Tray



  1. Using a flat-tipped screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 4 claws and remove the instrument panel under tray.



15. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel Lower NO.1



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Disengage the 4 claws and 2 guides.




  3. Pull in the direction of the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove instrument panel finish panel lower No.1.



16. Remove Instrument Panel Finish Panel Lower NO.2



  1. Remove the 2 screws <C>.




  2. Pull in the direction of the arrow in the illustration to disengage the 2 clips and guide, and remove instrument panel finish panel lower No.2.



17. Disconnect Transmission Control Cable Assembly

  1. Move the shift lever to the N position.




  2. Disconnect the cable end from the shift lock control unit assembly.




  3. Using a thin-bladed screwdriver, pull out the stopper of the transmission control cable assembly, turn the nut approximately 180° counterclockwise, and while holding the nut, disconnect the transmission control cable assembly from the shift lever retainer.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    1. If the nut is turned too far, the internal spring will come off the nut and the transmission control cable assembly cannot be reused; therefore, do not turn it too far.
    2. Do not remove the stopper. If it has been removed, reinstall it in the same position.



18. Remove Shift Lever Assembly



  1. Disconnect the 2 clamps and 2 connectors.




  2. Remove the 4 nuts and remove the shift lever assembly.
