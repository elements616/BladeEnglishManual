DTC B2060/41 VSC Malfunction

Preparation List


Circuit Description


  1. This DTC is output when a malfunction occurs in the brake system.
  2. This circuit consists of the ABS & traction actuator assembly and seat belt control computer, and receives information from the ABS & traction actuator assembly via CAN communication.


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
Inspection Area
B2060/41
Brake system malfunction is detected
  1. ABS with EBD & TRC & VSC & BA system
  2. Seat Belt Control Computer
