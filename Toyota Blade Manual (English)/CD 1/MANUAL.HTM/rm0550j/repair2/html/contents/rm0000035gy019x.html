DTC B15E1 Media Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Function Description


This DTC is output when the signal from the media inserted into the navigation receiver assembly cannot be read.


DTC.No.
Diagnosis Description
Inspection Area
B15E1
  1. Media is not recognized
  2. Cannot play
  3. Sound skips or noise occurs
  1. Media
  2. Navigation Receiver Assembly

Step 3Check Media for Dirt

  1. Clean Media

    1. Check the media for dirt, and clean it by wiping lightly in a radial direction from the center outward with a soft, dry cloth such as an eyeglass cloth for plastic lenses.

      ■ NOTICE ■
      1. Pressing hard by hand or rubbing with a stiff cloth may scratch the surface.
      2. Using solvents such as record spray, antistatic agents, alcohol, benzine or thinner, or chemically treated cloths, may damage the disc and make it unusable.

    2. Insert the cleaned media and check whether it can be played.
