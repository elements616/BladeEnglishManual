Rear Seat Outer Belt Assembly Center Removal

Print

Preparation List



1. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly


2. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Assembly CTR


3. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.1



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.1.



4. Remove Rear Seat Cushion Hinge Cover NO.2



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat cushion hinge cover NO.2.



5. Remove Rear Seat Assembly RH



  1. Remove the 2 front side bolts of the rear seat assembly RH.




  2. Fold the rear seat assembly RH forward.

  3. Remove the 2 rear side bolts and remove the rear seat assembly RH.



6. Disconnect Rear Seat Outer Belt Assembly Center



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear seat outer belt assembly center together with the rear seat inner belt assembly.




  2. Remove the washer and rear seat outer belt assembly center from the rear seat inner belt assembly.



7. Remove Rear Seatback Assembly RH



  1. Using a Torx socket wrench T45, remove the 2 Torx bolts and 2 bushings, and remove the rear seatback assembly RH.



8. Remove Center Armrest Hinge Cover



  1. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in protective tape, disengage the claws and remove the center armrest hinge cover by sliding it as shown in the illustration.



9. Remove Rear Seat Armrest Assembly CTR



  1. Remove the bolt and bushing.

  2. Pull the rear seat armrest assembly CTR off the pin and remove it.




  3. Remove the bushing from the rear seatback assembly RH.



10. Remove Rear Seat Lock Control Lever w/ Base



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  2. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the rear seatback lock control lever w/ base.



11. Remove Rear Seat Shoulder Belt Hole Cover



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seat shoulder belt hole cover.



12. Remove Rear Seatback Lock Cover RH



  1. Remove the 2 screws and remove the rear seatback lock cover RH.



13. Remove Rear Seatback Cover



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the rear seatback cover.



14. Remove Rear Seatback Board Carpet Assembly RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.




  2. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.




  3. Disengage each J hook and remove the rear seatback board carpet assembly RH.



15. Remove Rear Seat Headrest Support



  1. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the 2 rear seat headrest supports.



16. Remove Separate Type Rear Seatback Cover RH

  1. Remove the separate type rear seatback cover w/ pad from the rear seatback frame sub-assembly RH.




  2. Remove the 8 hog rings.




  3. Remove the 10 hog rings and remove the separate type rear seatback cover RH.



17. Remove Rear Seat Outer Belt Assembly Center



  1. Remove the nut and remove the rear seat outer belt assembly center.
