1. LED Check

  1. LED Illumination Inspection

    1. Check that each indicator/warning light comes on for the time below when the IG is turned OFF → ON.

      Specified Condition


      Indicator/Warning Name
      		Illumination Specification
      Brake, ABS, slip, automatic leveling, P/S (EMPS), eco drive
      		Light comes on for approximately 3 seconds at IG OFF → ON
      Airbag
      		Light comes on for approximately 6 seconds at IG OFF → ON
      Check engine, charge, 4WD AUTO
      		Light comes on at IG OFF → ON

      □ HINT □
      1. Except for the eco drive indicator, the illumination request is made by the other ECU. (The other ECU is)
      2. Inspect the brake warning light with the parking brake released.

4. Oil Pressure Warning Inspection

  1. Operation Check

    1. Disconnect the engine oil pressure switch assembly connector.

    2. Start the engine.

    3. Using SST, check the display in the combination meter assembly when the engine oil pressure switch assembly vehicle side wire harness connector terminal is shorted to body ground.

      SST
      09843-18040  

      Specified Condition
      "Low Engine Oil Pressure" is displayed on the multi-information display.
