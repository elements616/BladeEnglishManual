Steering Lock System Precaution

Print



    1. Precautions When Checking DTCs

    1. The steering lock ECU does not store past DTCs, so if a code is being output, do not turn the IG OFF or clear the codes until checking and recording are complete.

    2. When checking Smart Entry & Start System related DTCs or the data monitor with the IG OFF, repeatedly turn any door courtesy switch ON/OFF after connecting the tool to the vehicle until communication between the tool and the vehicle starts. (ON/OFF interval within 1.5 seconds)



      2. Precautions for Battery Disconnection and Low Voltage

      1. If the battery negative terminal has been disconnected or the voltage has dropped, the steering lock may not be released and the engine may not start unless the driver side door is opened and closed after the battery condition is restored.



        3. Precautions When Replacing Related Parts

        1. If the certification ECU has been replaced, the steering lock may not be released and the engine may not start unless the driver side door is opened and closed. (This makes the certification ECU store the current steering lock position)
