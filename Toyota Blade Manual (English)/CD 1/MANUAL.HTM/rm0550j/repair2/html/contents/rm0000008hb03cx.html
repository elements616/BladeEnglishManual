Back Guide Monitor System Precaution

Print



    1. Precautions for Vehicle Servicing

    1. When the battery terminal is disconnected, perform steering sensor neutral point memorization. (see)

    2. When the steering sensor is removed and installed or the steering sensor connector is disconnected, perform steering sensor neutral point memorization. (see)

    3. Depending on the parts replaced or the work performed during vehicle inspection and maintenance, registration and setting operations for other systems as well as the back guide monitor system may be necessary. (see)

    4. If the lens of the television camera assembly is dirty, a clear image cannot be obtained, so rinse it with water and then wipe it with a soft cloth. If it is very dirty, wash it off with a neutral detergent.



      2. Precautions for Television Camera Assembly

      1. Handling Precautions


        1. If the camera section of the television camera assembly is subjected to a strong impact, such as being hit hard or struck by an object, the back guide monitor system may not operate properly.

        2. Rubbing the cover (resin) of the television camera assembly hard may scratch it and adversely affect the image. Also, organic solvents, wax, oil film removers, glass coating agents, etc. may cause the resin to crack, so if any of these adhere, wipe them off immediately and rinse with water.

        3. If the television camera assembly is subjected to a sudden temperature change (such as pouring hot water on it in cold weather), the back guide monitor system may not operate properly.

        4. If the lens of the television camera assembly is dirty with water droplets, snow, mud, etc., a clear image cannot be obtained, so rinse it with water and then wipe it with a soft cloth. If it is very dirty, wash it off with a neutral detergent.

      2. When the image is difficult to see even when the system is normal


        1. Noise may appear in the image when adjusting the mirror surfaces of the driver side and front passenger side outer rear view mirrors.

        2. Noise may appear in the image depending on the electrical products used in the vehicle.

        3. Noise may appear in the image depending on aftermarket devices that emit radio waves.

        4. When the screen is cold. (Immediately after IG ON, the image may leave trails or be darker than normal)

        5. When foreign matter adheres to the camera lens surface.

        6. When the camera lens surface is covered by foreign matter, etc.

        7. When strong light such as sunlight or headlights shines on the camera.

        8. When the area around the camera is dark. (At night, etc.)

        9. When the temperature near the camera is high or low.

        10. When the vehicle or road is inclined.

        11. Depending on the surrounding brightness.

          □ HINT □
          When a high-brightness spot (such as sunlight reflected off the vehicle body) is captured by the camera, the smear phenomenon peculiar to CCD cameras (vertical trailing of the image) may occur.
