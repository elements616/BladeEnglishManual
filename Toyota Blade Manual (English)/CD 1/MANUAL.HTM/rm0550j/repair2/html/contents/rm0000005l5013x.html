DTC B1423/23 Refrigerant Pressure Malfunction

Print

Preparation List


Circuit Description


This code is output when the refrigerant pressure is abnormally low (0.176MPa{1.8kgf/cm2} or less) or abnormally high (3.025MPa{30.9kgf/cm2} or more).

The air conditioner tube & accessory assembly (pressure sensor) is installed on the high-pressure side piping to detect refrigerant pressure, and outputs the refrigerant pressure as a signal to the air conditioner amplifier assembly. The air conditioner amplifier assembly converts the signal into pressure data according to the sensor characteristics and controls the compressor.

However, if the diagnostic trouble code is no longer output after this work, it indicates that the dryer in the condenser is unable to absorb moisture in the refrigerant cycle, so the dryer must be replaced.
