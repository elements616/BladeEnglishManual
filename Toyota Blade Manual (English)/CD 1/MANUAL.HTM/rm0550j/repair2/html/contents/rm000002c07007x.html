3. Remove Steering Wheel Cover Lower NO.3



  1. Steering Wheel Assembly 3 Spoke:

    1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove steering wheel cover LWR No.3.




  2. Steering Wheel Assembly 4 Spoke:

    1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove steering wheel cover LWR No.3.



4. Remove Steering Wheel Cover Lower NO.2



  1. Steering Wheel Assembly 3 Spoke:

    1. Disengage the 3 claws and remove steering wheel cover LWR No.2.




  2. Steering Wheel Assembly 4 Spoke:

    1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove steering wheel cover LWR No.2.



5. Remove Horn Button Assembly

■ WARNING ■
When storing the horn button assembly, even temporarily, always keep the deployment surface facing up.

  1. Make sure that the ignition switch is OFF.

  2. Check that the battery negative terminal is disconnected.

    ■ WARNING ■
    After disconnecting the battery negative terminal, do not start work for 90 seconds.



  3. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, push in the 2 torsion springs shown in the illustration and disengage the 2 pins.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not drop the horn button assembly.




  4. Pull the horn button assembly toward the rear of the vehicle.

    ■ NOTICE ■
    Do not pull the wiring when pulling out the horn button assembly.




  5. Using a thin flat-tip screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, pull up the lock of the airbag connector to release it.

    Text in Illustrations 
    *1
    		Protective Tape

  6. Disconnect the airbag connector and horn connector, and remove the horn button assembly.



6. Remove Steering Pad Switch LH

  1. Disconnect the connector from the spiral cable.




  2. Remove the 2 screws.

  3. Disengage the 2 pins and claw, and remove the steering pad switch LH from the steering wheel assembly.



7. Remove Steering Pad Switch Assembly

  1. Disconnect the connector from the spiral cable.




  2. Remove the 2 screws.

  3. Disengage the 2 pins and claw, and remove the steering pad switch LH side from the steering wheel assembly.




  4. Remove the 2 screws.

  5. Disengage the 2 pins and claw, and remove the steering pad switch assembly from the steering wheel assembly.
