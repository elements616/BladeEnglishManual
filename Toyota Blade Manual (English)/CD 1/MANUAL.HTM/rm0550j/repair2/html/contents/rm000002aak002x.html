Back Door Lock Assembly Inspection

Print

Preparation List



1. Inspect Back Door Lock Assembly



  1. Push the latch of the back door lock assembly with a flat-tipped screwdriver or equivalent to put it in the locked (full latch) state.

  2. Inspect the door lock operation when battery voltage is applied to each terminal of the connector.

    Specified Condition

    Battery connection
    		Door Lock Operation
    Battery positive → 2 (ACT+)
    Battery negative → 1 (ACT-)
    		Locked (full latch) → Unlatched
